## Every week keeps

- Our week number and our book title(s) from `MAP.md`
- The four marks line, word for word
- Read this week: book chapter titles only (no file paths on the print)
- One job (one sentence the man can say when he leaves)
- Closer, in this order: Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this
- Room for more than one season (young, single, married, kids, older, house that broke)
- NIV when Scripture is quoted, cited

## Leader guide

Short enough to run in about an hour. Not a sermon.

1. Before the room: what he reads, one aim, one pitfall (do not lecture the book back)
2. Open (2-3 min): sit down, why this week
3. Hit: the one job, in Pike's words, drawn from the signed pages
4. Table: three questions, honest, not a quiz
5. Run the closer: Name it, write Do this week, send Tell a brother, say Carry this
6. Solo note: if he is alone, he still writes the worksheet and texts one man
