Before the room

Read this week
Chapter 15, The problem inside.
Aim
The problem is in you. Not only what was done to you. He needs to be made new, not coached into looking fine.
One job
Name the thing you keep blaming. Say the true sentence this week: the problem is also in me.
Pitfall
Do not reprint the last twelve weeks. Do not dump how that looks on a man. Do not preview two Adams. Kind: the wound is real. Do not let the old story be the only author of the week. A wife is not the problem. A clean résumé is not a clean heart.
Time
about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the inside.

Romans 7:18 NIV

For I know that good itself does not dwell in me, that is, in my sinful nature. For I have the desire to do what is good, but I cannot carry it out.

Open (2-3 min)

He can leave a house, walk with a man, and still wreck the next room. Then he reaches for the old story. If they had been better, he would be better. Some of that is true. It is not the whole wound.

Say the four. Sit down.

Hit

You are not taking back the empty years. You are refusing a full alibi.

Paul is not performing despair. He is telling the truth about a man who loves God and still finds a law in himself that will not obey. Desire is not delivery. A clean intention is not a clean heart. If Paul needed to say this, nobody in the room is deep for skipping it.

Hurt explains a lot. It does not author every hour. You can change zip codes. You cannot drop yourself off at the last apartment. The world likes a simpler math: people are basically good, the mess is what happened to them. Some environments are cruel and should be named. The Book will not let you stop there. Something in us leans the wrong way even in a kind house.

Sinful nature is Paul's word. Do not swap it for a softer one. A man who will only be the victim cannot be forgiven for what he actually did. Wounds get tending. Sin gets a cross. You will need both.

The gospel belongs here or this page ends in despair. Good does not dwell in me, that is, in my sinful nature. Christ does. A better childhood would have helped. It would not have made him good. A new heart is a gift. You cannot coach it out of a résumé. Point at Chapter 15. Then shut up.

Table

Three. Wait.

  1. 1.What do you keep blaming so you do not have to say the problem is also in you?
  2. 2.Where did wanting to be good fail you this week?
  3. 3.Can you say the sentence out loud: the problem is also in me?

Young: you are not only your dad; a sharp mouth in a car with friends is also you. Single: do not draft a future woman as the problem or the priest. Married: she is not the problem; this page is his. Hurt: naming the inside does not make the wound fake; you were sinned against, and you also sin. Older: a polished account of what was done to him and a thin account of this week is still hiding. The job going well: trusted with money is not the same as well.

Run the closer

One blame. One sentence. Seven nights.

Name it
What do you keep blaming so you do not have to say the problem is also in you? Then say the word about yourself, specific: "I sinned. I ___." Not "I struggle with." The thing you did.
Do this week
Say the true sentence: the problem is also in me. Every night this week, before sleep, take two minutes and name the day's sins to God by name. Then thank Him for the forgiveness that is already yours in Christ. Seven nights.
Tell a brother
Name the blame. Tell him the sentence. Confess one of the sins to him, out loud. Have him ask if you used the old story again. Notice that he does not leave.
Carry this
The problem is in you. Not only what was done to you.

If he only hears the wound, he gets sympathy and keeps the alibi. Get "also in me" on the page.

Solo note

Read Chapter 15. Name the blame. Say "I sinned. I ___." to God. Run the seven nights. Say the sentence to one man the same week, and confess one. You do not have to be finished by Friday. You have to stop using the old wound as the only author of your week.

Do not open week 14.
