Open (2-3 min)

He can tell you 4:30. Ask him what he is for, and watch the fog come in. He has been steering by nearby lights. Those lights are real. They move. A north star does not. Nearby glow gets him through Tuesday. It cannot tell him which way is north.

Say the four. Sit down.

Hit

You are not handing him a brand. You are putting a word over a packed week.

Revelation is a word from God, not a mood you wait on. Without that word, a capable man does not freeze. He unbuckles. Energy with nothing that can judge it. He still pays the bills. He still is liked. He just has no star that can tell a packed week, this is not north.

Restraint is what a heading does to a life. Nearby glow gets a yes: gym, cart, feed, the next win. You do not need a longer list. You need a word over the list. Motion is not a heading. Passivity sits still. This fills the squares. That is why it hides on men other people trust.

A poster is not a north star. A north star is a word you heed. Heed. Not brainstorm. Not wait for the fog to lift. Nearby lights are not evil. Work, a game, a paycheck, a body you can still use: God is not embarrassed. He is after a heading, and a packed week is a great way not to have one. The lights move. The star sits still.

A heading will cost you a yes you like. That is how you know it is a star. Nearby lights never cost you the glow. They only ask for more of it. At 11 the nearby lights are still on. The star is the same as it was at 6. If the heading changes with the last text, it was weather. Point at Chapter 17. Then shut up.

Table

Three. Wait.

  1. 1.What nearby light have you been steering by this week?
  2. 2.What packed thing has been doing the work of a heading?
  3. 3.What one sentence would still sit still if the week stayed full and the road stayed fog?

Young: delayed manhood loves a packed calendar and hates a star. Single: do not park a heading in a woman who is not in the room; write a sentence that would still be true in five years. Married: providing is a load, not the star; she needs him to heed one, not a speech. Hurt: the wound can become the whole map; it is not north. Older: years of competence are not vision; retirement is not a star. The job going well: a title is a nearby light with better parking; write a sentence that survives Saturday.
