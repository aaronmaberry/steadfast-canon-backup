Time

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the fire that does not go out when the feeling cools.

2 Corinthians 5:15 NIV

And he died for all, that those who live should no longer live for themselves but for him who died for them and was raised again.

Open (2-3 min)

Ask what he has been waiting to feel before he will call his week a life for God. Watch him reach for a spark that already died. A talk. A song. A drive that landed. Then a gray week, looking for the next lighter. Feeling is not the fire. The place is still his. The people are still named. His hands still work.

Say the four. Sit down.

Hit

You are not handing him a quiz about what lights him. You are naming who the life is for.

No longer live for themselves. Not wait until it feels like the one. He died. You live. The life is not for you. Living for the feeling of being a man with a cause is still living for yourself. You just dressed it.

A cause that fits inside his preferences is a hobby with a bigger vocabulary. If it cannot tell a week no, it is not a cause. It is weather. Energy has to go somewhere. If it does not go to Him, it will go to a smaller fire. A man whose cause is himself gets smaller. The people in reach live in that weather.

Destiny shows up as place, people, and hands. Where his boots are. Whose names he already knows. What he can actually do. The next thing God put in front of him counts more than the mood that did not arrive. Passivity waits for a feeling to tell him who he is. A man who lives for Him keeps the life when the driveway is just a driveway. You can borrow a feeling. You cannot borrow a life handed to Him.

The test is simple. If the lights went out on his name, would the thing still be worth doing? If not, he has been standing at his own fire. The cause has a name. Jesus. The open question is not what he is on fire about. It is whether he will keep living for himself, or do the next thing for the One who died and was raised. Point at Chapter 20. Then shut up.

Table

Three. Wait.

  1. 1.What fire did you stand at this week that was actually you?
  2. 2.If the lights went out on your name, would that thing still be worth doing?
  3. 3.What is one act for Him this week that does not wait on a feeling?

Young: they told you to find what lights you; you already have a Lord. Single: a wife is not the cause; you are not a man on hold. Married: she is not the sparkler; include her in a life that is for Him. Hurt: helping men who got what you got can be holy, and it can be the wound still running the show. Older: nostalgia is borrowed fire with better lighting; retirement is not a cause. The job going well: an offsite mission is not Him; would the fire remain if the badge came off.
