Before the room

Read this week
Chapter 10, The Father who does not leave.
Aim
God is a Father who stays. The empty chair is not the last word. Receive Him.
One job
Tell God the true sentence. Receive one word from Him as Father this week.
Pitfall
Do not reprint the chair as the whole hour. Do not tour the fruit. Do not open mother. Do not use God as a gag for the wound. Do not fake a childhood so He can be Father. Kind. Receiving is the hour.
Time
about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. This week is who is here.

Matthew 3:17 NIV

And a voice from heaven said, "This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased."

Open (2-3 min)

A boy in a dugout scans the bleachers after a strikeout. He is looking for one face. Some men are still scanning at thirty-eight. There is a Father you can stop scanning for. He was in the bleachers before you looked.

Say the four. Sit down.

Hit

This is not another inventory of what was missing. This is who is here.

You have been reading God through your dad. Every man does. Empty chair, silent chair, dangerous chair, or good with a gap: you held that picture up to heaven. The Bible flips it. Read your dad through God. Anyone who has seen Jesus has seen the Father.

Before the Son preached a sermon or healed a soul, the Father spoke. My Son. Whom I love. With him I am well pleased. Blessing first, then the work. If needing that voice is weakness, the beloved Son heard it anyway. You are not more mature than He is for going without.

Receiving means you stop running the experiment where a human man finally becomes God. He will not. Some of you still have one you can call. That call is not this page. This page is the Father who does not leave.

God is not a trick to make the hurt stop by Friday. He stays in it. He also does not share the throne with the hole. You can tell Him the truth and still belong to Him. Point at Chapter 10. Then shut up.

Table

Three. Wait.

  1. 1.Which picture of "father" have you been holding up to heaven: not coming, in the room but silent, dangerous, or good with a gap?
  2. 2.What does Jesus show the Father is actually like?
  3. 3.What would it mean, this week, to receive one word from Him as Father, not to feel it first?

Young: learn this now; do not wait for a family to start being a son. Single: a wife will not say the sentence He is saying. Married: she cannot be your Father; let her be a wife. Hurt: the word was used badly; God is not him. Older: it is not too late to be somebody's son. The job going well: applause is not "I am well pleased."

Run the closer

One picture. One word. Seven mornings.

Name it
Which picture of "father" have you been holding up to heaven? Say it. Then say what Jesus shows the Father is actually like.
Do this week
Every morning before your feet hit the floor, say out loud: "Father." Just the word. Then one sentence to Him about the day. Seven days in a row. Tell Him the true sentence about the chair once this week. Receive one word: Son. Loved. Pleased. Or what He says.
Tell a brother
Tell him which false father you have been praying to. Ask him to pray for you, out loud, right there, and let him. Being prayed over is practice at being carried.
Carry this
You've been reading God through your dad. Read your dad through God. Anyone who has seen Jesus has seen the Father.

If a brother rushes the hurt, ask him to sit still. The Father stays. So can a brother, for an hour.

Solo note

Read Chapter 10. Say Father out loud in the truck. Tell Him the true sentence. Sit with Matthew 3:17. Receive one word. Feeling is not the door. Receiving is.

Do not open week 11.
