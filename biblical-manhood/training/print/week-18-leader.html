Before the room

Read this week
Chapter 21, If there is no wife yet, and Chapter 22, Know her.
Aim
Marriage is good. It is not the finish line. Then know her. Knowing her is a job, not a memory.
One job
Name the thing you parked as not-yet. Then ask one real question this week and stay for the answer.
Pitfall
Two chapters, one hour. Do not turn it into a couples class. Singles are in the rule, not a cameo. Do not reprint last week's fire, the fork, the first morning, last week's lights, or last week's three ways. Do not dump wait as the whole night. Do not preview the next house pages. Kind: yet is not a taunt. A girlfriend is not the stamp.
Time
about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone. Two chapters. Not two sermons.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the house he already has, then the woman he has been guessing at.

1 Corinthians 7:17 NIV

Nevertheless, each person should live as a believer in whatever situation the Lord has assigned to them, just as God has called them. This is the rule I lay down in all the churches.

Proverbs 20:5 NIV

The purposes of a person's heart are deep waters, but one who has insight draws them out.

Open (2-3 min)

A garment bag still on the hook after a good wedding. He lives like the real life is the one with a first dance. Then a photo on the fridge. She is laughing at something out of the frame. He thinks the still is current.

Say the four. Sit down.

Hit

Assigned. Called. Live as a believer in it.

Paul is not stalling the men who will marry. He is not putting the unmarried in the lobby. The situation already has a Lord over it. A ring is a gift. It is not a promotion into manhood. If he needs a wedding to start, he will need another season to keep going. There is always a later house. The ache is real. Loneliness is not proof he is unfinished as a man. Pain can tell the truth about wanting a wife. Pain can also talk him into sitting his own life out.

Marriage is good. It is not the finish line. If he is walking toward a woman, character now. Not a hunt. Not a project she is supposed to complete. If he is married, he can still live like the house starts later. Same bag. Different paperwork. Stop talking past his brother. If every word starts with "your wife," he has made a doorway out of a gift.

Then the woman who already lives here. Some of you do not have her in the house. Stay. You are not a guest. You are also not the whole page.

Deep waters. Not a form from the first year. Insight draws them out. Draws. That is work. Passivity loves a finished picture. A still cannot ask anything of you. A living woman can. Logistics are real. They are not her purposes. The verse does not say the wise man finished. It says he draws. Present tense. If he studied her in the first year and then graduated, he knew a semester.

Some of you can read a room and cannot read her. That is a man who spends his insight where it pays him back. Some of you think she is not that deep. That sentence is about you. You have not gone down far enough to find anything that would cost you. Point at both chapters. Then shut up.

Table

Three. Wait.

  1. 1.What have you been treating as not-yet because a wife is not in it?
  2. 2.What have you been guessing at that you should have studied?
  3. 3.What is one real question this week, and will you stay for the answer?

Young: a girlfriend is not the stamp, and she is not a puzzle you crack so the life can start. Single: this is your page; marriage is good; you may marry, you may not; either way you are not on deck; if you are walking toward a woman, study a person, not a project. Married: the photos were not the finish; she already knows if you are a student or a visitor; last year's file is how you share a house with a stranger. Hurt: go slow; yet is not a taunt; if she left, this is not a courtroom; if she died, honor is not a fight with a year that cannot answer; if she never came, you are not a guest. Older: never-married is not a mascot; thirty years is not the same as current; do not retire from being a student. The job going well: a beautiful place can still be a hotel; you would not brief a client on last year's notes and then walk into your own kitchen on them.

Run the closer

One parked thing. One question. Stay.

Name it
What did you park as not-yet? What have you been guessing?
Do this week
Inhabit the parked thing once, as a man already assigned. Then ask one real question. Stay for the answer. Write what you heard.
Tell a brother
Name the parked thing. Tell him the question. Have him ask if you lived here, and if you stayed for the answer.
Carry this
Marriage is good. It is not the finish line.

If he only hears that he wants to get married, or that he wants a better marriage, he nods and goes back to the bag, or back to the photo. Get the parked thing and the question on the page.

Solo note

Read Chapter 21 and Chapter 22. Name what you parked. Inhabit it once. Ask one real question of an actual person in reach. Stay for the answer. Tell one man both. You are not finishing a love story by Friday. You are living the situation you already have, and dropping a line into water you have been skimming.
