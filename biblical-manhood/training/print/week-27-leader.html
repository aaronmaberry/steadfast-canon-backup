Open (2-3 min)

He can talk the year in the truck for twenty minutes. At the kitchen table the page stays blank. January notebook. September still page one. Then he closes the book, puts it on the shelf, and goes to bed. Nobody else knows the sentence exists.

Say the four. Sit down.

Hit

Plan the course. That is not playing God. That is being a man with a heart. The Lord establishes the steps. He does not fill in a page you refused to touch. Passivity loves a spoken plan. Spoken plans never fail in the air. They fail on Monday, when nothing was written and nobody can ask.

A blank page is not humility. Writing too much is the same dodge with better stationery. If you cannot say the next move in one sentence, you have a pile. Cut the pile. A wish has no date and no cost. A next move has both. "Be a better man" is a wish. "Tell him on Thursday" is a move.

Week 27Write the next move + These men are my brothers

Sit down tonight. Not in the truck. At a table. One course. One step with a day. Show the page to one man.

Then the men. A written move with no brother is still a speech. A handshake in a lobby is not a brother. A brother is a man who can call you when the week goes bad, and whom you would call. He has seen the page. He has a time. He will ask if the step happened.

Daily. Not when the calendar is kind. Today, while it is still called that. Sin is a liar, and a liar loves a man with a private page. Encourage is two ways. Stay is two ways. Two names is enough to start. One to show the page. One more so the first is not a hero you will exhaust.

Point at both chapters. Then shut up.

Table

Three. Wait.

  1. 1.What have you been giving to the windshield? What is still blank on the table?
  2. 2.Who are the two men? Not a lobby. Not a chat. Two names you would call.
  3. 3.What course and step go on the page tonight, and when will one of those men see it?

Young: the year you are in is already the plan; a group chat is not brothers; write ink tonight and show one name. Single: a later house is not the first page; a future wife is not the brother. Married: the family calendar is not the move; she is not a brother; honor what she has carried, then stay with men who can tell you the truth she should not have to carry alone. Hurt: go slow; write a small true course that includes the wound; some brothers failed you; one true one, one time. Older: a finished story is a cheap plan; advising is not staying; gray hair does not retire you from Today. The job going well: the Q3 deck is not this page; the team at work is not this; show a man who is not on the payroll.

If he only hears that you have thoughts, or that you liked the book, he nods and you drive, or you shelf it. Get the page and the names on the table.
