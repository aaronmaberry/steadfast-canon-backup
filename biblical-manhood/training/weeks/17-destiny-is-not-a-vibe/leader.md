# Week 17. Destiny is not a vibe
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 20, Destiny is not a vibe.

**Aim:** Destiny is not a vibe. It is serving God's purpose in your own generation until it is finished.

**One job:** Name which destiny lie you have been living under, and in one sentence with names, what God's purpose in your own generation looks like.

**Pitfall:** Do not reprint last week's blueprint as the whole hour. Do not write a man plan. Do not preview the house. Kind: the wound is not a calling. A wife is not the cause. Feeling is not the fire.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is assignment, not vibe.

:::margin
**2 Corinthians 5:15 NIV**
And he died for all, that those who live should no longer live for themselves but for him who died for them and was raised again.
:::

---

### Open (2-3 min)

Ask him what he is for. Watch the fog. Destiny is not a vibe. It is not a feeling of significance. It is an assignment, given by God, carried out in a place, in a generation, on ordinary days, and finished.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

You are not discovering a secret life. You are naming the next thing.

Place. People. Hands. This stretch. The first shape. Five clues on one card. Then do one thing from the card before the week is out. Waiting to feel ready is how a man spends a decade under a destiny lie.

Destiny is serving God's purpose in your own generation, in the place He put you, for the people He gave you, until it is finished. No vibe required.

Point at Chapter 20. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. Which destiny lie have you been living under?
2. In one sentence with names, what does God's purpose in your own generation look like?
3. What is one line from the five-clue card you will do this week?

Young: stop waiting for a perfect map. Single: a future wife is not the assignment. Married: names on the card, not a vibe. Hurt: the wound is not a calling. Older: finish the work in this generation. The job going well: early closes are not destiny.

---

### Run the closer

One lie. One card. One act.

**Name it.** Which destiny lie you have been living under, and in one sentence with names, what God's purpose in your own generation looks like. Out loud. Tonight.

**Do this week.** Write five clues on one card: your place, your people, your hands, this stretch, and the first shape, with one line under each. Then do one thing from the card before the week is out.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him what you have been waiting to feel and for how long. Read him the card. Ask him what he sees God's purpose looking like in your life right now.

**Carry this.** Destiny is not a vibe. It is serving God's purpose in your own generation, in the place He put you, for the people He gave you, until it is finished.

If he only hears that he wants more purpose, he nods and lights another wire. Get the card and the act on the page.

---

### Solo note

Read Chapter 20. Name the lie. Write the card. Do one line the same week. Tell one man both. You are not discovering a secret life by Friday. You are putting the lighter down long enough to serve the purpose.

Do not open week 18.
