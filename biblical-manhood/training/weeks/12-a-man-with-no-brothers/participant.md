### Do this week

Brother sentence (full): I am trying to be a better man and I do not have a brother; would you talk with me twenty minutes a week for three months. Set the day and time before you hang up.

Tight season (parking lot / text-sized): Got twenty minutes next week? Trying to walk with a wiser man. Day and time?

Younger toward the man ahead: one real question this week. Go do whatever he tells you.

### Tell a brother

If you have one, tell him about the twenty minutes and the three questions and start. If you do not, the asking is the whole job this week, and it is supposed to feel awkward. Tell him also the question you asked the man ahead and what he said, and let him hold you to doing it and going back to report.

I told (name): _______________   on (day): _______________

He will ask: _______________

Alone / no brother yet: the ask is the whole job. Same week. A hint is not an ask. A parking-lot sentence still counts.

---

### Carry this

Twenty minutes with one man, once a week, at the same time: what happened, what's heavy, what's next, and a prayer. There is a man twenty years ahead of you waiting to be asked. Ask him one real question, and when he says ride with me, go.

Write it once.
