# Week 6. Go first
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 6, Go first.

**Aim:** Go first, even when going first will cost you.

**One job:** Name the room, the people, and the thing that needs to happen first.

**Pitfall:** Do not preach the wait. Do not preach the load. Do not unpack God's pay. Do not baptize a bully. Do not let managing pass as leading. One room. One door. Eight seconds.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the third mark.

:::margin
**John 10:4 NIV**
When he has brought out all his own, he goes on ahead of them, and his sheep follow him because they know his voice.
:::

---

### Open (2-3 min)

Somebody ought to stop. One man opens the door and runs. Then six more. Going first was the whole job.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

You are putting him in front of a true thing he already has.

Leadership is not the head of the table. Watch me. Follow my lead. Do exactly as I do. If he cannot say that about how he treats her, his money, or his temper, he is managing, not leading.

First is exposed. Prayer. Clean apology. Named rubble. Sunday drive. Lobby walk. Prepare the first sentence. Expect the eight seconds alone in the street. Do it on a Tuesday.

Jesus goes on ahead. The LORD himself goes before you. You are never actually first. You are the second door open.

Point at Chapter 6. Then shut up. Do not start week 7.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. Room, people, thing that needs to happen first. What are they?
2. What decent name did you give for holding the door: humility, coaching, letting her lead?
3. What are the opening words, and will you stay through the eight seconds?

Young: ask plainly; stop making her guess. Single: set direction for the life you already have. Married: she should not have to ask for your attention. Hurt: go slow; history is real; go first anyway. Older: initiate with younger men; they will not ask. The job going well: consensus is not courage.

---

### Run the closer

One room. Not a lifestyle speech.

**Name it.** Room, people, thing that needs to happen first. Out loud. Write your opening words.

**Do this week.** One door: prayer, clean apology, named rubble, Sunday drive, or lobby walk. Stay through the eight seconds.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him the exact words you are going to open with. Tell him afterward how it went, including the eight seconds.

**Carry this.** The LORD himself goes before you. You are never actually first. You are the second door open.

If he cannot be asked next week whether he hid again, he told the wrong man.
