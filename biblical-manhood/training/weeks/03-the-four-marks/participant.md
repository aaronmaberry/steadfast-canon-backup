### Tell a brother

Read him the sentence. Ask which mark he would say is faintest on you. Let him finish before you answer.

I told (name): _______________   on (day): _______________

He said the faint mark is: _______________

Alone: still tell one man. Same day.

---

### Carry this

Find the mark that is faintest on you and hold that one for a while.

Write that once.
