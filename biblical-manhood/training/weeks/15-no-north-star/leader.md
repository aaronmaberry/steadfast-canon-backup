# Week 15. No north star
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 17, No north star.

**Aim:** A man with no north star. The wall does not get to argue with the string.

**One job:** Name what you have actually been measuring your life against this year.

**Pitfall:** Do not reprint last week's shapes as the whole hour. Do not dump a life plan. Do not preview destiny. Kind: the wound is not north. A wife is not the star. A packed week is not a heading.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the missing heading.

:::margin
**Proverbs 29:18 NIV**
Where there is no revelation, people cast off restraint; but blessed is the one who heeds wisdom's instruction.
:::

---

### Open (2-3 min)

He can tell you 4:30. Ask him what he is for, and watch the fog come in. He has been steering by nearby lights. Those lights are real. They move. A north star does not. Nearby glow gets him through Tuesday. It cannot tell him which way is north.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

You are not writing a life map. You are hanging a string.

Father, other men, culture, feelings, success: moving stars. They feel like north until they move with him. God's Word is the plumb line. The wall does not get to argue with the string. You do not have to understand why a thing does not move to be saved by it.

One stretch of Mark a day. One question: what does it say a man does. One line written. Then do it. That is how a lamp for feet works. Enough for the next step, not a floodlight for the whole valley.

Point at Chapter 17. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. What have you actually been measuring your life against this year: father, other men, culture, feelings, or success?
2. Where did that moving star pull you this month?
3. Will you open Mark tomorrow and write one line?

Young: stop waiting for a perfect plan. Single: a future wife is not the star. Married: she is not the plumb line; the Book is. Hurt: the wound is not north. Older: years of nearby lights is still fog. The job going well: early closes are not a heading.

---

### Run the closer

One star. One stretch. One verse.

**Name it.** What you have actually been measuring your life against this year: father, other men, culture, feelings, or success. Full sentence, out loud, tonight.

**Do this week.** Read one stretch of Mark each day, out loud if you can. Ask each time what it says a man does. Write the answer in one line.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him which moving star you have been using. Then read him one verse from this week and tell him what it said a man does and whether you did it.

**Carry this.** You do not have to understand why a thing does not move to be saved by it. The wall does not get to argue with the string.

If he only hears that you have been busy, he nods and you stay in the fog. Get the star and the stretch on the page.
