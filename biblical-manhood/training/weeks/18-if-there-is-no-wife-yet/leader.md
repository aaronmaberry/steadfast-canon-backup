# Week 18. If there is no wife yet + Know her
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 21, If there is no wife yet, and Chapter 22, Know her.

**Aim:** If there is no wife yet, you are not waiting to start. Proximity is not knowledge. One hit on the double.

**One job:** Name which of the three men you are, and whether you have been living as though life has not started. And the one area you know least about your wife, or if single, what a sister practice would expose.

**Pitfall:** Two chapters, one hour, one hit. Do not turn it into a couples class. Singles are in the rule, not a cameo. Do not reprint last week's destiny card as the whole hour. Kind: yet is not a taunt. A girlfriend is not the stamp.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the house he already has, then the woman he has been guessing at.

### Open (2-3 min)

A garment bag still on the hook after a good wedding. He lives like the real life is the one with a first dance. Then a photo on the fridge. He thinks the still is current.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

One hit. Garden now, then study her.

Assigned. Called. Live as a believer in it. If there is no wife yet, you are not waiting to start. You are a man with a garden, and it needs working today. A ring is a gift. It is not a promotion into manhood.

Then deep waters. Proximity is not knowledge. Insight draws them out. Present tense. Study her. Write it down. If single, practice the same listening on your mother or sister.

Point at both chapters. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. Which of the three men are you, and have you been living as though life has not started?
2. What area do you know least about your wife, or if single, what would a sister practice expose?
3. What will you build or ask this week?

Young: a girlfriend is not the stamp. Single: this is your page; unpack; set the table; ask clearly or release honorably. Married: she already knows if you are a student or a visitor. Hurt: yet is not a taunt. Older: never-married is not a mascot; thirty years is not the same as current. The job going well: a beautiful place can still be a hotel.

---

### Run the closer

One seat. One build or question. One page.

**Name it.** Which of the three men you are, and whether you have been living as though life has not started. And the one area you know least about your wife, or if single, what a sister practice would expose.

**Do this week.** If you have never married, pick one thing from what a single man builds and do it before the week is out. If you are single again, pick one from the same list and add one honest sentence to God about your grief and one about your part. If you are married, ask her one question from the thin area and listen for the whole answer without fixing, then write it down and start the page. If single, ask your mother or sister one real question and practice the same listening.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him which man you are and which thing you picked. If divorced and thinking about dating, tell him that and ask whether you are ready. If married, tell him what you found out that you did not know. Do not let either of you say I know her pretty well.

**Carry this.** If there is no wife yet, you are not waiting to start. You are a man with a garden, and it needs working today. Proximity is not knowledge. Study her. Write it down.

If he only hears that he wants to get married, or that he wants a better marriage, he nods and goes back to the bag. Get the seat and the ask on the page.
