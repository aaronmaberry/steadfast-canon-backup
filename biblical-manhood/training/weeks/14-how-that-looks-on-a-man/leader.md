# Week 14. How that looks on a man
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 16, How that looks on a man.

**Aim:** The inside has a shape. Competence is not righteousness. Open the drawer before someone else does.

**One job:** Name which shape is yours, and one place it showed this week.

**Pitfall:** Do not reprint last week's sentence as the whole hour. Do not dump a missing vision. Do not preview two ways of being a man. Do not teach a marriage class. Soft match the shapes. Do not dump the whole lecture. Kind: the pattern is not proof the wound was fake.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is how the week already told on him.

:::margin
**Proverbs 14:12 NIV**
There is a way that appears to be right, but in the end it leads to death.
:::

---

### Open (2-3 min)

He can look faithful from the parking lot. He can run a crew and still rot under a clean surface. Competence is not righteousness. The week already reported which shape he lived.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

You are not diagnosing a theory. You are reading a week he already lived.

Appears. That is the trap. Drawer, fortress, scoreboard, podium, throne, contempt, recliner. A way that looks right at 3 p.m. and still leads somewhere dead. A man can run a whole kingdom while he rots. Open the drawer before someone else does.

He can feel more than one. Pick the one that spent the most hours. Naming all seven as a dodge is still a dodge. Hurt can sit in a man and still not write every skipped night. Kind is not letting the shape keep the week.

Point at Chapter 16. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. One shape.

1. Which shape is yours: drawer, fortress, scoreboard, podium, throne, contempt, or recliner?
2. Where did it show this week?
3. Who will you ask the wife test or brother test, and will you thank them after?

Young: the shape can start before a lease. Single: kill the drawer before it holds a second life. Married: she already knows; listen without defending. Hurt: the shape is not proof the wound was a story. Older: fortress can dress as wisdom. The job going well: early closes can coexist with a rotting drawer.

---

### Run the closer

One shape. One test. One drawer.

**Name it.** Which shape is yours, and one place it showed this week. Drawer, fortress, scoreboard, podium, throne, contempt, or recliner. One sentence, out loud, tonight.

**Do this week.** Run the wife test or the brother test. Ask where they see you hiding and where you are harsh. Listen to the whole answer without defending yourself. Then thank them.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him about your drawer, even if it is only a habit of hiding your real thoughts, and give him permission to say you are the man when he sees it.

**Carry this.** Competence is not righteousness. A man can run a whole kingdom while he rots. Open the drawer before someone else does.

If he only hears a theory, he stays unaccused. Get the shape and the test on the page.
