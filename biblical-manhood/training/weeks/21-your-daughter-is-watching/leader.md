# Week 21. Your daughter is watching
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 26, Your daughter is watching.

**Aim:** Your daughter is watching. If you want to know what kind of man she will marry, look at how you treated her mother this week.

**One job:** Name what your face has been teaching, and what your words have been calling her, even if nothing. Then delight once, honor her mother daily, or honor where a girl can see.

**Pitfall:** Do not reprint last week's bed as the whole hour. Do not dump a purity culture lecture. Do not preview the son page. Kind: weekend drop-off is not a punchline. Men with no daughter stay. A girlfriend is not a trial daughter. Keep single-ownership / no-daughter seat on the page.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the other seat.

:::margin
**Deuteronomy 6:7 NIV**

### Open (2-3 min)

After practice. Passenger visor down. Hair still wet. In the little rectangle she can see his face. He thinks this is a ride home. She is taking notes.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

Sit at home. Walk the road. Lie down. Get up. She is already learning three things from you before you preach a word.

What a man is. From you. Whatever you are, that is what "man" means to her.

What a woman deserves. From how you treat her mother. If you want to know what kind of man your daughter will marry, look at how you treated her mother this week. That is the man.

What she is worth. From whether you are delighted in her or merely tolerating her. Approval is for what she did. Delight is for who she is. It is on your face or it is not, and she is reading it.

Honor her mother where she can see it. Never speak against her mother. Protection is a fence with a gate, not a tower. Your eyes: where they go. She notices.

If she is grown, the window did not close at eighteen. Say the thing you assumed she knew.

If you have no daughter, stay. Niece. Church. Brother's girl. Be a man she can watch in the light: visible, honorable, never alone with a girl who is not yours. You are not a guest on this page.

Point at Chapter 26. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. What has your face been teaching, and what have your words been calling her, even if nothing?
2. Which of the three lessons have you been teaching badly?
3. What delight, mother honor, grown call, or no-daughter honor this week?

Young: a girlfriend is not a trial daughter. Single: you are not a guest; niece, church, brother's daughter. Married: she watches how you talk to her mother. Hurt: weekend drop-off is not a punchline. Older: adult daughter still watching; never-had-a-daughter is not a mascot. The job going well: you can fund the sport and miss the visor.

---

### Run the closer

One face. One keep.

**Name it.** What your face has been teaching, and what your words have been calling her, even if nothing. Out loud tonight. (Daughter in the house, or girl in reach? Both own the page.)

**Do this week.** One specific delight sentence about who she is. Honor her mother in front of her once a day. If grown, make the overdue call. If no daughter, honor a mother or sister where a girl can see it once.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him the three lessons and which one you have been teaching badly. Ask him what his daughter is learning from him.

**Carry this.** If you want to know what kind of man your daughter will marry, look at how you treated her mother this week. That is the man.

If he only hears that he should try harder, he nods and goes back to the phone. Get the face and the keep on the page.
