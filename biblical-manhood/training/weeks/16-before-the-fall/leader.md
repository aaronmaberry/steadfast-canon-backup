# Week 16. Before the fall + Two men, two ways
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 18, Before the fall, and Chapter 19, Two men, two ways.

**Aim:** Design was first. Then two men, two ways. You do not get a third. One hit on the double.

**One job:** Name which piece of the blueprint is most bent in you, and the last garden you stood in this week, and which man's way you took.

**Pitfall:** Genesis first. Do not skip to the wreck. Do not reprint last week's lights. Do not preview destiny. Do not write a marriage class. Two chapters. One hit. Kind: the wound is not the first page, and it is not a third man.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the first morning, then the fork.

### Open (2-3 min)

Ask what was given before anything broke. Watch him reach for the wreck. Then the fork. Same week. He is already on one of them.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

One hit. Blueprint, then the fork.

Took. Put. Work it and keep it. The job came before the curse. Design is what was true before the leak.

Then two men. First Adam silent, blamed, hid, grabbed. Last Adam spoke, took the cup, sought the Father, trusted the far reward. The four marks are not a list of qualities. They are the portrait of Jesus in the worst hour of His life. Every time you live one, you are wearing His face.

Point at both chapters. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. Which piece of the blueprint is most bent in you?
2. The last garden you stood in this week, and which man's way you took?
3. Which sentence will you say tomorrow morning: Rise, let us go, or Not as I will, or Let these men go?

Young: delayed is still a way. Single: full man before a bride. Married: she already knows which man came home. Hurt: the wound is real; it is not first, and it is not a third man. Older: no retirement clause in Genesis 2:15. The job going well: gift first, then hands.

---

### Run the closer

One bent piece. One garden. One Man.

**Name it.** Which piece of the blueprint is most bent in you, full sentence. And the last garden you stood in this week, and which man's way you took.

**Do this week.** Read Genesis one and two out loud, start to finish, on an ordinary morning, and write one line about what it says a man is for. Each morning name the garden you are most likely to face and say His sentence out loud: Rise, let us go, or Not as I will, or Let these men go. Then walk into the day.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him which piece you named and what walking back toward it would look like this month, and ask which piece he sees bent in you. Tell him which man you are in, in the deepest sense. If you have never trusted Christ to move you out of Adam, tell him that and let him walk you through it. If you have, tell him one garden you walked the second Man's way through.

**Carry this.** You were made to work it and keep it. The job came before the curse. The four marks are not a list of qualities. They are the portrait of Jesus in the worst hour of His life. Every time you live one, you are wearing His face.

If he only hears a talk about Eden, he nods and straddles again. Get the bent piece and the garden on the page.
