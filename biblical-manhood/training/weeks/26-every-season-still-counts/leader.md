# Week 26. Every season still counts + What you are building when nobody claps
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 32, Every season still counts, and Chapter 33, What you are building when nobody claps.

**Aim:** The year you are in counts. Build the unseen hour. The clap is a liar.

**One job:** Name the season you are actually in, then one next move in this year. Name the strip you keep mowing and the gate that leans, then one unseen true hour this week.

**Pitfall:** Two chapters, one hour. Do not reprint last week's hallway or dash as the whole night. Do not preview a written plan. Do not dump brothers. Kind. A stolen season is a wound. Built and nobody came is a wound.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone. Two chapters. Not two sermons.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the year you are in, then the hour nobody grades.

### Open (2-3 min)

A twenty-two-year-old in a grocery line, waiting for his life to start. Two carts back, a man with gray at the temples, waiting for his life to be over. Same lie. Different decade. Then a man who mowed the strip that faces the street twice and left the backyard gate leaning. Nobody driving by can see the gate.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

Solomon did not pick a winner. Strength is a glory. Gray hair is a splendor. The young man is not a guest until he marries. The old man is not a mascot until he dies. Envy is how we skip ours.

Passivity loves a later season. A later season is a clean story. This one is messy. You can skip a stretch while you are in it. The season does not pause for the speech. It just passes, and you carry the skipped thing into the next one. You do not get a do-over of twenty-four. You get fifty-one with twenty-four still in the bag. Name it. Then take the next step in the year you actually have.

A man can spend a whole decade as a tourist in his own house. He was there. He was also waiting. The people in the house were not waiting.

Then the unseen hour. The street strip is not evil. The tell is what you do with the gate. A man who only builds what can be seen is a performer. Paul is talking to men whose work would never trend. The Lord is in the room. You are not working for the clap. You are working for Christ.

Busy can be a strip. Twelve visible tasks, none of them the lean. "Nobody noticed" is not a reason to stop. That was the point of the hour. God's reward is not the mention. Build the part that will still be standing when the mention is gone.

Point at both chapters. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. What season have you been waiting for? What season are you actually in?
2. What do you build when someone is watching? What leans when they are not?
3. What is one next move in this year, and one unseen true hour this week?

Young: this is not the lobby; strength is your glory; the feed is an audience; do one true hour that will not be posted. Single: your season is already the life; the unseen hour is already the life; do not save your first unposted hour for a family you do not have yet. Married: do not wait for a quieter house; she already knows which strip you mow twice; she lives with the gate. Hurt: go slow; some of you had a season stolen; some of you built and nobody came; carrying a skipped stretch is not the same as being finished; do the true hour, then let a brother know so you do not disappear. Older: gray hair is only splendor if it has a next move; a highlight reel will not raise a grandson; you are not a mascot. The job going well: a title can freeze you in last decade's fight; the clap is already on a schedule; use the same whole heart on the hour that will not promote you.

---

### Run the closer

One season. One step. One strip. One gate. One hour.

**Name it.** What season are you actually in? What leans when nobody is watching?

**Do this week.** One next move that belongs to this year. One unseen true hour, for the Lord, not for the clap.

The season, the step, the gate, and the hour:

**Tell a brother.** Tell him the year you are in. Tell him the gate. Have him ask if you skipped the season again, or if you did the hour.

**Carry this.** Every season still counts. What you are building when nobody claps.

If he only hears that time is flying, or that you work hard, he nods and you wait again, or you mow the strip again. Get both on the page.
