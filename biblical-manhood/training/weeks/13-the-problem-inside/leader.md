# Week 13. The problem inside
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 15, The problem inside.

**Aim:** The problem is in you. Not only what was done to you. He needs to be made new, not coached into looking fine.

**One job:** Say the word about yourself, to God, out loud and specifically: I sinned; I blank. Not I struggle with.

**Pitfall:** Do not reprint the last twelve weeks. Do not dump how that looks on a man. Do not preview two Adams. Kind: the wound is real. Do not let the old story be the only author of the week. A wife is not the problem. A clean résumé is not a clean heart.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is the inside.

:::margin
**Romans 7:18 NIV**
For I know that good itself does not dwell in me, that is, in my sinful nature. For I have the desire to do what is good, but I cannot carry it out.
:::

---

### Open (2-3 min)

He can leave a house, walk with a man, and still wreck the next room. Then he reaches for the old story. If they had been better, he would be better. Some of that is true. It is not the whole wound.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

You are not taking back the empty years. You are refusing a full alibi.

Paul is not performing despair. He is telling the truth about a man who loves God and still finds a law in himself that will not obey. Desire is not delivery. A clean intention is not a clean heart.

Hurt explains a lot. It does not author every hour. You can change zip codes. You cannot drop yourself off at the last apartment. Something in us leans the wrong way even in a kind house.

Say the word. I sinned. Not I struggle with. Soft words protect the surface. They also protect the rust. The gospel belongs here or this page ends in despair. The cure has been paid for.

Point at Chapter 15. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. Say it: I sinned; I blank. Not I struggle with.
2. What soft word have you been painting with?
3. Who will hear one of them this week?

Young: you are not only your dad; a sharp mouth is also you. Single: do not draft a future woman as the priest. Married: she is not the problem; this page is his. Hurt: naming the inside does not make the wound fake. Older: a polished account of what was done to him and a thin account of this week is still hiding. The job going well: trusted with money is not the same as well.

---

### Run the closer

One sentence. Seven nights. One confession.

**Name it.** Say the word about yourself, to God, out loud and specifically tonight: I sinned; I blank. Not I struggle with.

**Do this week.** Every night before sleep, two minutes. Name the day's sins by name. Thank Him for forgiveness already yours in Christ. Notice what happens to the shame.

**Tell a brother.** Confess one of them to him, to his face or on the phone. Tell him what you are going to do next. Let him pray for you.

**Carry this.** You've been painting over it. The problem inside has a name, the name has a cure, and the cure has been paid for.

If he only hears the wound, he gets sympathy and keeps the alibi. Get I sinned on the page.
