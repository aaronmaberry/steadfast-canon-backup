# Week 10. The Father who does not leave
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 10, The Father who does not leave.

**Aim:** God is a Father who stays. The empty chair is not the last word. Receive Him.

**One job:** Name which picture you have been holding up to heaven, and one thing Jesus shows the Father is actually like.

**Pitfall:** Do not reprint the chair as the whole hour. Do not tour the fruit. Do not open mother. Do not use God as a gag for the wound. Do not fake a childhood so He can be Father. Kind. Receiving is the hour.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Say it. Do not unpack it. This week is who is here.

:::margin
**Matthew 3:17 NIV**
And a voice from heaven said, "This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased."
:::

---

### Open (2-3 min)

A boy in a dugout scans the bleachers after a strikeout. He is looking for one face. Some men are still scanning at thirty-eight. There is a Father you can stop scanning for. He was in the line before the bell.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

This is not another inventory of what was missing. This is who is here.

You have been reading God through your dad. Every man does. Empty chair, silent chair, dangerous chair, or good with a gap: you held that picture up to heaven. The Bible flips it. Read your dad through God. Anyone who has seen Jesus has seen the Father.

Before the Son preached a sermon or healed a soul, the Father spoke. My Son. Whom I love. With him I am well pleased. Blessing first, then the work. If needing that voice is weakness, the beloved Son heard it anyway. You are not more mature than He is for going without.

Receiving means you stop running the experiment where a human man finally becomes God. He will not. Some of you still have one you can call. That call is not this page. This page is the Father who does not leave.

God is not a trick to make the hurt stop by Friday. He stays in it. He also does not share the throne with the hole. You can tell Him the truth and still belong to Him.

Point at Chapter 10. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. Which picture have you been holding up to heaven?
2. What one thing does Jesus show the Father is actually like?
3. Can you say Father out loud seven mornings without waiting to feel it first?

Young: learn this now; do not wait for a family to start being a son. Single: a wife will not say the sentence He is saying. Married: she cannot be your Father; let her be a wife. Hurt: the word was used badly; God is not him. Older: it is not too late to be somebody's son. The job going well: applause is not I am well pleased.

---

### Run the closer

One picture. Seven mornings.

**Name it.** Which picture have you been holding up to heaven, and one thing Jesus shows the Father is actually like. Out loud tonight.

**Do this week.** Every morning before your feet hit the floor, say Father out loud and then one sentence to Him about the day. Seven mornings in a row.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him which false father you have been praying to, ask him to pray for you out loud right there, and let him. Being prayed over is practice at being carried.

**Carry this.** You learned the word father from a copy. Jesus is the original, and the original was in the line before the bell.

If a brother rushes the hurt, ask him to sit still. The Father stays. So can a brother, for an hour.
