# Week 23. Give your son a hill
## Leader guide

**Steadfast Men**
*The way of Godly Men*
Wes Hart

### Before the room

**Read this week:** Chapter 28, Give your son a hill.

**Aim:** A name without a hill is only a compliment. Pick the right hill. Stay on the step.

**One job:** Name whether he has a hill, and if not, whether that is protect or you do it yourself. Then hand one hill, stay on the step, ask, and name.

**Pitfall:** Do not reprint last week's name as the whole hour. Do not dump daughter. Do not preview a day. Kind. Men with no son stay. Keep single-ownership / no-son seat + first-year beat on the page. The younger man in reach still has ears.

**Time:** about an hour. Church, small group, or a man alone.

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Say it. Do not unpack it. Tonight is ground.

**1 Thessalonians 2:11-12 NIV**

### Open (2-3 min)

Friday. New street. Last lot. Caliche mound. Boys charging it. Badge still on. He thinks: good, he is outside. That is not a hill. That is dirt.

Say the four. Sit down.

---

### Hit

Encourage. Comfort. Urge. A name without a hill is a compliment. A boy becomes what you called him by climbing something that proves it.

Boys do not lack fight. They lack ground. A hill is real. Slightly beyond him. Bounded risk. An outcome he owns. Followed by a name when he comes down.

What you have been handing him may be dirt: sport as the whole life, a fence of rules with no cause, an unfinished thing, himself as the project, or nothing. Protect him from every difficulty and he starves. Do it yourself and he never climbs.

If you have no son in the house, stay. Nephew. The boy you coach. The kid who stays after. One real job for a fatherless boy, you at the bottom, in the light.

First-year dad: the 3am feed is not dirt and it is not the whole hill. Show up tired without making the boy pay for your sleep. Stand where he can see. Do not lengthen this into a second sermon.

Point at Chapter 28. Then shut up.

---

### Table

Three. Wait.

1. Does he have a hill right now?
2. If not, protect or you do it yourself?
3. What hill this week, and will you stay on the step?

Young: you already stand for something or you do not. Single: you are not a guest; nephew, the boy you coach. Married: this is your page if you have a son; daughters-only still has boys in reach. Hurt: go slow; not a tyrant. Older: a grown son still needs a place to stand; never-a-son is not a mascot. The job going well: mission on the glass; boy gets fine.

---

### Run the closer

One false hill. One real keep.

**Name it.** Whether he has a hill, and if not, protect or you do it yourself. Out loud tonight. (Son in the house, or younger man in reach? Both own the page.)

**Do this week.** One hill, age scaled. Stay on the step. Ask. Name. If no son, one real job for a fatherless boy, you at the bottom.

**Tell a brother.** Tell him the hill you gave and how hard it was to stay put. Ask what hills his father gave him and what he is giving now.

**Carry this.** A name without a hill is only a compliment. Pick the right hill. Stay on the step. Say the right thing when he comes down.

If he only hears that he should try a harder chore, he nods and gets up off the step. Get the hill and the stay on the page.
