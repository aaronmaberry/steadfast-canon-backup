# Steadfast Men 24-week training map

Internal. Our program. Our titles. Do not print course labels, speaker names, or anyone else's class on a packet.

Source is the Wes Hart book in `/workspace/biblical-manhood/book/`. Backbone is the 24 teaching claims in canon. Men read the book in order. A church, a small group, or a man alone can print one week and run it.

Closer (every week): Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this.

Four marks (unchanged, on every packet): A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

## How this is built

36 book readings (Introduction plus chapters 1-35) into 24 weeks. Week 1 is already a double: Introduction + chapter 1. Eleven more doubles sit on clusters that belong together. No Hawks titles. No man cards. No Nate. No due dates.

Do not wait on unfinished chapters to keep this map. Start packets from locked and desk-signed pages. Week 1 ships off the introduction and chapter 1.
