## What delivered

These packets do what the book alone cannot: force ink, a day, and a name on the page.

**Participant sheets are the product.** One page, letter, navy mast + plumb, gold on "Do this week," write-in lines, "Alone: still tell one man." I would actually print these and put them on the kitchen table. Week 1 of 24 footer keeps the arc honest.

**One job is clear every week.** Not a seven-point lesson. Sit down. Name the wait. Put a hand on the weight. Ask a man. Circle which of the three. Lay it down and give her need. Put a date on a page. A tired 46-year-old can leave with one sentence.

**Best weeks for me (unfinished home):**
- **1 Tuesday** — meal without phone + day blank. Opens the whole training.
- **4 Stop waiting / 5 Pick up the weight** — Week 5 Do this week is gold for my house: "one thing you provide that is not money (coffee, bedtime prayer, twenty minutes after boots come off) every day." That is the post-6:30 move the book pointed at. Carry line: "Provision that costs your presence isn't provision." Buy-worthy alone.
- **12 Brothers + Ask** — sample ask script on the sheet ("twenty minutes a week for three months"). If he says no, who next. This fixes the circular brother problem better than the book did.
- **14 How that looks** — circle: skip the house / run the room / wear the job as a name. Then one contrary act. My mirror in three boxes.
- **19 Lay it down + What she needs** — piece you would not lay down + wrong gift + true need, do not announce it. Married week that earns the binder.
- **20 Bed / 21 Daughter** — covenant once; be in the seat once. Concrete, not a seminar.

**Leader guides stay short.** Before the room / Open / Hit / Table (3 questions) / Run the closer / Solo. Pitfalls tell the leader not to lecture the book back. I could run week 1 cold in an hour without becoming a preacher.

**Season lines in leaders** (young / single / married / hurt / older / job) keep a mixed room without making me feel like the youth pastor's afterthought. Married lane is sharp.

Four marks line every sheet, unchanged. Good drumbeat for a binder, not a slogan wall.

## What did not

**Double-chapter weeks are heavy for one hour** (11 leave, 12 brothers+ask, 16 garden+Adams, 18 no-wife+know her, 19 lay down+needs). Leader says one hour. Two book chapters + worksheet + closer will either skim the Hit or rush the writing. For me, 18 is the worst pairing: half the sheet is "not-yet / parked because no wife" while my job is know her. Married men will skip half the Name it. Split or lead with Know her for husbands.

**Son trilogy (22–24) is strong if you have a boy.** If you don't, "younger man in reach" keeps you in — but three straight weeks of son work will feel long for a room of mixed dads/non-dads. Week 24 (date on a page, marker day) is aspirational next to Week 1 Tuesday realism. Fine as a close; don't let it become a camping trip that excuses ordinary weeks.

**Writing the four marks every single week** will get mechanical by week 10. I still want the line on the sheet. Maybe write-once early, then say-aloud later — bounce, not rewrite prescription. Fatigue risk is real.

**"Tell a brother" every week** only works if week 12 stuck. Before that, alone notes help, but men will fake a name. Week 12 sample ask is the fix — lean on it in leaders earlier as foreshadow without opening the ask early.

**Part 2 wound stretch (8–11)** is needed and kind. As a buyer with limited evenings, I might stall before 14/19 where my house lives. Leaders should keep the room moving, not therapy-dump.

**Weeks 16–17 (theology / destiny)** feel thinner as packets than 14 or 19. True content; easier to nod through without a house move.

**Print stills look reused** (same plumb / well across weeks from STATUS notes). Cloth Store mast is clean. Decorative boxes next to verses sometimes look empty. Not a fire. Not Fortune-500-art yet either.

**Leader Hit bloat late** (especially weeks 18–22): Hit can creep toward the sermon the pitfall forbids. Dual weeks already tax the hour; a long Hit will steal the writing.

Footer says **Week N of 24** while 25–27 exist on disk outside this pass — fine for this bounce; just don't confuse buyers later.

**Desk nits (not content):** `training/STATUS.md` still reads refresh OPEN while week 24 / PACKET say COMPLETE through 24. Week 4 STATUS still sounds unsigned while PDFs exist. PACKET.md Carry suggestions drift from signed sheets on weeks 6–10. Week 24 Joshua 4:6-7 opens mid-clause on the leader — looks cut.

## Where to dig deeper

1. **Married path on double weeks** — especially 18: a husband should not have to wade through not-yet to get to "ask one real question." Same energy for any dual week where half the room is a guest.
2. **After week 12, how the ask holds** — three months at twenty minutes is on the sheet. Dig a mid-arc check (around 19–20) so the brother does not die by week 15.
3. **Week 5-style daily home moves** more often — boots-off twenty minutes is better than metaphor. Weeks 19–21 could use one "every day this week" option next to the once-this-week act.
4. **Teen / nearly grown kids** inside 21–24 — visor and jersey land; older kids need a different seat.
5. **Leader timing on double weeks** — explicit 15/20/25 minute splits so writing does not lose to Hit.
6. **Bind the 24** — cover sheet + which weeks are double-read, so a church knows the load before they start.

## Content, layout, print

**Content.** Original. Matches Fable book without dumping chapters onto the worksheet. Closer order held. NIV cited. Fortune 500 bar mostly met on words — not a LifeWay photocopy. Sample ask (week 12) and daily non-money provision (week 5) are the packet wins over the ebook alone.

**Layout.** Participant 1 page letter; leader 2 pages. Navy header, plumb, bone page, gold Do-this-week box. Write lines real enough to use with a pen. Season coaching lives in the leader, not cluttering the man sheet — right call.

**Print.** PDFs exist through the refresh set; titles in metadata (better than the ebook's anonymous ReportLab). File size ~1.6MB/page with stills — fine for print, heavy for email. Proofs folder exists. Looks shippable as a weekly printable, not a draft stamp mess.

## Would I use / pay for this

**Yes.** I would pay for the binder of weeks 1–24 as the training layer on top of the book — especially if my church or two other men ran it with me. Book names me. Packets make me write the day and text the man.

Worth paying for when:
- Participant stays one page and writable.
- Week 5 / 12 / 14 / 19 quality of "do" stays the bar.
- Double weeks get a married-clear path (or a timebox so writing wins).
- Brother ask from week 12 is treated as infrastructure, not a one-off.

I would not pay for packets as decoration in a binder I never open. Alone track works only if the text actually goes out — the sheets already say that. Good.

**Net:** Refresh pass through 24 is bounce-ready. Strongest packet weeks for men like me: 1, 5, 12, 14, 19. Softest: 16–18 pairing, son-heavy close if the room is mixed. No rewrite from me. Desk can rework from this.
