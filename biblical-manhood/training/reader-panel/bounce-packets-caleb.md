# Packets bounce — Caleb (22, single, church, still guessing)

Refresh pass weeks 1–24 (Reed-signed). Read participant worksheets + skim leaders + week-1 print PDF. Buyer lens. Bounce only. No rewrite. No dump.

---

## What delivered

**Form is right.** One job. Four marks line. Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this. Write-in blanks with a day on them. Alone note so I don’t hide. That is a packet I would actually fill out in a group, not a church binder I abandon.

**Week 1** — Sit down / last Tuesday. Meal without the phone. Text one man about Tuesday. Same energy as the book. I’d pay for a program that starts here.

**Week 3** — Say the four without looking down. Index card on Tuesday morning. Ask a brother which mark is faint on me before I defend myself. Concrete. Not four sermons.

**Week 12** — Who has me if I fell tonight + ask a man ~20 years ahead. Sample ask sentence included. “If he said no, who next.” That is the first packet that tells me *how* to ask, not only that I should. Book bounce wanted this. Packets deliver it better than the chapter alone.

**Week 18** — My week. Parked-as-not-yet + ask one real question and stay. “Marriage is good. It is not the finish line.” Know-her half is still there, but the Name it line is mine first. Carry this is “Knowing her is a job” which tilts married — I’d rather Carry the finish-line line — but the worksheet still lets a single guy work.

**Print (week 1 PDF):** Looks like a real product, not a LifeWay photocopy. Mast + line + scripture well + write-ins. Fortune-500 bar is visible. Letter spacing on “STEADFAST MEN” in the text extract looked odd (spaced letters) — check the visual; if that’s a real kerning bug on print, fix before anyone pays.

---

## What did not

**Weeks 21–24 (daughter + son trilogy)** stay guest seating for me even with “younger man in reach / men with no son stay.” Week 22–24 Name it still sounds like a dad page with a courtesy lane. I can do nephew / little brother. I will still feel like I’m auditing someone else’s house for three weeks in a row after already sitting through wife pages.

**Week 18 Carry this** should keep the single spine (“Marriage is good. It is not the finish line”) as the line I write. “Knowing her is a job” is true and belongs — just not as the only thing I carry out of *my* primary week.

**Double weeks** (11, 12, 16, 18, 19) are heavy for one hour if men actually read both chapters. Leader has to pick. Participant still works; risk is skimming the second half.

**Same closer stamp every week** is a strength until week 20+. By son trilogy it can feel like the same sheet with a new title. Fine for habit. Watch for boredom if the group is young and single-heavy.

**Week 10-ish spiritual receive** (Father who stays) will work for some of us and float for others — same as the book. Packet can’t fix that alone; table talk has to carry it.

## Where dig deeper

1. **Single lane on weeks 19–24** — one Name it / Do this week option that is clearly “no wife / no kid yet” without only “younger man in reach” as a footnote. Especially week 18 Carry this.
2. **Week 12 already did the ask well** — keep that sample-sentence energy on other hard weeks (leave home, bed, daughter-in-reach) so Do this week never goes vague.
3. **Double-chapter weeks** — leader pitfall: don’t lecture both. One hit. Packets already aim there; enforce in room.
4. **Print QA** — mast letterspacing / Image 2.0 stills consistency across 1–24 before sell. No review-draft feel on paid packets.
5. **Weeks 25–27** not in this bounce (pre-refresh). When that pass opens, same reader panel.
