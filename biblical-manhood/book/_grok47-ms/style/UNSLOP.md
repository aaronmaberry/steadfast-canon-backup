# UNSLOP — patterns to kill

**GLOBAL 2026-09-21 (Aaron):** applies to every frontier model / LLM on every project, not only Steadfast Men.

Standing Aaron Maberry / Steadfast Men writing lock (2026-09-14). Apply on every rewrite, Fable pass, and draft.

## Content
- Superficial -ing phrases: highlighting, ensuring, reflecting, showcasing, fostering.
- Vague attributions: "experts believe", "industry reports suggest". Name the source or delete.

## Language
- AI vocab: additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.
- Fancy "is": serves as, stands as, boasts, features → is / has.
- "Not just X, but Y." State the point.
- Forced rule of three.
- Synonym cycling. Pick one word and keep it.
- False ranges ("from X to Y" when X and Y are not on a scale).

## Style
- No em dashes. Periods or commas only.
- No mid-sentence colons.
- Don't bold every proper noun.
- No inline-header lists that restate the line ("**Performance:** Performance improved").
- Sentence-case headings.
- No decorative emojis.
- Straight quotes, not curly.

## Communication
- No chatbot phrases: "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...", "Of course!", "Certainly!".
- No sycophancy: "Great question! You're absolutely right!"

## Filler
- "Due to the fact that" → Because.
- Excessive hedging → may, or cut.
- Generic conclusions. State a fact or a plan.

## Jargon / metaphor nouns to replace
substrate→base, wedge→add, vector→way/method, locus, vantage, nexus, primitive-as-noun, harness-as-metaphor, API surface, bedrock, scaffolding-as-metaphor, modality, paradigm, gold-plating→more than the job needs, ratchet→the real mechanism or "a limit that only tightens", evacuate→move, endgame→last phase, north star, flywheel.

## Plain speech
- Say what it does, not how it feels.
- If the sentence could appear unchanged in another project's docs, cut it.
- One idea per sentence. Split if the reader has to backtrack.
- Active voice. Name the actor.
- Cut adverbs or use a stronger verb / a number.
- No mannered prose, aphorisms, personified code, figurative verbs.
- Write whole sentences with articles and verbs.

Also keep existing Steadfast locks: no em dashes, original only, four marks, Wes Hart byline, Cloth Store colors.
