## Title changes

Numbered chapter titles are unchanged. `# N. Title` is intact for chapters 1-35, so the binder does not print CHAPTER 0.

The byline changed from Max to Wes Hart on the title page and in About the Author. The copyright notice is Wes Hart / Steadfast Men. No home street address was added. The author note uses only the facts already in the source: husband, father of two, Texan, technology work, not a pastor or counselor, kitchen-table origin of the 24-week training, walksteadfast.com.

## What stayed locked

- Four marks, verbatim: "A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward."
- Robert Lewis (Fellowship Bible Church, Little Rock) and *Raising a Modern-Day Knight* are credited in the copyright page, the introduction, and chapter 3 as the origin of that sentence. His family stories are not in the book.
- NIV margin blocks are copied from the source, word for word, inside `:::margin`.
- Closers remain Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this. Chapter 35 uses Be a brother. The first three open with Because. `:::notes` rails are unchanged.
- Named composites already in the source stay (Anthony, Eli, Tomás, Walt, Roy, Micah, Dale, Cole, Hollis, Mr. Pruitt, Mr. Alvarez, and the others). No Hawks, Ted, or Red Rocks stories were added.
- Part look-backs were not in the source, so none were added.
- Process files (status, changelog, workflow, reed audits) were left as guide notes. They are not the live chapters.

## Gaps

- ISBN is still TBD. No NIV license was purchased, and nothing was uploaded to Drive, an app store, or social.
- The source of chapter 1 promised that Walt's wife "said a sentence in the kitchen" and that the sentence would be told later. No later chapter contains that sentence. The rewrite drops the unfulfilled promise. Walt's other facts remain: one truck to eleven, the hours he stayed, the part of the business he sold, the fall trip she picked, the bank parking lot and three hundred contacts.
- Contents, the half-title, the class-week list in Condensed Training, and the About the Author note stay close to the source because the titles and the allowed bio facts are fixed. Chapter 4 is the numbered chapter nearest the source in wording (similarity about 0.71). The introduction shares the teaching spine with the source and sits near 0.75.
- Numbered chapters are between 1,200 and 2,000 words on the count above. Chapters 18 and 19 were restored after they dropped out of the working tree, then rewritten again so they would fit that band.
