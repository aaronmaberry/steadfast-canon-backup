# 20. Destiny Is Not a Vibe

Warehouse 2. Dock C. The slip already said so. Fittings in a crate, tagged across the river to another man's shop. A calling was what the young man holding the paper wanted the hour to feel like. Down went the slip, onto the pallet. His eyes went past the truck, hunting a sign. Dock C had no interest in the hunt. The fittings were leaving.

The older hand returned the paper to his grip.

"That is the job," he said. "Not the one you're waiting to feel."

God assigns a destiny. A man carries the assignment in a real place, inside his own generation, through days that refuse to look important, and he comes to the end of it. Significance, as a feeling, is another category. So is the suspicion that a large thing with your name on it has not appeared yet. Dock men have assignments. You have one. Feeling chosen is what many of us are still waiting on, while the assignment sits in our hands.

## The verse that ends the argument

If I had to put a man's destiny in a single line, I would take the one Paul speaks in Acts. He is walking Israel down the years. David comes up, and Paul says this.

:::margin
Acts 13:36 (NIV)
"Now when David had served God's purpose in his own generation, he fell asleep; he was buried with his ancestors and his body decayed."
:::

He served God's purpose in his own generation. That line is the definition. David did what God purposed, among the people and in the years where God had set him. Then he died, and the body decayed. Paul keeps the decay in the sentence so you will see that David lasting was never the point. The purpose lasting was the point.

Served matters. Somebody over the man handed him the work, and he did it. The purpose was God's. Purposes David invented on his own nearly wrecked the story. Paul is still naming the one that came from God.

Your destiny is that kind of line. The line stays true whether you feel it or not.

## The man who picked the slip back up

Leaving stopped. A calling never settled on that warehouse as a feeling. He had been told to remain three years, feel it or not, and then watch what God did with a man who quit walking off. Those years are behind him. A crew answers to him. Two of them now sit in church on the strength of the way his shift treats people. He still refuses the word calling. Purpose is the word he keeps. It lived where the paper quit getting set down.

Nothing had been concealed. The slip was printed. Taking it back into his hand was the act that had been missing.

## Three lies, then three truths

A lie you have not looked at will stay in your hand.

The first lie says a feeling will identify it. Somewhere ahead sits the life that at last sits right in your chest, and discomfort is the proof you are still lost. Feelings report weather. Moses opened with an argument. Jeremiah pleaded his youth. Gideon asked for a sign, and then another, and then a third. Jonah paid fare on a ship pointed away. Grade them by feeling and all four stay home. A man goes, and a feeling might show up later. It also might not. Either way the work is counted.

The second lie says the thing must be large. A stage. A story other people retell. Weld for years, raise the children, be the neighbor folks can find, and he concludes the real assignment passed him. Jesus honored faithfulness with a few things. Few was the measure. A small day is not beneath notice. The eyes of God range the earth, and one man setting a stone gives Him joy. Destiny is located on that small day.

The third lie says the real one is elsewhere. Another employer. Another town. Once the ceremony is done. Once the children leave home. Once the job ends. Always one relocation out, and the relocation itself keeps shifting. God set the man on one piece of ground and told him to work it. The Athenians heard that God had drawn their seasons and the borders of where they lived. Town and century were chosen. Here is the purpose.

Now the three truths.

Destiny is assigned. You will not find it the way a man finds a fishing hole. The works were prepared before you arrived.

:::margin
Ephesians 2:10 (NIV)
"For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do."
:::

The works are already laid out. Do the next one. Hunt a single hidden purpose and you will live scared of the wrong trail. Trust the prior preparation and you can do the thing already in reach. Take the slip.

Second, the purpose has a local address. Her name, if a wife is yours. The children's names, if children are yours. The block, the shift, the church that already knows you. David was given Israel as his field. Esther's cousin still warns anyone who wants to wait. Keep quiet and help will rise from another quarter, while your share of the hour is lost. The binding line is for such a time as this. This seat. This year.

The third truth is that faithfulness finishes it. You complete a work. A mood will not finish anything.

:::margin
John 4:34 (NIV)
"'My food,' said Jesus, 'is to do the will of him who sent me and to finish his work.'"
:::

Jesus called it His food to do the will and finish the work. Paul, from a cell, in places he had not chosen, could say the fight was fought, the race was finished, and the faith was kept. Those are completion words.

The work is given, it has an address, and a man who keeps showing up brings it to an end. Ordinary days are nearly all of it. Someone will say well done. He is the only judge whose view of your life outlasts you.

Start with what those hands already hold. The first shape still stands. Tend the ground you were given and guard it. Hold a word, then say it. Do not go it alone. If a bride is yours, love her. Rest belongs in the shape. Name your place, town and job and street and church. A move, when it comes, will be His to make. Name your people. Those names matter more than a title you can frame. Spend the gift your hands actually have. Stay inside this stretch of years.

Do that. On an afternoon that will probably feel ordinary, you will see that God's purpose for your generation has been underway for years, and your hands were the whole of the equipment.

## This stretch

If you are single, and a wife or a bigger name is what you think will hand you the work, listen to the dock. The next thing God has assigned is the destiny. Stay and work it.

If a baby is in the house, the assignment in front of you is about twelve pounds and will not stay down. Nothing larger is waiting in the hallway. The missing sense of a grand calling is part of what this season is teaching.

If you are divorced, the plot has not closed, and the good part has not closed either. A prison lay on the road Joseph's purpose took. The night Peter failed worst still lay on the road his purpose took. Jesus had already said what the far side held.

:::margin
Luke 22:32 (NIV)
"But I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith may not fail. And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers."
:::

Turn back, then strengthen your brothers. Purpose survived the failure. The failure became part of the gear. Men who will not open their mouths to anyone else will open them to some of you. God's purpose in this generation, served by means of the very history you were sure had disqualified you.

Older, and the phrase about his own generation is still aimed at the generation you are standing in. Behind you is not where the purpose moved. Fruit remains in the later years. Breakfast with a man. A class. One word next to a truck. None of those ask you to feel a certain way first.

Story hands every man a set of names. Dad left. Marriage detonated. The promotion never came. Real names. Not the name that governs you. They belong to the first man. Naked. Afraid. Hiding. Blaming. Another set arrives from the master story. Image of God. Placed on purpose. Work given. Son to the Father who stays. In Christ. Servant of God's purpose inside your own generation.

:::margin
Revelation 2:17 (NIV)
"Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who is victorious, I will give some of the hidden manna. I will also give that person a white stone with a new name written on it, known only to the one who receives it."
:::

A name exists that no one else has heard. Stone, someday, in your hand, the name cut into it. Every label your story preferred will be gone.

Houses are where the largest share of a destiny is actually carried out. We go there next. Wife, where there is a wife. Children. Bed. Table. Rooms already bearing the family's names, or rooms you are framing in the wait.

Do the next thing God put in your hands. Do it where He put you. Do it for the people He gave you. Finish. Destiny is not a vibe.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

## Before you turn

Lightning is a poor thing to hunt if a feeling is what you have been hunting. Place, people, hands, this stretch. The assignment has been in that list. May the Father who laid your good works out beforehand put the next one in front of you this week. Do the thing with no vibe attached. It will have counted.
