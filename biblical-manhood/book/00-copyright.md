**A note on the four marks**

One sentence sits at the center of this book.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

Robert Lewis first taught this definition of manhood at Fellowship Bible Church in Little Rock, in the teaching that became *Raising a Modern-Day Knight*. The author gratefully acknowledges that origin. The stories and applications in these pages are the author's own.
