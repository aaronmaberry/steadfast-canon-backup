# Chapter map and merge options, Steadfast Men

Prepared 2026-10-04 for Aaron's question: "Should we condense the chapter count by combining chapters into fewer, longer ones, or would that take away from the project?" This is a planning document only. Nothing in the manuscript, the 03d print or Drive has changed, and the full rewrite has not started.

## Short answer

Combine a few, not many. Six merges take the book from 35 chapters to **28**. Each merge joins two or three chapters that are already one argument split across a page turn. For example, ch16 opens "That's what the trouble we just named looks like on a man."

- Every story, protected line and season branch stays.
- The 24 recorded training sessions (W01-W24) still line up cleanly. They now cover chapters 1-19 instead of 1-24, with five chapters carrying two weeks each.
- Nothing has to be re-recorded. I checked the W01-W24 scripts, and none of them cites a book chapter number.

Going down to about 20 chapters would take away from the project. The wound chapters, the four mark chapters and the household chapters each do a job a reader came for, and they need their own room.

- **Book:** 03d manuscript, `book/_opus55-second-pass-2026-10-01/book/`.
- **Training sessions W01-W24:** `scripts/_opus55-scripts-2026-10-01/sessions/` and the recorded audio in `/workspace/print-build/session-audio/`. Session NN teaches chapter NN for chapters 1-24. Chapters 25-35 have no session, and `37-condensed-training.md` lists them under "Also in this book" as chapters that "carry on past the first twenty-four class weeks".

**Word counts:**

- "File" is the whole chapter file: prose, verse boxes and the Your Turn page.
- "Body" is the prose only, with no boxes and no end sections.
- Under B-A-A, narrating each verse adds a little back. The ch10 sample went from 1,581 to 1,893 words.

## 2. Where the book repeats itself

These are the overlaps a merge or the rewrite should clean up. Some repetition is on purpose, like the mark verses in ch3 and again in ch4-7.

- **The Father's blessing at the baptism** appears three times: ch10 (Matthew 3:17), ch11 ("this is my son, and I love him") and ch27 (Mark 1:11).
- **Work before the curse** appears twice. ch18's protected line is "Work came before the curse." ch30's carry line is "God handed man work before anything went wrong."
- **Knowing your wife** appears twice. ch24 says outright, "We've already done the heavy lifting on knowing her."
- **The father who won't give the words** gets two close stories: Nate's dad in ch11 and Grant's father in ch12.
- **Matthew 6, "in secret":** ch4 ("Seen in secret"), ch7 and ch33.
- **Provider vs presence:** ch5 ("Absence purchased with a bigger paycheck") and ch24 ("She doesn't need more of what you make").
- **Seasons:** ch21's single, divorced and widowed seats, ch32's full walk through life's seasons, and a season branch at the end of nearly every chapter.
- **Ceremony and being counted in:** ch11 ("What fathers once did") and ch29 (the son's day). Both are flagged in FOCUS-GROUP as "borrowed in shape".
- **Plumb line:** taught in ch17 and carried by Alton in ch29. This one is by design.
- **The shapes of sin:** ch9's escapes and ch16's "What it wears" list cover nearby ground. ch31's two ditches touch it too.

## 3. Three options

### Option A. Keep 35

**What stays:**

- The week-NN-equals-chapter-NN rule for chapters 1-24.
- Every title as it is.
- The one-chapter-per-class-week rhythm of the training.
- No work at all on the session map.

**What it costs:**

- The repeats above stay, unless the rewrite trims them inside each chapter.
- Seven chapters read as halves of a pair. Under B-A-A, with no subheads, each half restarts with a new story and a new set-up. That restart is part of the "talking at men" feeling.

**Why each merge, and what it costs:**

#### 1. 11 + 12, the house you came from.

- **Why:** Both chapters stand in the same doorway. One is the father who never counted you in and the mother who kept the porch light on. The other is the slam and the grip, honor, and forgiving the debt. The source course teaches this ground as one run (sessions 04-05). Together they make one arc, from "nobody handed you off" to "leave without slamming the door".
- **Stays:** Nate's Saturdays, Jacob at the river, Grant's porch, and the protected line "I've had my eye on you for years. You're a good man."
- **Lost:** one of the two "dad won't say it" passages, and "Leave Without Slamming the Door" as a chapter title. It survives as the chapter's closing line and as the W12 session title.
- **Gained:** the FOCUS-GROUP "rite-of-passage checklist" (`11:40`, `11:58`) and Grant's four-step talk (`12:73`) both flatten into story.
#### 2. 13 + 14, the man beside you and the man ahead.

- **Why:** The course pairs these too (06-07). ch14 already says the man ahead of you and the man beside you "do different jobs". One chapter can show both jobs.
- **Stays:** Dale's ER form, Kenny's phone, Travis and Harlan, "One man, twenty minutes, every week", and "Ride with me Saturday / Well. You paid attention."
- **Lost:** four named men in one chapter is a crowd, so each story gets a little shorter. This is the merge I'm least sure of. If the draft feels crowded, keep 13 and 14 apart and the book is 29.
#### 3. 15 + 16, sin, and what it looks like on a man.

- **Why:** ch16 begins as ch15's second half. Course 08-09 is one two-part session.
- **Stays:** Cody's fender, Wade, David on the roof and Nathan's finger, "Paint's not going to fix that. It's coming from the inside", and "A man can run a kingdom well and rot inside it."
- **Lost:** some of ch16's catalog of shapes. Those shapes repeat ch9's escapes anyway.
#### 4. 18 + 19, two gardens.

- **Why:** This is one story, from God's design to the fall, then Gethsemane and the last Adam. It's also the heaviest box load in the book (23 of 158 boxes), so B-A-A narration helps most here.
- **Stays:** the two-Adams teaching the canon requires, Adam's silence at home in ch19, and "Work came before the curse".
- **Lost:** ch18's slow walk through Genesis 1-2 gets compressed, and its rest and naming beats get shorter. Part Three drops to two chapters.
- **Fix:** ch30's "work before anything went wrong" repeat moves out of ch30.
#### 5. 23 + 24, laying it down, and what she actually needs.

- **Why:** Ephesians 5 servant headship, then what it looks like at the counter. ch24 is 11 subheads long, one "She needs..." list, so as prose it becomes the second half of ch23's argument. That list also has the shape of the source course's "25 ways" session, so turning it into story moves the book further from that source.
- **Stays:** the wet floor, Russ and Renee, "Being right is not the same as being a husband", and the furnace sentence.
- **Lost:** "What She Actually Needs" as a chapter title (it stays as the W24 title), and some of ch24's itemized needs.
#### 6. 27 + 28 + 29, your son.

- **Why:** Name, hill and day are one father's job in three moves. The course covers it in one session (20), and the old training map ran it as one week.
- **Stays:** Ray's four seconds of video, Lyle's spring, Alton's creek and plumb bob, and "Well. I figured you could."
- **Lost:** it becomes the longest chapter (about 4,000 words), and two titles drop to line-break turns.
- **Gained:** the baptism-voice repeat goes away, and the "ceremony template" FOCUS-GROUP flagged (`29:42`-`59`) dissolves into Alton's morning.
- **Alternative:** if a single son chapter feels too long, split it in two (Name, then Hill and Day), and the book is 29.

**What B gains overall:**
- Seven fewer restarts and fewer repeated scenes and verses.
- Chapters of 3,000 to 3,600 words that read like book chapters under the no-subhead rule.
- Part Two goes from 10 chapters to 7.
- A shorter book, with seven fewer chapter openers and their blank versos. The exact page count needs a test build.

**What B costs overall:**
- **Session map:** "week NN = chapter NN" becomes a table. W01-W24 cover chapters 1-19, and five chapters carry two weeks.
- **Pages to rewrite:** the Condensed Training page and the training map.
- **Ending lines:** a merged chapter gets one anchor verse and one set of Your Turn lines, picked from the two sets it replaces.
- **Pace:** the intro asks for "one thing a week", not one chapter a week, so that line still works. But a merged chapter carries two weeks of work, and the Condensed Training line "Each class week works through one chapter, now and then a close pair" flips to "some chapters take two weeks". The intro's shorter road still points to the seasons chapter, which survives.

### Option C. 25 chapters (not recommended)

This is Option B plus three more merges:
- **17 + 20 (vision):** the Word as the fixed point, then purpose.
- **30 + 33 (work and legacy):** both cover course session 23.
- **32 + 34 (seasons and the written plan):** ch34 already has a "Your season's page".

It reaches the low end of your range, but each merge costs a home.
- Legacy loses its own chapter, and "The crowd gets straw" has to share the room with Gil's grader.
- The seasons chapter loses the job the intro sends men to it for.
- No Fixed Star would leave Part Two to sit beside Destiny.
- These pairs share a theme, not one argument, so the merged chapters would read like two chapters stapled together.

## 5. Things to settle before the full rewrite

1. **Option.** A, B or C. If B, keep 13 + 14 merged or apart, and keep the son chapter as one or split it in two.
2. **Titles.** For the merged chapters, use the working titles above (all existing titles) or new ones.
3. **Locks.** The intro and ch1 carry `.LOCKED` files. B doesn't merge either one, but B-A-A still changes their form: an anchor verse, no subheads and the closer moved to the back. Confirm that your whole-book approval covers them.
4. **Order of work.** I'd suggest Part Two first, as you're reading it, one chapter at a time and each backed up first. Then the training map and the Condensed Training page get updated after the book is final, per the canon holds.
