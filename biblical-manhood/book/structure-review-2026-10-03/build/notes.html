SCRIPTURE NOTES

10. The Father Who Does Not Leave

Opening verse: Luke 15:20 (NIV).

“Philip asked Him to just show them the Father”: John 14:8-9.

“the Father spoke from heaven”: Matthew 3:16-17.

“God had carried them the way a man carries his little boy”: Deuteronomy 1:31.

“the Father knows what you need before you ask Him”: Matthew 6:8.

“discipline is how you can tell you’re a son”: Hebrews 12:7-8.

“He would not leave them as orphans”: John 14:18.

“calling out to Him as Abba”: Romans 8:15; Galatians 4:6.

“Jesus told a story about a father like that”: Luke 15:11-24.

“the Father loves us enough to call us His children”: 1 John 3:1.

Each note is keyed by the phrase on the page, so the chapter carries no numbers or verse boxes. The opening verse is the chapter's only word-for-word quotation (NIV, with the Biblica notice on the copyright page).
