# Production bar

This has to look like a book you'd be proud to put on Amazon.

- Cover: 1600 x 2560, RGB, sharp at thumbnail size
- Interior: clean EPUB, real TOC, chapter openers, verse in the margin, Your turn box with write-in space
- Print later can add lined notes. Kindle still gets the questions and a write-in prompt.
- Type hierarchy, not a Word dump
- No Canva templates, no stock mashups
- NIV permission notice in front matter at publish
- No spend until Aaron says
