# Proposal: 02-you-are-not-crazy (Aaron-locked)

## Panel touchpoints

- Readers responded to the permission in this chapter: a man is not crazy for feeling that the culture's picture of manhood is contradictory and that nobody handed him a better one. Keep it.
- Marcus (maybe) wanted the book to say sooner what it is *for*, not only what it is against. The revised chapter 3 does that in its first page, so chapter 2 does not need to carry it. A single bridge sentence would help.
- If chapter 2 currently runs a season tour, consider trimming to one line. The revised chapters do not repeat age tours; the introduction proposal explains the approach.

## Proposals

1. **Bridge sentence (optional, end of chapter).** In Aaron's voice, one line that hands off: "So if you're not crazy, what are you? The next chapter gives you four marks, and you can hold your life up against them." Chapter 3 begins by picking up the phrase "not crazy," so the hand-off will feel intentional either way.

2. **Eldredge-shelf awareness.** Chapter 3 carries the paragraph that separates the four marks from the "wild heart" shelf. Chapter 2 should not add a second version of that argument. If it already has one, consider cutting it to avoid a double.

3. **Closer check.** Same as chapter 1: if a closer is present, labels must match exactly.

4. **Draft-stamp sweep.** Same as chapter 1.

## Continuity check: what the revised chapters assume about chapter 2

- That chapter 2 does not introduce the four marks by name.
- That chapter 2's core promise is "you are not crazy," and chapter 3 can quote that phrase back without quoting any other sentence from the chapter.
