# 9. Anger, the itch, and the drift

He has a sentence for it.

"That's just how I am." The short fuse, or the evenings he has to have, or the way he will not be boxed in. He can say it with a half smile, like he's being honest about himself. The people who live with him are not smiling. They know the temperature of the room when he walks in. They know which nights he will be gone even if he is on the couch. They stopped asking him to land.

He thinks he is describing a personality. He is describing fruit.

A hole does not stay empty. It grows something. Anger. The itch. The drift. A lot of us treated those like weather. Like the way a man just is. They are fruit. Fruit has a root. Fruit is also a choice you keep making.

I have called my snap a high standard. I have called my numbness a way to cope. I have called my drifting being easy to live with. None of that was a diagnosis. It was a costume that let the fruit keep growing.

A man treating a fruit like a personality is hard to reach. You tell him he is angry and he says he is just honest. You tell him he is numbing and he says he is just tired. You tell him he will not land and he says he is keeping his options. Those sentences sound like self-knowledge. They are how a foothold stays rented.

This is not the hole again. The hole got named. This is what grew. You, this week, with a live thing that started there and is not excused by it. Kind is not the same as letting present sin keep the house.

You will probably recognize more than one. Name the one that is running. If you name all three so you do not have to face one, you are still hiding.

Anger is the loudest. It can look like volume. It can also look like a man who never raises his voice and still makes the room smaller. A joke with a hook in it. A long memory for slights and a short one for his own. Some of you have never thrown a thing in your life. You just go still. The room learns to stop bringing you things. That is still anger. It is just cheaper to maintain.

Some of you can name the day you stopped asking and started punishing. I will not romanticize that day. Hurt happened. Then you began to spend it on people who were not the hole.

I have won the argument on the way home and walked in already finished with everyone. Nobody in the house had done a thing in the last ten minutes. I still came through the door like a verdict. I called it being honest. It was leftover heat, looking for a smaller person to land on.

Paul said, "In your anger do not sin." Do not let the sun go down while you are still angry, and do not give the devil a foothold. That's Ephesians 4:26-27.

Anger is not always the sin. Keeping it overnight is how it becomes one. A foothold is a small true place the enemy can stand. You do not have to invite him in with a speech. You just have to leave the door cracked. An unclosed day of anger is a cracked door.

The itch is quieter. It is the thing you reach for when the ache shows up. After the house goes quiet, the same chair. The same reach. A screen. A bottle. A feed. Another hour at a job that already had enough of you. A gym that stopped being health a long time ago. You can baptize an itch with a respectable name. Discipline. Unwinding. Providing. I have done that. The itch is still an itch. It numbs a hole that will not fill with more of itself.

I have told myself I was just tired, then gone looking for a feeling that had nothing to do with rest. Rest would have been sleep. I did not want sleep. I wanted an hour where the bruise was not in charge.

The foothold is not only for the man who yells. Paul put it after anger because anger is the obvious door. The itch is a door too. So is the drift. The devil does not need a scene. He needs a place to stand. A nightly reaching is a place. A life with no yes is a place.

I am not writing a memoir of anyone's bottom. I am naming a pattern. When the day presses the old bruise, you go get a feeling that is not the bruise. Then you need it again tomorrow. That is not personality. That is a foothold with better lighting.

The drift is the one men congratulate. He is easy. He is not intense. He will try anything and die for nothing. He samples jobs, churches, women, opinions. He is never quite in. He can tell you about four churches and no covenant. Four jobs and no yes. That looks like a search. After a while it is a refusal to land. Lostness looks like calm if you do not look long. Underneath it is a man with no inherited yes, and no practiced one of his own. Convicted by nothing. Available to everything. The foothold here is the unoccupied life.

I have drifted and called it open-mindedness. It was not open. It was unoccupied. I could tell you what I was against. I had a thinner time telling you what I would die for. People liked how little trouble I was. Low trouble is not the same as a life.

The hole helps explain how you got here. It does not write the next hour. A wound can tell you why the fruit started. You still picked it this week. You still picked it yesterday. If you use the hole to excuse the picking, every true word will sound like an attack on your dad, and you will become a man who cannot be reached. I know the pull. I have used a true wound as cover. The wound was true. The cover was not.

The other lie is that you are the hole. You are not. You are a man with fruit. Fruit can be refused.

If you're young, the itch often looks like fun and the drift looks like options. Anger looks like having a spine. None of those are a spine. You can be nineteen and already feeding one. You can be twenty-two and already calling it who you are. Name which fruit is running without waiting to become a worse version of it. The earlier you name it, the less of your life it gets to eat.

If you're single, do not park the fruit in a future marriage and hope a wife will starve it. She will not. She will live with it until you tell the truth. Name it with a brother now. The itch in a quiet apartment is still the itch. The drift with no one to disappoint is still the drift.

If you're married, she already knows which fruit is yours. She has been walking around it. Anger makes her careful. The itch makes her lonely. The drift makes her tired of steering. You can be kind in public and still grow all three at home. Do not make her the audience for a speech about your childhood. Refuse the fruit. She will know without the speech.

If you got hurt on the way here, hear me. I am not calling you the hole. I am calling the fruit fruit. Some of you got handed a lot. That is real. The snap, the numb, the fade are still yours to stop feeding. Kind is not the same as a pass. A pass is how a wound becomes a lifestyle.

If you're older, the fruit can look mellowed and still be running. A 62-year-old anger goes quiet and turns into a verdict on everybody younger. An older itch hides in habits nobody questions. An older drift gets called retirement of the soul. You can still name which one is yours. The younger men need to see a man refuse a fruit, not just describe how it grew.

If the job is going well, the itch can look like ambition and the drift can look like being easy in the meeting. Anger can look like standards. The office will pay for a lot of that if the numbers still close. None of that is the same as telling the truth. You can close a quarter and still go home as a man who has been feeding a foothold all day. Competence is not repentance.

Name which fruit is running. One. Then take one honest move this week that is not more of it. If it is anger, close the day before the sun does. Say the true thing without the hook. If it is the itch, starve one reaching. Not a personality overhaul. One reaching you do not do. If it is the drift, land on one yes and keep it for seven days. A 22-year-old can do that. A 62-year-old can do that.

Tell one man which fruit, and the move. If he only hears the wound, you will get sympathy and keep the foothold. If he hears the fruit and the refusal, he can ask you whether you went back. Let him ask.

The hole bears fruit. Name which one is yours. Do not excuse the picking. You are not the hole. You are a man who can stop feeding what grew there.

Name it. Which fruit is running this week: anger, the itch, or the drift?

Do this week. One honest move that is not more of the fruit.
