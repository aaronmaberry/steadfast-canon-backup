# 35. These men are my brothers

He closed the book and put it on the shelf.

The page he wrote is in the notebook. No other name on it. He felt finished. He felt like a man who had done the work. Then he went to bed. Nobody else knew the sentence existed. A written move with no brother is still a speech.

I have been that man. I have closed a good thing and called the closing the staying. It was not staying. It was me, alone with a page I could still hide.

This page is the men. These men are my brothers. Do not finish alone. Name them. Stay.

A handshake in a lobby is not a brother. A man who would like your post is not a brother. A brother is a man who can call you when the week goes bad, and whom you would call. He has seen the page. He has a time. He will ask if the step happened. If you cannot name two of those, you do not have brothers yet. You have a shelf.

I have mistaken recognition for being known. A room can say my name and still not have me. Brothers are smaller than that. Two is enough to start. One to show the page. One more so the first is not a hero you will exhaust. You can add later. You cannot stay with a crowd that has never seen the sentence.

I have collected men like that. I have been friendly. I have been in the room. I have left with no one holding the sentence. Friendliness is cheap. Daily is not.

I have walked out of a room still nodding, still shaking hands, still saying we should get together, and then I have driven home with nobody who could ask me anything true. The handshake felt like enough because it was public. It was not enough. It was a shelf with people on it.

But encourage one another daily, as long as it is called "Today," so that none of you may be hardened by sin's deceitfulness. That's Hebrews 3:13.

**Hebrews 3:13 NIV**
But encourage one another daily, as long as it is called "Today," so that none of you may be hardened by sin's deceitfulness.
:::

Daily. Not when the calendar is kind. Today, while it is still called that. The hardening does not wait for a better season. Sin is a liar, and a liar loves a man with a private page. A brother is how the lie gets interrupted. Not once a quarter. While it is still Today.

I have waited to need them. I wanted to be the man who encourages, not the man who is encouraged. That sounded strong. It was pride dressed as strength. You will harden if nobody is allowed to tell you the truth in time. You will also harden the men you only perform for. Encourage is two ways. Stay is two ways.

I have also used a good week as a reason to skip them. When I was fine I did not call. When I was not fine I was ashamed to call. That is how Today turns into a month, and a month turns into a hard heart with a clean calendar. Daily does not mean a meeting every morning. It means you do not let the lie get a long run. A text that tells the truth. A walk. A table. Then again, while it is still called Today.

Passivity loves a closed book on a shelf. The work looks done. The life is not. A last chapter that you read alone is how a year goes back to the old week with better language. You still need a mouth besides yours. Disappearing after the ink is how a man goes back to waiting. Letting a man see the page is how you stay.

If you already have a man, this page is the stay. A first conversation is a door. Staying is the house. Some of you started and then vanished because the first week felt awkward. Awkward is allowed. Awkward is not a reason to put the book back and go quiet. Put a time on this week. Keep it.

The last chapter asked you to put the next move on a page. This one asks you not to own that page alone. If the notebook dies in a drawer, the year will go back to the old week with better words. Show it. Let him ask. Stay when he does.

If you are twenty-two, a group chat is not brothers. A thread can pick a place and still never see the page. Write two names tonight and show one of them what you wrote. Do not wait for a later circle that feels more official. Today is still called Today.

If you are single, a future wife is not the brother. She is not coming to be the man who interrupts your lie. Write two names of actual men. Show one the page. Put a time. Do not draft a woman as the first person who gets to know you.

If you are married, she is not a brother. She may be the best person in your house. This page is still men. Honor what she has carried. Then stay with men who can tell you the truth she should not have to carry alone. Show one of them the page. Let her know you did, not as a performance, as a fact.

If you got hurt on the way here, I will go slow. Some brothers failed you. A man used the word and then left. A group used your story. I am not going to joke that. Kind is not the same as finishing alone to stay safe. You still need one true one. Name him if he exists. If he does not, this week is for finding the first, not for a speech about how men are. One name. One time. Not a rebuilt circle by Friday.

If you are older, advising is not staying. A 62-year-old can be useful to younger men and still have nobody who sees his own page. Usefulness is not the same as being held. Write two names. One can be younger. One should be a man who will ask you, not only listen to you teach. Gray hair does not retire you from Today.

If the job is going well, the team at work is not this. They can like you. They can need you. They will not ask if you did the step you wrote when nobody at the office was watching. Show a man who is not on the payroll the page. Put a time that is not a meeting.

Write two names. Show one of them the page from last chapter. Put a time you will talk this week. Not a program. Names, a page, a time. You are not installing a whole brotherhood by Sunday. You are refusing to finish alone.

Tell one of those men you are staying. If he only hears that you liked the book, he will nod and you will shelf it. If he sees the page and the time, he can ask you whether you stayed. Let him ask.

The shelf will still take the book. The page still needs another name. These men are my brothers.

Name it. Who are the two men? Not a lobby. Not a chat. Two names you would call.

Do this week. Show one of them the page. Put a time you will talk.
