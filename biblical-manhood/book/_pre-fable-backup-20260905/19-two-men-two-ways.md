# 19. Two men, two ways

You can stand at a cattle guard after a rain and still see both ruts.

One set turns toward the house. One set keeps the fence line, past the tank, and dies in grass. Same truck could have taken either. The mud does not argue. It just holds the choice.

I have tried to straddle a Y. The truck does not. You pick a way. Then you live in the ruts you cut.

Two men. Two ways. That is this page. Last page was the first morning. What was given. This one is the fork. You are already on one of them.

I used to think manhood was a mix. A little of this, a little of that. Custom blend. Keep the parts I like. Sand the parts that cost. The Book is less polite. It names two men, and then it is quiet. You do not get a third.

So it is written: "The first man Adam became a living being"; the last Adam, a life-giving spirit. That's 1 Corinthians 15:45.

Paul is talking about resurrection. I know. He still names two men. First Adam received life. Last Adam gives it. One becomes a living being and then spends the gift on himself. One is a life-giving spirit. You are already walking after one of them.

The first way is not a cartoon. It looks like a man. It can clock in and be liked. It just never leaves the borders of himself. He runs on now and instinct. He does not run on a word. He draws life from the people near him and calls it need. He sits a hard thing out and still wants the name. If the vision never leaves his own skin, he gets small. Not overnight. By inches. A small man other people still trust.

I have been that man. I have taken the air in a room and called it being present. I have treated a person like fuel. I have waited to see how a thing would go before I would own it. I have wanted a better seat at the same table and called it ambition. It was the first Adam, still hungry.

The last Adam is Jesus. Not a mascot. Not a tone you put on for an hour. A man who came to do a will he did not write. He did not improve his station. He gave life. People could sit near him without being used. That is not softness. That is a different way. A word, not a mood. A will that is not him. He does not sit the hard thing out. He expects the Father's reward, not a name he can spend this week.

The last Adam on a man is not a third man. This page is the fork. You do not get his name as a sticker and keep the first man's hours.

You cannot mix them. Hear that. A lot of us want the last Adam's name and the first Adam's ease. Give life when the room is watching. Take it when we are tired. Keep a word over the week until the word costs a yes we like. The mud does not grade that way. The ruts show.

You do not have to own a ranch to have ruts. Your week already cut them. The friend you only call when you need a thing. The younger man who left smaller. The apartment you treat like a closet with a microwave. Same truck. Different way.

I have wanted a third option. A nicer first Adam. A cheaper Jesus. That man is still the first one. He just has better lighting.

I have given a good talk about the second way and then taken from the first person who needed me. The talk was not the way. The taking was. I have left a table smaller than I sat down. The other men were polite about it. Polite is not the same as life.

The first man became a living being. He received. Then he spent what he was given on a better station. The last man does not only have life. He gives it. You can tell which way you are on by what the people near you have to recover from after you leave the room. If they have to put themselves back together, you took. If they can breathe, you gave. A quiet man can drain a room. A loud man can leave life in it. The tell is not volume. The tell is whether life increased.

Some of you are waiting to feel like the last Adam before you walk like him. Feeling is not the fork. The fork is the week. You do not climb into the last Adam. The first way is the one already in you. You have already seen that lean. This page is not a new ladder. The last Adam gives life to men who did not have it. You receive him. Then you walk. Walking is not the same as mixing.

If you're young, you already have a way. Nineteen. Twenty-two. Delayed manhood likes to think the ruts start later, once the life is official. They do not. A 19-year-old can take from a room as well as a man with a mortgage. He can also give life this week without waiting for a title. Waiting for a better stage is how a young man stays in the first way and calls it not starting yet. You do not get a third man called later.

If you're single, a wife is not the last Adam. Marriage is good. It is not the moment you switch men. Your apartment is already a place you can take from. So is a friendship. So is a woman you have not married and still treat like she exists to fill you. Do not park the second way in a wedding that is not on the calendar. Name which man you are now.

If you're married, she already knows which man came home. You do not have to announce it. She can feel a taker before you put the keys down. She can feel a life-giving spirit in the same kitchen without a speech. Do not make her the proof that you are the last Adam. Do not make her the one who gives life while you visit. Tell her which ruts you cut this week. Then cut different ones when the house is loud.

If you got hurt on the way here, I will go slow. The wound is real. It is not a third man. Hurt can sit in you and still not choose your Adam for you. The first way loves a wound, because a wound can excuse a taking. Kind is not the same as letting the injury pick the ruts. You can be wounded and still give life this week. The last Adam does not mock the wound. He also does not let it drive.

If you're older, years of the first way can look like wisdom. A 62-year-old can cut the same ruts for four decades and call it being realistic. Realistic is often the first way with more years on it. You still have a next move. The younger men need a man who will name which way he is still on, not a man who has had enough years to dress the taking as experience. Retirement is not a third man either.

If the job is going well, the first Adam can look like a win. Taking can look like excellence. A title can be a better seat at the same table. The office will pay you to never leave your own borders. It will call that drive. Name it. Then do one thing this week that gives life where the badge does not need you. A high performer can walk the last Adam's way on a day the scoreboard is quiet.

Name which man you walked like this week. First Adam or last. Not a percentage. Not a blend. Then one act that gives life instead of taking it. A person already in reach. A taking you stop. Once, on purpose.

A 22-year-old can do that. A 62-year-old can do that. You do not have to finish becoming the last Adam by Friday. You have to stop pretending you are a mix.

Tell one man which ruts you cut, and the act. If he only hears a talk about Adam, he will nod and you will straddle the Y again. If he hears the week, he can ask you next week which man showed up. Let him ask.

Two men. Two ways. The mud already knows. You do not get a third.

Name it. Which man did you walk like this week? First Adam or last.

Do this week. One act that gives life instead of taking it.
