# 20. Destiny is not a vibe

July. Driveway. A grocery box of sparklers and a lighter that only works if you baby it.

A boy wants the next one lit before the last one dies. You do it. Twenty seconds of white fire. His face in it. Then a wire, still smoking, already boring. He is holding the next. You know the feeling.

I have lived like that.

Not with sparklers. With a calling I could feel for twenty minutes. A talk. A song. A drive after a conversation that finally landed. For a little while I was a man with a spine. Then Thursday. I was a man with a calendar, looking for the next thing that would light me.

Destiny is not a vibe. A man was made for a cause that does not go out when the wire is cold.

That is this page. Last page was two ways a man can go. This one is the fire. Not a career talk. Not a life map you owe by Friday. The cause. The one that is not you.

I used to wait for a feeling to tell me what I was for. If the feeling was good, I had a destiny. If the week was gray, I was lost. That is not a calling. That is a mood with a lighter.

And he died for all, that those who live should no longer live for themselves but for him who died for them and was raised again. That's 2 Corinthians 5:15.

:::margin
**2 Corinthians 5:15 NIV**
And he died for all, that those who live should no longer live for themselves but for him who died for them and was raised again.
:::

No longer live for themselves. That is the line. Not find a passion. Not wait until it feels like the one. He died. You live. The life is not for you.

I have lived for the feeling of being a man with a cause. That is still living for myself. I just dressed it. I have walked out of a room aimed and been a small man by lunch. The talk was not the problem. Treating the talk like a life was.

Living for Him on a Thursday looks smaller than a destiny should. You keep a word. You tell the truth when a lie would protect your name. You show up for a man who cannot do anything for your brand. That is still a cause.

A cause that fits inside your preferences is a hobby with a bigger vocabulary. Transcendent means it is not you. It outlasts your mood. It can tell a week no. If it cannot tell you no, it is not a cause. It is a vibe.

You will give your best hours to something. Energy has to go somewhere. If it does not go to Him, it will go to a smaller fire. I have run a league like a calling and treated actual people like leftover. I have nursed a slight I would not drop. I have given a night to a feed and called it rest. I have lived in the version of me that was always about to start. None of that is evil on a Saturday. All of it will eat a life if it is what you live for.

A man whose cause is himself gets smaller. Not overnight. A year of it. He can still look strong. He is just increasingly about him. The people near him live in the weather of that even if they cannot name it. A roommate. A wife. A son. A younger man who thought you were going somewhere. They feel the shrink.

I am not ranking you above the women in your life. I am saying a man was built to carry a cause bigger than his own name, and when he will not, everyone in reach pays for the vacancy.

Passivity waits for the sparkler to tell him who he is. A man who lives for Him does not wait on a mood. He keeps the life when the driveway is just a driveway.

I have called a gray week proof that I had no calling. It was proof I had been using a feeling as a lord. The verse does not ask how the fire felt. It names who the life is for.

Some of you have only ever been handed other men's fire. A coach in a locker room. A founder talking about the early years. A preacher on a screen you watch in bed. You can feel it while they talk. Then the room empties and you are you again. Borrowed fire is not a cause. You can borrow a feeling. You cannot borrow a life. The life is already His. You either live it for Him or you live it for you.

A heading that is still you is not a cause. You can write a sentence and still be the point of it. The test is simple. If the lights went out on your name, would the thing still be worth doing? If not, you have been standing at your own fire.

The cause has a name. Jesus. You are not inventing a destiny in a quiet hour and hoping it sticks. He already died. He was already raised. The open question is not what you are passionate about. It is whether you will keep living for yourself.

If you're young, they told you to find your passion. Fine as a class. It is not a life. Nineteen. Twenty-two. You already have a Lord. Do not wait for a feeling at thirty to start living for Him. Delayed manhood loves a future calling because a present cause would cost him Tuesdays. A 22-year-old can live for Him this week without a title, a lease, or a mood that cooperates.

If you're single, a wife is not the cause. Marriage is good. She is not going to arrive and hand you one. You are not a man on hold. Your apartment, your word, the actual people in reach: those are already a life that can be for Him or for you. Name who this year is for.

If you're married, she is not your destiny and you are not hers as a stand-in for God. A house can become a closed loop that feels holy because it is busy. Include her in a life that is for Him. Do not make her the feeling that proves you have a life. Tell her who you are living for. Then live it when the week is loud. She already knows if the fire is you.

If you got hurt on the way here, I will go slow. Hurt can dress itself as a calling. Helping men who got what you got can be holy. It can also be the wound still running the show. Kind is not the same as baptizing the injury as a mission. You can be wounded and still live for Him, not for the story of what was taken. The cause does not mock the wound. It keeps the wound from becoming the god.

If you're older, nostalgia is a sparkler with better lighting. A 62-year-old can live in the old fire and call it wisdom. You still have a Lord. You still have a generation in reach. Retirement is not a cause. Neither is being left alone. Name who the years are for. Then take one step this week that would still be required if the old fire never came back.

If the job is going well, the office will hand you a mission and a wall of values. You can get misty at the offsite and live for yourself by Wednesday. A company cause is not the same as living for Him. Honor the work. Do not baptize it. If the title came off on Saturday, would you still have a fire that is not you? Name it. Then take one step that would still be required if the badge were gone.

Name the fire you stood at this week that was actually you. One. Then one act that is for Him, written, that does not wait on a feeling. Call the man. Keep the promise. Give the hour you were going to spend on your own name. Tell the truth. Stay in the small good. Not a career. Not a map of the rest of your life. One act.

A 22-year-old can do that. A 62-year-old can do that. You are not discovering a secret life by Friday. You are putting the lighter down long enough to live for the One who already died and was raised.

Tell one man the fire, and the act. If he only hears that you want more purpose, he will nod and you will light another wire. If he hears the act, he can ask you next week whether you did it when the driveway was just a driveway. Let him ask.

A man was not made to live for himself. The feeling will come and go. The cause does not.

Name it. What fire did you stand at this week that was actually you?

Do this week. One act for Him that does not wait on a feeling. Write it.
