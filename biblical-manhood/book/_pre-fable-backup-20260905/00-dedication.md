# Dedication

For Evan.

I did not want you having to guess what a man is. People will tell you work is enough, that a good mood is peace, that needing nobody is strength, and I have seen those lines fail. God made you on purpose and He is not confused about you. A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward, and I want that in you when I am not there to say it.

Dad
