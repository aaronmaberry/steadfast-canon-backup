# 21. If there is no wife yet

The garment bag is still on the hook.

Saturday was a wedding. A good one. He stood where they told him. He smiled in the photos. He meant the toast, even the sentences he finished in the car. He hung the bag up because the shop is closed on Sunday. It is Thursday. The bag is still there. He walks past it to get his keys. He lives like the real life is the one with a first dance, and this apartment is what you do until then.

I have been that man. I have also been the man up front who kept saying "when you get home" and watched a 26-year-old wait for a sentence that included him. I told myself I was being specific. I was skipping him. I made the house sound like it had a cover charge.

This page is the house, and the men who think they do not have a key.

Last page was a cause that is not a mood. This one is easier to dodge. If there is no wife yet, you are not in the lobby. Marriage is good. It is not the finish line of manhood. It is not the start of it either.

I am not talking you out of wanting a wife. Wanting one is not a defect. Rehearsal is. A whole week treated as what you do until someone says yes. Married men can do that too, and then they talk as if this part of the book is a couples class. A wounded man can hear "yet" and think we are mocking the quiet on the other side of the room. We are not.

Nevertheless, each person should live as a believer in whatever situation the Lord has assigned to them, just as God has called them. That's 1 Corinthians 7:17.

**1 Corinthians 7:17 NIV**
Nevertheless, each person should live as a believer in whatever situation the Lord has assigned to them, just as God has called them. This is the rule I lay down in all the churches.

Assigned. Called. Live as a believer in it. Paul is not stalling the men who will marry. He is not putting the unmarried in the lobby. He is laying down a rule in all the churches. All of them. The unmarried man is not a guest in the church that has a rule. He is in the rule. The situation already has a Lord over it. You do not get a new call when a ring shows up. You get a wife, if God gives one. That is a gift. It is not a promotion into manhood.

I have waited on a woman to make me serious. I have kept a life packed and called it being open to whatever God wanted. It was not open. I left it in the bag on purpose. I have also treated a wedding like the last real day, and then gone back to being a visitor in a house I already pay for. Same soul. Different paperwork.

I have called it freedom that nobody knew if I came home. Freedom is a good word for a cheap thing. Being a man would have cost me a Thursday. The lobby did not.

That call does not ask your marital status. Passivity loves a future wife because a future wife cannot hand him anything today. If you need a wedding to start being a man, you will need another season to keep being one. There is always a later house.

Some of you were trained to hear "man" and picture a driveway with two cars. Church did that. Uncles did that. A certain kind of talk does that and thinks it is honoring marriage. It is not honoring marriage. Marriage is good. Treating it like the only doorway is how you turn a gift into a gate and then leave half the room outside.

The ache is real. A quiet apartment at 11 is real. Watching other men leave with someone who knows their actual name is real. I will not sand that down. Loneliness is not proof you are unfinished as a man. It is a pain. Pain can tell the truth about wanting a wife. Pain can also talk you into sitting your own life out. Those are not the same sentence.

If you are walking toward a woman, good. Character now. Not a hunt. Not a project she is supposed to complete. The man she would meet is the man you are on a Thursday, bag still on the hook or bag returned and the week actually lived. Do not outsource your spine to a date on an invitation.

If you're young, they will talk as if you are not in it yet. Nineteen. Twenty-two. A girlfriend can start to look like the stamp that makes you official. She is not. Delayed manhood loves a plus-one because a plus-one would mean the life has finally begun. It has begun. A 19-year-old already has a situation assigned. Classes. A job that does not look like much. A church he visits. Live as a believer in that. Waiting for official is how a young man stays in the lobby and calls it not starting yet. A 22-year-old can inhabit a small life this week without a ring to make it count.

If you're single, this is not a guest chapter. This is your page. Marriage is good. You may marry. You may not. Either way you are not on deck. Your apartment is already a house you can refuse to live in. Your church is already a people you can keep visiting. Your word is already a load. Do not treat this part of the book as a brochure for a life you do not have. It is a house, and you have a key. Name what you have been leaving in the bag. Then pick it up as a man already called.

If you're married, you can still live like the house starts later. After the baby sleeps. After the job calms. After she becomes easier to love. That is the same garment bag with a ring on it. She is not the finish line. You did not become a man in the receiving line, and you do not get to retire being a man because the photos turned out. Include her in a life that is already going. Do not make her the proof. And stop talking past your brother. If every application you give starts with "your wife," you have made a doorway out of a gift. He is not extra seating. He is in the rule.

If you got hurt on the way here, I will go slow. If she left, I am not going to joke. If she died, I am not going to cheer you into a later. If she never came, I will not treat you as a project. The "yet" on this page is not a taunt. It is a way of saying the house does not evict you. You are a man in a situation the Lord has assigned. Live as a believer in it. The wound is real. It is not a third status that suspends the call. Kind is not the same as leaving you in the lobby until the story gets prettier.

If you're older, a long singleness can start to sound like a verdict, or a long marriage can start to sound like the whole name. A 62-year-old who never married is not a mascot and not a caution. He still has a next move. The younger men need to see a man who inhabited a life without waiting for a doorway that never opened. A 62-year-old whose wife is gone still has a house. Retirement is not a wife and it is not a pass. Name the situation you are in. Live in it as a believer. Do not spend the years narrating the door that did not open.

If the job is going well, you can buy a beautiful place and still live like a hotel. The title came. The furniture came. The wife is the last box on a list that has been about you. A high performer can get married and keep the bag on the hook. He can also stay single and treat every year as a layover between deals. Honor the work. Do not baptize the layover. If the badge came off on Saturday, would you still live here on Thursday? Name it. Then do one thing this week that belongs to a man already called, not a man still packing.

Name what you have been treating as not-yet because a wife is not in it. One thing. Then inhabit it once this week as a man already assigned. Sit down to the meal. Stay after at church. Carry the load that is already in your hands. Take the bag back, not as a joke, as a man who lives here. Not a dating plan. Not a map of the rest of your life. The actual Thursday.

A 22-year-old can do that. A 62-year-old can do that. You are not finishing a love story by Friday. You are stopping the rehearsal long enough to live the situation you already have.

Tell one man the thing you parked, and that you inhabited it. If he only hears that you want to get married someday, he will nod and you will leave the bag up. If he hears the Thursday, he can ask you next week whether you lived here. Let him ask.

Marriage is good. You may be given a wife. You may not. Either way, you are not in the lobby. The Lord assigned a situation. He called you in it. Live as a believer. Manhood already fits in a one-bedroom. It does not wait on a first dance.

Name it. What have you been treating as not-yet because a wife is not in it?

Do this week. Inhabit that one thing once, as a man already assigned.
