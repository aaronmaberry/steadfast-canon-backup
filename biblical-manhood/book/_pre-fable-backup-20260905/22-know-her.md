# 22. Know her

The trip is still on the fridge.

Or the lock screen. Same light. She is laughing at something out of the frame. He liked that weekend. He left the picture there. He has not asked what this month is actually doing to her. He thinks the photo is current.

I have been that man. I have walked past a living woman and consulted a still. I knew the birthday. I knew the allergy. I knew the story from the first year. I called that knowing her. It was a file I never opened again.

This page is a husband who actually studies his wife. Last page was the lobby. The men who think they do not have a key. This one is the woman who already lives here. Some of you do not have her in the house. Stay. You are not a guest. You are also not the whole page.

Knowing her is a job, not a memory.

The purposes of a person's heart are deep waters, but one who has insight draws them out. That's Proverbs 20:5.

:::margin
**Proverbs 20:5 NIV**
The purposes of a person's heart are deep waters, but one who has insight draws them out.
:::

Deep waters. Not a puddle. Not a form you filled out in the first year. Purposes. The actual things in her. They do not sit on the surface for a man in a hurry. Insight draws them out. Draws. That is work. You do not skim a well and call yourself a student.

I have skimmed. I asked how her day was like a question that only wanted fine, and felt relieved when it did. I waited for her face to change and then asked, which is not study. That is damage control. I finished her sentence and called it closeness. I was saving time. I was saving me. I talked until I won and called the win clarity. I had not drawn anything out. I had pushed her under.

Passivity loves a finished picture. A still cannot ask anything of you. A living woman can. If you need her to stay the woman in the photo, you will resent every year she keeps living. She will keep living anyway.

Guessing is how a man stays done. Becoming a student of the woman in your house is the job. Ask a question that might wreck the convenient version. The prize is not a quieter car.

Study is not a quiz, and it is not a trap. It is not a night where you suddenly care because you feel guilty and she has to unload on cue. Do not hand her a book so she can brief you. You do the work. Ask. Stay for the answer. Update the file, then do it again, because she is not a unit you passed.

I have treated logistics as intimacy. The list from the store. The pickup time. The bill that got paid. Those are real. They are not her purposes. A man can run a house like a shop and never draw a thing from the woman he shares it with. You cannot draw deep water with half an ear and a screen in your hand.

You will not arrive. Hear that. The verse does not say the wise man finished. It says he draws. Present tense. She will not freeze for your convenience. If you studied her in the first year and then graduated, you did not know her. You knew a semester.

In the first year I asked everything. Where she grew up. What Sunday lunch was. Why she went quiet in a certain room. Then I got the job, or the house, or the kid, and I treated the rest as known. I did not get mean. I got efficient. Efficient is a kind word for a man who stopped being curious.

Some of you think she is not that deep. You say it like a compliment. Simple. Easy. Low maintenance. That sentence is about you. It means you have not gone down far enough to find anything that would cost you. Deep water does not announce itself to a man who will not drop a line.

Some of you can read a room and cannot read her. You can tell when a boss is about to turn. You can tell when a deal is dying. You can tell which friend is lying in a group text. Then you go home and guess. That is not a mystery. That is a man who spends his insight where it pays him back in the currency he likes.

If you're young, they will talk as if this page is later. Nineteen. Twenty-two. A girlfriend can start to look like a puzzle you crack so the life can start. She is not a puzzle. Delayed manhood wants people to be easy so he never has to pay attention. A 22-year-old can ask one real question this week of an actual person in reach and stay for the answer. Not a hunt. Not a test. Attention. If you will not study a person now, a ring will not give you a new pair of eyes.

If you're single, this is not a brochure for a wife you do not have, and it is not a lecture you sit out. Marriage is good. You may marry. You may not. Either way, a man who wings the people in front of him will not suddenly become a student at a wedding. Name the way you have been guessing. If you are walking toward a woman, study a person, not a project. If you are not, stay in the room for your brothers. Ask the married man if he actually knows her. He needs that more than he needs another joke.

If you're married, this is your page. She already knows if you are a student or a visitor. The photo is not current. Neither is the story from the year you moved. A friend of hers may know a true thing you have not asked. That should bother you more than it does. Ask her a question you do not already have an answer for. Then be quiet long enough to hear it. Write what you heard. Live with her like she is deep water, because she is. Last year's file is how you share a house with a stranger.

If you got hurt on the way here, I will go slow. If she left, this page is not a courtroom and not a taunt. You can tell the truth about whether you studied her without making that the whole story of why it ended. If she died, the photo may be all you have. I am not going to cheer you into a later. Honor is not pretending you knew her if you did not. It is not picking a fight with a year that cannot answer you. If she never came, you are not a guest on a page about wives. You still do not get to guess at people and call it freedom.

If you're older, thirty years is not the same as current. A 62-year-old can be loyal and still be running a version of her from the house they got when the kids were small. Loyalty is good. A still is not loyalty. She kept living. So did you. Name one thing you have not asked. Then ask it. The younger men need to see a man who did not retire from being a student. Retirement is not a pass on knowing her.

If the job is going well, you already know how to study. You would be embarrassed to walk into a client's meeting on last year's briefing. You walk into your own kitchen on it. Honor the work. Do not baptize the skip. A high performer can know a market and guess at a wife. The house will not grade you on the market. Name the file you have not opened. Then open it once this week, on purpose, without a trophy in it.

Name the thing you have been guessing. One. Then ask one real question this week and stay for the answer. Write what you heard. Not a speech about being more present. Not a map of her whole soul by Friday. One question you do not already own.

A 22-year-old can do that. A 62-year-old can do that. You are not finishing a marriage class. You are dropping a line into water you have been skimming and calling a life.

Tell one man the guess, the question, and what you heard. If he only hears that you want a better marriage, he will nod and you will go back to the photo. If he hears the question, he can ask you next week whether you stayed for the answer. Let him ask.

She kept living. The photo did not. Deep water does not come to the surface for a man who will not draw.

Name it. What have you been guessing at that you should have studied?

Do this week. Ask one real question. Stay for the answer. Write what you heard.
