# Chapter desk

Quality over speed. One chapter at a time. Original pages only. Human voice.

Aaron reviews a finished ebook PDF at the end, not each chapter.

Bind order: dedication (`00-dedication.md`, locked for Evan), contents (`00-contents.md`), introduction, then chapters. Do not rewrite locked front matter. Reed signs, Jarvis does a quiet check, then the next chapter opens. Do not send pages to Aaron.

## Before anyone writes

- Chapter number and title
- One job (the reader should leave able to say this in one sentence)
- What this chapter uniquely owns
- Original picture
- Scripture: NIV, cited, :::margin word-for-word. SCRIPTURE.md
- Next move a 22-year-old and a 62-year-old can both hear
- Season check
- Copyright check
- Repeat check
- Target 1,200 to 2,000 words before the closer
- Closer: Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this

## Chapter shape

1. Warm picture
2. Clean hit
3. Stay with it
4. Next move in the prose
5. Closer box
6. Stop

## Closer rules

- Hits this chapter's job only. Do not preview the next chapter.
- Makes a man look at his actual week.
- Room for more than one season.
- Not a 10-item workbook. Not Goggins/Hughes copy.
- Layout: Ash designs a quiet box. Levi writes the questions.

## After the draft

Reed: human bar, NIV margins, closer present, no leftover locked lines.

Do not start the next chapter until Reed signs and Jarvis says move.

## When the book is done

Bundle a finished ebook PDF with cover and in-chapter art. That is what Aaron reviews.

Then hire the reader panel in FOCUS-GROUP.md. Christian men, different ages. They bounce as buyers. The desk reworks. Do not hire them before the PDF exists.
