# About the Author

Wes Hart writes for ordinary men who are tired of waiting for a better season before they start living like men.

He's a husband, a father of two, and a Texan. His day job is leading teams in technology. The hours he cares about most, though, are the ones spent across a table from other men: kitchen tables, folding chairs in a garage, church classrooms, and diner booths early on a weekday with a Bible open next to the coffee. Steadfast Men, the twenty-four-week training this book grew alongside, started at one of those tables with a handful of skeptical guys. Since then it has been taught in living rooms, garages, church basements, and online, to men in every season of life: single and married, young and older, holding steady and starting over.

Wes never planned to write a book or build a men's program. What he set out to do was quit being the checked-out husband and distracted dad he had slowly turned into. A few older men saved him a seat back when he had nothing to bring to the table. Years later he started saving seats for other men. He isn't a pastor, and he isn't a counselor, and he'll tell you both before you get around to asking. He's a layman who believes that ordinary men, walking together under the Word of God, can become the husbands, fathers, brothers, and leaders their homes and churches are starving for. He's seen it happen too many times to call it a theory.

When he isn't working or teaching, you'll usually find him outside with his kids, on a long walk with his wife, or across from one other man who's trying to get honest about his Tuesday.

He and his family live in Texas.

To learn more, find the full twenty-four-week class, or bring Steadfast Men to your church or group, visit **walksteadfast.com**.
