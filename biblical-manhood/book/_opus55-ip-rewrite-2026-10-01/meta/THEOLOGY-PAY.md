# Theology check: "God's pay" is inheritance, by grace

Brief §2: wherever the fourth mark is taught or "pay"/"reward" carries weight, the pay must read as the inheritance the Lord gives His children (Col 3:24), grace-given, never wages that earn salvation (Eph 2:8-10; Rom 6:23; Rom 8:17). No sentence a reader could take as "work hard and God will save you."

Method: every prose sentence in `book/` containing pay / paid / paycheck / reward / wage / inheritance / earn / saved / salvation was listed (132 sentences) and read. Ordinary uses (paying bills, "pay attention", a paycheck as a job's wage, a child earning a parent's approval) need no anchor. Every place where God's pay or reward carries weight is below, with its anchor. Line numbers are in `book/`.

## Result

No sentence in the book presents work as the way to be saved. Every chapter that teaches the fourth mark or leans on reward has an inheritance/grace anchor in the same chapter, and chapter 7 carries the full teaching with Colossians 3:23-24, Ephesians 2:8-10 and Matthew 25:21 in the margin.
