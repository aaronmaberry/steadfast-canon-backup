# 6x9 interior sample

Print treatment only. 6 in x 9 in at 300 ppi (1800 x 2700). Bone paper, charcoal type, rust rail. C059 for titles, P052 for body.

Margins: gutter 0.75 in, outside 0.60 in, top and bottom 0.70 in. Opener is treated as a right-hand page (gutter on the left). Closer is treated as a left-hand page (gutter on the right). `spread-6x9.png` is a review composite of the two pages side by side, not a facing pair as they would print.

Opener uses locked chapter 1 copy (A quiet Tuesday) and the existing opener mark. Body is 11 pt P052 at 1.38 leading.

Closer reuses `/workspace/biblical-manhood/book/art/01-closer-box.png` unstretched (scaled down uniformly to the measure). Existing 01-15 closer boxes were not redrawn.

This is a sample of how the book would sit on a 6x9 page. Not a redesign of signed art.
