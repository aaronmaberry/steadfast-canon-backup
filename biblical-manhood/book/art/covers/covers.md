# Steadfast Men cover comps

Front only. Master 1600 x 2560 (KDP ebook). Built for a later 6x9 paperback. No portrait. Wes Hart is a pen name. No spend. No stock. Type is set in our book fonts (C059 / P052), not generated lettering.

The four directions are distinct on purpose. Pick a road, do not average them.

## A. Iron Rail
Type-led object cover. Bone paper, charcoal title, rust rail with four ticks down the left. Same language as the chapter opener mark. This is the one a man can hand across a table. No photograph.

## B. Fence at last light
Photographic. A weathered cedar post and wire in a Central Texas pasture at last light. Title in bone over the sky. Grown and quiet. Not a ranch brochure, not a wilderness-warrior poster, no man on the cover.

## C. Four ticks
Still-life. Weathered iron post with four pegs against bone plaster and limestone. The mark is the cover. Title sits small in the wall. Same four-tick language as the book, not a ministry logo mashup.

## D. Night charcoal
Literary dark. Near-black field, bone title, one rust rule, a small four-tick rail. High-contrast at thumbnail. The serious-press direction.

Working textures for B and C live beside the comps. Renderers: `render_covers.py`.
