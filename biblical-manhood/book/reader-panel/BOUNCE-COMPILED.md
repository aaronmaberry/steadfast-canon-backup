## Consensus themes

### Delivered (panel agreement)

- **Tuesday spine.** Ch 1 names ordinary-day manhood (cereal bowl, phone, unfinished house). Sell chapter across all five.
- **Four marks.** Usable sentence in a truck. Ch 3 longhand + say-to-a-brother lands. (Earl: strong in the mouth; also flags Eldredge-shelf uniqueness risk — see Dig.)
- **Voice.** Human, not church-template, not hustle, not rage-bait. Scripture in margin as tool. Dedication to Evan honest.
- **House chapters (22–25).** Know her / Lay it down / What she actually needs / Bed as covenant — hard and fair for married readers (Marcus, Drew).
- **Wound Part 2 (esp. for James).** Empty chair, anger/itch/drift, Father who stays, leave without slamming, problem inside — wallet-open for divorced man.
- **Ch 21 for singles.** Best page for Caleb; manhood already fits a one-bedroom.
- **Brothers + next move (13, 14, 34, 35).** Wife is not a brother; ink not windshield; two names + a time.
- **Part 5 direction.** Next move / every season / brothers — why Earl stays to the end (and why thinness hurts).
- **Closers.** Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this — **the product**. Small enough men might actually do them.
- **Cover direction.** Navy / gold / plumb. Serious. No fake blurbs. Keep it that way.
- **Length as object.** 189 pages / 6x9 feels like a real book when production is finished — not a pamphlet.

### Missed / friction (panel agreement)

- **Season-tour drum.** Young / single / married / hurt / older / job — starts useful, becomes template by mid-book. Skimming, heat loss, "visitor" feel.
- **Part 3 thin.** Garden / two Adams / destiny feels like a hallway after Part 2 weight and before the house. Drew / James / Earl / Caleb all flag it.
- **Single lane thins after 21.** Part 4 after ch 21 leans husbands/dads; Caleb audits. Son chapters have courtesy lane, not owner lane.
- **Infant / first-year marriage under load.** Marcus: 3am, scorekeeping, passivity as "I worked today." Ch 29 early for newborn.
- **Post-divorce as season, not aside.** James: ring empty, drop-off calendar, desire without next-woman-as-repair — needs one honest stretch.
- **Older man as stamped visitor.** Earl: named often, owns few pages; Part 4 assumes car seat / rec league more than gray hair.
- **Brothers hard to picture.** Ask / stay-after / breakfast assume visibility and free mornings. Thin church + 20-min week need second steps.
- **Providing without disappearing.** Drew / Marcus: paycheck real; hide-in-hours dodge named; need clearer page without finance seminar.
- **Post-badge Tuesday.** Drew: after 6:30 practices that survive a real job / fifteen years of wrong gift.
- **Closer formula fatigue.** Same shape every chapter is a feature until it becomes skim-homework (Drew, Earl). Vary a few.
- **Draft / shop stamps.** Review-draft / not for distribution on paid file = deal-breaker language for buyers (all who mentioned it).
- **Production unfinishedness (Earl second pass).** Lagging footers (wrong chapter title), uneven closer boxes (designed box on some chapters, plain prose on others), blank verso / part-back padding inflating the 189. Cover loses trust by page 40.
- **Part 5 thinnest by word count (Earl).** Six short chapters where older next-move lives; book spent breath earlier — wrong place to go light.
- **Four marks / Eldredge shelf uniqueness (Earl).** Creed line may read as remix of famous manhood shelf before Tuesday pictures earn the man. Own honesty or sharpen what is uniquely Steadfast.

### Dig (where rewrite should go deeper)

1. Soften / signpost season tours — stop pasting every lane every chapter; let primary seats own pages.
2. Thicken single lane after ch 21 (and 22yo rooms for the four marks).
3. Infant / first-year marriage under baby load (Marcus Tuesday).

5. Older man primary seats in Part 4/5 — grandson, non-blood younger man, marriage after the noise, badge optional.
6. Thicken or sharpen Part 3 so it matches wound + house weight (or shorten the bridge cleanly).
7. Brothers with ~20 min/week — parking-lot text that tells the truth; second step when ask ghosts.
8. Providing without disappearing — tight money that still comes home.
9. Post-badge Tuesday practices — named week after the wrong gift for years.
10. Vary closer formula on a few chapters so men cannot coast the last page.
11. Make Part 5 as thick as the promise (next move / brothers / older lane).
12. Settle creed uniqueness: credit Lewis origin clearly in voice terms already locked; keep pictures original; do not invent Eldredge content or paste Hawks/Lewis/Ted/Red Rocks.

## Ordered rewrite priorities (for desk / Fable)

Actionable. Highest consensus and buy-bar impact first.

1. **Soften and signpost season tours.** Stop running all six lanes as a stamped block every chapter. Let the chapter's primary seat own the page; keep other seasons as short, real doors — not a checklist. Cuts skim, restores heat (all five).
2. **Thicken the single lane after ch 21.** Keep married content; give Thursday-apartment / practice-now ownership through more of Part 4 so Caleb is not a guest. Unpack four marks in 22yo rooms (roommate, group chat, internship, parents' house, lobby).
3. **Add infant / first-year marriage under load.** Same plain voice: 3am, who gets the nap, passivity as "I worked today," scorekeeping fog. Do not wait for jersey/hill/day chapters to speak to Marcus.
4. **Write a post-divorce season stretch.** Not a divorce book. One honest pass: honor without slam, fathering in the hours you have, desire without next-woman-as-repair, hope with Monday weight after confession (James). Own remarriage/dating-after briefly or stop implying "yet" covers every story.
5. **Give older men primary seats.** One or two Part 4/5 chapters written first for gray hair — grandson, non-blood younger man, marriage after the noise, work when badge is optional, brothers when work friends retire (Earl). Stop pasting "if you're older."
6. **Thicken or sharpen Part 3.** Garden / two Adams / destiny must match Part 2 and house weight — or become a shorter, sharper bridge that does not sag before Know her (Drew, James, Earl, Caleb).
7. **Brothers for a thin week / thin church.** What "tell a brother" looks like with ~20 minutes in a parking lot; second step when ask-the-man fails or ghosts; capable man starting without a small-group launch (Marcus, Caleb, Drew).
8. **Providing without disappearing.** One clear page: tight money / real paycheck that still comes home — not a finance seminar, not baptizing the overtime slot (Marcus, Drew).
9. **Post-badge Tuesday practices.** Named moves after 6:30 that survive a real job and years of the wrong gift — plan / face / honor as a week, not only a metaphor (Drew).
10. **Make Part 5 as thick as the promise.** Next move / every season / brothers / older lane currently thinnest by word count; wrong place to go light (Earl). Breathe here; tighten earlier loops if needed.
11. **Vary closer formula on select chapters.** Keep Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this as the spine; break shape on a few so men cannot skim the last page on autopilot (Drew, Earl). Closers remain the product.
12. **Settle four-marks uniqueness vs famous shelf.** Keep marks locked. Pictures stay original (Tuesday, chair, street strip, windshield). Credit Lewis origin as already required; do not reprint Hawks/Lewis/Ted/Red Rocks/One Mission; do not invent Eldredge. Sharpen Steadfast-only spine so a man who walked *Wild at Heart* does not smell a remix (Earl).
13. **Kill draft stamps and finish production evenness before any sale object.** Strip review-draft / not for distribution / shop talk. Even closer boxes; fix lagging footers; trim blank verso / part-back padding; fix PDF metadata. No fake jacket blurbs — ever.
14. **Optional enhancements (not spine):** one-page "if you're 22 and single, start here" map (Caleb); fillable man-plan / next-move sheet pointed from ch 34 (Drew); clearer "read this first" path for married-with-little-ones (Marcus).
