## Cluster claims

**Intro (01).** Manhood confused: industrial split of dads from sons; spiritual drop of revealed truth; 1960s-70s gender as construct. Dad is destiny. Confused men create problems (Mead: every society must define roles for men) and settle for less (provider-only, delayed manhood). No lofty vision. Bible has insight. Unfinished past, then a written plan.

**Nurture wounds (02-07).** Father (02 audio ASR, no YouTube captions; 03 recap matches): deficit ordinarily met by dad. Sons need memories, direction with why, modeled conviction, father's heart. Results: anger, addiction/obsession, drift. Do not excuse present sin. Heal: forgive, ask love/blessing, reconcile, enter the Father's love, break the cycle. Mother (04-05): unhealthy intimacy with mom; later threatened by women or overidentified with them. Passivity is not masculine. Gen 3:16 desire/dominate is curse, not design. Jesus leaves without disrespect (Luke 2; John 2; Matt 12; John 19). Call sons off mom: transcendent cause, vision, code, ceremony. Mentors (06-07): no healthy male comradeship = blind spots, short-sighted masculinity. Isolation: no challenge, accountability, cheer, intimacy. Mentoring is initiation, not only instruction. Ask; do not wait.

**Depravity (08-09).** Shift from nurture to nature. Fallen and cursed, at odds with the Creator. Separated, futile, enslaved, sentenced to death. Culture prefers "basically good." Most real problems are inside. Education and willpower cannot eradicate it; must be born again. Masks. Do not trust a path that seems right (Prov 14:12). Male patterns: avoid domestic responsibility; rule wrongly; get lost in career and lose a transcendent cause.

**Vision (10-12).** Born-good yields victimization; born-selfish yields salvation (own the response). No vision, people run wild (Prov 29:18; Ex 32). Wound: stunted masculinity misdirects energy into eat, drink, sex, brand, disposable wife. Genesis teaches by story: equal image; man created first, tasked, named, leaves; helper is partner for the mission; fall is passivity and blame.

**Definition (13-14).** Core is central, not comprehensive. Women: nurturers of life. Men: bearers of destiny. Two Adams, two masculinities. Four marks from Adam vs Jesus: reject passivity; accept responsibility (a will to obey, a work to do, a woman to love); lead courageously (direction, protection, provision); expect God's reward.

**Wife / sex / kids (15-20).** Live with her according to knowledge; co-heir; prayers on the line (1 Pet 3:7). Not boss (benefits up) and not 50/50 (nobody owns the whole). Head = servant leader; helper is exalted (Gen 2:18). He honors; she submits/respects. Cindy 25 ways (sorry, understand, include her in destiny, provide core costs, start over in the Spirit). Sex is gift-glue, not Puritan denial or Playboy demand. Purposes: children, purity, mutual pleasure. Isolation cycle: he withholds emotion until sex; she withholds sex until emotion. Daughters: define a real woman; date her; ceremony. Sons: dig (character), set (vision, family-name code, cause), spike (costly ceremony). Lewis knight / Journey to Manhood.

**Design / journey / legacy / close (21-24).** Eph 2:10 poiema; follow Jesus, provide, protect, serve. Feelings are dashboard, not wheel. Levinson seasons + Hicks/Lewis stages: adam, zakar, gibbor, enosh, ish, zaken. Fail a stage and you still advance, carrying it. Legacy is work-as-mission (~45 yrs at work vs ~18 with kids). Ambassador in the industry. Recap, gospel invitation, man cards, creed.

## Practices

- Small groups. Initiate. Find a man to run with.
- **Man plan:** past / present / future masculinity. "My next move as a man is..." Napkin or book. Hand to Nate. Due ~Apr 24. Required for the card.
- **Man cards (24):** elders, left-hand card / right-hand shake, widening circle. Creed: Christ is my leader; Christ is my teacher; these men are my brothers; each man is my mirror; this work is for men; our guidance and growth; my pledge is to God; and life is my goal.
- Father: forgive, ask love, reconcile; then raise sons (Eph 6:4); call them off mom with cause, vision, code, ceremony.
- Husband: servant-lead (Cindy 25); live by knowledge; blessing flow down; do not deprive.
- Work: daily prayer marker; coworker background / pain / dream; grace story + 3-4 min gospel.
- After 24: Journey to Manhood (sons 11-15; four Wednesdays then 9-12 months toward ceremony).
