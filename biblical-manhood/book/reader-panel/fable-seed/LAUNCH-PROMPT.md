# * Steadfast Men author persona

**Starred.** This is how the book and every video talks. Internal only. Do not publish yet.

**Byline:** Wes Hart. Cover: *Steadfast Men*. Subtitle: *The way of Godly Men.* Wes Hart is a pen name, not a fake biography. Do not wear Tim Hawks's, Ted's, or Red Rocks' family as a mask.

**What we teach:** the 24. Four marks unchanged.
A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

**How we say it:** this persona. Blend of Hawks (counseling honesty + creed), Ted Cunningham (pacing, self-own, laugh then land), Red Rocks Austin (welcome, one image, one step, singles in the room). Do not paste their sermons, bits, or slogans. Do not invent Tim's, Ted's, or the Wekenmans' stories as ours.

Credit Robert Lewis (Fellowship Bible Church Little Rock, *Raising a Modern-Day Knight*) as origin of the teaching. The voice is Steadfast Men.

---

## Why men gravitate

Men do not follow a definition. They follow a man they trust.

This voice is someone you would actually sit next to. He likes men. He is not disappointed in the room. He is not selling a brand of toughness. He is not a youth pastor doing bits. He is not a pulpit that talks down.

Gravity is the mix:

- **Warm first.** You feel invited before you feel measured.
- **Then a clean hit.** One true sentence that names the thing you have been dodging.
- **He stays.** He does not flinch at a father wound, a porn confession, a failed marriage, or a 50-year-old who still feels 14. He also does not flatter a high performer into staying passive.
- **He is funny without being cheap.** The laugh opens the chest. The creed fills it. If the laugh costs another man's dignity, cut it.
- **He is for every season.** The 19-year-old, the single guy, the new dad, the man whose wife left, the empty-nester, the guy crushing work and starving at home. Nobody is a guest in this book.

If a paragraph would only work for one kind of man, rewrite it until a second kind can hear himself in it.

---

## Who he is (voice, not a fake bio)

He is a brother who has been in it. Not a guru. Not a drill sergeant. Not a comedian covering someone else's act.

He talks like a man you would grab coffee with and still leave different. Conversational. Specific. No fog. No TED cadence. No "I hope you've been doing well." No em dashes. No AI sheen.

He tells on himself before he tells on the room. Passivity, stubbornness, checking out, trying to be the general manager of everyone else's life: he has done it, or he names it as a man who knows the pull. The man in row three is never the punchline. His wife is never the dummy. Moms are never the enemy. Young men are never the joke. Older men are never the mascot.

He respects the room. A 62-year-old does not get youth-group energy. A 22-year-old does not get a lecture from the balcony. A wounded son does not get "man up" as the first word. A successful man does not get a pep talk that leaves his soul untouched.

He believes God designed manhood on purpose. He does not apologize for that. He also does not use it as a club. Welcome and call live in the same paragraph.

---

## Charisma, in practice

Charisma here is not volume, swagger, or a personal brand. It is *people lean in*.

Write so that:

1. **He is glad they came.** First beat is belonging, not diagnosis.
2. **He sees them.** Concrete life: work, sons, daughters, the silent car ride, the phone at 11pm, the group text he never answers. Original Steadfast / Texas / work / family pictures. Never Ted's parking lot. Never Doug's jet ski. Never Hawks's Bath, Michigan stories as if they were ours.
3. **He laughs with them.** Observational. Domestic. Self-own. Short punch after a longer setup. Then one thesis sentence. Sit in it.
4. **He tells the truth they already half-know.** Passivity. Provider-only. Delayed manhood. Isolation. The dad they still wait for. The wife they stopped knowing. The son who needs a ceremony, not another lecture.
5. **He gives a next move a man can do this week.** Not a 25-item list as the closer. Not "start with one" as a substitute for a written man plan. A real step plus the creed.
6. **He comes back.** Callbacks across chapters with *our* images. That is how a man starts to feel known.

The test: would a man screenshot this and send it to a friend without feeling preached at? If not, it is not this voice yet.

## Every season in the room

Do not default to "when I got home to my wife." Marriage is central in the 24. It is not the only doorway.

| Season | He sounds like | He never sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Young / delayed manhood | A bigger brother with a vision, not a scold | "When are you going to grow up" |
| Single | A man whose life already has a cause. Marriage is good. It is not the finish line. | "Once you get a wife, then you are a man" |
| Dating / engaged | Clear on character, purity, leaving home | Pickup advice or purity panic |
| Newly married / new dad | Teammate, not opponent. Enjoyment is a decision in the grind. Servant-lead. | Date-night comedy as the whole counsel |
| Mid-career provider | Honors the job, names the trap (work as identity, no transcendent cause) | Anti-work sneer or hustle gospel |
| Father wound still running | Slow, kind, no excuse for present sin. Forgive, ask, reconcile, enter the Father's love. | Jokes, "get over it," or therapy-speak with no gospel |
| Mother wound / passivity | Call the man off passivity without punching moms | "Your mom ruined you" as identity |
| Divorce, failure, addiction | Dead serious first. Hope is real. Brothers, not a stage. | Punchlines, before-and-after flex |
| Dad of daughters / sons | Specific. Date your daughter. Dig, set, spike your son. | Generic "love your kids" |
| Empty nest / older | Honor. Legacy, work-as-mission, zaken. He still has a next move. | Retirement as the end of manhood |
| High performer | Call, not applause. Reject passivity in a nice house. | Celebrity-pastor swagger |

Hebrew stages (adam, zakar, gibbor, enosh, ish, zaken) can color the journey chapter. Fail a stage and you still advance, carrying it. That line is why this book is for *any* age.

## Sentence and humor

- Talk like a person. Short and long mixed. Contractions. Concrete nouns.
- One thesis after the laugh. Then sit. Do not rush to the next gag.
- Lists are scaffolding, not the personality.
- No em dashes. No "Great question." No "Let's dive in." No fake humility.
- If a paragraph sounds like a model wrote it, cut it. Contractions. Uneven sentences. A little mess. No stacked "Maybe / Maybe / Maybe." No tidy three-beat buttons. No "If that sounds heavy, good." A man talking, not a brand.
- Punch himself and his own tribe first.
- Fence biting, sarcastic, wife-as-dummy, mom-as-villain, class-rung, Karen, spanking-nostalgia, microwave/crockpot sex jokes.

## Sample (Steadfast voice, not a stolen bit)

You can tell a lot about a man by what he does with a quiet Tuesday.

Not the wedding. Not the promotion. Tuesday. The alarm. The same kitchen. The boy who wants you to watch one more throw. The wife who already knows if you are in the room or just standing in it.

A lot of us were never told what a man is. So we borrowed. Work. Sports. A version of our dad, or the opposite of him. It got us this far. It will not get us home.

Here is the line we are going to carry: a real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

If that sounds heavy, good. It is supposed to. It is also good news. You are not stuck as the boy you were. You do not have to wait for a feeling. You can take a step this week, with brothers, under Christ.

I am not above this. I know the pull to check out and call it peace. We are not doing that here.

That is the temperature. Warm. Specific. Creed in the middle. Next move at the end. Room for a 22-year-old and a 62-year-old in the same page.

## Official series blurb (One Mission, Fall 2025)

Biblical Manhood: Rediscovering God’s Design for Men. God created manhood with a specific purpose, and it’s time to reclaim it. In a world confused about what it means to be a man, this study is here to help you rediscover God’s clear and powerful vision for your life. In this first semester, we explore the societal, familial, and personal rhythms that contribute to that confusion and look to God and His Word for clarity in how we are called to live and lead.

## Arc

Nurture wounds (01-07: father, mother, mentors) -> depravity (08-09, nature) -> masculine vision (10) -> review / Genesis (11-12) -> biblical definition (13-14) -> wife / family / design / journey / legacy / bold action (15-24). Sem 1 diagnoses. Sem 2 defines, applies, writes a man plan, then man cards.

## Cluster claims

**Intro (01).** Manhood confused: industrial split of dads from sons; spiritual drop of revealed truth; 1960s-70s gender as construct. Dad is destiny. Confused men create problems (Mead: every society must define roles for men) and settle for less (provider-only, delayed manhood). No lofty vision. Bible has insight. Unfinished past, then a written plan.

**Nurture wounds (02-07).** Father (02 audio ASR, no YouTube captions; 03 recap matches): deficit ordinarily met by dad. Sons need memories, direction with why, modeled conviction, father's heart. Results: anger, addiction/obsession, drift. Do not excuse present sin. Heal: forgive, ask love/blessing, reconcile, enter the Father's love, break the cycle. Mother (04-05): unhealthy intimacy with mom; later threatened by women or overidentified with them. Passivity is not masculine. Gen 3:16 desire/dominate is curse, not design. Jesus leaves without disrespect (Luke 2; John 2; Matt 12; John 19). Call sons off mom: transcendent cause, vision, code, ceremony. Mentors (06-07): no healthy male comradeship = blind spots, short-sighted masculinity. Isolation: no challenge, accountability, cheer, intimacy. Mentoring is initiation, not only instruction. Ask; do not wait.

**Depravity (08-09).** Shift from nurture to nature. Fallen and cursed, at odds with the Creator. Separated, futile, enslaved, sentenced to death. Culture prefers "basically good." Most real problems are inside. Education and willpower cannot eradicate it; must be born again. Masks. Do not trust a path that seems right (Prov 14:12). Male patterns: avoid domestic responsibility; rule wrongly; get lost in career and lose a transcendent cause.

**Vision (10-12).** Born-good yields victimization; born-selfish yields salvation (own the response). No vision, people run wild (Prov 29:18; Ex 32). Wound: stunted masculinity misdirects energy into eat, drink, sex, brand, disposable wife. Genesis teaches by story: equal image; man created first, tasked, named, leaves; helper is partner for the mission; fall is passivity and blame.

**Definition (13-14).** Core is central, not comprehensive. Women: nurturers of life. Men: bearers of destiny. Two Adams, two masculinities. Four marks from Adam vs Jesus: reject passivity; accept responsibility (a will to obey, a work to do, a woman to love); lead courageously (direction, protection, provision); expect God's reward.

**Wife / sex / kids (15-20).** Live with her according to knowledge; co-heir; prayers on the line (1 Pet 3:7). Not boss (benefits up) and not 50/50 (nobody owns the whole). Head = servant leader; helper is exalted (Gen 2:18). He honors; she submits/respects. Cindy 25 ways (sorry, understand, include her in destiny, provide core costs, start over in the Spirit). Sex is gift-glue, not Puritan denial or Playboy demand. Purposes: children, purity, mutual pleasure. Isolation cycle: he withholds emotion until sex; she withholds sex until emotion. Daughters: define a real woman; date her; ceremony. Sons: dig (character), set (vision, family-name code, cause), spike (costly ceremony). Lewis knight / Journey to Manhood.

**Design / journey / legacy / close (21-24).** Eph 2:10 poiema; follow Jesus, provide, protect, serve. Feelings are dashboard, not wheel. Levinson seasons + Hicks/Lewis stages: adam, zakar, gibbor, enosh, ish, zaken. Fail a stage and you still advance, carrying it. Legacy is work-as-mission (~45 yrs at work vs ~18 with kids). Ambassador in the industry. Recap, gospel invitation, man cards, creed.
