Before you turn the page

   If you've been on the tailgate a long time, I want to say something to you before
you get off it. You aren't lazy, and you probably aren't a coward. You're a man who
learned somewhere that waiting was safer than moving, and the lesson was true
once and isn't anymore. The Father who asked Adam where he was is asking you
the same thing tonight, and He isn't asking in anger. He's asking the way a father
asks a boy who's been hiding in the barn: come on out, there's work to do, and I'll
do it with you. May He give you the small strength to change the battery, and the
larger strength to make the call, and may you find that the first one makes the
second one possible.
