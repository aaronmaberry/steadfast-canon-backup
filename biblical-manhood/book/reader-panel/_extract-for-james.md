## Chapter notes (01–35)

Each: main claim · tone · sticky image/line · closer usability (from signed MD).

### 01 — A quiet Tuesday
Manhood is proven on ordinary Tuesdays, not highlights; childish ways = life that never takes a yes that survives the feeling. Tone: concrete, domestic, slightly accusing without roast. Sticky: “A lot of us can rise for a camera. Fewer of us can rise for a cereal bowl.” / boy + one more throw while dad watches the clock. Closer: usable — Name disappearance; one meal without phone / small yes; text a man about Tuesday; Carry: “Tuesday is where a man is made, or he settles.” **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box in ebook PDF (prose still nudges the text/meal moves).

### 02 — You are not crazy
Wanting a clear sentence for “what a man is” is sane; culture/church/fathers went quiet; mess vs shrink are both fruit of fog. Tone: validating then sharpening. Sticky: younger guy at work who laughs first so the hunger doesn’t show; Genesis 1:27 as “not a costume.” Closer: usable — name the sentence your week preaches; write the ugly one; read it to a brother without explaining; Carry: “You are not crazy. The quiet was real. God was not guessing.” **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box.

### 03 — The four marks
Gives the holding definition after the ugly lived sentence; four marks are one line to catch yourself mid-decision, not a stack of talks. Tone: definitional, anti-vague. Sticky: false sentences that pay (“last one to leave the office” / “doesn’t need anybody”). Closer: usable — name the ugly substitute; write four longhand; say them aloud both ways; Carry = full four-marks line. **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box.

### 04 — Stop waiting
Reject passivity: knowing the good and not doing it is sin (James 4:17); busy motion ≠ moving; wait dressed as wisdom. Tone: direct, confessing (“next time was a coward's favorite day”). Sticky: phone screen going dark on a name; pacing warehouse / mowing strip to avoid the talk. Closer: usable — name the wait called wisdom; one known good done; brother asks next week. Carry usable. Flags: wound (real waits acknowledged).

### 05 — Pick up the weight
Accept responsibility: the load with your name is still on the floor after you stand up; everyone has learned the path around it. Tone: household parable. Sticky: weight on the floor kids/wife step over; “good host” as good avoider. Closer: usable — name the weight; put a hand on it one inch; brother checks if you dropped it.

### 06 — Go first
Lead courageously: you already know the next true thing and let someone smaller step into it; “after you” as costume. Tone: sharp on second-row talent. Sticky: true sentence tasted then swallowed so a braver fool says it. Closer: usable — name the room; one small true first move; brother asks if you hid again.

### 07 — Live for a reward that lasts
Expect God’s reward; plaque/count/respect prizes cannot hold you; greater reward is not prosperity gag. Tone: afterglow emptiness, then kind to the wounded. Sticky: man with plaque still hungry in ten minutes; sparklers later in ch20 rhyme. Closer: usable — name cheap prize; one move that only makes sense if God’s reward is real. Flags: wound, older, single/married lanes.

### 08 — The empty chair **[FATHER HOLE]**
Names the father hole; empty chair can have a body in it; don’t make the chair destiny or a pass. Tone: slow, non-sentimental, honest hunger. Sticky: “Sometimes a man is even sitting in it. That does not always mean the chair is full.” Closer: usable — one true sentence about your father; brother sits still and does not fix it; Carry: “The father hole is real…”

### 09 — Anger, the itch, and the drift **[WOUND / FRUIT]**
Hole bears fruit: anger / sexual itch / drift; “that’s just how I am” is fruit not personality; foothold (Eph 4). Tone: diagnostic, refuses excuse. Sticky: half-smile “personality” while household knows the room temperature. Closer: usable — name which fruit; one honest refusal; brother checks relapse.

### 10 — The Father who does not leave **[FATHER HOLE → GOD]**
Earthly empty chair is not last word; receive God as Father who stays; stop making a man do God’s job. Tone: pastoral, anti-rush. Sticky: thumb still knows the number; Voice already in the house. Closer: usable — tell God the true sentence; receive one Father-word; brother asks if you faced the wrong door again.

### 11 — When home never handed you off **[TIE / LAUNCH]**
Grown man still orbits a house that never said go; love mother without belonging to childhood weather; handoff is yours. Tone: careful not to villainize mom. Sticky: same driveway patch at sixteen; rearranging a week from a cold front in her voice. Closer: usable — name the tie; one adult non-orbit step. Flags: father-adjacent, marriage-after (leaving home to belong to your life).

### 12 — Leave without slamming the door
Leave with honor: refuse both stuck-in-doorway and crater-slam proofs of manhood. Tone: concrete door/storm-door sound. Sticky: “Most of us still know the sound of the door at the house we came from.” Closer: usable — name which lean; one leave-with-honor move; brother asks slam vs stay.

### 13 — A man with no brothers **[BROTHERS]**
Isolation is not strength; capable man with twelve names and nobody to call when he falls. Tone: pity as warning, not insult. Sticky: truck in driveway, week closed, phone full, house quiet. Closer: usable — who has you when you fall; one step toward a brother; if none, tell a man you’re done calling isolation strength. **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box (carry line still in prose).

### 14 — Ask the man **[BROTHERS]**
Do not wait to be chosen off the wall; older/wise men are full—you ask; pick wise not famous/report-to-you. Tone: lobby/coffee-cup practical. Sticky: third row two years; nod treated as relationship. Closer: usable — name the man; ask in one sentence; stay off the wall. **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box.

### 15 — The problem inside **[WOUND → AGENCY]**
After naming chair/house/brothers you can still wreck the next room; problem is also in you (nature not only nurture); blame as dodge of repentance. Tone: hard kindness. Sticky: twenty minutes on the chair, twenty seconds on what you did Tuesday. Closer: usable — “The problem is also in me.” **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box.

### 16 — How that looks on a man
Inside has shapes: skip the house / run the room / wear the job as a name; week already tells on you. Tone: parking-lot faithful vs porch-light home. Sticky: “You did not skip a meeting. You skipped the house.” Closer: usable — name which of three; one contrary act (go home / yield room / tell truth about job).

### 17 — No north star
Full capable week ≠ direction; steering by nearby lights (office, field, invoice) with no sitting-still heading. Tone: fog after competence. Sticky: can tell you 4:30; cannot say what he is for. Closer: usable — name nearby light; write one heading that sits still.

### 18 — Before the fall
Design was first; stop starting the story at the wreck; work and keep what was given (Genesis vocation). Tone: closing-day empty-house walkthrough. Sticky: July closing, AC not caught up, feet too loud in empty rooms. Closer: usable — name one given thing; work/keep it once as gift.

### 19 — Two men, two ways
First Adam vs last Adam; two ruts after rain; no third way; talk about the second way while taking from the first. Tone: cattle-guard / mud choice. Sticky: “I have tried to straddle a Y. The truck does not.” Closer: usable — which man this week; one life-giving act.

### 20 — Destiny is not a vibe
Calling is not sparklers; live for Him when driveway is just driveway; passivity waits for mood to name you. Tone: July driveway fireworks with boy. Sticky: twenty seconds of white fire then boring smoking wire. Closer: usable — name the fire that was actually you; one act that doesn’t wait on feeling.

### 21 — If there is no wife yet **[SINGLE / AFTER-LOSS]**
Marriage is good, not the finish line / ticket in; manhood fits a one-bedroom; “yet” is not a taunt; if she left/died/never came—kind, still assigned. Tone: garment bag still on hook after Saturday wedding. Sticky: toast finished in the car; bag still hanging Thursday. Closer: usable — name what you treat as not-yet; inhabit it once as already assigned. Flags: remarriage-hope adjacent (no lobby eviction).

### 22 — Know her **[MARRIAGE]**
Knowing her is a job not a memory; fridge/lock-screen photo is not current; study then stay for the answer. Tone: efficient husband who stopped asking. Sticky: trip photo still on fridge; consults a still. Closer: usable — ask one real question; write what you heard. Hurt lane: if she left/died—truth without courtroom.

### 23 — Lay it down **[MARRIAGE / CROSS]**
Christlike love lays down your life; heavy-end sofa heroism ≠ home dying; fair 50/50 often refuses the Cross. Tone: Saturday church move. Sticky: “I have taken the heavy end and thought I knew what kind of man I was. Then I went home and kept the life.” Closer: usable — lay one kept thing down without thanks. Flags: marriage still died / widower / older “I did my years.”

### 24 — What she actually needs **[MARRIAGE]**
Stop giving the help that would have worked on you; fixed bulb / four-pack ≠ met need; projects vs person. Tone: wry then convicting. Sticky: new bulb over sink; she stands in new light like the week hadn’t moved. Closer: usable — name wrong gift; give actual need once, don’t announce. Flags: she left / marriage died / empty-nest loyalty as wrong gift.

### 25 — The bed is a covenant **[SEX / COVENANT]**
Marriage bed honored by all (Heb 13:4); not laundry table; unmarried/widower/kid in first apartment included; purity + God will judge; affair not “complicated season.” Tone: towels piled on bed midweek. Sticky: sits on edge to take boots off around terry-cloth range. Closer: usable — keep covenant once (go to it or name a refusal). Flags: strong hurt/failure/older lanes; not remarriage schedule.

### 26 — Your daughter is watching **[FATHERING]**
Daughter reads the miles; be in the seat; phone in back; give better sentence about womanhood than the market. Tone: after-practice visor light. Sticky: cheap yellow visor mirror; “I thought the miles were the gift. She was reading the miles.” Closer: usable — be in the seat once. Flags: if she left and took the girl—father in hours you have.

### 27 — Give your son a name
Jersey letters ≠ father-claim; stop “bud” / unfinished-project names; say his name + what he is. Tone: rec-complex Saturday. Sticky: heat-pressed last name a little high; useful yells; no true name. Closer: usable — say true name once out loud.

### 28 — Give your son a hill
Boys lack ground not fight; dirt mound ≠ hill; authority that dies with “because I said so” vs God’s hill that stands at thirty. Tone: new-street caliche pile. Sticky: boy on mound with heels dug in; dad in driveway thinks “good, he's outside.” Closer: usable — name real hill; stand on it where he sees.

### 29 — Give your son a day he will not forget
Birthday pavilion ≠ marked day; witnesses, words, cost, date he can point to at thirty. Tone: cake/icing/phones up. Sticky: leftover sheet cake on floorboard called a day. Closer: usable — put a date on a page; tell one man; write the sentence you will say.

### 30 — You were made for a work
Saved toward a work in Christ; badge/slot ≠ calling; hallway passivity vs work entered on purpose. Tone: Part 5 opener, vocational. Sticky: “Good at the tasks… somebody asked what I was made to do, and I gave them a title.” Closer: usable — name the hallway; do one prepared piece.

### 31 — Feelings are the dash, not the wheel **[OLDER / DISCIPLINE]**
Feelings inform; they don’t drive; “I didn’t feel it yet” as hall pass; Psalm 42 talk-to-soul. Tone: truck key in, mood never arrives. Sticky: dash light running the trip. Closer: usable — one true thing anyway while dash talks. Flags: notes some need more than pep talk—tell a man who can help.

### 32 — Every season still counts **[OLDER SEASONS]**
22 waiting for life to start and gray-temples waiting for life to be over share a lie; four marks don’t retire; strength vs gray are weights for their years. Tone: grocery-line parable. Sticky: two carts, same coffee, same lie, different decade. Closer: usable — name season you’re in; one next move that belongs to this year.

### 33 — What you are building when nobody claps
Clap is a liar; mow street strip twice, leave leaning gate; build unseen for the Lord. Tone: Saturday lawn/neighbor nod. Sticky: “He did the strip twice… left the posts in the dirt.” Closer: usable — one unseen true hour. **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box (carry still in prose).

### 34 — Write the next move
Truck speeches aren’t plans; ink on a dated step; blank notebook since January. Tone: cab year-talk then empty kitchen page. Sticky: “I have given a good year to a windshield.” Closer: usable — one course + one step with a day; show brother the page. **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box.

### 35 — These men are my brothers **[BROTHERS]**
Closing the book alone isn’t staying; written move with no brother is still a speech; name two men, show the page, put a time; wife/group chat/work team ≠ brothers; Heb 3:13 daily. Tone: final, practical, anti-shelf. Sticky: book on shelf, notebook with no other name. Closer: usable — two names; show page; keep time; Carry: “These men are my brothers.” **PDF gap:** no YOUR TURN box.
