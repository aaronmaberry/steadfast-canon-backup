## What delivered

Not a celebrity bio. Not an Aaron dump. Gift-readable. I could hand this to a brother without explaining who the author thinks he is. Dedication matches: tables, wife who won't pretend he arrived, kids still watching. That is a man talking, not a brand.

**Intro does seasons, not ages.** Quiet house after the kids left. Badge or hard hat turned in. Buried your wife. Divorced and still a father. I found my seat without being herded into a "sixties" box. He says he will turn toward you inside the paragraph when season changes counsel, and he will not stop every chapter to run the whole list. Good. I already lived the stamped-age fatigue in an earlier draft. This is better.

**No Part scaffolding in the front.** Intro walks the book by pictures (Tuesday, chair, beginning, house, long road) without hanging Part 1 / Part 2 signs on the doorway. TOC is chapter titles only. Readable spacing. Real titles I recognize from the FLOW body. The closer note at the bottom of the TOC is useful, not cute.

**Training pointer is honest.** Book stands alone. Condensed map in the back. Full class at walksteadfast.com. "Buy either. Buy both. Just do not binge four chapters on a Saturday and call it finished." That is a man talking to a tired man, not a brochure. Lewis sentence-shape acknowledgment on the copyright page is the right kind of honesty. A man my age who has walked that shelf will not smell a stolen spine.

Skim on body: Tuesday still decides a life. Season chapter still puts February and the hill country where they belong. FLOW body holds.

## What did not

**Condensed training stops at week 24.** Map ends at *What She Actually Needs*. Son trilogy and the long road are a parenthetical: "continue in the full class guide on the site." Intro promised the long road in the same breath as the house. TOC still lists those chapters. Back of the book then maps only through the wife stretch. From my seat that is the wrong place to go thin. A brother buying for the next-move chapters gets a useful group agenda and a missing spine for the pages that matter most after the kids leave. Useful map for weeks 1–24. Incomplete map for the book he just finished.

**Production TBDs still show.** ISBN TBD. Translation TBD. Cover design TBD. Interior design TBD. Fine for a panel. As a buyer flipping a paid ebook, TBD on the copyright page is the same family of problem as a review-draft stamp. Kill it before money.

**Body still whispers Parts.** Chapter openers I skimmed still carry lines like *Part 1* / *Part 5*. Fronts and TOC dropped the scaffolding. Inside pages did not. A gift reader flipping will feel the seam. Either own Parts everywhere or cut them everywhere. Do not leave the inconsistency.
