# Condensed Training

## A 24-Week Map

This is the bones of the Steadfast Men training, fitted to the book you just read.

The full class runs 24 weeks with video teaching, a leader's guide, group discussion material, weekly challenges, and a workbook for each man. All of that lives at walksteadfast.com. What follows is the condensed version: the same 24-week rhythm, the same arc, stripped down to what one man or a small group can run with nothing but this book, a Bible, and a standing time on the calendar.

It is enough to start. Do not wait until you have the full guide to begin.

### How to use it

**Get one other man.** Two is a group. A dozen is a group. One man alone is a reader, and readers drift. Text somebody tonight.

**Set a time and defend it.** Same day, same hour, every week for 24 weeks. Early mornings before work have held up best for the men I know. Put it on the calendar as an appointment you would not cancel on your boss.

**Keep it simple.** Each week has four parts.

- *Read* tells you which chapters to have finished before you meet.
- *Anchor* is one passage of Scripture to read aloud together at the start.
- *Ask* is one question. Every man answers it. Nobody gets to pass and nobody gets to answer for the group.
- *Do* is one action for the week ahead. When you meet next, the first thing you talk about is whether you did it.

**Ninety minutes is plenty.** Ten to read the anchor and pray. Twenty to report on last week's action. Forty on the question. The rest on the new action and prayer for each other. If you run long, that is fine. If you consistently run short, someone is not being honest.

**Finish.** The men who quit almost always quit between weeks six and ten, when the newness has worn off and the real work has started. Decide now that you will finish, and tell the other men in the group that you have decided it.

## The Map

### Week 1: The Chair

**Read:** Introduction and chapters 1 and 2
**Anchor:** Genesis 2:15 through 3:9
**Ask:** Where in your life right now are you present in the room but absent from the fight?
**Do:** Tell your wife, or the person closest to you, that you are starting this and why. Ask her one question: "Where do you most need me to show up?" Do not defend yourself. Write down what she says.

### Week 2: Drift

**Read:** Chapters 3 and 4
**Anchor:** Hebrews 2:1 through 4
**Ask:** What is one area where you have been drifting so long that it now feels normal?
**Do:** Pick one recurring decision you have been letting someone else make for you, and make it this week.

### Week 3: The Cost

**Read:** Chapters 5 and 6
**Anchor:** Proverbs 24:30 through 34
**Ask:** Who is paying the bill for your passivity, and what does it cost them?
**Do:** Have the conversation you have been putting off. You already know which one.

### Week 4: Grown Boys

**Read:** Chapters 7 and 8
**Anchor:** 1 Corinthians 13:11 and 16:13 through 14
**Ask:** What childish thing are you still holding onto, and what would it mean to put it away?
**Do:** Identify one older man you respect. Ask him to coffee. Ask him what he wishes he had known at your age.

### Week 5: The Sentence

**Read:** Chapters 9 and 10
**Anchor:** Micah 6:8
**Ask:** Of the four marks, which one comes hardest to you, and why do you think that is?
**Do:** Memorize the sentence. Say it out loud every morning this week before you look at your phone.

### Week 6: The First Move

**Read:** Chapters 11 and 12
**Anchor:** Genesis 3:6 and 3:17 through 19
**Ask:** When was the last time you stood there silent while something in your home went wrong?
**Do:** Initiate three things this week that you would normally wait on: a conversation, a plan, a prayer, a repair, an apology. Report back on all three.

### Week 7: Reject Passivity

**Read:** Review chapters 11 and 12
**Anchor:** James 4:17
**Ask:** What is the one thing you know you should do that you are not doing?
**Do:** Do it. This week. Bring proof.

### Week 8: What Is Yours

**Read:** Chapters 13 and 14
**Anchor:** Ezekiel 22:30 and Nehemiah 1:1 through 11
**Ask:** What in your life have you been treating as somebody else's job that God has actually handed to you?
**Do:** Write down everything and everyone you are responsible for. Read the list to the group. Circle the two items you have most neglected.

### Week 9: The End of Blame

**Read:** Review chapters 13 and 14
**Anchor:** Genesis 3:12 through 13 and Psalm 51:1 through 4
**Ask:** What do you blame most often, and who or what do you blame it on?
**Do:** Go one full week without blaming anyone or anything out loud, including traffic, your boss, and your kids. Keep a tally of every slip.

### Week 10: Courage

**Read:** Chapters 15 and 16
**Anchor:** Joshua 1:6 through 9
**Ask:** What are you afraid of that is keeping you from leading well?
**Do:** Name the fear to the group. Then do the thing anyway, once, this week.

### Week 11: Servant, Not Boss

**Read:** Review chapters 15 and 16
**Anchor:** Mark 10:42 through 45 and John 13:1 through 17
**Ask:** Where have you confused being in charge with leading?
**Do:** Serve your wife or your household in a way that costs you something and that nobody asked you for. Do not announce it.

### Week 12: The Right Applause

**Read:** Chapters 17 and 18
**Anchor:** Matthew 6:1 through 6 and Colossians 3:23 through 24
**Ask:** Whose approval are you actually living for? Be specific.
**Do:** Do one good thing this week that no one on earth will ever know about.

### Week 13: What God Promises

**Read:** Review chapters 17 and 18
**Anchor:** Matthew 25:14 through 23 and 2 Timothy 4:6 through 8
**Ask:** If you kept going the way you are now, what do you honestly expect to hear at the end?
**Do:** Write a paragraph describing the man you want to be at the end of your life. Read it to the group. Keep it where you will see it.

### Week 14: The Man Who Held All Four

**Read:** Chapters 19 and 20
**Anchor:** Philippians 2:5 through 11 and Hebrews 4:14 through 16
**Ask:** Where do you most need to see Jesus as the pattern rather than only as the pardon?
**Do:** Read one of the four Gospels this week, start to finish, watching for the four marks in Jesus. Come with examples.

### Week 15: Grace for the Fallen

**Read:** Review chapter 20
**Anchor:** Luke 15:11 through 32 and 1 John 1:8 through 9
**Ask:** What failure are you still carrying that you have never actually confessed to another man?
**Do:** Confess it. To the group or to one man in it. This is the week the training either becomes real or stays theoretical.

### Week 16: Repentance and the Long Road

**Read:** Chapters 21 and 22
**Anchor:** Psalm 32:1 through 5 and Galatians 6:9
**Ask:** What is the difference, in your own life, between feeling bad and repenting?
**Do:** Pick one habit of repentance you will practice weekly for the rest of the training: a standing confession time with one man, a weekly review with your wife, or a written examination before God. Start it this week.

### Week 17: Your Wife

**Read:** Chapter 23
**Anchor:** Ephesians 5:25 through 33 and 1 Peter 3:7
**Ask:** If your wife described how you pursue, protect, and provide for her, what would she say? Not what you hope. What she would say.
**Do:** Ask her. Then plan and execute one act of pursuit this week that has nothing to do with what you want from her.

### Week 18: Your Children

**Read:** Chapter 24
**Anchor:** Deuteronomy 6:4 through 9 and Psalm 78:1 through 7
**Ask:** What does each of your children need to hear from you that they have not heard?
**Do:** Bless each child out loud this week. Name something true and good about them, and tell them God is for them. If you do not have children, do this for a young man you have influence over.

### Week 19: Your Work

**Read:** Chapter 25
**Anchor:** Genesis 2:15, Colossians 3:23, and Ecclesiastes 2:22 through 26
**Ask:** Is your work an idol, an escape, or an offering? How do you know?
**Do:** Identify one place at work where you have been passive. A conversation, a decision, a standard. Address it this week.

### Week 20: Your Money

**Read:** Chapter 26
**Anchor:** Matthew 6:19 through 24 and 1 Timothy 6:17 through 19
**Ask:** What does your bank statement say you actually worship?
**Do:** Sit down with your wife or a trusted man and look at where every dollar went last month. Make one change and one gift.

### Week 21: Your Body and Your Mind

**Read:** Chapters 27 and 28
**Anchor:** 1 Corinthians 6:19 through 20 and Philippians 4:8
**Ask:** What are you putting into your body and your mind that you would be ashamed for the men at this table to see?
**Do:** Cut one thing. Add one thing. Tell the group both. Ask one man to check on you daily.

### Week 22: Your Friends and Your Church

**Read:** Chapters 29 and 30
**Anchor:** Proverbs 27:17 and Hebrews 10:24 through 25
**Ask:** Who has permission to tell you the truth about yourself, and when did they last use it?
**Do:** Take one concrete step into deeper commitment at your church this week. Join, serve, give, or show up somewhere you have been avoiding.

### Week 23: Your Words and Your Time

**Read:** Chapters 31 and 32
**Anchor:** Ephesians 4:29 and Psalm 90:12
**Ask:** If your family only had your words and your calendar to go on, what would they conclude you love most?
**Do:** Audit last week's calendar hour by hour. Then plan next week on purpose, with your wife, and put the things that matter most on it first.

### Week 24: Steadfast to the End

**Read:** Chapters 33, 34, and 35
**Anchor:** 1 Corinthians 15:58 and 2 Timothy 2:1 through 2
**Ask:** Who is the next man you are going to keep a chair for?
**Do:** Name him. Text him this week. Then plan when your group will start again with him at the table.

&nbsp;

## When you finish

Do not close the book and move on. Steadfast men are made in repetition, not in a single pass.

Take a few weeks off. Then start again, either with the same men going deeper or with new men you have invited to the table. The material will read differently the second time because you will be a different man reading it.
