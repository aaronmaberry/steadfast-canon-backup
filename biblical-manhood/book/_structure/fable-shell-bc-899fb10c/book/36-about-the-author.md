# About the Author

Max is a husband, a father of two, and a Texan who spends his working hours leading teams in the technology world and his best hours teaching men what it means to be steadfast.

He did not set out to write a book or run a men's training. He set out to stop being the passive husband and distracted dad he had quietly become, and a handful of older men helped him do it by keeping a chair for him at their table every week. Years later he started keeping chairs for other men. Steadfast Men, the 24-week training that this book grew out of, began at a kitchen table with a few skeptical guys and has since been taught in living rooms, garages, church classrooms, and online to men in every season of life.

Max is not a pastor or a counselor, and he is quick to say so. He is a layman who believes that ordinary men, walking together under the Word of God, can become the kind of husbands, fathers, and leaders their families and churches are starving for. He has watched it happen too many times to doubt it.

When he is not working or teaching, he is usually outside with his kids, on a long walk with his wife, or at a diner early on a weekday morning with a Bible, a cup of coffee, and one other man.

He and his family live in Texas.
