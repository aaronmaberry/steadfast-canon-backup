## What 2026 Christian living / manhood frontlists are doing

| Habit | Typical range (2025–26 shelf) | Steadfast read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book length | ~176–304 pp; ~40–70k words common for CL trade | Keep ~50–70k; 35 ch only works if lean |
| Chapter count | Manhood: ~9–17 body ch + intro/close; CL: ~8–12 in 2–5 parts | Already locked: 35 body + intro — Hughes-short, not textbook |
| Chapter body | Classic CL often 3.5–4k; men’s “table” titles & habit comps closer to 1.5–3k | **Lock: ~1,200–1,800 body** (intro shorter). Even rhythm; don’t balloon early ch |
| Part bands | 2–5 labeled PARTS; uneven sizes OK (virtues/roles, 3 characteristic bands, 4–5 theme bands) | Keep five PART labels + blank-band scan; no hashtags |
| One job / ch | One virtue, role, practice, or wound per chapter | One job per chapter; finish → turn page |

**TOC / part-band habits (generic):** Page titled *Contents*. PART label (small caps / tracked) above a tight chapter cluster; extra leading *between* parts; intro + conclusion/back matter **outside** PART numbering. Flat lists work only under ~12 chapters — with 35, bands are required. Stop at PART → chapter (no nested section soup).

## The loop bestsellers repeat (steal shape only)

**Hook (concrete scene or felt tension) → name the principle → explain plainly → one worked example / case → exercise or prompt → short takeaway → bridge.**

Pacing notes for Max:
- Open on an ordinary-day picture, not a thesis lecture.
- Land the principle early enough that the middle can *show*, not re-argue.
- One illustration doing real work beats a stack of name-drops.
- Close the teaching beat before the closer (blessing / “before you turn” optional); then the locked **Your Turn**.

## Closers & exercises on NYT / Amazon CL frontlists (generic — no lift)

What the shelf is doing:
- **In-book:** short reflection sets (often 3–7 Qs), “this week” challenges, journaling lines, occasional self-check or rule-of-life step.
- **Beside-book:** companion guides / study guides with denser Q banks and weekly practice reflection (formation titles especially).
- **Tone:** practitioner prompts (“name / try / tell”), not quiz-bank theology tests.
- **Rhythm:** chapter ends on a *move*, not a fade-out pep talk. Men’s shelf still praises finishable chapters + a next step a table can use.

**Steadfast already solved the closer.** Do not expand toward frontlist Q-banks.

| Locked Your Turn (4 lines) | Frontlist trap to avoid |
| --- | --- |
| **Name it** (Because…) | Long multi-question “Discuss” dumps |
| **Do this week** (Because…) | Multi-step homework worksheets in-chapter |
| **Tell a brother** (Because…) | Group-guide density inside the trade chapter |
| **Carry this** (line only) + `:::notes` | Closing prayer essays / creed recaps every chapter |

Keep write-in space; keep breath before each bold label. Companion-level study stays in Condensed Training / packets — not chapter bloat.

## One-page rewrite checklist (Max)

1. Body ~1.2–1.8k; one job; hook → principle → example → locked closer.  
2. PART band visible in TOC; teal readable breaks in manuscript; no `#`.  
3. No chapter numbers, no Texas identity, no teacher parade, no seasons roll-call.  
4. Your Turn exactly four labeled lines + notes; don’t “improve” it toward the frontlist Q-bank.  
5. Structure from comps; sentences, slogans, and stories original from Steadfast canon.
