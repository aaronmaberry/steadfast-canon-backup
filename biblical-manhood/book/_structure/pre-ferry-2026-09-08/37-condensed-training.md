# Condensed Training

The chapters you just read are the book. What follows is a short map for the twenty four week training that runs alongside them.

Each week of the class walks one chapter (or a tight pair) with a video teaching, a leader guide, and a participant worksheet. The closer stays the same: Name it. Do this week. Tell a brother. Carry this.

Use this back section as a compact agenda if you are already inside a group and need the spine on paper. For the full printable packets, host scripts, and week by week run order, get the twenty four week class guide at walksteadfast.com.
