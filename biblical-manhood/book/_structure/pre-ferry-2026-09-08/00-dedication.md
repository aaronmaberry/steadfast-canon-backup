# Dedication

For the men who let me sit at their tables before I had anything to bring to them, and who kept a chair open long after they'd stopped needing to.

For my wife, who has watched me try to become the man in these pages for a long time now, and who has never once pretended I'd already arrived. Every good sentence here was lived in front of her first, and she is the reason most of them are true.

And for our children, who are still watching. I hope you find your father in here, and I hope you find him better than he was when you were small.
