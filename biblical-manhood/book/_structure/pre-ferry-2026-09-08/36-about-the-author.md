# About the Author

Max is a husband and a dad of two in Texas. He has spent years sitting with men at kitchen tables and in fellowship halls, watching the same question show up under different jobs and different seasons: what a man is, and whether it is too late to become one.

He works in technology and leadership by day. By night and on weekends he writes and teaches biblical manhood for Steadfast Men. He does not wear another man's stories. He tells the truth about passivity, responsibility, courage, and reward the way he has seen it land in ordinary Tuesdays.

This is his first book.
