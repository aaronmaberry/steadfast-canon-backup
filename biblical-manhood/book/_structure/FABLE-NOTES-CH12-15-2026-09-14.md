## 12. Leave Without Slamming the Door

- New central picture: the knock. Three men at their parents' front door: one walks in without knocking (hand on the knob, never left), one hasn't stood on the porch in years (back turned, slammed it), one may still carry the key but knocks and waits. The knock is the one gesture that leaves and honors at once. Replaces the retired "two ways out of one house."
- Teaching kept: Genesis 2:24 with Exodus 20:12; the man who never left and the man who slammed it; honor the office, not the performance; forgiveness as handing the debt to God (Romans 12:19, Genesis 50:20); the four-part conversation; the mother; the older man on the other side of the door (Luke 15 father in prose, no margin).
- Worked example: Marcus drives back years after slamming out, knocks, says the four things on the porch; his father answers "You eat yet?" and leaves the door open.
- Killed: "chapter 8" letter and "last two chapters" refs; Tuesday; the roll-call section; corner post / line post (too near the retired load-bearing wall); the chair at the end of the table; hard ages.
- Carry this: "Honor the office, tell the truth about the man, hand the debt to God, and knock when you come home."

## 14. Ask the Man

- New central picture: "Ride with me." Young Max asks an old man named Harlan how he stayed married; the answer is a Saturday riding shotgun on his feed route, and the lesson is the noon pay-phone call to his wife, made every working day of the marriage. Replaces the church parking lot, hands in pockets, "come by Tuesday," and the fence and paintbrush.
- Teaching kept: both men waiting; the chain (Moses to Joshua, Elijah to Elisha, Paul to Timothy; 2 Timothy 2:2); don't say "mentor," ask one specific question; the twenty-minute shape with a time box; how to pick him (wife's face, scars over wins, reachable, the deacon and the back pew and the hardware store); come with a question, do what he says and report back, don't make him your father, thank him specifically; to the older man (Titus 2:6-7; go first; ask, don't lecture; scars are the curriculum; beside, not across; steadiness is the teaching; you'll get more than you give).
- Dropped for length: Titus 2:2 and Psalm 71:18 margins (the Psalm's "what a man does with his mornings" point is folded into the older-man paragraph).
- Killed: Tuesday (eight instances in source); "chapter 10" and "the last chapter"; "the empty chair"; Buck, Roy's badge, Kenny's Tuesday-night man; the roll-call; "twenty-three," "forty years," "under fifty."
- Third Your Turn head restored to "Tell a brother" (source had "Ask the man"); the Because paragraph distinguishes the man beside from the man ahead.
- Carry this: "There's a man twenty years ahead of you waiting to be asked. Ask him one real question, and when he says ride with me, go."

## 15. The Problem Inside

- New central picture: rust bubbling through paint from the back of a fender. Max repaints the bubble every spring; a body man presses it, brown water runs out, "It's coming from the inside." Managing the outside is painting over it; God makes the metal new. Replaces the cracked engine block and the summer of replaced parts.
- Teaching kept: the turn from inheritance to the problem inside; even with a perfect father (Adam); Mark 7:23 "from inside"; why this is good news (you can't repent of what somebody else did; Romans 5:8); the word sin and the two reasons men avoid it; the bend toward himself; Cain and the animal at the door (Genesis 4:7 paraphrased in prose); Paul's "what I hate I do" and "Who will rescue me" (paraphrased); say the word specifically; guilt is the bubble in the paint, shame is a liar; turn, don't just feel bad; bring a brother (James 5:16); ask for a steadfast spirit (Psalm 51:10).

- Dropped for length: margin blocks for Jeremiah 17:9, Genesis 4:7, Romans 7:15, Romans 7:24-25, and Psalm 32:3-5 (all carried in prose paraphrase); source had nine margins, kept four.
- Killed: "seven chapters," "chapter 13"; the chair and the exits; "handed off at fourteen"; last Tuesday; Roy at sixty-nine, Eli, Anthony; the roll-call; "opened the drawer."
- Four marks sentence used verbatim in the close, as the source note allowed.
