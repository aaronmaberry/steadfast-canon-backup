## 7. Work for God's Pay

Opening verse: Colossians 3:23-24 (NIV).

"Don't pile up treasure down here, He said": Matthew 6:19-21.

"don't let your left hand know what your right hand is doing": Matthew 6:3-4.

"Paul's word to those worn-out workers was whatever you do": Colossians 3:23-24.

"saved by grace through faith": Ephesians 2:8-10.

"Well done, the master said": Matthew 25:21.

"he counted disgrace for Christ's sake worth more than all the treasure in Egypt": Hebrews 11:24-26.

"He called his troubles light and momentary": 2 Corinthians 4:17-18; his beatings and shipwrecks, 2 Corinthians 11:23-27.

"good done for a crowd has already gotten everything it's going to get": Matthew 6:2.
