## 11. When Home Never Handed You Off

Opening verse: Genesis 32:26 (NIV).

"Jacob wanted his father's blessing so badly": Genesis 27.

"a Man took hold of him and they wrestled till sunup": Genesis 32:22-31.

"David told Solomon he was about to go the way of all the earth": 1 Kings 2:1-3.

"fan into flame the gift": 2 Timothy 1:6.

"Years later, worn thin and scared, Timothy got a letter": 2 Timothy 1:3-8.

"what He said over His Son at the river": Matthew 3:17; for sons in Christ, Galatians 4:4-7.

"Solomon was young and frightened and told God so": 1 Kings 3:7.

"honor their father and mother": Exodus 20:12.

"a man leaves his father and mother and is joined to his wife": Genesis 2:24.

"not to take revenge but to leave room for God": Romans 12:19.

"You meant it to hurt me, he said, but God meant it for good": Genesis 50:20.

"the father in Jesus' story who went out after both of his sons": Luke 15:11-32.
