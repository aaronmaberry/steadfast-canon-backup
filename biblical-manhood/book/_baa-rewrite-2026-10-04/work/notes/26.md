## 26. What You Are Building When Nobody Claps

Opening verse: Hebrews 6:10 (NIV).

"He went home to Nazareth with His parents and did what they told Him": Luke 2:51-52.

"called Him His beloved Son": Matthew 3:17.

"a town people made jokes about": John 1:46.

"Go into your room, He said, shut the door": Matthew 6:1-6.

"fire will show what each man built": 1 Corinthians 3:10-15.

"faithfulness in very little": Luke 16:10.

"treasure in heaven": Matthew 6:19-21.

"he'd worked for nothing and spent his strength for no reason": Isaiah 49:4.

"harvest comes in its season": Galatians 6:9.
