## 19. Lay It Down

Opening verse: Ephesians 5:25 (NIV).

"the one telling wives to submit": Ephesians 5:22-33.

"made Himself nothing and took a servant's form": Philippians 2:5-7.

"tied a towel around Himself, and washed feet": John 13:3-5.

"whoever tries to save his life will lose it": Matthew 16:25.

"praises his wife in front of everybody": Proverbs 31:28-29.

"Eve was called a helper": Genesis 2:18.

"Christ came to serve": Mark 10:45.

"a gentle answer turns away anger": Proverbs 15:1.

"don't break faith with the wife of your youth": Malachi 2:15.

"gentle and humble in heart": Matthew 11:29.

"He went looking for the lost": Luke 19:10.

"it was His body, given for them": Luke 22:19.

"He cried at a friend's grave": John 11:35.
