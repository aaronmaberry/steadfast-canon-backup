## 12. A Man With No Brothers

Opening verse: Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 (NIV).

"the first thing God calls not good is a man by himself": Genesis 2:18.

"David had Jonathan. Jesus had twelve, and three of them closer still": 1 Samuel 18:1-3; Mark 3:13-19; Mark 9:2.

"He told them His soul was crushed with sorrow": Mark 14:32-34.

"Saul's own son Jonathan walks out to David at Horesh": 1 Samuel 23:15-18.

"iron sharpens iron": Proverbs 27:17.

"he hadn't arrived and was still pressing on": Philippians 3:12-14.

"Paul told Titus to encourage the young men": Titus 2:6-7.

"Moses went ahead of Joshua. Elijah went ahead of Elisha": Deuteronomy 34:9; 2 Kings 2:1-15.

"hand what he'd heard on to reliable men": 2 Timothy 2:2.

"The Father who sets the lonely in families": Psalm 68:6.
