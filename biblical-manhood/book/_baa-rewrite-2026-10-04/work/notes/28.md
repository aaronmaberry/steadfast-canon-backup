## 28. These Men Are My Brothers

Opening verse: Proverbs 17:17 (NIV).

"the Father who sees in secret": Matthew 6:4.

"his heart is steadfast and trusts in the Lord": Psalm 112:7.

"spur one another on toward love and good deeds": Hebrews 10:24-25.

"the Father is standing out on the road": Luke 15:20.

"the first Adam's or the second's": 1 Corinthians 15:22, 45.

"their labor in the Lord is not in vain": 1 Corinthians 15:58.

"the Lord would bless them and keep them": Numbers 6:24-26.

"Keep was Adam's second job in the garden": Genesis 2:15.
