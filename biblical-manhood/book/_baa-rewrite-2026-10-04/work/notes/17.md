## 17. If There Is No Wife Yet

Opening verse: 1 Corinthians 7:35 (NIV).

"it's not good for a man to be alone": Genesis 2:18.

"Paul was single, and so was John the Baptist": 1 Corinthians 7:7-8; Matthew 3:4.

"Adam got nearly his whole assignment while he was still alone": Genesis 2:15-20.

"the man who finds a wife finds a good thing": Proverbs 18:22.

"Charm fools people and beauty fades": Proverbs 31:30.

"better to marry than to burn": 1 Corinthians 7:9.

"only to help them live well and give the Lord their undivided devotion": 1 Corinthians 7:32-35.

"his true son in the faith": 1 Timothy 1:2.

"The marriage bed is a covenant": Hebrews 13:4.

"older women as mothers and younger women as sisters": 1 Timothy 5:1-2.
