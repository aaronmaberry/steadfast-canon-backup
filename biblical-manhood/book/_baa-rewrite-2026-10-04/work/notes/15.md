## 15. Two Men, Two Ways

Opening verse: 1 Corinthians 15:22 (NIV).

"God shaped the man from the dust of the ground": Genesis 2:7.

"God made mankind in His own image, male and female": Genesis 1:27.

"a charge to be fruitful, fill the earth": Genesis 1:28.

"calling Jesus His beloved Son": Matthew 3:16-17.

"He planted a garden in Eden and put the man there": Genesis 2:8.

"to work it and take care of it": Genesis 2:15.

"You're free to eat from any tree in the garden": Genesis 2:16-17.

"By the seventh day God had finished His work": Genesis 2:2-3.

"It's not good for the man to be alone": Genesis 2:18.

"whatever the man called each one, that was its name": Genesis 2:19-20.

"bone of his bones and flesh of his flesh": Genesis 2:21-24.

"both naked, and they felt no shame": Genesis 2:25.

"one man's disobedience made the many sinners": Romans 5:19.

"Did He really tell you that?": Genesis 3:1-5.

"gave some to her husband, who was with her": Genesis 3:6.

"afraid because he was naked, so he hid": Genesis 3:8-12.

"clothes from an animal's skin": Genesis 3:21.

"crush the serpent's head": Genesis 3:15.

"falls with His face to the ground": Matthew 26:36-44.

"the sweat came off Him like drops of blood": Luke 22:44.

"with our guilt laid on Him": 2 Corinthians 5:21.

"the betrayer is here": Matthew 26:46.

"went out and asked who they wanted": John 18:4.

"let these men go": John 18:8.

"He says it is finished": John 19:30.

"In Adam all die, and in Christ all will be made alive": 1 Corinthians 15:22.

"being born again": John 3:3.

"crucified with Christ": Galatians 2:20.

"The Spirit who raised Jesus lives in a man": Romans 8:11.

"the last Adam": 1 Corinthians 15:45.
