## 20. The Bed Is a Covenant

Opening verse: Hebrews 13:4 (NIV).

"With my body I thee worship": the marriage service, Book of Common Prayer (1662).

"the two become one flesh, and they're naked and not ashamed": Genesis 2:24-25.

"the mystery is Christ and His church": Ephesians 5:31-32.

"the husband's body isn't his alone": 1 Corinthians 7:3-5.

"drink from his own well": Proverbs 5:15-19.

"a lustful look is adultery": Matthew 5:27-28.

"the Father who sees what's done in secret": Matthew 6:4.

"learn control of your own body": 1 Thessalonians 4:3-5.

"David confessed, and God gave him back his footing": Psalm 51; 2 Samuel 12:13.
