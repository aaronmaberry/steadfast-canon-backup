## 3. The Four Marks

Opening verse: 1 Corinthians 16:13 (NIV).

"have the same mind Christ Jesus had": Philippians 2:5.

"If you know the good you ought to do and you don't do it": James 4:17.

"Each one should carry his own load": Galatians 6:5.

"the shepherd who brings His sheep out and then goes on ahead of them": John 10:4.

"Whatever you do, he told them, put your whole heart in it": Colossians 3:23-24.

"Nobody earns an inheritance": Ephesians 2:8-9; Romans 8:17.

"the Father Himself saying": Matthew 25:21.

"Watch yourself, stand firm in the faith, have courage, be strong": 1 Corinthians 16:13.
