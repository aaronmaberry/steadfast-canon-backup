## 6. Go First

Opening verse: John 10:4 (NIV).

"He brings His own sheep out and goes on ahead of them": John 10:4.

"the pioneer of our faith": Hebrews 12:2.

"If their Lord and Teacher had washed their feet": John 13:3-15.

"'Watch me,' he told them": Judges 7:17-18.

"You see the trouble we're in, he told them": Nehemiah 2:12-17.

"the Spirit God gives us doesn't make us timid": 2 Timothy 1:7.

"The Lord Himself goes before you, he told Joshua": Deuteronomy 31:7-8.
