"Gideon asked God for a sign": Judges 6:17, 36-40.

"the day of small things": Zechariah 4:10.

"Paul spoke it in a synagogue in Asia Minor": Acts 13:14-37.

"with Bathsheba and with the census": 2 Samuel 11-12; 2 Samuel 24.

"still bear fruit in old age": Psalm 92:12-14.

"God's handiwork, made in Christ Jesus for good works": Ephesians 2:10.

"Mordecai's word to Esther": Esther 4:14.

"His food was to do the will of the One who sent Him": John 4:34.

"fought the fight, run the race, kept the faith": 2 Timothy 4:7.

"Joseph's purpose went through a prison": Genesis 39-41.

"He told Simon He'd prayed his faith wouldn't fail": Luke 22:31-34.

"a white stone": Revelation 2:17.
