"He grew in wisdom and stature": Luke 2:51-52; Mark 6:3.

"take his share of hardship like a good soldier": 2 Timothy 2:3.

"what the stones meant": Joshua 4:1-7.

"Passover had a child's question built right into the meal": Exodus 12:26-27.

"stand firm in the faith and be strong": 1 Corinthians 16:13.
