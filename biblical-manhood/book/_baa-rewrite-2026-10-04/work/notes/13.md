## 13. The Problem Inside

Opening verse: Mark 7:23 (NIV).

"God told him sin was crouched at his door": Genesis 4:6-8.

"And he reached for the fruit": Genesis 3:6.

"Jeremiah called the heart crooked beyond a man's own repair": Jeremiah 17:9.

"every evil they could name came out of the heart": Mark 7:20-23.

"while we were still sinners, Christ died for us": Romans 5:8.

"Saul kept that tally": 1 Samuel 18:6-11.

"the kind Jesus seemed angriest at": Matthew 23:25-28.

"not be harsh with them": Colossians 3:19.

"he sent his general and the army and stayed in Jerusalem": 2 Samuel 11:1.

"What David had done displeased the Lord": 2 Samuel 11:2-27.

"His body wasted, and God's hand pressed on him": Psalm 32:3-5.

"told him he was that man": 2 Samuel 12:1-7.

"he had sinned against the Lord": 2 Samuel 12:13.

"confess our sins to each other": James 5:16.

"I want the good thing and don't do it": Romans 7:15-25.

"make a pure heart in him": Psalm 51:10.

"every word He'd taught was spoken in public": John 18:19-21; Matthew 26:59-60.
