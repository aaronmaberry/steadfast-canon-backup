## 5. Pick Up the Weight

Opening verse: Galatians 6:5 (NIV).

"Each man should carry his own load": Galatians 6:5.

"Adam pointed two directions at once": Genesis 3:12.

"Am I my brother's keeper?": Genesis 4:9.

"a man who won't provide for his own people": 1 Timothy 5:8.

"Carry each other's burdens, he writes": Galatians 6:2.

"When Jesus called His burden light": Matthew 11:30.

"he couldn't carry all these people by himself": Numbers 11:14; the seventy elders, Numbers 11:16-17.

"He took up our pain and carried our suffering": Isaiah 53:4.

"There was joy ahead of Him": Hebrews 12:2.
