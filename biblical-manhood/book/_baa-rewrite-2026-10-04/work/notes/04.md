## 4. Stop Waiting

Opening verse: James 4:17 (NIV).

"If you know the good you ought to do and you don't do it": James 4:17.

"walked past a lazy man's field once": Proverbs 24:30-31.

"When God came walking through the garden in the cool of the day": Genesis 3:8-9.

"Wait for the Lord, he says, and be strong": Psalm 27:14.

"Think about Abraham": Genesis 12-21; the rescue of Lot, Genesis 14:14-16.

"Jesus set out for Jerusalem with His mind made up": Luke 9:51.

"the Father sees what's done in secret": Matthew 6:4, 6.
