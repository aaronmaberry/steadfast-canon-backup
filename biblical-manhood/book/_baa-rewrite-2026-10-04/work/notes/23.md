## 23. You Were Made for a Work

Opening verse: John 5:17 (NIV).

"work at whatever they did with all their heart": Colossians 3:22-24.

"in a garden, before anything broke": Genesis 2:15.

"Jesus worked with His hands": Mark 6:3.

"whoever refuses to work shouldn't eat": 2 Thessalonians 3:10.

"Paul even told thieves what to do instead of stealing": Ephesians 4:28.

"what God felt on the sixth day": Genesis 1:31.

"the Levites quit the heavy work at fifty": Numbers 8:25-26.

"eat and drink and find some satisfaction in his work": Ecclesiastes 2:24.

"Your Father notices what's done in private": Matthew 6:4.

"finishing the work He'd been given to do": John 17:4.
