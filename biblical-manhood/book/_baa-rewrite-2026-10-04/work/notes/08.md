## 8. The Empty Chair

Opening verse: Psalm 68:5-6 (NIV).

"Joseph had a father who loved him, and the pit happened anyway": Genesis 37.

"David was out with the flock when the prophet came": 1 Samuel 16:10-11.

"Joseph, Mary's husband, quietly exits before Jesus' public years": Luke 2:41-52; John 19:26-27.

"Even if my father and mother leave me, he prayed": Psalm 27:10.
