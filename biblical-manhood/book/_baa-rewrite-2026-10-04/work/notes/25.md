## 25. Every Season Still Counts

Opening verse: Ecclesiastes 3:1 (NIV).

"David served God's purpose in his own generation": Acts 13:36.

"remember their Creator before the days of trouble come": Ecclesiastes 12:1.

"asked for the hill country the Lord had promised him": Joshua 14:6-12.

"even to their old age and gray hair": Isaiah 46:4.

"a dad holding his small son": Deuteronomy 1:31.

"God called a man alone not good": Genesis 2:18.

"Paul begged three times to lose his thorn": 2 Corinthians 12:7-10.

"fought the good fight, finished the race, and kept the faith": 2 Timothy 4:6-8.

"Bless your children from your bed, like Jacob": Genesis 48-49.
