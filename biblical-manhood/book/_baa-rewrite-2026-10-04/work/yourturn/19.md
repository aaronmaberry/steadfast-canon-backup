## 19. Lay It Down

**Name it**

What are you clutching? The money, the argument, your phone, your mother's say, your side in public, your sleep, your Saturday, your temper, the title, or a plan you made without her. Write it down in one full sentence. Then imagine her sister asks how you're doing as a husband, and write the Renee answer you'd hate to overhear. Single, or the marriage is over? Write the answer she would have given.

**Do this week**

Sit down and ask her Russ's question. "What do you need from me that you're not getting?" No defending, no explaining. Do whatever she names first, before Saturday, and put the thing you're clutching down every day, quietly, without keeping score. Single? Give up one small preference a day for someone already in your life. If the marriage ended, set down your need to win at the next handoff.

**Tell a brother**

You can't see your own grip, and he can. Tell him how she answered and what you let go of. Ask what you're still holding that you've stopped seeing, and admit where each of you has drifted toward the louder applause.

**Carry this**

Being right is not the same as being a husband. Lay it down.
