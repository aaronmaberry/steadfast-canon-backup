## 8. The Empty Chair

**Name it**

Out loud, finish this sentence. "The chair at the end of my table was ___." Then name one thing it taught you. Left unnamed, the chair keeps running things from the attic.

**Do this week**

Write your father a letter you won't send. Put in what you needed, what never came, and whatever thanks is true. Keep it somewhere safe, because you'll want it again before this book is done. Grief held in the body comes out sideways, in your marriage and your temper.

**Tell a brother**

Read one man your finished sentence about the chair. The letter stays private unless you choose otherwise. He may say "me too," or "I'm sorry." Let that be enough. A hard sentence weighs less once another man has heard it.
