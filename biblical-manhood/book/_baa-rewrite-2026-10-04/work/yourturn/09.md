## 9. Anger, the Itch, and the Drift

**Name it**

Write one complete sentence that begins "When things get heavy, I," and name yours, the drift, the itch, or anger. You'll keep calling it your personality until you admit it's an escape.

**Do this week**

Each escape has a first step that fits in seven days. For the drift, one dark-screen evening fully present, plus one question you wait out to the end. For the itch, one man who hears everything and holds the passwords. For anger, the mailbox walk, then the sentence under it.

**Tell a brother**

Trade escapes. Yours, the last time you used it, then his. Neither of you is allowed to say "I'm fine." Secrecy is what keeps these escapes alive.

**Carry this**

Every escape walks you out of your own house. Pain was looking for a Father, and an exit was the wrong address.
