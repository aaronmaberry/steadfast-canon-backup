## 17. If There Is No Wife Yet

**Name it**

Which seat is yours, never married, divorced, or widowed? Write it down. Then write how long you've been talking as if your real life is still coming. Plenty of men spend years in a waiting room and call it patience.

**Do this week**

Get one thing done before Sunday. Your rooms, a meal for someone, a call to a brother, an afternoon with a fatherless boy, or the honest ask. Single again? Pick one of those, and tell God two true things, one about what you lost and one about what you did. Houses go up one task at a time.

**Tell a brother**

Let him know what seat you're in and what you picked to do. If you're divorced and starting to think about dating, ask him straight whether you're ready, and listen to the answer. Nobody drifts faster than a man whose week no one sees.

**Carry this**

No wife yet still leaves you a man, with ground to work and a Father who already calls you son.
