## 7. Work for God's Pay

**Name it**

Applause, the number, being wanted, being right, the nod, comfort, being remembered. Which small reward is driving right now? Write it down, and under it write where it's been taking you. A prize you won't name is a prize you can't trade in.

**Do this week**

Do one kindness no human being will find out about. Your wife or your roommate doesn't hear about it. Your brother doesn't hear about it. The men you meet don't hear about it. After that, keep quiet for good. The Father who sees in secret is the one who rewards secret work.

**Tell a brother**

Saying it out loud to another man loosens its grip, so name the small reward that has its hooks in you and what it's cost you. Keep the hidden kindness to yourself. That one stays between you and your Father.

**Carry this**

Some kind of pay already has you. Name it, and aim higher.
