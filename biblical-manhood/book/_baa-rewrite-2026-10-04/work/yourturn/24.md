## 24. Feelings Are the Dash, Not the Wheel

**Name it**

Tape over the gauges, or the needle on the wheel? Pick your ditch. Then put one word on today's dash. Joy, fear, shame, anger, numbness, sadness, or loneliness. A reading you refuse to look at is still driving.

**Do this week**

Start each day with two lines to God. "Father, I'm feeling ___," and "Here's what I'll do anyway, ___." A good driver checks his dash every morning, not only when smoke is coming out of the hood. If it's been dark for weeks, book the doctor before Saturday.

**Tell a brother**

Show him the full panel, including the ugly gauges. Kept secret, a reading is what drives men off the road. If you're sitting under the bush right now, call him today.

Your feelings read the engine. The Word drives the truck.
