## 5. Pick Up the Weight

**Name it**

Write one line that opens with "That's mine" and stops at the period. Leave off any "but." Leave off any explanation. The mark begins with a sentence you let stand on its own.

**Do this week**

Each day this week, give your household something money can't buy. Cook supper. Sit up front at the game. Ask a question and hear the whole answer. Living alone or with roommates? Claim the chore nobody claims, the trash or the sink, and keep it.

**Tell a brother**

A weight another man has heard you claim is harder to drop. Tell him your "That's mine" line and what you did next, and ask him to check back in a week.
