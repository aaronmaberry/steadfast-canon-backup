## 21. Your Daughter Is Watching

**Name it**

Write what she sees from you when her mother walks in, what she sees when she fails, and what you've been calling her lately. If the honest answer is nothing, write that. Silence teaches too. No daughter of your own? Picture a girl in your church watching you with the women around you, and write what she'd conclude.

**Do this week**

Tell her one specific thing you love about who she is, nothing to do with what she did. Honor her mom in front of her every day this week, a thank-you, a gentle hand, taking her side, an apology. If she's grown, call her and say the thing you assumed she already knew. No daughter? Once this week, honor a mother or a sister where a girl can see it.

**Tell a brother**

Dads of daughters almost never talk about this with each other. Walk him through what she's learning from you and admit which lesson you've been teaching worst, then find out what his girl is picking up. No daughter? Read him what you wrote about the girl at church, and give him permission to watch you this Sunday and say what he sees.

**Carry this**

How you treat her mother this week is a preview of the man your daughter will marry.
