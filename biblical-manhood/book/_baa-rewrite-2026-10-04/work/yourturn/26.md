## 26. What You Are Building When Nobody Claps

**Name it**

Write down three things you're building that no one notices. Then be honest. Lately, have you been resenting the silence, or quietly working for an audience? Work nobody sees can go sour and turn into a grudge.

**Do this week**

Put your rhythm on an index card, the handful of things that stay put, sized for your season. Keep it on the dash of the truck. Live it for seven days and don't mention it to anyone. Big days only harden in the plain ones after.

**Tell a brother**

Show him the card, and give him permission to ask next week how it went. Then ask what he's pouring in the dark, and tell him you see it. Nobody sees his pour either, and every man needs one witness.

**Carry this**

The crowd gets straw. The Father gets the gold you pour in the dark.
