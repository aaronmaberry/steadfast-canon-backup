## 10. The Father Who Does Not Leave

**Name it**

Which picture have your prayers been landing on? The dad who never came, the silent one, the dangerous one, or the good one with gaps? Name it, and then write one true thing Jesus shows you about the Father. Prayers keep hitting a false picture until you notice you're holding one.

**Do this week**

Every morning, before your feet touch the floor, say "Father" out loud and give Him one sentence about the day ahead. Seven mornings straight. You learn to be a son by talking like one.

**Tell a brother**

Tell him which false father your prayers have been aimed at. Then ask him to pray for you, out loud, right there, and don't wave him off. Letting another man pray over you is practice in being carried.
