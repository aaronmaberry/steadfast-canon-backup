## 3. The Four Marks

**Name it**

Which of the four is faintest on you right now? Write one full sentence that names it and says why you picked it. A mark you won't look at never gets worked on.

**Do this week**

Before next Tuesday, do one small thing that belongs to your faint mark. Get off the tailgate. Say "that's mine" and stop talking. Walk into one room first. Do one good thing nobody will ever hear about. Grooves only come from doing the same thing again.

**Tell a brother**

What stays in your head stays shapeless. Tell one man which mark is faint on you and what you did about it this week, and then ask him which one is faint on him.
