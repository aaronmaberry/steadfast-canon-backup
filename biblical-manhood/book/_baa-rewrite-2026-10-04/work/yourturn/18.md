## 18. Know Her

**Name it**

How much of what you'd tell me about your wife comes from the early years? Write that down, then circle your weakest area, her past, her fears, her body, her faith, her everyday, or the wants she's stopped saying. Single? Write what you'd hope to know about a woman before you make her a promise. Divorced? Write where you knew her least when it fell apart.

**Do this week**

Pick a question from your weakest area, ask it, and let her answer all the way through without fixing or defending. Then write down what she told you, on a new page if you need one. Single men, try it with a sister in Christ or a couple you serve with. If the marriage ended, ask your kids' mother one question about them and let her finish.

**Tell a brother**

Bring him one thing you learned that surprised you. Then ask when he last really studied his own wife, and don't either of you settle for "we're good." Single? Tell him what you heard and ask if he's ever listened that way.
