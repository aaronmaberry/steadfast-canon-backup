## 1. A Quiet Tuesday

**Name it**

Replay your last plain weekday from alarm to pillow. Name two spots where you chose something without feeling like you chose. You can't change a day you've never really looked at.

**Do this week**

Take one of those two spots and handle it differently next Tuesday. Pick up the call you'd usually let ring out. Leave the phone face down through supper. Sit at the counter for the ten minutes. Small things done again are what shape a man.

**Tell a brother**

Who heard about your last Tuesday? Nobody, probably. Send one man a text with what you saw in your ordinary day and what you'll try differently on the next one.
