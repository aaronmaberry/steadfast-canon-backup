## 14. No Fixed Star

**Name it**

What did you actually measure your life by this year? Your father, the men around you, the culture, your feelings, or your success. Write one honest sentence. Any north you won't name keeps steering you long after you think you've let it go.

**Do this week**

Read a section of Mark every day, aloud when you can. Each time, write one line about what it asks a man to do, and whether you'll do it before lunch. Scripture only becomes your fixed point once you open it and ask it something.

**Tell a brother**

Which north have you been steering by? Let him hear it. Then read him one verse from your week, tell him what obeying it would cost, and be honest about whether you did. Two men holding the same line catch a lean one man can explain away.

**Carry this**

You don't need to know why it holds. Put the string on the wall and trust the weight.
