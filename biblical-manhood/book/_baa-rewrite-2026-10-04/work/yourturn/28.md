## 28. These Men Are My Brothers

**Name it**

Say it aloud. One man in this chapter looks most like you right now, and another is the man you're turning into. Then name your season once more, like a pasture you could walk blindfolded. You need to know your ground and the man you're walking toward.

**Be a brother**

You've spent the book telling a brother. Now be one. Look around church, the job site, your street, for the man standing where you stood, and ask if he'd be willing to talk twenty minutes a week. When he calls, pick up. Somebody has to make the first move, or this company never grows.
