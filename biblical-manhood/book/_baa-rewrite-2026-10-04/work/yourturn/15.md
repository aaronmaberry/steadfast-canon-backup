## 15. Two Men, Two Ways

**Name it**

Which part of that first week feels most bent in you now? Being made and wanted, your place, your work, the rest, the people, your words, or living with nothing hidden. Then pick the most recent real moment from this week, the kitchen, the late phone, the 2 a.m. hallway, and write down honestly which man's way you took.

**Do this week**

Some ordinary morning, read the first two chapters of Genesis aloud in one sitting. Every morning after that, picture the moment you're most likely to face that day and say the Gethsemane line that fits it, out loud. Rise, let us go. Not as I will. Let these men go. Then head out the door.

**Tell a brother**

Whose you are is the largest question this book asks. Give him your real answer. Never trusted Christ to bring you out of Adam? Say so, and let him help you take that step. If you have, tell him about one moment this week when you went Jesus' way instead of Adam's.
