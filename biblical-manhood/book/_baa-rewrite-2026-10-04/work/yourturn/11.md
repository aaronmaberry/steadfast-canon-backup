## 11. When Home Never Handed You Off

**Name it**

Can you point to the day somebody counted you in? Say who and when, or say the plain truth that nobody ever did. Then say which way you've handled home since, the slam, the grip, or the long orbit, and what it has cost your marriage or your kids.

**Do this week**

Younger? Ask an older man whose life looks like these pages, straight out, whether he sees a man when he looks at you, and take whatever answer he gives. Older? Tell a younger guy one specific thing you've seen him do that only a grown man does. If you slammed the door at home, pray for your dad by name every day this week. If you never really left, decide one thing without running it past your parents, and tell them kindly after.

**Tell a brother**

Show him the date going inside the cover of your Bible and tell him what it marks. If a porch is coming, give him that date too, so it lands on another man's calendar. A date only you know about tends to fade.

**Carry this**

You can leave home without slamming the door. Give God the debt, and knock when you come back.
