## 27. Write the Next Move

**Name it**

Which kind have you been, the man with the blank page or the man with the binder? Say it, then say the move that's already waiting at the bottom, with a verb, a person, and a day. Once you know how your plans usually die, you can guard against it.

**Do this week**

Put the page on one sheet, ten lines at most, each one fitted to the season you're in. The worksheet on the next page is a place to start. Sign it and date it, and get the bottom line done before the week runs out. What never reaches paper never reaches Tuesday.

**Tell a brother**

Read him every line and give him a copy. Ask him to check on the bottom line next week and on the whole page in ninety days. Writing one too? Swap. A herald runs only after somebody else has read the plan.

**Carry this**

Paper makes a plan real, with a verb, a person, and a day. You plan the course. The Lord sets the steps.
