## 20. The Bed Is a Covenant

**Name it**

If there's a weak spot you keep hidden, write it down. Wandering eyes, the phone, rushing her, a scorecard in your head, the cold shoulder, or plain neglect. It stays strong until you confess it to God. Single, or the marriage is over? Write honestly whether you're saving yourself toward a vow you haven't made, or spending it ahead of time.

**Do this week**

Married? Sit down with your wife in daylight, somewhere other than the bedroom, and ask what she'd want more of there and what she'd want less of. Single or divorced? Give a brother the filter password and plug your phone in on the kitchen counter tonight. Promises get said out loud, in the open, long before anybody keeps them in the dark.

**Tell a brother**

Hand him the weak spot you wrote down, out loud. If a screen is involved, give him the specifics, what, how often, and since when. Then hand him the password. This hunger feeds on secrets.

A contract keeps score on her. A covenant uses every year you've got to show her she's worth it.
