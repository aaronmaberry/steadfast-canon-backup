## 2. You Are Not Crazy

**Name it**

Pick two things you were told about manhood that can't both be true. Say them out loud, along with who taught you each one. A contradiction nobody names keeps running your week.

**Do this week**

Write one line about the work you think a man is here to do. No good answer yet? Then write the one you've actually been living, even if it's a poor one. Keep that paper. Soon you'll set another line beside it.

**Tell a brother**

Read your line to one man and ask to hear his. A hum you keep to yourself starts to sound like a sentence handed down. It gets quieter with company.

**Carry this**

Other hands drew the maps that got you lost. The line coming next is short enough to learn by heart and big enough to spend your life on.
