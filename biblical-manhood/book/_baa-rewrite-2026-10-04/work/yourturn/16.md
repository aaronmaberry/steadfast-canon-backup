## 16. Destiny Is Not a Vibe

**Name it**

Three stories try for the wheel. It has to feel right, it has to be big, or it has to be somewhere else. Write down the one that's had it, then one sentence, using real names, about what serving God's purpose looks like where you actually live. The lie that steers you rarely introduces itself.

**Do this week**

On one card, write four headings. Place, people, hands, and these years. Add a line under each. Before Sunday, do one thing from that card, whether you feel anything or not.

**Tell a brother**

Read him your card, and admit which feeling you've been waiting on and for how long. Then ask where he sees God pointing your life right now. Another man can often see your purpose more clearly than you can.

**Carry this**

Do what God handed you, where He put you, for the people He gave you, until it's finished. Destiny is not a vibe.
