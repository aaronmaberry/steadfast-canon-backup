## 4. Stop Waiting

**Name it**

Write down the one thing you've put off longest. Beside it, write how long. Be honest about the number. Whatever you never say out loud stays in charge.

**Do this week**

Pick a workday and, before noon, make the smallest real move on that thing. Feeling ready isn't required. New baby, and you've been saying "after this crazy stretch"? The stretch is the season. Ten minutes with her at the counter tonight, both phones face down, counts.

**Tell a brother**

It's easier to move when someone's watching for it. Tell one man what you've been stepping around and what you did about it, and ask him to bring it up again next week.

**Carry this**

Where are you? He isn't asking for directions. Get up and answer Him.
