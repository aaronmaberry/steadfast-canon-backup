# Your Turn

Every chapter in this book has a page here. Read the chapter, then turn back here and work its page, one a week if you can. Each page has the same four lines. Name it asks you to put the thing on paper honestly. Do this week gives you one move to make before next Tuesday. Tell a brother sends you to another man, out loud, and on the last page it becomes Be a brother. Carry this is the line the chapter ended on, short enough to take into Tuesday.

Write in the lines. Bring the book to the table if you're working through it with other men. A page you fill in and act on will do more than a chapter you only underline.
