# Eight-Week Reading Plan

Some men read this book alone. Some read it a week ahead of the class. Some read it with one brother over breakfast. This plan is for all three. It takes you through the whole book in eight weeks, about fifteen minutes a day, six days a week, with the seventh day left for rest and for catching up.

Each day gives you a piece of the book and a passage of Scripture to read for yourself. Read the passage in whatever Bible you own. Read it aloud if you can, in the truck, on the porch, or at the kitchen table before anyone else is up. Then do the one thing the day asks. Most days that's a single question or a single move, and it's meant to be done that same day.

On the day you finish a chapter, turn to its page in Your Turn and work it. That page is the real homework. The plan only gets you to it.

If you're in the twenty-four week class, you don't need this plan for the class itself. The class takes a chapter a week. Use this plan instead if you want to read the whole book through before or alongside the class, so you can see where it's going. Then come back and work the Your Turn pages one week at a time with your group.

If you miss a day, don't start over. Pick up where you left off. If you miss a week, tell your brother, and pick up where you left off. Nobody gets graded. The only failure is quitting.

## Week 1: The Ordinary Day

Day 1. Read the Introduction. Read 1 Corinthians 15:58. Write down one thing you hope this book does in you. Keep it short, and keep it where you'll find it in eight weeks.

Day 2. Read A Quiet Tuesday. Read Luke 16:10-12. Tonight, before bed, replay your day from alarm to pillow and name two spots where you chose something without noticing you chose it.

Day 3. Read Matthew 7:24-27 twice. Ask what you heard this week that you haven't done yet. Do it before tomorrow night. Then work the Your Turn page for chapter one.

Day 4. Read You Are Not Crazy. Read Genesis 1:26-31. Write two things you were taught about manhood that can't both be true, and who taught you each one.

Day 5. Read Genesis 2:15-25. Notice what God gave the man before anything went wrong. Work the Your Turn page for chapter two.

Day 6. Read The Four Marks. Read 1 Corinthians 16:13-14. Say the sentence at the center of the book out loud three times today, once in the morning, once at noon, once at night. Name the mark that runs faint in you.

Day 7. Rest. Read Philippians 2:5-11 slowly. Catch up on anything you missed. Work the Your Turn page for chapter three.

## Week 2: The Four Marks

Day 8. Read Stop Waiting. Read James 4:13-17. Name the one thing you've been waiting on the longest. Write down the smallest real move you could make on it.

Day 9. Read Genesis 3:8-10. God asks Adam where he is. Answer that question honestly today, out loud. Make the move you wrote down yesterday, before noon if you can. Work the Your Turn page for chapter four.

Day 10. Read Pick Up the Weight. Read Galatians 6:1-5. Make two lists, what's yours to carry and what you've been carrying that isn't yours.

Day 11. Read Go First. Read John 10:1-11. Pick one door to walk through first this week. Work the Your Turn page for chapters five and six over the next few days.

Day 12. Read Work for God's Pay. Read Colossians 3:22-24. Ask what pays you right now, money, praise, being needed, or being left alone.

Day 13. Read Ephesians 2:8-10 and Matthew 6:1-6. Do one kindness today that nobody will ever hear about, and don't mention it. Work the Your Turn page for chapter seven.

Day 14. Rest. Look back over Part One. Which of the four marks moved this week, and which one didn't? Tell one man what you noticed.

## Week 3: What You Were Handed

Day 15. Read The Empty Chair. Read Psalm 68:4-6. Think about who sat in your father's chair at the table growing up, and what you learned there without being told.

Day 16. Write the letter to your father that you won't send. What you needed, what never came, and whatever thanks is true. Put it somewhere safe. Work the Your Turn page for chapter eight.

Day 17. Read Anger, the Itch, and the Drift. Read Ephesians 4:25-32. Name your usual exit when the day goes bad.

Day 18. Read James 1:19-21. Take the first step for your exit, one that fits in seven days. Tell one man what it is. Work the Your Turn page for chapter nine.

Day 19. Read The Father Who Does Not Leave. Read Luke 15:11-24 slowly. Where did your picture of God as Father come from?

Day 20. Read Romans 8:14-17. Tomorrow morning, before your feet touch the floor, say "Father" out loud and give Him one sentence about the day. Work the Your Turn page for chapter ten.

Day 21. Rest. Read Psalm 103:8-14. Keep the morning word to the Father going all week.

## Week 4: Home, Brothers, and the Fixed Point

Day 22. Read When Home Never Handed You Off. Read Genesis 32:22-32. What blessing have you been wrestling for?

Day 23. Read Ephesians 6:1-4. Write down what you still owe your parents, and what you've been waiting for them to give you. Then give God the debt. Work the Your Turn page for chapter eleven.

Day 24. Read A Man With No Brothers. Read Ecclesiastes 4:9-12. Write down who you'd call at two in the morning. If the line is blank, write one name you could ask.

Day 25. Read 1 Samuel 18:1-4. Make the ask today. Twenty minutes a week for three months, with the day and the hour settled before you hang up. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twelve.

Day 26. Read The Problem Inside. Read Mark 7:14-23. Tonight, give God two minutes before sleep and name that day's sins one at a time. Then read 1 John 1:9 and thank Him. Work the Your Turn page for chapter thirteen.

Day 27. Read No Fixed Star. Read Amos 7:7-8 and 2 Timothy 3:14-17. Name what you've been steering by. Start reading a section of Mark every day, and keep it up past the end of this plan. Work the Your Turn page for chapter fourteen.

Day 28. Rest. Read Psalm 119:105-112. Look back over Part Two. What did you find out about where you came from, and what's yours now?

## Week 5: The Two Men

Day 29. Read Two Men, Two Ways, up to the first break. Then read Genesis 1 and 2 aloud in one sitting.

Day 30. Read Genesis 3. Notice where the man was, what he said, and who he blamed. Where do you see his pattern in your own week?

Day 31. Finish Two Men, Two Ways. Read Matthew 26:36-46. Put the two gardens side by side.

Day 32. Read Romans 5:12-19 and 1 Corinthians 15:21-22. Pick the moment you're most likely to face today, and the line from Gethsemane you'll say when it comes. Work the Your Turn page for chapter fifteen.

Day 33. Read Destiny Is Not a Vibe. Read Acts 13:36. Write a card with four headings, place, people, hands, and these years, and a line under each.

Day 34. Read Ephesians 2:10 again. Do one thing from your card today, whether you feel anything or not. Work the Your Turn page for chapter sixteen.

Day 35. Rest. Read John 19:28-30. It is finished. Sit with what that means for you.

## Week 6: The House

Day 36. Read If There Is No Wife Yet. Read 1 Corinthians 7:32-35. Married men, read it too, and think of one single brother you could have over.

Day 37. Single men, get one thing done today from the chapter's list. Married men, call the single brother and set the day. Work the Your Turn page for chapter seventeen.

Day 38. Read Know Her. Read 1 Peter 3:7-9. Write down what you know about her fears, her hopes, her days, and her history. Notice where the page is thin.

Day 39. Ask her one question from the thinnest part of that page, and listen all the way through without fixing or defending. Write down what she told you. Work the Your Turn page for chapter eighteen.

Day 40. Read Lay It Down. Read Ephesians 5:21-30. Name what you're clutching.

Day 41. Read John 13:1-15. Ask her what she needs from you that she isn't getting. Don't defend. Do the first thing she names before the week is out. Work the Your Turn page for chapter nineteen.

Day 42. Rest. Read Philippians 2:3-8. Look back over the week. What did you lay down?

## Week 7: The Bed, the Daughter, the Son

Day 43. Read The Bed Is a Covenant. Read Hebrews 13:4 and Malachi 2:14-16. Ask whether you've been treating your marriage, or your idea of marriage, like a contract or a covenant.

Day 44. Read 1 Corinthians 6:18-20. Take the step the chapter asks. Married men set a daylight conversation with their wives. Single and divorced men give a brother the filter password. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty.

Day 45. Read Your Daughter Is Watching. Read Zephaniah 3:14-17. Tell your daughter one specific thing you love about who she is, nothing to do with what she did. No daughter? Think of a girl in your church or family who needs a kind, steady man.

Day 46. Honor her mother in front of her today, a thank you, a gentle hand, or an apology. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty-one.

Day 47. Read Give Your Son a Name, a Hill, and a Day. Read Deuteronomy 6:4-9. Catch one thing worth naming in your son today and say it to him.

Day 48. Tonight, put your hand on his head and bless him in words that sound like you. No son? Think of a boy you could encourage, with his parents' blessing. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty-two.

Day 49. Rest. Read Psalm 127. Look back over Part Four. Who in your house got more of you this month?

## Week 8: The Long Road

Day 50. Read You Were Made for a Work. Read John 5:17 and Genesis 2:15. Do one part of your job today for the Lord, and keep it to yourself. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty-three.

Day 51. Read Feelings Are the Dash, Not the Wheel. Read Psalm 42. Start the day with two lines to God, "Father, I'm feeling this," and "Here's what I'll do anyway." Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty-four.

Day 52. Read Every Season Still Counts. Read Ecclesiastes 3:1-14. Write your season's assignment on one line with an action word in it, and put it where your coffee will find it. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty-five.

Day 53. Read What You Are Building When Nobody Claps. Read 1 Corinthians 3:10-15 and Hebrews 6:10. Put your rhythm on an index card and keep it on the dash. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty-six.

Day 54. Read Write the Next Move. Read Habakkuk 2:1-3 and Proverbs 16:9. Write your page, ten lines at most. Sign it and date it. Work the Your Turn page for chapter twenty-seven.

Day 55. Read These Men Are My Brothers. Read Proverbs 17:17 and Hebrews 10:23-25. Do the bottom line on your page. Work the last Your Turn page.

Day 56. Rest. Find the line you wrote on the first day. Read it. Then read the four marks slowly, once, and hang this week against them. Tell one brother what changed. Then set the day you'll start the book again, this time with a table of men.


## Reading with one brother

If you're working this plan with another man, read on your own during the week and meet once, on the rest day if you can. Bring two things. The line from the week's reading that wouldn't let you go, and whatever you did with the day's moves. Ask each other about the misses as well as the wins. Twenty minutes is enough. The guide called Twenty Minutes a Week has questions if you want them.

## After the eight weeks

Don't put the book on a shelf. Three things are worth doing next.

Keep reading Scripture every day. If you started Mark on day twenty-seven, finish it, then read John, then a chapter of Proverbs a day for a month. A man who reads every day for a year is a different man at the end of it.

Find a table. Ask your church whether a group is working through the class, or ask two or three men to start one with you. The Small-Group Guide will walk you through it, and you'll go deeper the second time.

Go back to your page from chapter twenty-seven. Read it on the first of every month. Cross off what's done, rewrite what's changed, and keep the bottom line fresh.
