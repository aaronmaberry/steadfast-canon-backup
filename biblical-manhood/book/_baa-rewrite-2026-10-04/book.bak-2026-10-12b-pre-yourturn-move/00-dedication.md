# Dedication

For the men who pulled a chair out for me before I had anything worth setting on the table, and who left that chair open long after they stopped needing me in the room.

For my wife, who has watched me try to become the man in these pages and has never once pretended I had already arrived. The true sentences here were lived where she could see them. She is the reason most of them hold.

And for our children, who are still watching. I hope you find your father in this book, and I hope you find him further along than he was when you were small.
