# About the Author

Wes Hart writes for ordinary men who are done waiting for a better season before they start living like men.

He is a husband, a father of two, and a Texan. By day he leads teams in technology. The hours that matter most to him are spent across a table from other men, at kitchen tables, in garage chairs, in church classrooms, and in early weekday diners with a Bible and a cup of coffee. Steadfast Men, the twenty-four-week training behind this book, began at one of those tables with a few skeptical guys. It has since been taught in living rooms, garages, church basements, and online, to men in every season, single and married, young and older, still standing and starting over.

Wes did not set out to write a book or to build a men's program. He set out to stop being the passive husband and distracted dad he had quietly become. A few older men kept a chair for him when he had nothing worth setting on the table. Years later he started keeping chairs for other men. He is not a pastor and he is not a counselor, and he will tell you so before you ask. He is a layman who believes ordinary men, walking together under the Word of God, can become the husbands, fathers, brothers, and leaders their homes and churches are hungry for. He has watched it happen too many times to treat it as a theory.

When he is not working or teaching, he is usually outside with his kids, on a long walk with his wife, or sitting with one other man who is trying to get honest about his Tuesday.

He and his family live in Texas.
