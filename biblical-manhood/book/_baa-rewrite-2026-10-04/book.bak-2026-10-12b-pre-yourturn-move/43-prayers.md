# Prayers for the Road

Plenty of men don't pray because they don't know what to say, and they figure God expects something polished. He doesn't. These are short prayers, one for each chapter, written the way a man might actually talk to his Father on the drive in. Pray them as they are, or use them to get started and then keep going in your own words. Groups can use them to close a meeting. A man can tape one to the dash for a week.

## The Introduction

Father, I picked up this book because something in me knows I'm not the man I'm supposed to be. I don't want another plan I'll quit by spring. Make me steady. Let nothing I do for You be wasted. Amen.

## Chapter 1. A Quiet Tuesday

Lord, most of my life happens on days nobody will remember. Help me see them. Let me be faithful with the little things today, the call, the supper, the ten minutes, so You can trust me with whatever comes next. Amen.

## Chapter 2. You Are Not Crazy

God, I've been handed a lot of maps, and most of them disagree. You made me in Your image, and You made me for work You called good. Clear out the voices that lied to me, and teach me what You meant when You made a man. Amen.

## Chapter 3. The Four Marks

Lord Jesus, You never waited, You carried what wasn't Yours to carry, You went ahead of us, and You did it all for Your Father's joy. Make me like You. Start with the mark I'm weakest in. Amen.

## Chapter 4. Stop Waiting

Father, You know the thing I've been putting off. You've known it longer than I have. I don't feel ready, and I'm done waiting to feel ready. Give me the nerve to take the first step before noon. Amen.

## Chapter 5. Pick Up the Weight

God, show me what's mine to carry. Forgive me for the weight I've left on the floor and for the weight I picked up just to look important. Give me strong shoulders and a soft heart for my people. Amen.

## Chapter 6. Go First

Good Shepherd, You went out ahead of me before I knew I needed You. Today, help me go first. The first apology, the first prayer, the first hard word spoken in love. When I'm scared, remind me You're already up ahead. Amen.

## Chapter 7. Work for God's Pay

Father, I've worked for a lot of paychecks that didn't satisfy. Thank You that my inheritance is a gift and not a wage. Let me do today's work for You, and let me be content if nobody else ever sees it. Amen.

## Chapter 8. The Empty Chair

God, You know who sat in my father's chair, and You know what was missing. I bring You the hurt and the thanks both. You are a Father to the fatherless. Be mine. Help me fill my own chair better than it was filled for me. Amen.

## Chapter 9. Anger, the Itch, and the Drift

Lord, I know my exits. You know them better. When the day goes bad and I start reaching for the door, stop me. Turn me toward You and toward my brothers. Heal the pain I've been running from. Amen.

## Chapter 10. The Father Who Does Not Leave

Father. That's the word I want to start with. You saw me a long way off, and You ran. I don't have to finish my speech. I'm Your son. Teach me to talk to You like one, every morning, before my feet hit the floor. Amen.

## Chapter 11. When Home Never Handed You Off

God, I've been waiting a long time for a blessing. Some of it I may never get from the people I wanted it from. I give You the debt. Bless me the way You blessed Jacob, and help me hand off a blessing to the young men coming behind me. Amen.

## Chapter 12. A Man With No Brothers

Lord, I've been alone longer than I've admitted. Give me one brother. Give me the courage to ask, and the faithfulness to show up every week, same hour, no matter what. And make me the kind of brother I'm asking for. Amen.

## Chapter 13. The Problem Inside

Father, the trouble isn't just out there. It's in me. Here's what I did today. I name it to You. Thank You that it's already paid for in Christ. Don't let shame do the talking tonight. Let Your forgiveness have the last word. Amen.

## Chapter 14. No Fixed Star

God, I've been steering by things that move. Hang Your plumb line on my life and show me where I've started to lean. Give me a hunger for Your Word, a section a day, and a will to do what it says before lunch. Amen.

## Chapter 15. Two Men, Two Ways

Lord Jesus, I was born in the first man's garden, hiding and blaming like he did. You went into Your garden and said, not My will but Yours. Thank You that Your "It is finished" is the last word on me. Help me say Your line when my moment comes today. Amen.

## Chapter 16. Destiny Is Not a Vibe

Father, I've been waiting for something big to feel like my calling. Open my eyes to the place You put me, the people You gave me, the hands You made, and these years. Let me finish the work You handed me. Amen.

## Chapter 17. If There Is No Wife Yet

God, You know I'm alone, and You know what I want. Thank You that I'm already Your son and already a man with work to do. Give me an undivided heart this season. Keep me pure, keep me busy with good things, and keep me close to my brothers. Amen.

## Chapter 18. Know Her

Lord, I stopped paying attention somewhere along the way. Make me a student of the woman You gave me. Give me questions worth asking and the patience to listen all the way to the end without fixing anything. Amen.

## Chapter 19. Lay It Down

Jesus, You laid down Your life for Your bride. I struggle to lay down an argument. Show me what I'm clutching. Help me let go of being right so I can love her the way You love me. Amen.

## Chapter 20. The Bed Is a Covenant

Father, keep my marriage bed honored and pure, or keep me pure while I wait. Wash out what porn and the world have taught me. Help me love my wife with my whole life, every year I've got, and never keep score. Amen.

## Chapter 21. Your Daughter Is Watching

God, You delight in Your children and sing over them. Help me delight in my daughter the same way, for who she is and not what she does. Let the way I treat her mother show her what a good man looks like. Amen.

## Chapter 22. Give Your Son a Name, a Hill, and a Day

Father, You spoke over Your Son, "This is My Son, whom I love." Help me speak over mine. Give me the words to name what's good in him, the wisdom to hand him a hill he can climb, and the faithfulness to keep showing up afterward. Amen.

## Chapter 23. You Were Made for a Work

Lord, You are always at work, and You made me to work too. Let me do my job today as worship, for You and not for the room. If I've lost my old work, give me a new one, and help me finish it. Amen.

## Chapter 24. Feelings Are the Dash, Not the Wheel

God, here's what I'm feeling today, and You already know it. I'll tell You the truth about it, and then I'll do what's right anyway. Steady me when the weather inside me turns. Put my hope in You. Amen.

## Chapter 25. Every Season Still Counts

Father, I don't love the season I'm in. Show me its job. Help me do it well, and keep me from wasting these days wishing for the next ones. Amen.

## Chapter 26. What You Are Building When Nobody Claps

Lord, most of what I build, nobody will see. You will. Let me pour gold in the dark and not straw for the crowd. Keep me faithful in the small, steady things, week after week. Amen.

## Chapter 27. Write the Next Move

God, here's my page. I've planned my course. You set my steps. Give me a verb, a person, and a day, and the grit to get the bottom line done this week. Amen.

## Chapter 28. These Men Are My Brothers

Father, thank You for the men You've put beside me. Make us brothers who love at all times and show up in trouble. Don't let us quit meeting together. And give us the next man to bring to the table. Amen.


## A prayer for the table

Father, thank You for these men. Thank You for what was said tonight and for what was hard to say. Keep what we heard safe among us. Give each man the strength to do the one thing he named before we meet again. Watch over our homes, our work, and our roads. Bring us back next week, and bring the man who's missing. In Jesus' name. Amen.

## A prayer for the man who can't pray

God, I don't have the words. I'm tired, or angry, or ashamed, or just empty. You know all of it already. I'm here. That's all I've got today. Hold on to me until I can hold on to You. Amen.

## A prayer for the man starting over

Lord, I've been here before. I started strong and then I quit. I'm starting again. Thank You that Your mercies are new every morning and You haven't given up on me. Give me one good day, and then another. Amen.


## A prayer before a hard conversation

Father, I'm about to say something hard, or hear something hard. Guard my tongue. Make me quick to listen and slow to speak. Let me go first in humility, and let love be louder than being right. Amen.

## A prayer at a child's bedside

Lord, thank You for this child. Keep them safe tonight and all their days. Let them know they are loved by You and by me. Make me the kind of father they can trust. Amen.

## A prayer at the end of the week

God, here's my week. The good I did, I give back to You. The good I missed, I bring to You. The wrong I did, I confess to You. Thank You for carrying me through it. Give me rest, and get me ready for Tuesday. Amen.


## A prayer for a brother in trouble

Father, my brother is in a hard place. You know it better than I do. Hold him up. Give him rest, clear thinking, and the help he needs from the people around him. Show me what I can carry for him this week, and give me the faithfulness to keep showing up. Amen.

## A prayer before work

Lord, this work is from You and for You. Give me strength for it and honesty in it. Let me treat the people around me well, especially the ones who can do nothing for me. Let my work today be worth offering You. Amen.

## A prayer on a birthday

Father, another year. Thank You for every one of them, the good ones and the hard ones. Show me what this next year is for. Let me spend it well, on the people You gave me and the work You handed me. Amen.

## A prayer at a graveside

God, we come to the end of a life, and it hurts. Thank You for the man or woman we've lost and for every good thing You gave us through them. We trust You with them. Teach us to number our days, and to live the ones we have left with a heart of wisdom. Amen.
