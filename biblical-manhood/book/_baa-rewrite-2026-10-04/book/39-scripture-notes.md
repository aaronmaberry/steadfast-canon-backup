# Scripture Notes

*Each chapter opens with one verse quoted word for word from the New International Version. Everywhere else in the book, Scripture is told in plain words. These notes key each of those passages by a phrase from the page, so you can open your Bible and read the whole thing for yourself. The NIV permission notice is on the copyright page.*
