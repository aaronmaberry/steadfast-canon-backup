# About the Author

Wes Hart writes for ordinary men who are tired of waiting for a better season before they start living like men.

He is a husband and a father of two, and by day he leads teams in technology. The hours that matter most to him, though, are the ones he spends across a table from other men, whether that's at a kitchen table, in a garage, in a church classroom, or in a diner early on a weekday morning with a Bible open and the coffee going cold. Steadfast Men, the twenty-four-week training behind this book, started at one of those tables with a few skeptical guys, and it has since been taught in living rooms, church basements, and online to men in every season of life.

Wes never set out to write a book or build a men's program. He set out to stop being the passive husband and distracted dad he had quietly become. When he had nothing worth bringing to the table, a few older men kept a chair open for him anyway, and years later he started keeping chairs open for other men. He is not a pastor or a counselor, and he'll tell you so before you ask. He is a layman who has watched ordinary men, walking together under the Word of God, become the husbands, fathers, and brothers their homes and churches need, and he has seen it happen too many times to call it a theory.

When he isn't working or teaching, you'll usually find him outside with his kids, on a long walk with his wife, or sitting across from one other man who is trying to get honest about his Tuesday.

Learn more, find the full twenty-four-week class, or bring Steadfast Men to your church or group at **walksteadfast.com**.
