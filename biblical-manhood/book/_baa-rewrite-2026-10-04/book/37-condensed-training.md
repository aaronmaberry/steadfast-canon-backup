# Condensed Training

The chapters you just finished are the book. What follows is a short map of the first twenty-four weeks of the class, not an index of every chapter.

Each class week works through one chapter, and some chapters take two weeks, with a video teaching, a leader guide, and a participant sheet. The page for each chapter in Your Turn is the week's homework, and its four lines never change. Name it. Do this week. Tell a brother. Carry this.

If you're already in a group and want the outline on paper, use this page as a quick agenda. For the printable packets, the host scripts, and the full week-by-week order, including the weeks that cover the rest of the book, get the class guide at walksteadfast.com.
