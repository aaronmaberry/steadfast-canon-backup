# Dedication

For the men who pulled out a chair for me before I had anything worth setting on the table, and who kept it open until I learned to pull one out for someone else.

For my wife, who has watched me try to become the man in these pages and has never once pretended I had arrived. The true sentences here were lived where she could see them. She is the reason most of them hold.

And for our children, who are still watching. I hope you find your father in this book, and I hope you find him further along than he was when you were small.
