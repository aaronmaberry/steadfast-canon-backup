# 16. How that looks on a man

The week already told on you.

You did not skip a meeting. You skipped the house. The porch light was on when you pulled in. Someone had already eaten. You had a story about being needed. The people inside had a story about waiting. You called the first story duty. You did not ask about the second.

Or you won the room. You talked last. You decided. The other men went quiet and you took that for respect. You called it leading. It was you, taking the air so nobody else could.

Or someone asked who you are and you said the title. Not your name. Not whose you are. The job, as if the job were a person.

The inside has a shape. On a man it usually looks like this: skip the house, run the room, or wear the job as a name. That is this page. Not a theory about the heart. Not a missing vision. Not two ways of being a man. Not a marriage class. How it looks, in a week you already lived.

I have done all three. I have sat in a driveway with the engine off, telling myself five more minutes of mail would make me better company, and then walked in empty. I have taken a room because I could not stand not being the one. I have answered "who are you" with a role before a name. The week did not surprise me. It reported me.

There is a way that appears to be right, but in the end it leads to death. That's Proverbs 14:12.

:::margin
**Proverbs 14:12 NIV**
There is a way that appears to be right, but in the end it leads to death.
:::

Appears. That is the trap. Skipping the house appears as providing. Running the room appears as strength. Wearing the job appears as being a man who has his life together. The verse does not argue with how it looks at 3 p.m. It waits until the end. The end is not a mood. The end is where that way takes a son, a friend, a wife if you have one, and you.

Sometimes the shape wears a nice face. School. Money. Being the guy everyone likes after church. Fine. We are not doing a list of covers. The covers are not the diagnosis. The three are.

Skip the house. You can do this before you have a house. A 22-year-old skips the cheap apartment that is already his. He skips the people who would actually know if he came home. He skips the meal he said he would make. He is always elsewhere, even when the door is his. A married man does the same thing with a mortgage. He is current on the bills and late to his own table. This is not a seminar on how to love a wife. This is a man who treats presence like optional overtime. I have been that man. The work was real. The skip was also real. I wanted credit for the work and a pass on the skip. The house does not grade that way.

Run the room. You do not have to yell. You can do it with a joke that lands last. You can do it by restating what another man just said, only louder, so the idea now has your name. You can do it by deciding before anyone else has finished a sentence. It feels like courage. It is often hunger. I have left a meeting "won" and felt smaller, not larger, because I knew what I had done. A young man can run a friend group the same way. An older man can run a board and still be a boy in the chair. Yielding the room is not weakness. It is a man who does not need the air to prove he exists.

Wear the job as a name. This one flatters. The world pays you to do it. Ask a high performer who he is and watch how fast the title arrives. Ask him on a Saturday with the title off and watch how little of him is left in the room. A single man can already be this. A future wife is not the excuse and she is not the cure. You do not get to say you will become a person after someone marries you. You are already answering with the job. An older man can retire the title and still wear it. The tell is not hours. The tell is what you reach for when someone asks who you are.

You can feel more than one in your week. Pick the one that spent the most hours. Naming all three as a dodge is still a dodge.

This is not a verdict that your wound was fake. Hear that. Hurt can sit in a man and still not write every skipped night, every taken room, every title he hides in. Kind is not the same as letting the pattern keep the week.

If you're young, the pattern can start before a house. You do not wait for a lease to skip people. You do not wait for a title to wear a name that is not yours yet. A 22-year-old can name which of the three he lived this week. Waiting for a worse stage is how a young man stays unaccused.

If you're single, the job can already be the name. Do not draft a woman who is not in the room. She is not why you stay late, and she is not why you take the air. Name the one you lived. Then do the contrary act with the life you actually have.

If you're married, skipping the house is this page. Not a list of date nights. Not a talk about her. Go home. Be in the room you pay for. If running the room is yours, yield it at the table too. If the job is the name, tell her who you are without the title and mean it.

If you got hurt on the way here, I will go slow. The pattern is not proof that the wound was a story you made up. You can hold both. You were sinned against. You also skip, or run, or hide in work. Both can be true in one man. Only one of them is this page.

If you're older, the years can make the shape look like wisdom. A 62-year-old can skip a house that is quieter now and call it giving them space. He can run a room of younger men and call it mentoring. He can wear a title he no longer holds. The younger men need a man who will name which one he still lives.

If the work is going well, the title is the tell. The office will reward the skip, the run, and the name. It will not ask what the end of that way is. You can be trusted with a lot and still be a man whose week already told on him.

Name which of the three you lived this week. One. Then one contrary act. If you skipped the house, go home and stay. If you ran the room, yield it once on purpose and do not take it back. If you wore the job as a name, tell the truth about the job: it is work. It is not who you are. A 22-year-old can do that. A 62-year-old can do that. You do not have to retire the whole pattern by Friday. You have to stop calling the way that appears right a life.

Tell one man which of the three it was, and the act. If he only hears a theory, you will stay unaccused. If he hears the week, he can ask you next week whether the house, the room, or the name ran again. Let him ask.

The inside has a shape. Skip the house, run the room, or wear the job as a name. The week already told on you. Believe it.
