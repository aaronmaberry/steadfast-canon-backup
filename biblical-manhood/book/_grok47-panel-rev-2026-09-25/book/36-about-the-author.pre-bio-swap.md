# About the Author

Wes Hart is a husband, a father of two, and a Texan. His working hours go to leading teams in technology. His best hours go to teaching men what it means to be steadfast.

He did not set out to write a book or to run a men's training. He set out to stop being the passive husband and distracted dad he had quietly become. A handful of older men helped him by keeping a chair for him at their table every week. Years later he started keeping chairs for other men. Steadfast Men, the 24-week training this book grew out of, began at a kitchen table with a few skeptical guys. It has since been taught in living rooms, garages, church classrooms, and online, to men in every season.

Wes is not a pastor and he is not a counselor, and he will tell you so before you ask. He is a layman who believes ordinary men, walking together under the Word of God, can become the husbands, fathers, and leaders their families and churches are hungry for. He has watched it happen too many times to doubt it.

When he is not working or teaching, he is usually outside with his kids, on a long walk with his wife, or at a diner early on a weekday with a Bible, a cup of coffee, and one other man.

He and his family live in Texas.
