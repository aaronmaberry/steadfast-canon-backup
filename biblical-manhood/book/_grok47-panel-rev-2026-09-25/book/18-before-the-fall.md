# 18. Before the Fall

The packet on the fridge is doing more work than the row. Tomato. Full sun. A bare patch is only dirt until you know fruit was supposed to stand there. You cannot call the row a failure if you never read what it was for.

Under the marriage, the anger, and the boy, the men at my tables want the same thing. What was I built to be, before I bent it? Miss the design and you cannot tell a break from a style.

We go back to Adam. A blueprint. What God built when He built a man, before anyone fell. Every deep ache I have heard traces to this list.

## He was made, on purpose, in an image

Scripture's first word about you is not sinner. Sinner comes later. This comes first.

:::margin
Genesis 1:27 (NIV)
"So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them."
:::

In his own image. Before a single success and before a single failure, you were made to look like God. Nobody earns that. Male and female both carry it, the whole of it. Manhood is not a rung above your wife or your daughter. It is one way of carrying the same image, and the way was God's idea.

:::margin
Genesis 2:7 (NIV)
"Then the LORD God formed a man from the dust of the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living being."
:::

Formed, then breathed into. Hands in the clay, then a face close enough to give the clay breath. You were wanted. God came near.

## He was blessed and given a job

The first words He speaks over them are a blessing, and only then a commission.

:::margin
Genesis 1:28 (NIV)
"God blessed them and said to them, 'Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky and over every living creature that moves on the ground.'"
:::

Blessing first. Who you are, then what you do. The Father will speak that way again over Jesus at the Jordan. Then He hands the man a task big enough for a life.

Be fruitful. Fill. Subdue. Rule. Raw creation was supposed to become more in a man's hands. That is why a finished repair, a planted row, or a thing you built leaves you feeling like yourself. The feeling is the design.

Subdue and rule are not permission to bully. God rules by making things flourish. Domination is rule after the fall. Before the fall, rule looked like a garden.

## He was placed

Do not skip where He put him.

:::margin
Genesis 2:8 (NIV)
"Now the LORD God had planted a garden in the east, in Eden; and there he put the man he had formed."
:::

There he put the man. Adam was not released to wander. Place was part of the making.

You have a place too. Most men file their address under accident, or under waiting room. The dirt under your boots is a garden with your name on the assignment. A man staring at the next town never tends this one.

## He was given work, and told to guard it

:::margin
Genesis 2:15 (NIV)
"The LORD God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it."
:::

Work it. Take care of it. Two verbs, and the fall has not happened yet. The curse has not been spoken. The job already has dirt on it.

The curse did not invent work. It put thorns in work that was already good. A man with nothing real to do is not resting. He is misfiring. A layoff, an empty retirement, a body that can no longer do the old job, all of them knock a man sideways for this reason. The garden got taken, and he was made to work one.

The second verb means keep it, guard it. Worker and keeper are one man. Provider and protector were the same assignment before anyone sinned. Remember that. Something is about to come through the fence.

## He was given a word, and a line

God speaks to him with no woman in the garden yet.

:::margin
Genesis 2:16-17 (NIV)
"And the LORD God commanded the man, 'You are free to eat from any tree in the garden; but you must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, for when you eat from it you will certainly die.'"
:::

You are free. He says that first. Almost every tree is a yes. One tree is a no. A wide field, one bright line.

A man lives under a word he did not write. He receives it from Someone over him and stays inside it. The word is what makes a man possible.

He heard it from God's mouth, alone, before Eve was there. Keeping the garden from anything that would talk them past the line sat on him.

## He was given words of his own

Then God let him name.

:::margin
Genesis 2:19-20 (NIV)
"Now the LORD God had formed out of the ground all the wild animals and all the birds in the sky. He brought them to the man to see what he would name them; and whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name. So the man gave names to all the livestock, the birds in the sky and all the wild animals. But for Adam no suitable helper was found."
:::

Whatever he called the creature, that was its name. The naming was handed over.

Your words weigh something. A son leans toward the name you give him. A wife lives inside the names you use in the house. The few sentences a man does say land hard.

## He was told it was not good to be alone

:::margin
Genesis 2:18 (NIV)
"The LORD God said, 'It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper suitable for him.'"
:::

Not good. He had called the rest of it good. Then He looked at one man, perfect garden, real work, God's own voice, and He said not good.

The man had not sinned. He had God. God still said alone was not good. Needing people is the design, put there before anything broke.

The animals were not the answer. A dog is company. He needed a counterpart, like him and not him.

It is a marriage verse. It is also wider than a wedding. Brothers, a man ahead of you, a church. Single men are inside the sentence.

## He was given a bride, and he spoke

God put him to sleep, built the woman from his side, and brought her. The man spoke. Those words are the first human speech the Bible records.

:::margin
Genesis 2:23 (NIV)
"The man said, 'This is now bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called \"woman,\" for she was taken out of man.'"
:::

The first thing a man said was a poem about his wife. Ask most husbands for the last thing they said to her that was not a schedule, and they will have to think. The design has a man looking at his bride until speech comes out of him.

He leaves father and mother and holds fast to her. Marriage is in the blueprint before anyone falls. Holding fast was his job. Then the last line before the break.

:::margin
Genesis 2:25 (NIV)
"Adam and his wife were both naked, and they felt no shame."
:::

Seen, and not afraid. Nothing tucked away. That is a man before the fall. Every locked room since is a long attempt to get back to that garden.

## He was given rest

Men walk past this one.

:::margin
Genesis 2:2-3 (NIV)
"By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. Then God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done."
:::

God rested. Fatigue was not the reason. Rest belongs to good work. He set it in the week before the man lifted a tool, and He called the day holy.

You are not a machine. Six days of work, one day of stop. A seventh day of grinding is not a promotion. A man who never stops goes numb, and numb is not the holy day.

## The shape of a man

Image. Blessing before output. A real job. A place. Dirt to work and a fence to keep. A word, and words of his own. Company. A bride. No shame. A day to stop.

That is what you are for. Work it and keep it. Carry the word. Speak, name, go first. Remember the walk with God in the cool of the day.

## The blueprint in your house

Single, hear this. Nearly every line landed on Adam before a bride existed. A wedding does not mint a man. Work the plot. Guard it. Carry the word.

A baby in the house means you are inside be fruitful. Tired means you are on the blueprint, curse piled on blessing.

Divorced, it is not good for the man to be alone still stands, and I know that line can hurt. God did not rewrite the design, and He is not shoving you at the next marriage. Brothers. A man ahead of you. A church that knows your name.

Older, search Genesis 2:15 for a retirement clause. Abe read it after the paycheck ended and quit calling himself let go. Same job, he said. Different garden. Grandchildren, younger men, a wife who has carried plenty.

## What's coming

He was told to work the garden and keep it. The word about the tree is in his ear. A bride he sang over is beside him. Nothing is hidden.

Something is about to come through the fence. Next we will watch what he did, and what another Man did in a different garden when His hour came. Two men, two ways.

A garden, a word, a work, a bride, other people, a rest, a walk with God when the day cools. You were bent away from that shape. You are being bent back.

## Before you turn

Sit with it before the garden breaks. Formed by hand. Breathed on. Blessed before you had done anything. Placed, with dirt and a word and a day of rest. May the Father who made you in His image remind you tonight what you are for.
