Layout fit: in ch11, ch12, ch15 and ch26, the shorter openers let the Your Turn box move up a page, which left a new blank page before the next chapter. Not changed (body prose, not a closer): `03:88` "Why four...? Because they're one life...", `32:57` "Because the danger is real." (in "When the house empties"), and the mid-sentence "Because his dad stayed put" at `28:15`.
