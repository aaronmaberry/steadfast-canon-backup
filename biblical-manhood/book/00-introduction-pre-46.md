# Introduction

You picked this up for a reason. I won't pretend I know which one.

Maybe nobody ever sat you down and told you what a man is. Maybe they did, and it just doesn't match the man you're trying to be. Could be you're nineteen and already tired of guessing. Could be you're forty-two, the bills get paid, and you still feel unfinished. Or sixty, and you figured this talk had passed you by.

It hasn't. Sit down.

Most of us learned manhood the way some of us learned to drive. Somebody tossed us the keys, pointed at the road, and hoped we wouldn't hit anything. We borrowed what was close. Work. Sports. A version of our dad, or the opposite of him just to prove a point. That got a lot of us here. It's a weak way to finish.

God isn't confused about you. He made you on purpose. He isn't embarrassed by manhood and He isn't impressed by the knockoffs. There's no book in the Bible called Manhood. There's a story. A garden. A fall. A second Adam. A people. An ending. We're going to walk that, and we're going to tell the truth about the leftover stuff that still runs a regular week.

This is the line. Say it until you don't have to look down.

A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.

It's a lot. That's alright. It's also good news. You're not stuck as the boy you were, and you don't have to wait around for a feeling to show up.

I'm not above this. I know how easy it is to check out and call it peace. I know how easy it is to run a job and still lose the table. We're not doing that here.

Five parts. The man you were built to be. What you inherited, and what is still yours. The story that names you. The house. Then the long road. We take them one at a time.

I'm not here to roast you. The guy next to you isn't the joke. Getting married will not finish you. If you're single, your life already has a call on it. If you blew it, hope is real. If you're older, you still have a next move.

Get a pen. Find one man who will tell you the truth and not flinch. When we get to the plan, write it down.

Paul told a room of ordinary men to get on their guard, stand firm, be courageous, be strong, and do the whole thing in love. Not a slogan. A way to live.

**1 Corinthians 16:13-14 NIV**

Anyway. Here we go.
