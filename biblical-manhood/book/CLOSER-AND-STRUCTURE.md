# Closer and structure (craft only)

Looked at what sells. Do not copy their names or pages.

## What the shelf does

- Can't Hurt Me: a Challenge after almost every chapter. Men remember the homework.
- Atomic Habits: one job per chapter. The workbook is a second product. We keep the work in THIS book.
- Purpose Driven Life: one chapter a day, one sentence to carry, one question. 50 million. The 40-day frame is his, not ours.
- Wild at Heart: sold a second Field Manual. Men who buy one book should not need a second to do the work.
- Hughes: short chapter, questions, memory verse. Group-ready. We open each chapter with one NIV verse. Don't double it.
- Kindle: write-in lines are for print/PDF. On a Kindle the questions still work. Highlights are how they "write."

## What we run (locked unless Aaron changes it; B-A-A layout approved 2026-10-04)

**The chapter.** Title, one NIV anchor verse, then continuous prose. No subheads, no bold in the body, no lists. Longer connected paragraphs. Two to four quiet breaks (`:::break` / `:::`), set as a small diamond. No "Before you turn the page" section. No chapter-number cross references ("chapter 13", "ch. 4"); point to a person or idea instead.

**The ending.** The last paragraph is the quiet charge, a blessing or charge folded into the prose, and it ends on the chapter's Carry line, word for word.

**The work.** The four closer lines live at the back of the book in **Your Turn**, one page per chapter (`38-your-turn.md`):

1. **Name it**, one honest look at his actual week
2. **Do this week**, one move, written
3. **Tell a brother**, so he does not white-knuckle it (the last chapter uses **Be a brother**)
4. **Carry this**, the same sentence the chapter ended on

Each of the first three gets three write-in lines in print. Openers vary from page to page (a question, an instruction first, the reason last). Never open with "Because", and don't let any one opener word become the pattern.

The Write the Next Move page in Your Turn is followed by the sample man plan.

No second workbook. No 40-day brand. No numbered "Challenge #." No Field Notes.
