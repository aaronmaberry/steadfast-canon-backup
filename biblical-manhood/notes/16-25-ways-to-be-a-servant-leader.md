# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: 25 WAYS TO BE A SERVANT LEADER

- 
A servant-leader keeps leading and initiating, even when it doesn’t go well.

- 
A servant-leader stays alert to the “blessing-flow” within the family.

- 
A servant-leader accepts spiritual responsibility for his family by personally committing to his own spiritual growth and by investing in the growth of his family.

- 
A servant-leader is willing to say “I’m sorry” and “Forgive Me” to his family.

- 
A servant-leader lets his wife and children into the interior of his life.

- 
A servant-leader seeks to understand his wife and interacts out of that understanding.

- 
A servant-leader includes his wife in envisioning the future.

- 
A servant-leader frequently tells his wife what he likes about her and praises her often in public.

- 
A servant-leader prays with and for his wife on a regular basis.

- 
A servant-leader encourages his wife to grow as an individual and ensures time for his wife to pursue her own personal interests.

- 
A servant-leader discusses household responsibilities with his wife and makes sure they are reasonably distributed.

- 
A servant-leader provides financially for his family’s basic living expenses and consults his wife on all major financial decisions.

- 
A servant-leader keeps his family financially sound and out of harmful debt.

- 
A servant-leader makes sure he and his wife have drawn up a will and arranged a well-conceived plan for their children in case of death.

- 
A servant-leader follows through with commitments he has made to his wife and family.

- 
A servant-leader anticipates the different stages his marriage and his children will pass through.

- 
A servant-leader manages the schedule of the home and anticipates any pressure points.

- 
A servant-leader deals with distractions so he can talk with his wife and family.

- 
A servant-leader initiates meaningful family traditions and regular fun family-outings.

- 
A servant-leader takes the lead in establishing with his wife sound, biblically-supportable family values.

- 
A servant-leader takes the time to give his children practical instruction about life, which in turn gives them confidence with their peers.

- 
A servant-leader explains sex to each child in a way that gives them a wholesome perspective.

- 
A servant-leader joins a small group of men who are dedicated to improving their skills as a man, husband, and father.

- 
A servant-leader asks for help when he or his family is floundering.

- 
A servant-leader keeps starting over.

## QUESTIONS FOR REFLECTION OR SMALL GROUP DISCUSSION

- What are one or two things you could change right now that would make you a better servant-leader?

- Would your wife say she feels honored and valued as a “co-equal” and partner in your home? Why or why not?

- Would you be willing to plan a weekend getaway with your wife? What would stop you?
