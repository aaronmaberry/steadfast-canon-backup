# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: CONSEQUENCES OF AN “ABSENT” FATHER.

THE FATHER WOUND IS: An ongoing emotional, social, or spiritual deficit, ordinarily met in a healthy relationship with Dad that must now be overcome by other means.

- 
Describing your relationship with Dad.

- 
The Absent Father Wound results in the following:

- Anger – expresses the pain.

- Obsessions and Addictions – suppress the pain.

- Lostness: drifting through life

We all have father wounds and we cannot use them to excuse current bad behavior

-  What every Son needs from his Father:

- 

- 
Lifelong positive memories

- 
Direction with solid “why” answers.

- 
Conviction through modeling.

- 
His Father’s heart.

QUESTIONS FOR REFLECTION OR SMALL GROUP DISCUSSION

- Have you ever been wounded by an absent dad? In what way? Which of the four “results” of the Absent Father Wound could you identify with? Why?

- If you could ask you dad for one thing, what would it be? Explain.

- How would you describe your current relationship with your dad?
