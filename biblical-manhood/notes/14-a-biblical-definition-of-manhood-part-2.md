# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: A BIBLICAL DEFINITION OF MANHOOD (PART 2)

## III. THE FOUR DEFINING DIFFERENCES BETWEEN ADAM AND CHRIST.

- The first Adam fell into passivity; the second Adam rejected passivity.

- The first Adam disregarded his responsibilities; the second Adam accepts responsibility.

- For a will to obey.

- For a work to do.

- For a woman to love.

- The first Adam abandoned his post of leadership; the second Adam chose to lead courageously.

- He gave direction

- He gave protection

- He made provision

- The first Adam sought a great reward; the second Adam expected the greater reward – God’s reward.

- Hebrews 12:1,2

- Hebrews 11:24-26

- Corinthians 2:9

- 2 Timothy 4:8

- John 10:10

## IV. SO WHAT IS A REAL MAN WHEN CUT FROM A BIBLICAL MOLD?

A REAL MAN IS ONE WHO….

- REJECTS PASSIVITY

- ACCEPTS RESPONSIBILITY

- LEADS COURAGEOUSLY

- EXPECTS THE GREATER REWARD, GOD’S REWARD
