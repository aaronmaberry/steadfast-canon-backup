# Session #12

# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: Genesis and Manhood

- The Genesis “epic”

- The epic story of Genesis explains and measures manhood.

- It explains the original ideal and our ongoing fallenness.

- What Genesis Says About Manhood

- Genesis 1

- Male and Female value (Genesis 1:26-27)

- 
Shared image

- 
Equal value

- Male and Female calling (Genesis 1:28)

- Genesis 2-3

- Male and Female Distinctive

- God created Adam first. (Genesis 2:4-7)

- God gives Adam an occupation with responsibility. (Genesis 2:15)

- God instructed Adam about the responsibility of leading with His word. (Genesis 2:16-17)

- God instructs Adam to name the animals, a sign of leadership over creation. (Genesis 2:19-20)

- Adam is given a helper suitable for him, a phrase that defines the core social identity for both the man and the woman. (Genesis 2:18, 21-22)

- Adam names his helper. (Genesis 2:23)

- It is the man who is to leave and initiate a new household. (Genesis 2:24)

- The temptation seeks to reverse God’s original social and spiritual order. (Genesis 3:1-6)

- God holds Adam, not Eve, accountable for this transgression. (Genesis 3:8-9)

- Adam’s response to sin has an unacceptable passivity attached to it. (Genesis 3:11-12)

- Adam’s judgment is based on the reversal of the created order. (Eve’s curse is also based on her usurping God’s created order.) (Genesis 3:17)

- Adam’s judgment falls not just on him (Genesis 2:16-17), but on all those under his leadership. (Romans 5:16-18)

- Adam renames his wife as an additional sign of his leadership after the fall. (Genesis 3:20)
