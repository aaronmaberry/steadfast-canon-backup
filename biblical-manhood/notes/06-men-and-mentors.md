# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: MEN AND MENTORS

“Social, emotional, and spiritual blindspots and/or deficits caused by a lack of healthy male comradeship that results over time in foolish actions, serious setbacks, and a short-sighted masculinity.”

## I. EVERY MAN BENEFITS FROM THE COMPANY OF OTHER MEN.

- 
 The friendless American male.

- The significant downside to isolation.

- No challenge. (You develop a warped perspective on life.)

- No accountability. (You allow yourself privileges you wouldn’t allow others.)

- No cheerleaders. (You lose motivation for the critical areas of life.)

- No intimacy. (You lose transparency.)

- What the Scriptures have to say:

- Proverbs 27:17 “As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another.”

- Proverbs 18:24 “A man of many companions may come to ruin, but there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother.”

- Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 “Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their work: If one falls down, his friend can help him up. But pity the man who falls and has no one to help him up!”

- Hebrews 10:24 “And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds.”

- What you can do:

- 
Reach out to other men.

- Challenge a man at a deeper level.

- Pick someone you can enjoy life with.

## II. EVERY MAN BENEFITS FROM THE COMPANY OF A MENTOR.

- From Dad to whom?

- The difference between discipleship and mentoring.

- Discipleship focuses on instruction.

- Mentoring is about initiation.

- Characteristics of a mentor.

- Supports rather than competes with you.

- A cheerleader, not a critic.

- Helps develop your gifts while seeking to protect you from costly mistakes.

- One who encourages.

- One who admires and delights in you because he instinctively recognizes your value and untapped potential.

- Biblical examples of mentoring and mentors.

## QUESTIONS FOR REFLECTION OR SMALL GROUP DISCUSSION

- Who are the male heroes in your life?

- What men, other than your dad, made a significant, personal contribution to your life?

- Have you ever had a mentor? Explain.

- Is there a man (or men) who is watching out for you now?
