# Session #9

# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: WHAT DEPRAVITY MEANS SPECIFICALLY FOR MEN

A REVIEW OF WHAT DEPRAVITY MEANS, IN GENERAL:

- We are all dysfunctional by nature.

- Most of my real problems in life are inside of me, not outside of me.

- Depravity cannot be eradicated by education, environment, self-understanding, or will-power.

- We attempt to cover our depravity with all kinds of sophisticated masks.

- We must not trust ourselves.

- Admitting my depravity is the first step in finding God.

- Progress in godliness is marked by an increasing apprehension of the depth and extent of my depravity.

WHAT DEPRAVITY MEANS, IN SPECIFIC, FOR MEN:

- AN INTERPRETIVE METHOD.

- THREE PRINCIPLES DERIVED.

- Men have a natural tendency to avoid domestic responsibility.

- Men tend to rule wrongly over women and children.

- Men tend to get lost in careers and lose sight of their transcendent cause.

 

QUESTIONS FOR REFLECTION OR SMALL GROUP DISCUSSION

- What kind of mask has your depravity worn? Ex: my procrastination isn’t sin it’s just the “laid-back” aspect of my special temperament with which I was born.

- Which of the three masculine characteristics of depravity do you struggle with most? Explain.

- What personal insights have you gained about depravity and how it affects you?
