# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: A DEFINITION OF MANHOOD

## 

## I. CORE CONTRIBUTIONS OF MEN AND WOMEN FROM HISTORY AND THE SCRIPTURES

- Women are the nurtures of life. Where that core contribution is honored, society prospers.

- Timothy 2:15

- “Women are happiest, not when given position and power, but when the creative contributions of the maternal role are upheld.” –Margaret Mead

- Men are the bearers of destiny. As men go, so goes society.

- Genesis 2-3: The man was created by God with a unique social and spiritual leadership in mind.

- Defection from this leadership spells trouble not just for the man, but for everyone involved.

## II. TWO MEN/TWO MASCULINE IDENTITIES

- Adam and Jesus Christ

- Corinthians 15:45-49

- They are the leaders of two distinct spiritual destinies. Romans 5:12-19
