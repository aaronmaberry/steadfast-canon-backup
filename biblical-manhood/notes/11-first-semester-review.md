# Session #11

# BIBLICAL MANHOOD: FIRST SEMESTER REVIEW

## I.  PRESUPPOSITIONS AND PROMISES

- 
Presuppositions:

- Manhood is in a state of confusion.

- 
Confused men create major problems. 

- 
Confused men settle for less.

- There is no lofty vision of manhood that is compelling to men today.

- The Bible has insight and answers to all of the above.

- 
Promises for those who finish:

- You will have a clear definition of manhood.

- You will make some significant personal discoveries about yourself.

- You will have a personalized plan to achieving authentic manhood.

- You will make new friends who are pursuing a common goal.

II.  THE MANHOOD WOUNDS

- 
The Absent Father Wound

- 
Definition: An ongoing emotional, social, or spiritual deficit, ordinarily met in a healthy relationship with Dad that must now be overcome by other means.

- 
The Overly Bonded with Mother Wound

- 
Definition: An unhealthy emotional intimacy with mother that causes a son to either be threatened by the influence of women later on, or to over-identify with that influence

- 
The Disconnected from other Men & Mentors Wound

- 
Definition: Social, emotional, and spiritual blind spots and/or deficits caused by a lack of healthy male comradeship that results over time in foolish actions, serious setbacks, and a short- sighted masculinity.

- 
The Depravity Wound

- 
Definition: We are all fallen and cursed creatures at odds with our creator.

- 
The Lack of A Compelling Vision for Manhood Wound

- 
Definition: A stunted or perverse masculinity that prevails over a culture misdirecting masculine energies into destructive patterns.

 

## QUESTIONS FOR REFECTION OR SMALL GROUP DISCUSSION

Welcome any newcomers to the group and spend a few minutes getting acquainted.

- For those who are new – what are you hoping to get from your experience?

- For those who were here last semester – what was your single biggest take away? Have you done anything yet and what happened?

- Discuss how we, as a group, can best encourage you this semester.
