## Public description

Ten-session guide covering major Bible teaching specific to men, set in the big picture of redemption through Christ. Publisher: unpacks male identity — God-given role in creation, how the fall ruined it, and how restoration begins through "the man above all men," Jesus. Aims to free men from slavery to human expectations so they can live purposefully as men of God. Explicitly not aiming at political correctness but at Scripture. Leader's guide included. 88 pages.
