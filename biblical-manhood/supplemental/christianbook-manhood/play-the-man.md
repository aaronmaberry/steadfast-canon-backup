## Public description

Publisher: culture lost its definition of manhood; Batterson's mantra is "play the man" (from the voice Polycarp heard). Four-session DVD study unveils seven virtues of manhood, tells Polycarp and other stories, and offers practical ideas for discipling the next generation of men who put God first, family second, career third. Participant's guide: pre-meeting reading, video notes, discussion, personal reflection, journaling. 80 pages.

## Public ToC / lesson list

Four sessions (author site):

1. Rewilding
2. The Seven Virtues of Manhood
3. Be a Man!
4. Rite of Passage
