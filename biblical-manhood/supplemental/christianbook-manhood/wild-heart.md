**Do not confuse Eldredge's six stages with Hicks/Lewis Hebrew stages in session 22.** Different map. 24 wins.

## Public description

Companion to Eldredge's initiation-into-manhood book. Publisher: reaches "unfinished men" who lacked spiritual role models for a rite of passage; helps them name weaknesses, baggage, and uncertainties that block becoming the man and mentor they want to be. Six stages named on the product page: beloved son, cowboy ranger, warrior, lover, king, sage. Workbook: Scripture, personal stories, movie/book excerpts, questions. 256 pages. Personal or group.
