## Public description

NFL All-Pro / Hall of Famer Tim Brown uses his story (struggle with God, temptation, husband and father) to teach honor and integrity. Six-session video curriculum; study guide is the print companion. Publisher: challenges men of all ages to measure life and success by a higher standard than sport or career. Videos ~13–15 minutes each.
