## Public description

Cole's signature men's title, marketed as the CMN classic that "manhood and Christlikeness are synonymous." Christianbook: responsible male leadership is still lacking in many homes, churches, and businesses; the revised edition tackles pornography, adultery, television addiction, and immaturity, and presents a prophetic charge to honest manhood. The 112-page workbook tracks the companion book with fill-ins, questions, and a self-test per lesson. Publisher claims wide circulation (nearly two million copies of book plus curriculum, 40+ languages).
