## Public description

Volume 1 of 33 is a six-session study that adapts timeless truths from Robert Lewis's Men's Fraternity and adds new material on authentic manhood modeled by Jesus' 33 years on earth. Publisher copy says it starts with the current state of manhood and cultural confusion, then unfolds God's mandates for men and a definition of authentic manhood. Participants are told they will learn the four "faces" of manhood and how to anticipate and transition through seasons of life. Sessions run about 30 minutes plus group discussion.

## Public ToC / lesson list

Six sessions (public church listing of the volume):

1. Manhood Realities
2. Create and Cultivate
3. Manhood Definition
4. King / Warrior
5. Lover / Friend
