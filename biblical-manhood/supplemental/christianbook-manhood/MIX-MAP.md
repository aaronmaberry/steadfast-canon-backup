# Mix-map: commercial studies → the 24

Internal. The 24-session One Mission course is PARAMOUNT. These Christianbook manhood titles are **fill / illustration / structure sources only**. If a mix-in conflicts with the 24, the 24 wins. Do not paste lessons, workbooks, or DVD transcripts. Do not sell or reprint One Mission / Hawks / Lewis speech as-is.

**Skipped from the 23-result catalog:** True Woman 101 (Kassian / DeMoss) and Mujer Verdadera 101 — womanhood, not manhood.

**Closest cousin:** Lifeway **33 The Series** (Design / Story / Traps / Work in this catalog; Marriage and Fatherhood exist as vols. 5–6 but were not in the 23). Same family as Robert Lewis / Men's Fraternity. Session 21 of the 24 is titled *A Man and His Design*. Treat 33 as cousin curriculum, never a substitute.

Notes files live beside this map. Each notes file has title, author, year, public blurb, public ToC if posted, and a one-line fill map.

Clusters below follow FRAMEWORK.md (not a 24-for-24 swap).

## Wounds (sessions 01–07)

Father deficit and healing (02–03), mother wound and leaving (04–05), mentors and male comradeship (06–07). Intro (01) is the confusion diagnosis that opens the wound arc.

**Might supply extra illustrations or structure:**

- **33 Story** (Dad, Mom, Healing, All-Alone, Heart) — closest commercial story-work cousin for father/mother/isolation. Fill 02–07 / 03; do not replace Hall or Hawks.
- **Stepping Up** sessions 2–3 (boyhood / adolescence / mentor) — initiation and "ask, don't wait" texture for 06–07.
- **Wild Heart** — beloved-son / unfinished-men language for 02–03 and 06–07. Different stage map than session 22; keep it in the wound/mentor cluster, not the Hebrew journey.
- **Making of a Man** session 4 (forgives others) — extra story for 03's forgive / reconcile posture.
- **Kingdom Men Rising** (own story, get up, influencers) — light fill for 01 confusion and 06–07 "a man to run with."
- **Checkpoints** — father–son dialogue; use on the *sons* side (20) more than as adult wound-work, but the minefield metaphor can illustrate 02's "do not excuse present sin."

Cole / Evans / Allberry / Titus Ten / Play the Man are weaker here (they assume the man more than they diagnose dad/mom).

Shift from nurture to nature. Fallen, not "basically good." Masks. Education and willpower cannot eradicate it. Male patterns: avoid domestic responsibility, rule wrongly, lose a transcendent cause in career.

**Might supply extra illustrations or structure:**

- **33 Traps** (Idols, Empty Promises, Battle Plan, Control, Significance & Comfort) — mask-off / heart-idol structure next to 08–09. Fill only.
- **Maximized Manhood** — porn, adultery, immaturity as named public topics; illustrations for "most real problems are inside."
- **Courage** (Cole) — warfare-against-passivity rhetoric; keep it under 08–09's born-again requirement, not mere grit.
- **Strong Men in Tough Times** — cultural ideologies vs. better men; fill for "culture prefers basically good."
- **Titus Ten** Gospel / Character / Doctrine — extra Scripture scaffolding for new birth and inner life.
- **Man of God** (Allberry) — creation / fall / restored-in-Christ frame that matches 08–09 + 12.
- **Kingdom Men Rising** Dry Bones / Get Up — Ezekiel-style "not willpower" illustration; still subordinate to Hawks' depravity wound.
- **Checkpoints** — youth snares; use as younger-brother illustrations, not the adult theology.

Core (not comprehensive): women nurture life; men bear destiny. Two Adams. Four marks: reject passivity; accept responsibility (will to obey, work to do, woman to love); lead courageously; expect God's reward.

**Might supply extra illustrations or structure:**

- **33 Design** sessions 3–5 (Manhood Definition, King/Warrior, Lover/Friend) — **strongest commercial cousin to 13–14**. Same Lewis family. Fill examples of the four faces; do not replace the four marks.
- **Stepping Up** 1, 5–8 (courage, stand firm, initiative, wisdom, provide) — extra "reject passivity / lead courageously" stories (Lewis himself is on the videos).
- **Titus Ten** Identity / Authority / Character — fill, especially "bearer of destiny" without borrowing Smith's ten as a rival creed.
- **Kingdom Man** Life of a Kingdom Man / Greatness Is Your Destiny — destiny/responsibility illustrations.
- **Play the Man** seven virtues — illustration bank only; the 24 already has four marks.
- **Real Man** (Cole) — Christlikeness-as-manhood slogan as a parallel one-liner, not a fifth mark.
- **Courage** (Cole) — fill for "lead courageously."
- **Making of a Man** Takes Responsibility — story fill for the second mark.
- **Man of God** (Allberry) — Jesus as true man / two-Adams texture for 13.

Memorize line stays the 24's: *A real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.*

## How to use (book-writing rule)

1. Write from the 24 (transcripts, briefs, notes, FRAMEWORK.md).
2. If a chapter needs another story, a discussion shape, or a named cultural illustration, pull from the notes file's **public** blurb/ToC — then write original Steadfast copy.
3. Never reprint Cole, Evans, Eldredge, Rainey, 33, or Batterson lessons.
4. When 33 or Stepping Up (Lewis on camera) is used, credit Robert Lewis as the source Tim adapted, and keep the cousin label visible internally.
