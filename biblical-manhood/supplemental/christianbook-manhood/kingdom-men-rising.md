## Public description

Follow-on to Kingdom Man. Publisher: culture is confused about manhood and often calls masculinity toxic; Evans speaks a biblical purpose and future for masculinity. Men wrestle with their own stories and with landmark texts and figures, aiming at identity, responsibility, and leaving passivity. Named benefits on the product page include God-honoring sexuality, overcoming addiction/emotional setbacks, and becoming an active influence at home. Eight sessions; videos ~12 minutes each. 160-page study book.
