## Public description

Study guide to Cole's *Courage*. Publisher: a call to men who want to be champions, a challenge to young men, a cry for mature men to come alive again. Framed as physical and spiritual warfare against an enemy that would emasculate manhood, shame ambition, and produce passivity. Claims to train in manhood, responsibility, maturity, and warfare. Twelve sessions, 112 pages.
