## Public description

Smith distills ten foundations for godly men from Paul's short letter to Titus. Publisher: ten small-group studies to help a man mature as God designed — leading at home, church, and in the world; rooting identity in Christ; teaching godly dominion; and shoring up essential beliefs so he can stand in a wayward culture. Study book: 10 sessions, personal study between meetings, leader guide, and ten videos with the author (access included). 176 pages.
