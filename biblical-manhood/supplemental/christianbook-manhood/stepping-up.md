## Public description

FamilyLife: a ten-session small-group video series calling men to reject passivity and lead with courage. Christianbook: Rainey and other leaders unpack biblical manhood, define courage across five stages of a man's life (boyhood, adolescence, manhood, mentor, patriarch), and commission men to evaluate where they stand. Workbook correlates to the videos, with discussion questions and three days of follow-up exercises, plus a personal "Stepping Up Plan." Videos ~25–40 minutes; group time ~60–90 minutes total.
