## Public description

Youth pastor + U.S. Army officer. Aimed at young men (fathers/sons, pastors/students): adolescence in a truth-hostile culture is "crossing a minefield without a map." Eight-week Scripture-based guide with practical "force multipliers" to help guys recognize and avoid enemy snares, grow in faith, and become courageous warriors for God. Devotional style; about 15 minutes a day to prepare for small group. 176 pages.
