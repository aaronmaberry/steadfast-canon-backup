## Agreement and tension with the four marks

Legend: **Agrees** = usable mix-in beside the 24. **Tension** = different frame; keep, do not let it steer. **Conflict (24 wins)** = do not import as Steadfast definition or override sessions 13–14.

### 1. GotQuestions — five principles

**Agrees**

- Anti-passivity: “not… cowed and weak-kneed toward their families.” Adam “blamed for failing to lead her” (Gen 3:11, 17) matches session 12–14 (fall is passivity and blame).
- Accepts responsibility: humility/salvation, self-control of appetites, primary provision (1 Tim 5:8), spiritual leadership in the family.
- Leads courageously: protect (even to death, Eph 5:25; Neh 4), provide, lead through love/service/sacrifice (Eph 5). Anti-tyrant, anti-iron-fist — matches session 15 “not boss.”
- Jesus as pattern; equal worth / different roles.

**Tension**

- Definition is a **five-item family-role list**, not the four marks. Humility and appetite-control are real in the 24 (depravity 08–09; sexuality 18; will to obey) but they are **not** two of four definitional marks.
- Scope is almost entirely husband/father. The 24 *defines* a real man first (13–14), *then* applies to wife, sex, daughters, sons (15–20). Single men are barely in view here.
- Named examples (Daniel, Caleb, Joshua, Paul, Jesus) are not the 24’s two-Adams method.

**Conflict (24 wins)**

- Does **not** teach “expects the greater reward, God’s reward” as a mark. Do not drop mark four.
- Extra claims not in the 24’s definition: “women are never called to [fight to protect] their husbands”; “the Bible calls on men to provide and for women to care for the home.” Provision is a leadership function in session 14; do not harden it into a house/work split as Steadfast canon.
- Do not replace the memorize sentence with GQ’s five principles.

### 2. Bible Study Tools / Britt Mooney — five ways

**Agrees**

- Leadership tied to responsibility (Gen 2:15 work and keep; ownership of what God entrusts).
- Provision (1 Tim 5:8) beyond money; protection (Neh 4; Eph 6 armor; prayer as manly).
- Teach godliness (Deut 6; Eph 6:4) — sessions 19–20.
- Servant heart in marriage (Eph 5:25; 1 Pet 3:7 co-heir; prayers hindered) — sessions 15–17.
- Anti-domination; protection that is not control.
- Single men still “lead and bear burdens in the church.”

**Tension**

- Headship glossed as “alignment, walking with another intentionally, as a partner.” The 24: head = servant leader; **not 50/50**; helper is exalted partner for the *mission*, not a flattening of headship.
- Eve / Proverbs 31 as co-providers is compatible with Cindy session 16 “provide core costs” only if male responsibility to steward is not erased.

**Conflict (24 wins)**

- **Flag:** “The Christian life isn’t masculine or feminine. Accountability, community, spiritual disciplines, living and passing on a mission-oriented life – none of these depend upon being married or male or female.” Session 13 teaches **core contributions**: women nurturers of life; men bearers of destiny; unique male social/spiritual leadership (Gen 2–3; Isa 3). Shared Christian practices exist; they do **not** cancel sexed calling. The 24 wins.
- Missing marks: reject passivity (named), expect God’s reward.
- Do not use Mooney’s five ways as the definition.

### 3. Unforsaken — godly man study

**Agrees** (closest mix-in on marks 1–3)

- **Reject passivity (named):** “Adam was not made for passivity.” “reject both passivity toward evil and the counterfeit strength of aggression.” “He does not use strength to control others or hide behind weakness to avoid responsibility.”
- **Accept responsibility:** no blame-shift (Gen 3:12); confess; repair; will to obey (Josh 1; 1 Kgs 2:2–3 “prove yourself a man” = keep the charge); work (*abad/shamar*); woman to love as sacrifice not coercion (Eph 5:25).
- **Lead courageously:** strength protects rather than preys; courage submitted to the Word; 1 Cor 16:13–14 courage *and* love.
- Jesus as pattern *and* Savior; manhood not earned by impressive performance — matches 08–09 born-again, 14 Jesus as second Adam, 24 gospel invitation.
- Mentors / church / not solitary — sessions 06–07.
- 1 Tim 5:8 not a club against the unemployed/disabled — pastoral caution; 24 still holds provision as a leadership function.

**Tension**

- Standing with God “not earned by” husband/father roles; unmarried/childless/disabled can walk faithfully. True of the 24’s *definition*, which is for a man as such. Do not use this to mute 15–20 application.
- Male church office (1 Tim 3 / Titus 1) is extra to the 24’s arc (the 24 is home/mission/legacy, not elder qualification).
- Abuse: call civil authorities; Scripture never requires remaining accessible to violence. The 24 does not contradict; it also does not develop this. Keep as mix-in caution, not a rewrite of 15–17.

**Conflict (24 wins)**

- Does **not** install mark four (God’s reward / Heb 11–12 / 2 Tim 4). Do not let “lordship / obedience” swallow “expects God’s reward.”
- Do not substitute Unforsaken’s conclusion paragraph for the memorize sentence.
- Two-Adams *masculine destinies* method stays the 24’s, even though this study uses Adam and Christ.

### 5. Lifeway / Bob Bunn — five handles for leaders

**Agrees**

- Eden damaged manhood; recovery begins with salvation — 08–09, 12–14.
- Jesus as the example, defined by **sacrifice** not power/wealth (Mark 10:45) — session 14 lead courageously / Eph 5.
- Provision (1 Tim 5:8) financial, emotional, physical, spiritual; includes aging parents.
- Mentors answering “So what?” — 06–07.
- Internal transformation over “outward activity” / busyness — session 09 lost-in-career, 10 stunted vision, 21 feelings-are-dashboard.

**Tension**

- Written to **ministers** about men’s ministry, not a definition of a real man.
- Five handles: salvation, identity, service, sacrifice, provision. Overlap marks 2–3 only in part.
- Identity = remaining imago Dei. True, but session 13 adds sexed core contributions (nurture life / bear destiny). Incomplete if used alone.
- State of Discipleship comparisons (men vs women on mentoring, sharing faith, Bible study, prayer) are Lifeway research, not 24 teaching.

**Conflict (24 wins)**

- Do **not** replace the four marks with Bunn’s five handles.
- Missing: reject passivity as a named mark; expect God’s reward as a named mark.
- Sequence difference is not a conflict (24 diagnoses wounds *then* defines); do not let “salvation first as the definition” skip sessions 01–12.

### 6. Cru / Dennis Rainey — five themes (*Stepping Up*)

**Agrees**

- Stand the post in the storm; do not abandon duty — **adjacent to reject passivity** and lead courageously (session 14 “abandoned his post”).
- Provide (1 Tim 5:8), including emotional/spiritual; father trains children for culture’s currents — 19–20.
- Protect physically (Piper/Grudem 3 a.m. bat) and by passing wisdom (Prov 4) — 14 protection; 20 sons.
- Serve *and* lead (Eph 5:23, 25); servant-leader; needs of wife and children first — 15–17, Cindy 16.
- Sexual self-control; not defined “below the waist”; protect women and children — session 18.
- Micah 6:8 walk humbly — will to obey.

**Tension**

- Parallel popularization (FamilyLife, 2011), historically near the same complementarian men’s-ministry world as Lewis, **but it is not the Lewis/Hawks definition.**
- Almost entirely husband/father (“stand guard over… my wife and children”).
- Theme 1 (tame passions) is 24 session 18 content, not a definition-level mark.

**Conflict (24 wins)**

- **Flag:** Do not swap Rainey’s five themes for the four marks. “Stepping up” / “never off duty” is mix-in language, not the memorize sentence.
- Missing mark four (expects God’s reward). Rainey’s “motivated and empowered to step up” is God-relationship, not the 24’s judgment-seat / joy-set-before-him reward.
- Soldier/Tomb of the Unknowns illustration is Rainey’s, not the 24’s (24 uses Abel-field vs Garrett reciting the definition).

### 7. Bible Hub — topical + concordance

**Agrees**

- Adam work/keep (Gen 2:15); stewardship and responsibility.
- Eph 5:23 leadership as love/sacrifice/service.
- Strength and courage (Josh 1:9).
- Fatherhood (Eph 6:4); provision (1 Tim 5:8).
- 1 Cor 13:11 put away childish ways — used in the 24’s key-scripture list.

**Tension**

- Unsigned compilation; “comprehensive guide” overclaim vs the 24’s core-not-comprehensive warning (session 13).
- Role models (Abraham, Moses, David, Paul) are not the two-Adams method.
- Webster: manhood = adult male, or courage. Fine as dictionary; not the four marks.

**Conflict (24 wins)**

- **Flag:** Concordance “manhood” is often **not** male discipleship:
  - Eph 4:13 / Heb 6:1 “mature manhood” = the church growing into Christ.
  - Heb 11:24 Moses “grew to manhood” = became an adult (the 24 *does* use Heb 11:24–26 for **reward**, Moses looking ahead — different use than this hub’s WEY line).
  - Ps 105:36 “manhood” = virility/firstborn; Hos 12:3 Jacob’s adulthood.
  - Library “Godhead and Manhood” = Christ’s human nature.
- Do not let this page redefine “manhood” for Steadfast Men.
- Missing all four marks as a set; missing reject passivity and expect God’s reward as marks.

## Cross-cutting flags (24 wins every time)

1. **Definition lock.** The memorize sentence stays Lewis/Hawks four marks. No five-principle (GQ/BBL/Rainey/Mooney) or five-handle (Bunn) list replaces it.
2. **Mark four is the orphan.** None of the seven install “expects the greater reward, God’s reward.” Mix-ins stop at duty, sacrifice, or lordship. Keep Heb 12:1–2; 11:24–26; 2 Tim 4:8; John 10:10 in the definition.
3. **Two Adams stay the method.** Mix-ins name Jesus as pattern; only the 24 (and Lewis) squeeze fallen Adam vs Jesus into two *masculine destinies* and four contrasts.
4. **Family-role vs man-as-such.** Most mix-ins define manhood as husband/father duties. The 24 defines a real man, then applies. Do not make marriage the definition.
5. **Sexed calling.** Mooney’s “Christian life isn’t masculine or feminine” does not override session 13 core contributions. Image-of-God equality (GQ, Bunn, Unforsaken) is already in the 24 (Gen 1) and does not cancel unique male leadership.
6. **`04` is derivative.** Use `01` if quoting the five-list.
7. **Bible Hub is a lexicon trap.** Do not preach Eph 4:13 “mature manhood” as the four marks.
8. **Credit always.** These are their sentences. Steadfast copy is original later, blending Ted Cunningham / Red Rock *style* with 24 *content* — not these articles pasted.
