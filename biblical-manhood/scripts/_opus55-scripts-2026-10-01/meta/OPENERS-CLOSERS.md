# Openers and closers registry

Generated from the scripts by `_work/registry.py`, then checked by hand.
- **Opener:** the welcome, the first lines until the first paragraph break.
- **Recap:** the first line that turns to the previous session. W01 has none.
- **Closer:** the first line of the last section.
- **Send-off:** the last two lines.

No opener, recap line, closer or send-off is repeated across the 24 training sessions (S01-S04 are shelved).
None reuses the locked lines in `rules/LOCKED-SESSIONS.md` or says "look who's back".
