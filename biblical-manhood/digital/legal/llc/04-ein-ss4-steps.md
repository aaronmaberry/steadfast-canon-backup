## Warn against paid EIN mills

The IRS issues EINs at **no cost**. Paid “EIN filing,” “Federal Tax ID now,” pop-up ads, and formation-company EIN add-ons are **not** the IRS. Typical mill tells:

- Charging $49–$250+ for a number the IRS emails/shows for free
- Applying **before** state formation (IRS says form the entity first)
- Using a **nominee** as responsible party (IRS: nominees are **not** authorized; privacy/risk)
- Calling from a lookalike URL that is not IRS.gov

**Do not buy those. Do not let Bizee / LegalZoom / anyone upsell EIN as if it were required.** If a human wants help, use the official Assistant or a CPA/attorney who files **Form SS-4** as a disclosed third-party designee — still **$0 to the IRS**.

## Online (preferred) vs paper SS-4

**Online (U.S. applicants):** IRS.gov EIN Assistant. Immediate assignment if the session completes. Print/save the notice at the end. The responsible party must have an SSN/ITIN/EIN. **One EIN per responsible party per day.**

**Fax:** Form SS-4 to **855-641-6935** (domestic, per IRS EIN page). About **4 business days** if a fax number is provided. IRS no longer returns a notated SS-4; they fax a cover sheet with the EIN.

**Mail:** Form SS-4 to Internal Revenue Service, Attn: EIN Operation, Cincinnati, OH 45999. About **4 weeks**.

High inventory can delay fax/mail. Check IRS “Processing status for tax forms.”

## After the EIN issues

- Store the notice offline in company records. **Do not commit the EIN to public GitHub or the public site.**
- Wait up to **2 weeks** before the EIN works for some IRS e-file / EFTPS / TIN-matching uses. Banking and paper filing can usually use it immediately (IRS: “When you can use your EIN”).
- Texas Comptroller franchise / Public Information Report cycle is **separate** (typically due **May 15**). Most small LLCs currently owe **$0** franchise tax under the no-tax-due threshold but still have a **PIR** filing. Confirm current Comptroller rules at https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/ when the entity exists. **Do not file Comptroller papers from this pass.**
- FinCEN beneficial-ownership (BOI) reporting: Treasury/FinCEN final rule effective **14 August 2026** permanently exempts **U.S.-formed** entities from CTA BOI reports (Treasury release https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0603). A Texas LLC owned by a U.S. person should have **no FinCEN BOI filing** under that final rule. Counsel of record should still confirm at time of formation in case the rule changes.
