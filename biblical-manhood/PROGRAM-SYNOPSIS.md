## Biblical Manhood - Fall 2025

**Biblical Manhood: Rediscovering God’s Design for Men**

God created manhood with a specific purpose, and it’s time to reclaim it. In a world confused about what it means to be a man, this study is here to help you rediscover God’s clear and powerful vision for your life. In this first semester, we explore the societal, familial, and personal rhythms that contribute to that confusion and look to God and His Word for clarity in how we are called to live and lead.
