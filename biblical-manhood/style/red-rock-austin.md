### House style (how Red Rocks Austin sounds when any of them preach)

They sound like **one church**, three accents.

1. **Welcome is liturgy.** Almost every message re-says imperfect people, perfect God, you’re welcome. Lane Murray and online/other campuses get love from the room.
2. **Thesis is a tweet, sermon is a story.** Culture diagnosis (FOMO, control, self-help, ick, TikTok Bible-doubt) → one image → one text → one step.
3. **Friendship is the brand.** They preach with and about each other (jet ski with Ryan and Ethan; whiteboard in Ryan’s office; “Ethan tried to do this last week”). Afterthoughts is the same three guys without slides.
4. **Seeker-friendly without dropping a claim.** They will say porn, divorce-for-happiness, self as God, and “good people don’t go to heaven” (Afterthoughts title; not heard). They will also laugh, under-promise the map, and refuse extra shame.
5. **Presence > plan.** God’s will is proximity, a next step, and not missing the life you already chose. That is Doug’s book, Ryan’s Abraham, Ethan’s “follow me.”
6. **Excellence + health.** Production is high; they will cut a Saturday service to keep breathing room for the Spirit and the lobby. “You can’t build a healthy church on exhausted people.”
7. **Books in the lobby, money to the prison campus.** Ryan and Doug both treat writing as extra space the sermon clock doesn’t allow; proceeds talk is mission, not hustle-flex.

Shawn Johnson’s **network** weekend openers (Wave notes, not Austin-channel ASR): high-energy “high five somebody,” shout-outs to Denver / Austin / Brussels / correctional facilities, “messed up imperfect people.” That is the **family** sound. Austin weekends are a slightly more conversational, coffee-shop version of the same church.

## Do / don’t for Steadfast Men writers

Blend this **onto** the Steadfast spine (the four marks: Stop Waiting, Pick Up the Weight, Go First, Work for God's Pay; wounds; Genesis manhood; man plan). Do not replace the 24-week spine with a Red Rocks weekend.

### Do

- **One image, one thesis, one next step.** Jam display, jet ski, weight vest, lamp for the feet — then sit. Our sessions can open like that **with original Austin/Hill Country/work/dad images**, not theirs.
- **Self-own before the room owns.** Control, phone, FOMO, not praying with your wife, livestream-of-24. The preacher is in the diagnosis. Fits Hawks’s honesty; keep it.
- **Welcome the imperfect without sanding the call.** Their liturgy of welcome can sit in front of Lewis’s depravity sessions **if** session 08–09 still names sin. Do not skip the wound because “you’re welcome just as you are.”
- **God’s will as a Person and a step**, not a secret career code. Useful for men who overthink calling. Pair with Lewis’s “masculine vision” so “start with one” does not become aimlessness.
- **Servant-lead like Christ (Ethan’s Ephesians 5 close)** as **color** on sessions 15–17, in original words. Lay-down-life, go first, don’t make her your mom, don’t make him your project.
- **Single men in the room.** Ryan’s voice is the reminder: not every man has a wife waiting at home. Write applications that work for the unmarried without making marriage the only finish line.
- **Culture in the illustration, Bible in the authority.** FOMO, TikTok, dopamine, ick — then Proverbs, Jesus, Ephesians. Do not let the TED-adjacent research *become* the text.
