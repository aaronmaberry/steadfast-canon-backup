## The four marks (renamed 2026-10-01, Aaron; supersedes all earlier wording)

A steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

1. **Stop Waiting** (James 4:17). When it's yours to do, start now, before you feel like it.
2. **Pick Up the Weight** (Galatians 6:5). Carry what's yours, even when somebody else dropped it.
3. **Go First** (John 10:4). Step out ahead of your people, scared or not.
4. **Work for God's Pay** (Colossians 3:23-24). Do it for the Lord's reward, not the room's applause. "Pay" is the inheritance the Lord gives his children (Col 3:24), never wages that earn salvation (Eph 2:8-9). Say that plainly wherever the theology needs it.

Same four principles, same order. Keep the label "four marks" and "No fifth."
