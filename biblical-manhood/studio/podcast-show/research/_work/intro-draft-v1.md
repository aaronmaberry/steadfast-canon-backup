# Intro system, draft v1 (for critique)

## Beats, fixed order, every episode
1. Show name "Walk Steadfast", said once, by whoever leads.
2. Wes Hart plus one true credential or life fact (tables with men 20+ years, Hill Country, married 27 years, three grown-ish kids, wrote the book). Never his job, town, wife's name or kids' names.
3. Jonathon plus one true life fact (Dani, Ruby 7, Theo 4, San Antonio, HVAC sales manager, came to faith at 29, coaches coach-pitch, Thursdays with Wes for seven years).
4. What the show is. Mains: following Jesus as a man, plain talk, twice a week. Field Notes: the Friday report on Tuesday's challenge.
5. The Steadfast offer. The book, the video training and walksteadfast.com, all three, every episode, in new words. Present tense, descriptive. No "out now," dates or prices.
6. A today hook, 14 words or fewer. Points at the episode question. Doesn't spoil the cold-open payoff, doesn't repeat the title, doesn't steal the Pull up a chair line.
7. The slate (unchanged, Aaron's rule): number, date, title. Starts with the man who didn't say the last intro line.

## Rules
- Length. Mains 45-62 words before the slate (about 20-26 seconds). Field Notes 40-56. E001 up to about 75 with launch wording.
- Four or five lines, alternating. Who leads varies. The show-name position varies (first line most weeks, second line sometimes).
- No banter exchange in the intro. At most one dry line of personality.
- No "welcome back," no "hey guys," no "on today's episode."
- Lower thirds on each man's first line, as now.
- Anti-repetition lint. No 5-gram shared by any two intros (locked tokens collapsed). No 4-gram in more than 3 intros. First three words not repeated within 6 episodes.
- Progression. Season 1 explains the rhythm (Tuesday long, Friday report, the challenge). Midyear tightens. Fall is the leanest and most familiar, with a rare "since January" touch, but every beat stays.

## Samples

### E001 (Tue Jan 5, launch) The Man You Keep Saying You'll Be
Cold open: Jonathon took 1604 home at 6:15 for two years to miss bath time.
- WES: This is Walk Steadfast. I'm Wes Hart, and I've sat at tables with men for twenty-some years.
- JONATHON: I'm Jonathon. Dani's husband, Ruby and Theo's dad, sales manager in San Antonio.
- WES: Starting today, twice a week, we talk plainly about following Jesus as a man.
- JONATHON: Steadfast is Wes's book, a video training for men, and this table. You'll find it all at walksteadfast.com.
- WES: And today we start where Jonathon just did. On the long way home.
- Slate: JONATHON "Episode one." WES "Tuesday, January fifth, twenty twenty-seven." JONATHON "The Man You Keep Saying You'll Be."

### E002 (Fri Jan 8, Field Notes) The First Check
Cold open: Wes texted his oldest "How's your Tuesday?" and got "Are you dying?"
- JONATHON: It's Friday, which means Field Notes on Walk Steadfast. I'm Jonathon. Ruby's seven, Theo's four, and my wife works nights.
- WES: And I'm Wes Hart. I wrote the Steadfast book after a lot of years listening to men.
- JONATHON: Tuesday we gave you a challenge and took it ourselves. Friday we tell you how it actually went.
- WES: The book and the video training are both at walksteadfast.com.
- JONATHON: Today, who put the phone down at supper, and who didn't.

### E013 (Tue Feb 16) The Prize That's Gone by Thursday
Cold open: the Monday 8 a.m. ranked sales email.
- WES: Walk Steadfast. I'm Wes Hart. Twenty-seven years married, three kids, a lot of Thursday breakfasts with men.
- JONATHON: And I'm Jonathon. I manage a sales team, so somebody ranks me every Monday.
- WES: Twice a week we sit here and talk plainly about following Jesus as a man. Tuesdays run long. Fridays we check our work.
- JONATHON: If you want more than this table, there's Wes's book and the Steadfast video training. Both live at walksteadfast.com.
- WES: Today is about pay. The kind that runs out, and the kind that doesn't.

### E040 (Fri May 21, Field Notes) The Ask Report
Cold open: Wes asked the 81-year-old mechanic how you get old without getting small. "No, thank you."
- JONATHON: Field Notes. Walk Steadfast on a Friday. I'm Jonathon, and I sell air conditioning in Texas, so May is my busy season.
- WES: I'm Wes Hart. Hill Country. I've been sitting at men's tables for twenty-some years.
- JONATHON: Tuesday we each had to ask an older man one real question. Fridays we tell you how that went.
- WES: There's more of this in the Steadfast book and the video training, at walksteadfast.com.
- JONATHON: And today, what you do when the man you ask says no.

### E079 (Tue Oct 5) Grief at the Table
Cold open: a man whose dad died went to work Monday.
- JONATHON: This is Walk Steadfast. I'm Jonathon, and I've sat across from this man most Thursdays for seven years.
- WES: I'm Wes Hart. The Steadfast book came out of what men told me at tables like this one.
- JONATHON: Twice a week we talk about following Jesus as a man, on the regular days and the hard ones.
- WES: The book and the video training are at walksteadfast.com, whenever you're ready.
- JONATHON: Today is about what men do with a loss when nobody's watching.
