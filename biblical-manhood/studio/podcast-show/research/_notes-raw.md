- Secondary (blogs, treat as practitioner opinion): one primary CTA, 20-30 s outro, payoff first then ask; spoken CTA must match end-screen element. https://www.versely.studio/blog/end-screen-after-the-payoff ; https://www.automateed.com/podcast-outro-call-to-action-ideas
