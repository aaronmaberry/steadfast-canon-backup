# Wardrobe plan, E001-E104

Internal. Draft 2026-10-02. Aaron's rule: a new outfit every episode. The pieces repeat across the year the way a real man's closet does, but no two episodes match on both hosts. Colors stay inside the navy, gold and bone set, with warm earth tones. No logos, no slogans, no hats at the table, no crosses on clothing.

**Continuity rules**
- Tuesday and Friday of the same week are two different days, so they get different outfits.
- Wes always wears the same plain steel watch and a wedding band. Jonathon wears a wedding band and a cheap black digital watch (Ruby picked it).
- Wes's reading glasses are pushed up on his head in about one episode in three. Note it in the script.
- Holiday episodes take the holiday note in the Notes column.
- Props on the table every episode: two mugs (Wes uses Mr. Abernathy's old white diner mug, Jonathon a navy travel tumbler), one water glass each, one Bible (Wes, worn brown cover), Jonathon's phone face-down until he flips it once, which is a gag in E001.
