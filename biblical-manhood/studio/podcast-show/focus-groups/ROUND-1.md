## What landed (keep)

- **The cold opens.** Every panelist named one. "There's no traffic on 1604 at six-fifteen" (E001), "Are you dying?" (E002), "Daddy, what's a man? ... You're medium" (E003). Dana: "These are real cold opens, not teasers. They earn the first 30 seconds."
- **Accountability on air** (Check the Box). Ben: "I'd come back Friday just to see if Wes called his brother." Dana: "That's your retention engine across episodes. Protect it."
- **Wes telling on himself.** The recliner, "pay the bills and don't make trouble," the gutter, "I'll get to it." Trevor, the skeptic: "The old guy keeps losing. That's why I believe him."
- **Wives and mothers.** Rachel: "Dani is the smartest person in every story, and Wes's wife is never a nag. Thank you." The mother storyline (the hip, physical therapy, the brother) is handled with care.
- **Real disagreements.** "Loud, not quiet" (E003, Jonathon wins), which mark is hardest (E005, unsettled), the double standard about his dad (E007, unsettled). Dana: "Leaving them unsettled is the right call."
- **The standing outro and sign-off.** Nobody found it canned. Ben liked that the sign-off is born on air in E001 ("Is that ours now?").
- **Gary on E005's widower beat.** "'The room's mostly gone.' That's my life. Nobody's said that to me."
- **Kevin on E005's divorced-dad beat.** "'The fun parent is room pay.' Ouch. True."
