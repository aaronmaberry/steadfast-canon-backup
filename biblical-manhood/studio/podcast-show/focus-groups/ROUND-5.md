## The read-through first (executor, before the panel)

I read E017-E024 end to end as a listener would, not as lint. These are the things I found and fixed before the panel saw the scripts.

- **Canon slip, E023.** The draft said Jonathon hadn't "seen" his dad since he was nine and that a fish was "all I know about my father." Canon (E001) is that his dad calls about twice a year from Lake Charles. Rewritten: "Fifty-some phone calls in twenty-eight years, and I learned more from that picture than from all of them." "Back in February" is now "back in January," because the request aired in E006 (Jan 22).
- **Canon slip, E023.** The twin who struck out was the one *not* scared of the ball, but that trait belongs to Harper (E021). She's now the twin who asked to bat with her eyes closed (E021), so "I closed my eyes" pays off a setup the audience already has. The team is twelve kids, mixed, not "eleven girls."
- **Canon slip, E024.** Wes said he and his wife had planned to start praying together in January. Canon (E006) is that they don't, after about four tries. Fixed.
- **Templating: the mechanic as the over-60 example.** He was the "it's not too late" proof in E014, E021 and the E023 draft. E023 now uses Wes's mother praying out loud at seventy-eight ("She stepped out from behind us").
- **Templating: the ask tics.** "What'd she say?" / "What'd you do?" / "Then what?" came up four to six times an episode. Varied in E017, E019, E023 and E024 ("Until." / "I can see where this is going." / "Did you go?" / "How'd he take it?" / "And you said?" / "And?").
- **Templating: the deadpan-reply rule (round 4) was broken in E020.** The episode had three: Mom's "baby about his knees," the therapist's "You'd be surprised how often I hear that," and the brother's "Is Mom okay?" The therapist now answers plainly ("Tell me when it's too much, and I'll stop").
- **Templating: "the family rule."** It ran in E020 and E021 back to back. E021's is cut. Wes now says, "I'm going to have to stop asking people for things in that voice."
- **Stage-direction density.** E023 had 16 "(beat)" cues and E024 had 13, enough to make the read feel metronomic. They're down to 10 and 9. Rule below.
- **Field Notes running order.** E018, E020 and E022 all went cold open, check, question, tool. E024 (Good Friday) runs question, tool, check, review.

## Theology read (executor)

- **E023.**
  - 1 Corinthians 15:45: "last Adam" is read as "no third man," which is the plain sense of Paul's two-humanity contrast.
  - John 18:8 is substitution in miniature, and the script says outright that "we don't save anybody. He did that part. Once. All of it."
  - Going first is following, not earning.
- **E024.** "It is finished" is the work completed, not defeat. "He bows" is in the text. Ruby's "mean people" answer is plain substitution in a seven-year-old's words. Sunday is held until the outro.
- **E021.** "Does God hate me?" is answered with grace before any plan. The plan is to confess to a man, with no shame language.
- **E019.** Anger itself isn't sin (Eph 4:26, quoting Ps 4). The script draws the line at physical harm, with the hotline.
