# The Budget Talk
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 84 · October 22, 2027

A young father in a school pickup line said every money conversation in his house turns into a fight. It came out that he opens the credit card bill at ten at night, alone, and walks into the bedroom with it in his hand. The fight isn't about the money. It's about the ambush.

> "Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed."
> Proverbs 15:22 (NIV)

A man who plans his household money alone isn't carrying his duty. He's leaving out the best adviser in his house. After twenty-six years of paying every bill himself, Wes handed his wife the register, and she said, "Nobody's ever asked me to look."

## The monthly money date

Pick the same night every month, never the night a bill comes. Make it a real date. Start with one good line, then each of you brings one line of your own. Look at what came in, what went out and what's coming, and end with one decision.
