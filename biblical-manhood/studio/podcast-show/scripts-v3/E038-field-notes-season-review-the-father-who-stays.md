# Field Notes: Season Review: The Father Who Stays
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 38

Jonathon's nephew sent one line by text. "what did yall learn about dads this season. asking for me."

## He got the man

Wes's father said "proud" to him once, from a hospital bed. Wes carried that word for nine years, sure he was the son his father was proud of, the one who left and made something. Then his older brother told him he still talks to their father every morning at the barn. "My brother got every morning," Wes says. "I got one word in a hospital. He got the man." A father who stays mostly looks like nothing, like feeding cattle next to your boy and not saying much.

## You could learn

Jonathon called his father this spring. There was no apology, and the answer about the grandchildren is still "not yet." He's still angry, and he says a man can make the call and stay angry. But when his daughter asked if they could go fishing and he said he didn't know how, she said, "You could learn." In June he will, at a stock tank, from a man who isn't his father.

Wes wanted to add a word about forgiveness for the nephew. Jonathon refused. "He asked what we learned. He didn't ask what he owes." Wes kept his line.
