## A question men ask

> "The way of fools seems right to them, but the wise listen to advice."
> Proverbs 12:15 (NIV)

"My wife says I run the room. I think she's wrong." Don't argue for a day. Ask for one example. If part of it still seems wrong, say so kindly, later. A wife can be wrong, and she's still worth listening to.

## The tool

Ask one person. Pick someone who sees you up close and isn't scared of you. Ask one specific question. Write down exactly what they said. Say thank you. Jonathon's sister told him, "After Dad left, you were at everybody else's house. I was home with Mom."
