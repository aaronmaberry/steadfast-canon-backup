# Roommates No More
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 68

Jonathon wrote one line a day on the fridge whiteboard that wasn't logistics. Tuesday, nothing back. Wednesday, under his line, Dani drew a cartoon duck. "It's a duck." He's decided it means she read them.

On the Saturday ride to the feed store, Wes asked his wife what she's looking forward to this year. After about a mile she said, "I'd like to finish something." "Like what?" "I'll tell you when I know." He left it there. It's hers.

A young husband does date night every Friday and spends it talking about the baby. Date night isn't wrong. It just can't carry six other nights of roommates. The marriage isn't the Friday. It's the Tuesday.

## The fifteen-minute couch talk

Most days, phones in another room, sideways on the couch. She goes first, and you ask one follow-up before your own day. Then one true thing from yours. No logistics, no big fights, no keeping score.
