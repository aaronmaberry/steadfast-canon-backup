## Reading narrative

# The Father Who Stays
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 25 · March 30, 2027 · Easter week

On Easter, Jonathon's mother stood on his porch holding a ham and asked, "Is this okay?" Dani got to her first and hugged her. Later, while they did the dishes, the way they have since he was nine, Jonathon told his mother that his father had sent him a friend request. She kept washing. "He sent me one too. Two years ago. I said yes." Then she added, "He posts a lot of fish," and, "I'm not going to tell you what to do. He's your father."

That night at 9:40, Jonathon accepted the request without sending a message. At 9:52 his father liked a photo of Ruby and Theo in the bluebonnets, the first picture of his grandchildren he had ever seen. Nothing more came. Wes wanted the heart to mean something. Jonathon wouldn't let it yet: "A heart is a heart."

## Not because of anything

Wes realized that everything good he'd said to his sixteen-year-old that year had come with a reason attached, the way his own father's one "I'm proud of you" did. Jonathon pointed out that Wes does it to him too. At a red light Wes told his son, "I'm glad you're mine. That's all. Not because of anything." After two blocks the boy said, "Okay," and asked if he could drive home.

## Not bad, kid

When Jonathon wrote down whose "well done" he works for, it wasn't his father. It was his first sales manager, a man from Beaumont who never said more than "Not bad, kid." Jonathon still has the sticky note, and he still writes his emails in that man's voice.

## The Word

> "And a voice from heaven said, 'This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased.'"
> Matthew 3:17 (NIV)

Jesus hadn't preached, healed anyone, or been tempted when the Father spoke. The "well done" came before the work, and the first temptation in the next chapter went straight after it: "If you are the Son of God." A heart on a photo is not that voice, and no earthly father can give it. Whoever is in Christ is in the Son the Father spoke to. "Work from the river, not toward it."

## What they did

Jonathon has written down the one true sentence he owes Ruby. During bath time he told Theo, "I'm glad you're mine," and Theo said, "I know." Before calling his brother about their mother, Wes read the verse in his truck and decided the call isn't a test.

## The challenge

Name whose "well done" you've been working for. Hear it from the Father first. Then say it to someone else with no receipt.
