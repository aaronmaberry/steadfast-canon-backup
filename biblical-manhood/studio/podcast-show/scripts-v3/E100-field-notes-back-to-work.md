# Back to Work
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 100 · December 17, 2027

Every year Christmas morning feels real, and by the second Monday in January it's gone. Shane, a dad from Jonathon's coach-pitch team, asked how to keep it.

> "But Mary treasured up all these things and pondered them in her heart."
> Luke 2:19 (NIV)

Everyone else was amazed, and amazement fades. Mary kept specific things she had seen and kept turning them over for years. A man can't keep a feeling. He can keep a thing.
