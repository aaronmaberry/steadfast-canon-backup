## You can't yell at a well

Wes's granddad kept a hand-dug well, and the first time Wes threw the bucket down hard it floated. You let it down slow, wait till it's heavy, and bring it up careful. A husband is owed the asking, not the answer. She has a lid, and it's hers.

Both men named why they quit asking. Jonathon heard "she's leaving," because his father once left. Wes didn't want to drive to the mountains. "We stay up on top, where the answers are cheap."
