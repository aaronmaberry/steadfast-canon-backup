# The Hill Report
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 50

Wednesday night Wes walked out to the shop for a flashlight and found his sixteen-year-old with the chainsaw clamped in the vise and his phone propped on a coffee can, watching a man in Oregon sharpen a chain. On the second tooth the boy was filing the wrong way. Wes had the correction in his mouth and kept it there. On the third tooth the boy caught it himself, turned the file around, and went back. Wes said "Night," and left.

Jonathon's report was worse. On Monday his four-year-old refused to do his own shoes, cried for six minutes, and Jonathon took over to make a nine o'clock call. By Thursday, Theo was doing them himself. It isn't a straight line. The call moves to nine-thirty.

## Only video games

A man at Wes's Thursday table asked what to do with a fourteen-year-old who only wants games. Jonathon was that kid. After his dad left, a baseball game at a friend's house was the one place where effort paid off. Find out what it's giving him before you take it. Wes would put it in the closet for the summer anyway. They don't agree on that. They agree on the Saturday.

## The Saturday project

One real project with a reason he can see. The same time every Saturday, for a season. Work next to him. Let the phone come as a tool. End with food. One Saturday is an event. Ten is a hill.
