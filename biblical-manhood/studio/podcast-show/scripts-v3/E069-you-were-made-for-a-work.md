JONATHON: Did he ever say why he did it that way?

WES: No. He'd have said that's just how you build a chest. But that's what got me about it as a man. He didn't build a chest and then go looking for somebody to give it to. He built it for a bed. [CAM: JON MS]
JONATHON: Are you ever going to build one?
[CAM: WES MS]
WES: I've thought about it. If my daughter has a baby someday, I'd like to measure the foot of a bed that kid's going to grow into. I don't have his hands. Mine would have nails in it. ## Reading narrative

# You Were Made for a Work
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 69

On the last call of a hot Thursday, an 84-year-old widow had been sitting in her running car in the driveway because her air conditioner had died. It was 138 degrees in her attic. The senior tech found a bad capacitor and a burnt wire and fixed what she had. Jonathon's job is selling new units. "Thank you for not selling her one," he said. The tech looked at him like he was crazy. "She didn't need one."

Wes's granddad built a cedar chest for each grandchild, measured to the foot of that child's bed. He built Wes's two years early, for the bed he'd grow into. When Wes moved into the bed, the chest was already there.

> "For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do."
> Ephesians 2:10 (NIV)

Grace first, then work. You don't work to get in, and you don't work for a verdict. You work because of who made you, and He measured the spot before you got there.

## Hard isn't the same as wrong

Some jobs are hard. Hot, boring, under a bad boss. You can do that work for God. Some jobs ask you to do something wrong. Those you change or leave, carefully, with your wife in on it. And if you can't work at all right now, letting people carry you is a work too.

## The challenge

Name what your work is for. Find the person on the other end of it.
