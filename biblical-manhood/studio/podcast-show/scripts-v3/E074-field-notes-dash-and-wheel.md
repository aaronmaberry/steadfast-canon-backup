# Dash and Wheel
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 74 · September 17, 2027

A divorced dad watched his nine-year-old strike out to end a game and cry in the truck. His own father's sentence was right there: quit crying or I'll give you something to cry about. He didn't say it. He just drove. Later, after dropping the kids at their mom's, he cried alone at a gas station for the first time since March. He asked: is that weak?

> "And he wept so loudly that the Egyptians heard him, and Pharaoh's household heard about it."
> Genesis 45:2 (NIV)

Joseph ran Egypt through a famine. Standing in front of the brothers who sold him, he cleared the room and wept so loudly the palace heard. Then he told them the truth. Tears and the wheel can belong to the same man.

## Sad and steady

Let your kids see you cry at a ballgame, a funeral, a movie. Carry the grief about their mother with men, not with them. "I'm sad. That's mine to carry. I'm okay."

## Name it in two words

The first word is the loud one. The second is what's underneath, and it usually tells you where to go. Say both to one person, then stop talking.
