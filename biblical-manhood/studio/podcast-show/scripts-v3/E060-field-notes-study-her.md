# Field Notes: Study Her
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 60 · July 30, 2027

Wes asked his wife what else she'd told him that he didn't hear. "Do you want the little ones or the real one?" The little ones: the TV off at supper, a day's notice before company, a phone call instead of "K." The real one: years ago she wanted to finish her degree, and he said, "What for?" She never raised it again. "I just wanted you to have heard it once."

Jonathon told Dani, over a lasagna pan, that part of him had heard "she's leaving" when she said she wanted to sing. "I thought you didn't want me to." Silence doesn't stay empty. The other person fills it in.

> "My dove in the clefts of the rock, in the hiding places on the mountainside, show me your face, let me hear your voice; for your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely."
> Song of Songs 2:14 (NIV)

He doesn't drag her out. He asks, and tells her why: he likes the sound of her. And right after, catch the little foxes that ruin the vineyard.
