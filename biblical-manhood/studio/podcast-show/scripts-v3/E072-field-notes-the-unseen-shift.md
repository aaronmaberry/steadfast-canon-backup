# The Unseen Shift
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 72

Every morning at 6:45, a custodian unlocks the gym at Jonathon's daughter's school and says every child's name as they walk in. Jonathon walked past him for two years. On Wednesday he stopped and said thank you for the door. The man told him his name and said, "Somebody's got to open it."

A young man who works nights at a warehouse sang hymns for his grandmother on Thursday afternoon. That night he texted a question. How do you keep going when nobody notices?

> "You discern my going out and my lying down; you are familiar with all my ways."
> Psalm 139:3 (NIV)

Being noticed is a crowd. Being known is a table. You don't need many people to see you. You need a few to know you, and you need to know that God is already familiar with your upside-down day. To Him, the night shines like the day.

## The secret list

One line a day, something you did for somebody that nobody saw. Sunday night, read it to God. "This was for You." Show it to nobody. If it becomes a bill, throw it away.
