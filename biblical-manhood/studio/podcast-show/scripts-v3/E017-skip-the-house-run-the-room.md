## Reading narrative

# Skip the House, Run the Room
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 17

If the trouble starts inside a man, what does it look like on the outside on an ordinary day? Wes and Jonathon take three shapes and stay with them. Skip the house: the best of you goes to everyone except the people you live with. Run the room: you win every conversation and call it leading. Wear the job as your name: you answer "who are you" with a title.

## The coach voice

Jonathon coaches his daughter's coach-pitch team, the Bluebonnets. He knows all twelve names and one fact about each, and he can talk a crying seven-year-old down from a bucket in ninety seconds. By noon he's on the couch with his phone. When his four-year-old brought his glove, Jonathon said, "Not now, buddy," without looking up. Theo asked, "Why do you only do the coach voice at practice?" Dani covered for him. Jonathon says she was wrong. "I wasn't tired. I was done performing." He traces the voice to the month his father left, when he was nine and became "the man of the house."

## The title

In 2019, at forty-four, Wes lost the title he'd held for nine years in a reorganization. Same pay, different desk. For three weeks he told no one at home and got short with everyone, until his seventeen-year-old daughter asked, "Are you mad at us?" When he told his wife, she said, "Honey, I didn't even know what your title was."

## The crew

A contractor friend ran his house for thirty years like a job site, until his wife set down a dish towel and said, "I'm not on your crew." Jonathon admits his own version. He ran his own apology to his VP without asking the rep whose deal it was.

## The Word

> "There is a way that appears to be right, but in the end it leads to death."
