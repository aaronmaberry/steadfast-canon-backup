# Waiting on God Isn't Sitting Still
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 97 · December 7, 2027

For seven years Wes prayed the same prayer in his truck every morning: "Lord, fix it with my brother." His older brother was forty minutes away, feeding cattle alone at six in the morning. Wes never drove out. Then Mr. Abernathy asked him at the diner, "If He answered you tomorrow, what would you do Saturday?"

> "Wait for the LORD; be strong and take heart and wait for the LORD."
> Psalm 27:14 (NIV)

David wrote it surrounded by enemies, expecting to see God's goodness in the land of the living. Waiting for the Lord takes strength. It isn't the same as waiting it out.

## Hold the cup

The night Ruby was born, a nurse handed Jonathon a cup of ice chips. "Dads either pace or scroll. Hold the cup." He couldn't change the outcome. He could do something every minute.
