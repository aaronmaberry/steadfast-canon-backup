# Small Ways to Serve at Home
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 87 · November 2, 2027

Every night for twenty-seven years, Wes's wife has been the last one up. She locks the back door, turns off the porch light and checks the stove knobs. Wes is asleep by then, and he had never once wondered who did it. At Jonathon's breakfast table, his seven-year-old reached for the burnt pancake and said her mother always gets it. He had never noticed.

> "For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."
> Mark 10:45 (NIV)

Two disciples asked Jesus for the best seats, and the rest were angry they hadn't asked first. Jesus didn't tell them to stop wanting to be first. He told them where first is. He went first, down and not up, all the way to the cross. We don't copy the ransom. We copy the towel.

## The trash cans

A seventy-seven-year-old who runs a church repair crew for widows rolled one widow's trash cans to the curb at the end of a long day, because pickup was Monday. Then he wrote it in his pencil notebook, right under the steps six men had spent the day building, in the same size handwriting.
