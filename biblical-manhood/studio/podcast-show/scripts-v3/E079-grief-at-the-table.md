# Grief at the Table
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 79

Wes's father died on a Sunday night. Monday morning Wes went to work. For about a year after, he drove out to his father's place every Saturday and fixed fences that didn't need fixing, until his wife sat on the tailgate and said, "You know he's not going to come check your work." That was the first time he cried.

> "Jesus wept."
> John 11:35 (NIV)

Jesus stood at Lazarus's tomb knowing that in a few minutes Lazarus would walk out of it. He knew how the story ended, and He wept anyway, in front of everyone. Grief isn't a lack of faith. The crowd said, "See how he loved him." His tears were how they knew.

## Where'd you put it?

Years before, when Wes's granddad died, Mr. Abernathy listened to him describe the funeral and the dry ham, then tapped the table twice. "Where'd you put it? A man's going to put it somewhere. Better on a table than in a truck. Tell me one thing about him." They stayed at the diner until ten.
