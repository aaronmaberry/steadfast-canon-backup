### Walk Steadfast, Episode 1

Most men are measured by their highlight days. Walk Steadfast starts with the day that actually makes a man, an ordinary Tuesday.

## Two men at one table

Wes is 52, lives in the Texas Hill Country, and has been married 27 years. He calls his thirties "the fog years." At 36 an old lineman at a men's breakfast asked him, "What are you waiting on?" and then met him every other Thursday for six years. Wes still drinks from that man's diner mug. On air, Jonathon finds out the question Wes asked him at their first breakfast was borrowed.

Jonathon is 36, a sales manager from Pasadena, Texas, married to Dani, a night-shift labor and delivery nurse, with two kids. His dad left when he was nine. At eleven he started checking every lock in the house every night. He came to faith at 29.

## The upside column

Every Sunday night Jonathon had a plan, and by Tuesday he was merging onto the long way home. He calls that man "the upside column," a deal you feel good about and haven't worked. Intentions aren't fake, Wes says. They're cheap until Tuesday sends the bill.

Jonathon flags the context (Paul is talking about partial gifts and the love that lasts), and Wes agrees, then points to the picture Paul chose: a man grows by putting something down. Luke 16:10 adds that small things are where a man is honest or not.
