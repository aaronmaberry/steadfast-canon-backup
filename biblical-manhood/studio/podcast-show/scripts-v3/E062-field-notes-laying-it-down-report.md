For twenty years Wes has come home and gone straight to the bedroom to empty his pockets. This time he came in the kitchen door first. His wife turned around from the sink and asked, "What's wrong?" The next day he forgot, came back, and she handed him a knife and a bowl of okra, and told him about her sister's knee surgery while he cut it too thick.

## An actual Wednesday

A friend whose wife moved out in March texted: "what does that look like on a wednesday. like an actual wednesday." Most of life doesn't have a big day in it. It's made of Wednesdays, and dying to self there is small, boring and unthanked. For a separated dad, it's calling the kids at bedtime, paying on time without a comment, and not saying the thing about her you've been saying in your head all day. It is not signing away his kids out of guilt. Give in on the small things. Get help on the big ones.
