# The Wait Report
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 98 · December 10, 2027

A divorced father with a custody hearing in January asked how a man waits on God about something too big to have a first piece. He can't call a judge.

> "For the revelation awaits an appointed time; it speaks of the end and will not prove false. Though it linger, wait for it; it will certainly come and will not delay."
> Habakkuk 2:3 (NIV)

God told Habakkuk it would feel slow and come anyway, and told him to write it down first. The verse isn't a promise about a court date. It is a promise that God keeps every word He has actually given.

## The written prayer and the date

Write the prayer in one sentence and date it. Write your part underneath. Keep it where you'll touch it and read it on a set day. When it's answered, write the second date. Tell one man it exists.
