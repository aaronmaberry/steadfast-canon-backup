# Black Friday
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 94

Christmas eats a family alive when nobody in the house sits still on purpose. In the middle of shepherds running and angels singing and a census and no room at the inn, one person is still.

> "But Mary treasured up all these things and pondered them in her heart."
> Luke 2:19 (NIV)

Somebody has to guard the quiet. Move the day to fit your real family. Leave some squares empty on the calendar.

## The family gift rule

Set the whole number with your wife before the leftovers run out. One gift each, and one gift away, picked and wrapped together. Say it to the kids early. Give the grandparents a job inside the rule. Keep it on the twenty-second of December.
