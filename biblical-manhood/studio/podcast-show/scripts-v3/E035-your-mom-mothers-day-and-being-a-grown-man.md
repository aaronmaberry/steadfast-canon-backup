## Do not despise

> "Listen to your father, who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old."
> Proverbs 23:22 (NIV)

Despise means more than hate. It means to look down on, to stop taking seriously. Jonathon's mother once bought a car by herself for the first time, and he called the dealership behind her back to get her a better trade. "I took the deal seriously," he says. "Not her." He still thinks the number was right, and Wes doesn't argue the number. He argues the phone call. Tell her to her face and let her choose. Jesus once went after religious men who dedicated their parents' support money to God and called it holiness. A boy needs his mother's sign-off. A stranger doesn't pay attention. A grown son takes her seriously without needing her approval.

## The work nobody sees

Paul tells the Colossians to work for the Lord, who gives an inheritance, not wages. It's a gift to his children, never a way to earn salvation. Most of what our mothers did, nobody paid them for. Wes's mother kept a coffee can of quarters for each boy's school pictures. Jonathon's mother ironed his white baseball pants at midnight after her second job. A deacon whose mother died decades ago gives a bag of orange-slice candy to the oldest woman in church every Mother's Day, and only his wife knows why.
