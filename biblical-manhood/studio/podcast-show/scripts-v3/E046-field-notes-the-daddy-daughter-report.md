# Field Notes: The Daddy-Daughter Report
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 46 · June 11, 2027

Ruby is seven. At Wes's brother's stock tank she held up a worm and asked whether it could feel the hook. The brother looked at it a long time and said, "Not as much as the fish." She baited her own hook on the first try and caught a bluegill about the size of a phone. On the drive home, half asleep, she told her dad what she thinks about when she's by herself. Part of it was whether her grandfather in Louisiana liked her picture.

## "Fine" is not "go away"

At thirteen, Jonathon said "fine" through his bedroom door every night. If he'd said anything real, everything else might have come out with it. The one man who reached him was a coach who drove him home, asked "You hungry?", and talked about the Astros. About every third time, somewhere around the french fries, Jonathon said something true, and the coach never made a big deal of it.

> "a time to tear and a time to mend, a time to be silent and a time to speak, ..."
> Ecclesiastes 3:7 (NIV)

## The drive-through date

Same night, same time. She orders, she picks the music, and you don't lecture in the car. If something needs saying, give it its own time the next day. Ask one question you don't know the answer to, then eat your fries. If she says no, ask again next time. Keeping the offer standing is the whole point.

If her silence is more than being fifteen, don't wait for the drive-through. Call or text 988.
