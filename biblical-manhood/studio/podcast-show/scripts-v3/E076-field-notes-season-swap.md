### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 76

Wes's twenty-one-year-old had spent four Saturdays under cars with an eighty-one-year-old mechanic who doesn't talk. One morning he asked, "How'd you learn all this?" A long silence. Then, still looking at the brake line: "Same way you are. Somebody handed me wrenches."

> "But Rehoboam rejected the advice the elders gave him and consulted the young men who had grown up with him and were serving him."
> 1 Kings 12:8 (NIV)

A young man who listens only to his own age inherits his own blind spots. An old man who talks only to old men turns bitter. Many men connect best side by side, not face to face: under a car, on a ladder, in front of a Sunday golf tournament.

## The cross-generation lunch

Pick one man twenty years away. "Can I buy you lunch? I want to ask you something." Ask three questions, listen most of the time, and set the next one before you leave. Whoever sees it first goes first.
