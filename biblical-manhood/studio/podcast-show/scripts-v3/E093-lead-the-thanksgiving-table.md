# Lead the Thanksgiving Table
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 93

Last Thanksgiving Wes prayed for peace at his mother's table, and forty minutes later he was arguing with his brothers about her car keys. She set her fork down and said, "I'm right here." He left before the pie. He'd prayed for peace and planned to do none of it himself.

> "If it is possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone."
> Romans 12:18 (NIV)

The verse puts up two fences before it gets to peace. Some tables can't have it, and Paul says so. And there's a line down the middle of every table. You don't control the other side. Your side, your mouth, your face, your keys on the hook, you keep at peace. At Emmaus, the guest took the bread and gave thanks, and that's when they knew Him.

- **Source and plug.** Fresh topic. The B slot carries chapter 6, Go first (SHOW-BIBLE section 14, item 17 suggests it), because the episode's mark and its main move are Go First. Plug is present tense, no date, no price, about 15 seconds, after the Word and never inside it.
- **Verses.** Romans 12:18 is the card. Romans 12:21, Proverbs 15:1, Matthew 5:9 and Luke 24:30 are quoted. Romans 12:20 is told in Wes's words, not quoted (it quotes Proverbs with a colon and a quote inside a quote). Romans 12:1-16 is summarized, Matthew 5:23-24 and Luke 24:13-31 are told.
- **Payoff map (E093).** Main payoff, Wes's mother at Thanksgiving in her new place: the card tables, "It's all elbows," Dad's chair by the window. The apartment itself is told as the plan (the episode releases two days before Thanksgiving); E094 reports. The prayer-aloud thread with Wes's wife is not carried. Wes's table prayer is a different thing (he's prayed at that table for nine years), and the episode never ties it to his wife.
- **Last year's Thanksgiving.** Consistent with the E007 and E016 rows (the argument about Mom's care, Wes left early, apologized to his brothers in the winter, the older brother ate the pecan pie). New on air: Wes prayed first, the mailbox, "I'm right here," "I've got cows," and that he never apologized to his mother.
- **Uncle Rudy.** New on air, named here for the first time. Tied to the E047/E049 rows ("You're the man of the house now," said by relatives at the first Thanksgiving after Jonathon's dad left). This script makes Rudy the one who said it first. Batch report lists it as an assumption.
- **Jonathon's father.** One small line through Ruby ("Okay. Six."). He doesn't call or write, so under the round 12 rule there's no report on him, only Ruby counting plates.
- **Safety.** 988 said twice on mic (the man across from his abuser, the divorced father alone), in the challenge note in the description, and no jokes near either.
- **Pushback.** Jonathon pushes twice for real (peace as a doormat, the man across from his abuser). Jonathon's two stories are Rudy at nine and Rudy last year. Sniper-prayer cousin in Baytown is a one-line color, no name used for the cousin, "Kevin" is a name in the joke.
- **"Sir":** two (the sixty-six-year-old father, the sober man's script). **Kid line:** one, Ruby's "Okay. Six." **Knee beat:** none. **Show about itself:** one (the Friday tease).
