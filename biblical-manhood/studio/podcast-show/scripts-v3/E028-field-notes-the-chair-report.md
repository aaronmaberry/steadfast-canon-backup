One of the coach-pitch dads had heard about the lamp. "Mine took the TV," he said, and then went to help his daughter with her glove.

## Tool on the table: one honest sentence

Jonathon wrote two drafts and crossed both out. The first was a soft dodge, and the second was a speech "for me." Dani thought "far away" was enough for a seven-year-old, and Wes saw her point. Jonathon thought Ruby had asked a different question, and that she'd rather hear the hard word from him. They didn't settle it. At bedtime he said, "I do have a dad. He lives in Louisiana. He left when I was nine. It was sad, it wasn't my fault, and I'm not going anywhere." Ruby asked, "Does he know about me?" He told her that her grandfather had seen her Easter picture. "Did he like it?" "He did." Then Ruby said, "I'd be sad if you left." "I'm not leaving." "I know. I'm just saying."
