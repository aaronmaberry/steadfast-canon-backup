# What She Actually Needs
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 63 · August 10, 2027

When Wes is sick, he wants to be left alone. For twenty-seven years, when his wife was sick, he brought her a glass of water and shut her door, quietly. He had a technique. Then a friend of hers got a hard diagnosis, and Wes went out to the porch to give her room. In the morning she said, "I sat in that kitchen by myself last night." He'd given her his want and called it hers. That Saturday he asked, "What can I carry?" She said, "Drive me over there. I don't want to walk in by myself."

Jonathon gave Dani the pep talk that works on him before a big pitch. "Please stop," she said. What she needed was the kids out of the house for three hours so she could sing in her own living room before her audition.

> "No one should seek their own good, but the good of others."
> 1 Corinthians 10:24 (NIV)

Paul wrote it to people who were right about the meat and wrong about the man. Most husbands aren't selfish with their wives. They're generous in their own size. Sincere, and the wrong size. You can't seek her good if you've never asked what it is.
