# Field Notes: Not in the Lobby
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 58 · July 23, 2027

Jonathon almost asked his nephew "Anybody special?" again and caught it halfway. He asked what was hard at work instead, and for twenty minutes he heard about a journeyman who calls the boy "College" and a job he'd been thinking about quitting. On the back step, asked to be one of the men at Theo's morning someday, the nephew ate four orange slices and asked, "Is that a real thing or a show thing?" Wednesday he texted: "you didnt ask about girls sunday. noticed."

Wes spent two hours in a 120-degree attic and found a wasp nest. The single man who runs his church's sound board found the cracked vent-pipe boot in ten minutes, then sat at Wes's kitchen table for an hour talking about tomatoes. Fifteen years, and nobody had asked him over.

## Should he stop dating to focus on God?

God isn't in a contest with a good woman. Wes once ended a relationship by saying God told him to, and then skipped church for three months. Don't hand God the breakup. Ask instead whether she's a place you go to God with, or a place you go instead of Him.

> "LORD, you alone are my portion and my cup; you make my lot secure."
> Psalm 16:5 (NIV)

The priests drew no land. God was their share. A man who knows who's holding his cup can date a good woman without making her his portion, and can go a year alone without feeling like half a man.

## Tool on the table
