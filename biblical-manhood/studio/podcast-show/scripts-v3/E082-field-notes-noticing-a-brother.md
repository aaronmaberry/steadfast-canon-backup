## Reading narrative

# Noticing a Brother
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 82 · October 15, 2027

A rancher in his late sixties sold most of his cattle after a dry summer. Then he stopped going to the Saturday sale barn and to church, gave his good saddle to a neighbor's boy, and told the man who sells him crop insurance, "You won't have to worry about me much longer." The crop-insurance man wanted to know if he was making something out of nothing. He isn't. Those are warning signs.

> "Be sure you know the condition of your flocks, give careful attention to your herds;"
> Proverbs 27:23 (NIV)

The verse is about livestock, and a rancher already lives it. He can tell which heifer is off her feed before she knows it herself. Look at a friend with the same careful attention, and then don't stand at the fence and hope.

## The direct question

Get him alone and side by side. Tell him what you've seen. Ask in plain words whether he is thinking about killing himself. If he says yes, stay with him, put distance between him and any guns, and call or text 988 together. If he says no, believe him and keep coming back.
