## Reading narrative

# The Bed Is a Covenant
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 65

At six on a Saturday, Theo climbed into his parents' bed and found them both on their backs, phones up, faces lit. "Are you guys at work?" That morning Jonathon moved his charger to the truck in the garage. Dani's phone went in her drawer, no speech. The second night, without screens, she told him about a nineteen-year-old who came into labor alone, and how Dani stayed with her through the delivery on her break.

Wes's father and the neighbor shared a mile of fence for forty years with no paper between them. Whoever saw the post down fixed it. When Mr. Kubecka couldn't get out of his truck anymore, Wes's dad fixed the whole mile for two years. He told Mr. Kubecka about it in his kitchen. He never sent a bill. A covenant has room for a complaint. It has no room for an invoice.

> "Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral."
> Hebrews 13:4 (NIV)

Honored by all, including the single man, the divorced man and the widower. Kept, the way you keep a fence, every season. Judgment for the man who hides it. Mercy for the man who brings it into the light.

## Before it grows up

At thirty-four, Wes felt his chest jump at a text from a married coworker before he even looked. Nothing had happened. He told his wife that night anyway, over the next day's lunches, told an older man the next morning, and asked off the project. It cost him a promotion. A man who can't leave can still put light on it.
