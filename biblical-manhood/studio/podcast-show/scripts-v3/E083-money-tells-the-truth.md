## Reading narrative

# Money Tells the Truth
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 83

For twenty-six years Wes paid every bill in his house himself and was never once late. He balanced the register on Sundays after lunch, to the penny. And in all that time he never showed it to his wife, who had kept the books for a dentist for a decade. When she asked, he said, "We're fine." He calls it a wall with good manners.

> "For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."
> Luke 12:34 (NIV)

Jesus doesn't say the money follows the heart. He says the heart goes where the money went. The rich man in the parable isn't a thief. He had a real harvest, and he planned to build bigger barns. What was wrong was that the whole plan was a conversation with himself. He was rich toward his barns and not rich toward God.

## The drawer

In his first year of marriage, Jonathon kept a credit card under the takeout menus in the kitchen drawer. A mortgage officer found the balance. That night his wife said, "I don't care about the seven thousand. I care that it was in a drawer." The number was survivable. The secret was what hurt.
