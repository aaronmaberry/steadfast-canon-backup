WES: (laughs) He probably was. But I'd go out of my way to get to that shop. My dad wasn't saying much that year. That man said two words and my name, and I drove across town for it. That's how small a blessing can be and still do something.

JONATHON: And don't attach a lesson. That's the rule I'd add, because I'm the one who'd break it. The second you say "and you should," it turns into a talk. A blessing doesn't have homework.

JONATHON: Mine's my nephew. Twenty-two. His dad hasn't been around much. He's been texting me all spring about hard things, and he counts days. # Field Notes: Father's Day When It's Complicated
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 48 · June 18, 2027

In fourth grade, Jonathon's class made Father's Day cards. His dad had been gone about four months. He made one anyway, glitter and a tie on the front, because everyone else was making one, and he threw it away at the bus stop on the way home. For the next twenty years, Father's Day was a day to get through.

## Say it before Sunday

If Father's Day is the worst day of your year, you're allowed to hate it. Say so to one person before Sunday. Then plan the day so it can't ambush you: where you'll be at noon, who you'll call, and what you'll do when the pastor asks the dads to stand. You can sit. God knows why. If you need the afternoon alone, tell someone where you'll be. Alone is fine. Missing isn't.

This Sunday Jonathon will text his father "Happy Father's Day" for the first time in twenty-eight years. He won't send the photo of his daughter's first fish. If his father doesn't answer, Jonathon doesn't want it to be her picture that went unanswered.

> "A father to the fatherless, a defender of widows, is God in his holy dwelling."
> Psalm 68:5 (NIV)

## Bless a younger man

Pick one younger man whose father isn't in the picture. Call him. Say his name, one thing you've seen in him, and one hope for him. Then stop talking. A blessing doesn't have homework.

If Sunday is more than hard, call or text 988.
