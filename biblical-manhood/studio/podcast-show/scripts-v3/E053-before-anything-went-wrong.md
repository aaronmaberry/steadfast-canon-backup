# Before Anything Went Wrong
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 53

Jonathon added one line to his region's vision that wasn't about numbers: every rep takes all their vacation days. At the July planning meeting his VP put it on the big screen, loved it, and turned it into a dashboard column with a target. "I tried to put one thing in my work that wasn't a number, and it's a number now."

Wes's father was offered an agriculture scholarship as a young man and stayed to run the family place instead. Wes always thought the place was his father's cage. On Saturday at the barn he asked his brother whether their dad ever liked the work. "I don't know. He never said." Wes remembers his father on Sunday afternoons with his hand flat on a corner post he'd set the year Wes was born. Wes thought he was checking it.

> "The LORD God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it."
> Genesis 2:15 (NIV)

The work was there on the first good day. The thorns came later. There are two jobs in the verse: work it, and take care of it. Most men are good at the first. Jonathon pushed back on "placed": after his father left, a woman at church told his mother, who was working two jobs, "God's got you right where He wants you." Wes agreed. Some people are placed, and some are stuck and need help getting out.

## What did you keep?

Wes's old mentor, Mr. Abernathy, was a power-company lineman for forty years. A lineman doesn't make anything. He keeps the lights on, and when he does it right, nothing happens. One Thursday, after Wes ran through everything he'd gotten done, Mr. Abernathy tapped the table twice. "What did you keep?"

Jonathon sells commercial air conditioning in San Antonio. Last August, when a library that's also a city cooling center lost its air conditioning, one of his reps drove the part across town at eleven at night. Jonathon's first question was about the margin. Years earlier, his first manager made him walk back into a machine shop and undo a sale the owner didn't need.
