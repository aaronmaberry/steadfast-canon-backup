# Season Review: The Long Road
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 86

A sixty-one-year-old who lost his job in June asked what the fall taught two working men about work and its weight. He brought fall tomatoes to the table in a grocery sack and talked about them for five minutes. Then he said, "I don't know what I'm for if I'm not for that."

> "Teach us to number our days, that we may gain a heart of wisdom."
> Psalm 90:12 (NIV)

A man who has worked forty years counts in pay periods. When the paychecks stop, it feels as if time has stopped. But the days haven't. Moses tells us to count those, and a man can gain a heart of wisdom in a season without a paycheck.

## The season letter

At the end of a season, write one page to yourself by hand: what happened, what stuck, what didn't, what's still open, and one thing you keep. Read it to one person, seal it, and write on the envelope the date you'll open it.
