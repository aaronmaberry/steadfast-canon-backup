### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 66

Jonathon's phone slept in the truck every night. But on Tuesday he sat in the driver's seat in the dark garage for twenty minutes, scrolling nothing worse than scores and the weather, and knew he was hiding. He told his wife Thursday morning, and Wes a few minutes later. "It's not clean. It's whole."

Austin, a dad of two who's living apart from their mom, asked how you talk to kids about this, and who goes first. Not someday. Now. Not one talk, a hundred small ones.
