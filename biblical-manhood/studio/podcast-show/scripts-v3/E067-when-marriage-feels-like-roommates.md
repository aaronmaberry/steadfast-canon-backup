[CAM: JON CU]

[CAM: WES MS]

[CAM: WES CU]
WES: And I think if you lead with choice, a lot of men hear "you're a bad husband," and they go defend themselves instead of going out on the step. I've watched it. A man who feels accused argues. A man who feels understood moves.

JONATHON: So which is it?

JONATHON: Fair enough. I'd still say "gravity" is a little too comfortable for most of us. But the day Ruby asked, the excuse was gone. That's right.
[CAM: WES CU]

[CAM: WES MS]
WES: My granddad had a back forty he ran cows on for fifty years. Clean as a golf course. When he got too old, he sold the cows, and he didn't run anything on it. [CAM: JON MS]
JONATHON: And that's hope, by the way, for the man who's already in the mesquite. It came back. It took three years and a dozer, but it came back. Nobody looked at that field and said, sell it, it's gone.

[CAM: JON CU]

[CAM: JON MS]
JONATHON: And that scares me, because useful is replaceable. You can hire useful. Somebody else can tell you where the shoes are. Nobody else can know you. [CAM: WES CU]
WES: That's how a friendship goes, too. You don't lose a friend in a fight. You lose him because you only call when you need a truck.
