# Thank-You Report
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 92

Jonathon's father left when he was nine. The one good memory is small: steering his dad's truck in a church parking lot on Sunday afternoons, sitting in his lap, his dad on the pedals. Is it weird to thank a man for one afternoon?

> "Honor your father and your mother, so that you may live long in the land the LORD your God is giving you."
> Exodus 20:12 (NIV)

To honor a father is to give him his real weight, the good he did and the harm. A thank-you for one true afternoon doesn't sign off on the rest. And if a father hurt you, honor can look like distance.

## The thank-you text

One small, true memory. What it did, at its honest size. No ask and no hook. Short enough to read without scrolling, read out loud before you send it. Then let it go.
