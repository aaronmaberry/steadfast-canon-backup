Walk Steadfast is two men at a table, talking plainly about following Jesus as a man on the ordinary days.

Wes Hart has sat at tables with men for more than twenty years and wrote Steadfast Men: The way of Godly Men. Jonathon is a husband, a dad of two and a sales manager in San Antonio who came to faith at 29. Every Tuesday they take one question a man actually carries, about marriage, kids, work, anger, money, fathers or grief, read the Bible on it, argue a little, and say what they really did. Every Tuesday ends with one challenge. Every Friday, on Field Notes, they report whether they did it, including the misses.

The book and the Steadfast video training are at walksteadfast.com.

New episodes every Tuesday, plus Field Notes every Friday. Clean, and fine for the truck with your kids in the back seat.
