# Reading narrative format (show notes and blog post)

Every episode script ends with a **reading narrative**. It's a short article built from the episode, for the podcast show notes, the episode page on walksteadfast.com, and a blog post. It isn't a transcript. Transcripts are a separate export from Descript.

## Why

- A man who reads instead of listens gets the whole idea in four minutes.
- Search engines can read it, while they can't read a video.
- Apple and Spotify show notes get real content instead of a link dump.

## Length and voice

- **Length.** 600-900 words for main episodes, 350-500 for Field Notes. The show-notes version is the first 150 words plus the links block.
- **Voice.** Third person, warm and plain, in the house style ("Wes and Jonathon start with..."). Quotes from the hosts are lifted word for word from the script, at most four per piece.
- **Rules.** Unslop and canon rules apply in full: no em dashes, the exact NIV with the Biblica notice, the focus mark named plainly, no banned names.

## Template

```
# [Episode title]
### Walk Steadfast, Episode [n] · [release date]

[Dek: one sentence, the promise, with the main search words.]

[Opening paragraph, 2-4 sentences: the question of the episode, in a man's words.]

## [Section 1 heading, a plain phrase]
[The first story, retold in 1-2 paragraphs. One host quote.]

## [Section 2 heading]
[The scripture. Show the verse as a block quote with the reference and (NIV). One paragraph on what it means at a kitchen table.]

## [Section 3 heading: the pushback]
[The objection and how they worked it through. One quote each.]

## This week's challenge
[The challenge in one sentence. Single version and married version, one line each.]

## Carry this
> [Wes's line]
> [Jonathon's line]

---
**Listen or watch:** YouTube · Apple Podcasts · Spotify (links)
**Chapters:** [copied from the description]
**Mentioned:** [book chapter or training session, only when plugged]
**Scripture:** [references] (NIV)

walksteadfast.com · @steadfastwalk

```

## Field Notes version

Title and dek, then "Check the box" (what each man did or didn't do, in one paragraph), "From the table" (the question and the short answer), "Tool on the table" (the tool in 2-3 sentences), "Carry this," and the links block.
