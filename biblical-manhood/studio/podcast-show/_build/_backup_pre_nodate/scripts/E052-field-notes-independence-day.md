## Reading narrative

# Independence Day
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 52 · July 2, 2027

Jonathon's nephew is twenty-two and past a hundred days on the thing he's been fighting. On the Fourth of July he texted a question: what does freedom even mean for a Christian, when it feels like a list of things you can't do? In his twenties, Jonathon called it freedom too: a steakhouse booth, three drinks, nobody to answer to. "I wasn't free. I was alone, and I'd named it free so I didn't have to call it alone."

> "You, my brothers and sisters, were called to be free. But do not use your freedom to indulge the flesh; rather, serve one another humbly in love."
> Galatians 5:13 (NIV)

Free for, not free from. The nos make room for the yes.

## The porch prayer

Wes has prayed in front of a whole church. He almost never prays out loud by himself. So on Tuesday night he sat on the porch and did it: one thanks by name, one person by name, and one thing to put down. He still can't pray out loud sitting next to his wife. The porch is where he's practicing.

## What stuck

At the end of the fathers season, Wes has spent "proud" on both sons and stayed out of his daughter's marriage. Jonathon sent his father the first Father's Day text in twenty-eight years. His daughter's card for her grandfather is still half done on the counter.

## Production notes

- **Wes's prayer crack stays open.** The porch prayer is alone, and he says it on mic: "Sitting next to my wife, out loud, just the two of us, I still can't do it." Don't resolve it before about E083.
- **The Fourth, no politics.** "I love this country" and "a free country can't make a free man," with no party, policy or candidate. Paul's prison letters are mentioned in general (no specific letter claimed).
- **The nephew's recovery.** "Past a hundred days" is deliberately loose. The canon day counts (36 at E032, 59 at about E040) don't share a start date that's exact enough for a precise number on mic.
- **Round 8, kids.** No diagnostic kid lines. Ruby asking about the card is a question, not a read on her father.
- **One-word replies.** One: Dani's "Oh." The nephew's "k" is predicted, not quoted.
- **Wives aren't oracles.** "Say it slower. You rush the end" is a practical note. She's a person reading a card twice, and Wes doesn't know what she thought.
- **Round 7, older men.** Dani's dad's lines are plain ("I'll be eighty-two." "Well, put it on my calendar too."), with no wisdom line, and he can't find the calendar on his phone.
- **Deadpan payoff:** "My neighbor's porch light came on about halfway through. Then it went off." It's in the cold open and called back once in the tool, which counts as the same payoff.
- **Knee or age beat:** none.
- **Two-chairs:** none.
- **Setups for E053 (Season 6, The House, premiere: Before Anything Went Wrong; Gen 2:15; Pick Up the Weight; training plug W18; the monthly Mr. Abernathy segment).** Work before the fall, so the work arena (Jonathon's VP and the reps; Wes's unnamed job stays unnamed). Ruby's card stays undecided. The October day is quiet until about E065.
