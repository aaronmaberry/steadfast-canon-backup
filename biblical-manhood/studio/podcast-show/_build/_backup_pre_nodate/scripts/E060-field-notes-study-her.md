## Reading narrative

# Study Her
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 60 · July 30, 2027

Wes asked his wife, "What else have you told me that I didn't hear?" She asked, "Do you want the little ones or the real one?" The little ones: the TV off at supper, a day's notice before company, a phone call instead of "K." The real one: years ago she told him she wanted to finish her degree, and he said, "What for?" She never brought it up again. She isn't asking him to do anything now. "I just wanted you to have heard it once."

Jonathon told Dani that when she said she wanted to sing, part of him heard that she was leaving. "I thought you didn't want me to," she said. Then she asked him to say out loud that he was okay with Tuesday nights. He did.

## What questions aren't weird?

Tell her what you're doing. Start with what she's already living, and ask one more about it. And don't interview her across a table. Go side by side.

> "My dove in the clefts of the rock, in the hiding places on the mountainside, show me your face, let me hear your voice; for your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely."
> Song of Songs 2:14 (NIV)

## The three-question walk

Once a week, walk together. Glad, want, us. Answer your own questions too, and write down what she says when you get home.
