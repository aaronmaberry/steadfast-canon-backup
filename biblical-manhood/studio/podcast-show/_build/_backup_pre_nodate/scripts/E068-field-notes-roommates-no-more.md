# Roommates No More
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 68 · August 27, 2027

A young father takes his wife out every Friday, and they spend the whole date talking about the baby. Is a date night really the answer? Keep it, Wes says, but don't make one night carry six nights of roommates. If the restaurant is the only time you talk, it becomes the board meeting.

For years, Wes and his wife went to a catfish place on the highway on the first Friday of the month and sat on the same side of the booth, watching the trucks come in. They weren't interviewing each other. They were watching the same thing.
