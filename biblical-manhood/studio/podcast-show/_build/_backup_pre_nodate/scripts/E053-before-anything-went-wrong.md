# Before Anything Went Wrong
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 53 · July 6, 2027

Jonathon added one line to his region's vision that wasn't about numbers: every rep takes all their vacation days. At the July planning meeting his VP put it on the big screen, loved it, and turned it into a dashboard column with a target. ## What did you keep?

One Thursday, after Wes ran through everything he'd gotten done that week, Mr. Abernathy tapped the table twice. "What did you keep this week?"

Jonathon sells commercial air conditioning in San Antonio. Last August, when a library that's also a city cooling center lost its air conditioning, one of his reps drove the part across town at eleven at night. Jonathon's first question was about the margin.
