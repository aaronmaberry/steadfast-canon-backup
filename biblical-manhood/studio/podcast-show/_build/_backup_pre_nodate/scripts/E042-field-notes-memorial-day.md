## Reading narrative

# Field Notes: Memorial Day
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 42 · May 28, 2027

A coach-pitch dad named Shane has spent nine years of nights building a model of the battleship Texas in his garage. On the bridge there's a brass plate smaller than a stick of gum. It's for Christen Christensen, the helmsman, who was twenty-one when a German shell hit the bridge off Cherbourg in June 1944. He was the only man aboard the Texas killed in action in the whole war. Shane's grandfather was below in a gun mount that day: "Everybody on that ship got to come home but one." Every time Shane sits down to work, he says, "Evening, Chris." Jonathon's daughter says it now too.

## You can't love a phrase

"The fallen" is a phrase. Every name on a memorial was somebody's boy. Wes has driven past the granite memorial on his courthouse lawn for twenty-five years and has never read the names on the back. On Monday he will, out loud.

> "Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one's life for one's friends."
> John 15:13 (NIV)

Jesus said it the night before He did it. A soldier isn't Jesus, but the love points to Him.

## Read the names

Find your town's memorial and read every name out loud, one to a breath. Pick one man and look him up, then tell your family one true thing about him. Pray for whoever he left behind.

If the day is heavy for you, call someone who knew him. Veterans and their families can dial 988 and press 1.

## Production notes

- **Order change (round 7, round 5 holiday rule).** From the Table leads. Check the Box comes second. The cold open (Deputy) is from the check.
- **Real history, checked 2026-10-03.** USS Texas (BB-35), Battle of Cherbourg, June 25, 1944: a 240 mm shell hit the top of the conning tower. QM3c Christen Norman Christensen (b. Feb 7, 1923), the helmsman, died of his wounds. He was the only crewman killed in action in the ship's career. Buried at Long Island National Cemetery. Sources: battleshiptexas.org history page; National WWII Museum, "Engage Until Neutralized"; VA Veterans Legacy Memorial page. The Texas was moored at the San Jacinto Battleground (La Porte, near Pasadena) from 1948, which fits Jonathon's fourth-grade trip. Don't add more details about Christensen's family or life on mic without a source.
