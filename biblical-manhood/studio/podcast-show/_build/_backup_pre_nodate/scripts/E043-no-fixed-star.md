At thirteen, Wes drove his granddad's tractor over the bottom field for the first time alone. He was so afraid of ruining the disk that he spent the whole first pass looking back at it. ## A full week is not a heading

In his forties Wes had three church committees, a ball team, and fifty-five-hour weeks. One night his wife asked, "Where are we going?" He told her, "Where do you want to go? I can do Saturday." She meant the life. A calendar shows you what you did. It can't show you where you're going.

> "Where there is no revelation, people cast off restraint; but blessed is the one who heeds wisdom's instruction."
> Proverbs 29:18 (NIV)

The word is revelation, not strategy. You don't make up the tree. God has already put it at the end of the field.
