## Reading narrative

# Labor Day
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 70 · September 3, 2027

Wes asked his sixteen-year-old to show him a weld he was proud of. Behind the feed store, on a cattle gate, the boy pointed at one bead. Wes said what he saw: "It's even the whole way. You didn't rush the end." His son said, "That's the hard part. Everybody rushes the end."

A tech with thirty-one years in attics has Labor Day off and dreads it. The last day off he had, he rebuilt a mailbox that wasn't broken. How do you rest without feeling lazy?

> "'Six days you shall labor, but on the seventh day you shall rest; even during the plowing season and harvest you must rest."
> Exodus 34:21 (NIV)

The work will never tell you it's time to stop. God knew the farmer would say he'd rest after harvest, and He said, even at harvest. Rest isn't lazy. It's trust that the world will keep turning for a day without you.

## The real Sabbath hour

Pick one hour. Start it out loud. No work and no phone. Fill it with something that fills you back. If an hour goes well, try two.
