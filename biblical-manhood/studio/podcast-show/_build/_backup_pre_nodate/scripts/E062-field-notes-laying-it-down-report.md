# Laying It Down Report
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 62 · August 6, 2027

For twenty years Wes has come home and gone straight to the bedroom to empty his pockets. This week he came in the kitchen door first. His wife turned around from the sink and asked, "What's wrong?" By Wednesday she was telling him about her sister's knee surgery. By Thursday she handed him a knife and a bowl of okra.

Jonathon laid down one thing for Dani every day, and he isn't saying what. His report: "Yes. Not Wednesday."

## An actual Wednesday

A friend whose wife moved out in March texted: "what does that look like on a wednesday. like an actual wednesday." Most weeks don't have a big day. They're made of Wednesdays, and dying to self there is small, boring and unthanked. For a separated dad, it's calling the kids at bedtime, paying on time without a comment, and not saying the thing about her you've been saying in your head all day.

> "Then he said to them all: 'Whoever wants to be my disciple must deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me."
> Luke 9:23 (NIV)

Not flawlessly. Daily. Thursday counts.

## The first-pick swap

Notice the first picks you get without thinking. Swap one a day, and don't say a word about it.
