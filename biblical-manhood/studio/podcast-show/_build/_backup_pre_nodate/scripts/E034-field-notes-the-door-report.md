## Reading narrative

# Field Notes: The Door Report
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 34 · April 30, 2027

At Easter lunch a man's father pulled out his phone and showed him a listing for two acres right next door. His wife smiled through the whole meal. In the car she said, "If they buy that lot, I'm moving to Ohio." She has never been to Ohio. He's thirty-three and has never once told his parents no. His father, he says, doesn't hear no. He hears "not yet."

## Every reason is a handle

> "All you need to say is simply 'Yes' or 'No'; anything beyond this comes from the evil one."
> Matthew 5:37 (NIV)

Jesus is talking about oaths, not about anybody's mother. Jonathon picked the verse for the words "anything beyond this." He coaches seven-year-olds and has learned it from a parent group chat: give one honest sentence and the conversation is over, give three reasons and you get three arguments.

## Thank you, no, I love you

First, thank them for the true thing they're offering. Then say no in one sentence that actually uses the word. Then say I love you, and put a real yes next to the no. After that, stop talking. The man called his parents on Wednesday, before the realtor visit. His father said, "Well. We were just looking." His mother asked whether it was about the pantry. On Friday morning she texted him a different listing, twenty minutes away. "Twenty minutes is perfect," his wife said.

## The box

Jonathon took his phone line off his mother's plan, where it had been since he was sixteen. "You'll lose your number." "I keep the number." "Then what's the point?" "That's the point." The account is still called "The Kids." Wes gave up his phone for his son's curfew night. At 10:58 his wife texted Jonathon: "Wes has been on the same page since nine."
