[CHAPTER: Gravity]
[CAM: WES MS]
WES: I want to say something to the man who's listening and feeling guilty right now. Roommates isn't usually a sin. It's gravity. You don't decide to drift. You just stop deciding not to. Every marriage drifts that way if you leave it alone, the same way a pasture goes back to brush if you leave it alone. You don't have to do anything wrong. You just have to do nothing.
[CAM: WES CU]
WES: And here's the trap. Because it's gravity, men think the fix has to be big. A trip. A weekend away. A big talk. And so we wait until we can afford the trip, or find a sitter for the weekend, or work up to the big talk. And meanwhile, gravity's still working. Every day. A pasture doesn't go back to brush because of one bad year. It goes back because of a lot of ordinary days nobody cut anything.
[CAM: WES MS]
WES: My granddad had a back forty he ran cows on for fifty years. Clean as a golf course. When he got too old, he sold the cows, and he didn't run anything on it. Nobody did anything wrong to that field. Nobody poisoned it. They just quit using it. And in about six years it was mesquite and prickly pear so thick you couldn't walk a dog through it. My older brother bought it back from the estate, and it took him three years with a dozer and a chainsaw to get it to where you could see the fence. Six years to lose it. Three years to get it back. And he still has to mow it.
[CAM: JON MS]
JONATHON: And you don't clear it in one weekend either.
WES: No. You clear it a little bit every Saturday. You keep it clear by showing up.
[CAM: JON CU]
JONATHON: I'll tell you what I've noticed about the roommate thing in my house. It's not that we don't love each other. It's that we only use each other. And I mean that in the plain way, not the ugly way. I use Dani to know where Theo's shoes are. She uses me to know if the water got paid. We're each other's most useful person. And somewhere, being useful replaced being known. And here's the thing that scares me about that. Useful is replaceable. You can hire useful. Somebody else can tell you where the shoes are. Nobody else can know you. If all we are to each other is useful, then the day the kids leave, or the day the schedule changes, we're two people who used to have a job together.
[CAM: WES MS]
WES: That's how a friendship goes, too. You don't lose a friend in a fight. You lose him because you only call when you need a truck.

[CHAPTER: More than drift]
[CAM: WES CU]
WES: Now I need to be careful here. Because some of you are hearing "roommates," and that's not what you've got. What you've got is colder than that.
[CAM: WES MS]

# When Marriage Feels Like Roommates
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 67 · August 24, 2027

This August, Jonathon and Dani pass each other in the driveway at 7:15 in the morning, car windows down, forty seconds. Last Wednesday their whole conversation happened on the fridge whiteboard: shoes, the dentist, "Love you." Under it, in her handwriting: "Who is this."

Nothing is wrong. That is what's wrong. In sales, an account goes quiet and transactional right before it's lost, with no fight to warn you.

The year Wes's daughter was a senior, his family ran like a relay race. After a ballgame his wife asked, "When's the last time we talked about anything that wasn't on the calendar?" That Saturday he said, "Ride with me," on the twenty-minute drive to the feed store. She's ridden most Saturdays since. When she can't, she says she's sorry. You don't apologize for missing an errand.
