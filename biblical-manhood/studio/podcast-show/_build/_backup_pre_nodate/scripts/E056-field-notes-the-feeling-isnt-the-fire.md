# The Feeling Isn't the Fire
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 56 · July 16, 2027

Wes promised an honest number for his week of porch prayer, and he gave it: five out of seven. One night he fell asleep in the recliner with his boots on. One night there was lightning, and he could have prayed in the kitchen. One prayer was forty seconds long: "Lord, I'm tired. Thank You for the water." The feeling showed up once. He can't tell which of the five God used.

## What if I don't feel called?

A man at Wes's Thursday table sells crop insurance. Everyone at his church seems to know their calling. He doesn't feel called to anything.

> "Trust in the LORD and do good; dwell in the land and enjoy safe pasture."
> Psalm 37:3 (NIV)

Almost every time the Bible says "called," it means called to Someone before it means called to something, and for a man in Christ that has already happened. The verbs are trust, do good, dwell and enjoy. After a hailstorm, this man drives out the same day and sits at the farmer's kitchen table. He calls that "just my job." Nobody else shows up at that table.

## The next-faithful-thing list

Three lines on Sunday night: one at home, one at work, one for somebody who can't pay you back. Each one fits on a line and can be done by Saturday. Keep the papers, and by December you can see what your calling looked like, one line at a time.
