## Reading narrative

# Field Notes: Inside Job
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 16 · February 26, 2027

Jonathon had dreaded Thursday's call with his regional VP for eight years. He read the sentence off a sticky note, "The 2019 district deal should have been his, and I put my name on it," and then he stopped talking. The VP thanked him, explained that deals that old can't be reassigned in the system, and said, "Let's not make this a thing." The room he had spent his career trying to win didn't punish him. It shrugged. That night the rep texted: "You didn't have to do that. Now he's going to be weird with me." Jonathon admits he made it about himself again, a little. He asked the rep what he wanted to do, and he's waiting for an answer. "The lunch was a movie. This is a Thursday."

He also apologized to Ruby for yelling about her shoes, without blaming it on being tired. She pointed out that he had also yelled about the cereal. Wes told his older brother he was wrong to leave Thanksgiving early. After a long pause his brother said, "Mom made a pecan pie for you. I ate it." The gutter has an appointment for Saturday. Wes's twenty-one-year-old son told his foreman he wired a panel wrong without blaming the drawings, and then texted, "they were bad though."

## From the table: isn't this just blaming myself for everything?

No. The point is to find your part, not to take everybody's. "It's all me, I'm garbage" isn't humility. It skips the harder work of sorting what's yours from what's someone else's and what was just a hard year. If the voice says you're worthless, that's not conviction. Tell someone today.

> "Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts. See if there is any offensive way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting."
> Psalm 139:23-24 (NIV)

David asks God to do the searching. A man searching himself tends to find either nothing or everything. God finds what's actually there, and then he leads.

## Tool on the table: the evening examen

Three questions, about three minutes. Where was God today? What's mine from today, just one thing? What do I need for tomorrow? Then hand it over and go to sleep.

## Carry this

> Find your part. Not everybody's part. Yours.
