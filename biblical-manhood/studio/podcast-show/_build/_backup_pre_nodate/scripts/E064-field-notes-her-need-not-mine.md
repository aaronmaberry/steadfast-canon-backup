# Her Need, Not Mine
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 64 · August 13, 2027

Dani asked Jonathon for three things on her audition day: no text, supper made, and no questions. He made spaghetti. She sat in the driveway for ten minutes before she came in, and he didn't go to the window. Late that night she said one sentence: "I sang it the way I sing it in the car."

Wes asked his mother whether she'd wanted his dad in the house or in the barn when she was sick. "The barn. He asked me once, when you were a baby. That's how he knew." Then: "Ask her, not me. I'm not her."

## When she won't say

Sometimes "I don't know" is true. Sometimes "nothing" means it hasn't been worth the trouble before. Watch what she does for other people, because people give what they want. Ask smaller.

> "Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ."
> Galatians 6:2 (NIV)

## Ask, repeat, do

Ask a small, specific question. Say it back in your own words. Do it within two days, without announcing it.
