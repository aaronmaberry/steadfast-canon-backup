## Reading narrative

# Two Gardens
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 23 · March 23, 2027 · Holy Week

The Bible opens with a man in a garden, and on the night before he died Jesus was in a garden too. The two men did opposite things. Paul says every man is following one of them. The focus mark is Go First, because the second man went first into the worst night in history.

## The first garden

When God found Adam, the first man said, "The woman you put here with me, she gave it to me." The first thing he did when he got caught was step behind his wife.

## Four o'clock

In spring 2018, Wes's sixteen-year-old daughter was caught drinking at a party. His wife called him at work at four in the afternoon. He said he'd be home as soon as he could, then stayed until nine finishing a report that wasn't due for a month. When he got home, his wife said only, "I needed you at four o'clock." That was seven years after the fog years ended. "The first man doesn't move out just because you've had a good stretch."

## The fish

Jonathon's father left when he was nine and has called about twice a year since, five minutes about the weather and the Texans. On Christmas Day his father sent him a friend request, and it's still pending. The profile photo shows a sunburned man on a boat holding a redfish. Jonathon has learned more from that picture than from all those calls. Jonathon admits he's been hiding, partly waiting for Dani to decide for him. He has set a date: Easter Sunday night, yes or no. "Whatever you pick, I'm in," Dani said. "Please actually pick."

## The Word

> "So it is written: 'The first man Adam became a living being'; the last Adam, a life-giving spirit."
> 1 Corinthians 15:45 (NIV)

The last Adam, not the second one. There's no third. On the night of his arrest, Jesus walked out to the soldiers and told them he was the man they wanted.

> "Jesus answered, 'I told you that I am he. If you are looking for me, then let these men go.'"
> John 18:8 (NIV)

"The first man put his wife between himself and God," Jonathon says. "The last man put himself between his friends and the soldiers." Jesus did the saving, once, completely. Our part is to follow the one who walked out.

## What they did

The Bluebonnets lost their first game by one run when a seven-year-old struck out with the bases loaded. Before any parent could reach her, Jonathon walked out to home plate, knelt, and said, "That was a brave swing." "I closed my eyes," she said. "I know. Still brave." Then he walked her back to the dugout, keeping himself between her and the bleachers. Wes walked out to his brother's barn and named the conversation they've avoided for two years: their mother's house and will. "Not at Easter," his brother said. "Tuesday after," said Wes.
