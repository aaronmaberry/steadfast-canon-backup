# Lay It Down
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 61 · August 3, 2027

Jonathon has run a pipeline call every Tuesday evening for four years. It's the last hour of his week that still feels like a leaderboard. If Dani gets into the chorale, Tuesday nights are hers, so he moved the call to the morning before he even knew. Then, at three red lights on the drive home, he wrote a speech telling her about it, and by the third light he heard what it was. "The speech was the paycheck." He didn't give it.

At thirty-one, after a fight, Wes wrote his wife a three-page letter saying he'd die for her. That night the baby woke at two, and he lay there. In the morning she said, "That was sweet. Can you get up with him tonight?"

> "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her ..."
> Ephesians 5:25 (NIV)

Christ didn't give a talk. He gave Himself, for disciples who couldn't stay awake an hour. And He gave Himself for her, for her good, not for the credit. If you keep a receipt, it's a loan.
