# Work It and Keep It
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 54 · July 9, 2027

On Tuesday at noon Wes knocked on a coworker's truck window. The man had been eating lunch alone in his running truck since April. The window came down two inches: "You need something?" Wes asked if he could eat in the air conditioning. "Door's open." They talked about baseball. On Thursday the man said, "You don't have to do this." Wes said, "I know. It's air-conditioned." He doesn't know what's wrong, and he isn't going to ask.

## Is it wrong to like your job?

One of Jonathon's reps likes his work so much he doesn't want his vacation days, and his small group makes it sound like a problem.

> "A person can do nothing better than to eat and drink and find satisfaction in their own toil. This too, I see, is from the hand of God, ..."
> Ecclesiastes 2:24 (NIV)

Liking your work is a gift. Needing it is the problem, and from the inside the two feel the same for a long time. You find out which one you have when the work is taken away for a week.

## The end-of-day shutdown

Write down the first thing for tomorrow. Name what you kept today. Close it, and say out loud, "That's enough for today." Wes found out he's been doing a version of this for twenty years: emptying his pockets on the dresser before he walks into the kitchen.

## Production notes

- **The man in the truck isn't a rescue.** Wes knocks, eats and talks baseball. He doesn't guess on mic, ask, fix, or arrange anything. The man's "You don't have to do this" is the go-first's worse reaction (annoyance), with no "I'll take it." Leave his reason unknown at least through E056.
- **Round 8, one-word replies.** One: the youngest's "Okay?" (then a full sentence about potatoes). The man's "Door's open" is two words.
- **Round 8, kids.** The youngest's "Okay?" is puzzlement, not a read on his father. No kid-catches-dad line.
- **Round 8, the older brother.** No lines. Wes describes the gate from watching him.
- **Wives aren't oracles.** "You've never told me the name of anybody you work with" is a fact Wes partly disputes ("Mostly true"). Dani's "Supper's cold" is a fact, said plainly, not mad.
- **Deadpan payoff:** "I know. It's air-conditioned." Only one.
- **Knee or age beat:** none.
- **Tuesday's title.** The outro names E055 by its new title, "Some Saturdays" (retitled 2026-10-03).
- **Ecclesiastes 2:24's trailing comma.** Kept, with an ellipsis after it on the card and in the spoken line (2026-10-03). Wes lets it trail.
- **Setups for E055 (Some Saturdays, from book ch20; ch20/W20; 2 Cor 5:15; Work for God's Pay; training plug session 20).** The truck continues quietly. Jonathon's August week off. The rep and his days. The Rangers stay bad.
