## Reading narrative

# Grief Report
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 80 · October 8, 2027

Seven months after his mother died, a sixty-one-year-old salesman still drives past the exit to her house and almost takes it. Everyone else seems back to normal. He wanted to know how long grief is supposed to take.

> "Brothers and sisters, we do not want you to be uninformed about those who sleep in death, so that you do not grieve like the rest of mankind, who have no hope."
> 1 Thessalonians 4:13 (NIV)

Paul doesn't tell the church not to grieve. He tells them not to grieve like people with no hope. Grief has no clock. What changes over time is the weight, from a feed sack carried up a hill each day to something in your pocket you can still put your hand on.

## The anniversary calendar

Write the dates down. Set reminders a week ahead. Plan the day on purpose, tell someone ahead of time, and put other people's dates on your calendar too.
