## Reading narrative

# Field Notes: The First Check
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 2 · January 8, 2027

On Tuesday, Wes and Jonathon gave themselves and their listeners a small challenge. One meal with no phone, a real question at the table, and one text to one man: "How's your Tuesday?" Friday is where they report back.

## Check the box

Wes asked his wife what Tuesday is like for her. She told him she drives his mother to physical therapy every week, forty minutes each way, and has since August. He hadn't known. His text to his oldest son got an answer that was funny and then wasn't. "Are you dying?" Wes's verdict on himself: "That's a report card." His son called back and talked for twenty minutes about a decision he hadn't told anyone about.

Jonathon didn't check the box the first night. The phone buzzed in the junk drawer, he got up, and his seven-year-old said, "Daddy, you said." He tried again on Thursday. His wife Dani told him about sleeping from nine to two after a night shift and pretending she isn't tired. He offered to buy blackout curtains. They already had blackout curtains. Then he stopped fixing and listened. His text went to the contractor who invited him to his first men's breakfast, whose wife had surgery the week before. Nobody at work knew.

The rule: the second try counts.

## From the table

A question men ask a lot: how do you start if you've never been the spiritual one at home? Wes's answer comes in three small pieces. Pray one sentence out loud at one meal. Tell your wife plainly, "I want to grow in this. Will you help me?" And read one book of the Bible on your own (Mark is short), then tell her one thing you read. "Your wife doesn't need you to be her pastor," Wes says. "She's waiting to not be the only one."

## Tool on the table: the phone basket

Put a basket where everyone walks in, not on the table. Every phone goes in at supper, starting with yours. Keep a charging cord inside so nobody needs to charge anywhere else. Set the one number that truly matters to ring loud, and let the rest wait.

## Carry this

> The second try counts. Don't let the first one be the only one.
> Somebody at your house is carrying a Tuesday you don't know about. Ask.
