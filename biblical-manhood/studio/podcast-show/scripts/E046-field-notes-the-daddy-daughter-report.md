## Reading narrative

# Field Notes: The Daddy-Daughter Report
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 46 · June 11, 2027

Ruby is seven. At Wes's brother's stock tank she held up a worm and asked whether it could feel the hook. The brother looked at it a long time and said, "Not as much as the fish." She baited her own hook on the first try and caught a bluegill about the size of a phone. On the drive home, half asleep, she told her dad what she thinks about when she's by herself. Part of it was whether her grandfather in Louisiana liked her picture.

## "Fine" is not "go away"

At thirteen, Jonathon said "fine" through his bedroom door every night. If he'd said anything real, everything else might have come out with it. The one man who reached him was a coach who drove him home, asked "You hungry?", and talked about the Astros. About every third time, somewhere around the french fries, Jonathon said something true.

> "a time to tear and a time to mend, a time to be silent and a time to speak, ..."
> Ecclesiastes 3:7 (NIV)

## The drive-through date

Same night every week. She orders, she picks the music, and you don't lecture. Ask one question you don't know the answer to, then eat your fries. If she says no, ask again next week. Keeping the offer standing is the whole point.

If her silence is more than being fifteen, don't wait for the drive-through. Call or text 988.

## Production notes

- **Round 7: the divorced dad's small practical win, no moral.** The ponytail. Jonathon: "I'm not adding anything to it." Wes's "(beat) Twice, not three times." is the episode's deadpan payoff. It's not a lesson. Austin's thread can rest now. His next appearance is optional, and he's still never named.
- **Round 7: older men.** The one quotable line is the older brother's "Not as much as the fish." "She's all right." / "Both of them." is plain and deliberately ambiguous (Ruby and the fish, or Ruby and Jonathon). Don't explain it later.
- **The Louisiana grandfather.** Ruby's "if Grandpa in Louisiana liked my picture" is unresolved. Jonathon doesn't fix it. It sets up E048 (Father's Day when it's complicated) and his first Father's Day text, without promising one.
- **Wes's daughter.** "He said he didn't know I was waiting" is the couple's, and Wes stays out of it. Keep him out on later episodes too.
- **Jonathon's arena:** the stock tank is Wes's brother's place, not the field. The VP noticed him leaving at three (work, light). The field count is unchanged.
- **Tool vs the book.** Book chapter 26 has "a drive, radio off." The show's tool turns that on purpose: her music stays on ("Radio off means 'now we're going to have a talk.'").
- **Teen safety.** 988 is said once, in the rail. The signs are named plainly (stopped eating, self-harm, doesn't want to be here). Nothing nearby is a joke.
- **No host-to-host affirmation.** "That's the top of the scale" is about the brother's "Okay." "Side by side" is a summary.
- **No brand names on mic.** "The drive-through by the high school." The Astros and Corpus are fine.
- **"What'd":** one ("What'd he say?").
- **Setups for E047 (Give Your Son a Name, ch 27, Isa 43:1 paraphrased, Pick Up the Weight, training plug T).**
  - Theo (4), and Jonathon's impatience with his son.
  - Wes's sons (21 and 16). The oldest's job decision stays held for E077.
  - Jonathon's grandfather's name (given to Ruby in spring, canon E038). How a name is carried.
