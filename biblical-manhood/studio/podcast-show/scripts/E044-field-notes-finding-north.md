## Reading narrative

# Field Notes: Finding North
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 44 · June 4, 2027

A twenty-four-year-old works overnights at a warehouse, rides the early bus home, and visits his grandmother at the nursing home on Tuesdays and Thursdays. After Tuesday's episode he wrote a sentence on the back of his work badge: "live with purpose, love hard, leave a legacy." Then he texted Wes that it sounded like a T-shirt at the mall, like something he could buy in three colors.

## Put a name in it

A slogan fits every man who reads it, so it costs none of them anything. The moment he writes his grandmother into the sentence, it stops being a T-shirt. He thought he didn't have "the stuff yet." He has Tuesdays and Thursdays on a bus after a night shift.

> "Give careful thought to the paths for your feet and be steadfast in all your ways."
> Proverbs 4:26 (NIV)

## The one-page house plan

Write one sentence at the top. Before it goes up there, it has to pass three tests: you couldn't buy it on a T-shirt, someone at home could catch you breaking it, and it costs you something. Under it, write three lines that begin with "This means." Your numbers belong here, under the sentence, not in place of it. Add one thing you're saying no to and a date to read the page again. It's a page, not a tattoo.
