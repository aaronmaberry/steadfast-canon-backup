# Field Notes: Mother's Day Field Notes
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 36 · May 7, 2027

The Bluebonnets finally won one for real, in a make-up game under the lights. ## When it's complicated

After the game, the twin's father asked Jonathon a question in the parking lot. His mother is in memory care. She raised two kids alone on night shifts and was hard: no games, no "proud." Now she's gentle with everyone. Last month she held his hand and said, "You have kind eyes. My son had kind eyes." He has been waiting forty years for her to say something like that. She said it to a stranger, and the stranger was him.

> "Her children arise and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praises her:"
> Proverbs 31:28 (NIV)

The verse doesn't say her children call her perfect. It says they stand up and say the good thing out loud, even if it's only one thing. His one thing: "We never once went to school hungry."

## The note

Write it by hand, and keep it short: one true thing, thank you, your name. She won't remember Sunday, Jonathon told him, but a card stays on her table, and the nurses read cards aloud. Every morning she may hear it for the first time.
