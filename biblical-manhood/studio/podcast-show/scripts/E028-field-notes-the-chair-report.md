## Production notes

- **The father stays unnamed** on mic and on the card ("Grandpa lives far away").
- **Round 5.**
  - Dani is wrong, and **it's not settled.** She doesn't concede; this is different from Wes's wife in E026, who owned it.
  - The order is changed (check, tool, question).
  - The single check is Wes's oldest son, with no lowercase text.
  - The only deadpan payoff is "What time?"
  - Ruby's "I know. I'm just saying" is the emotional button, not a joke.
- **The heart pays off.** E025's "like" becomes the true good thing Jonathon can tell Ruby. Don't add a message from the father here.
- **Wes's oldest and the brush** is a generational mirror, told plainly. The Saturday polish (Apr 10) gets reported in E029 or E030. Wes talks, and it's awkward.
- **Mom's birthday: May 18 (new).** The lamp-shopping trip is the weekend of May 15 or 16 (E037/E038 window). It's also near Mother's Day (May 9).
- **The parent chat is circulating the show.** That's a soft in-world note. Don't make it a running bit.
