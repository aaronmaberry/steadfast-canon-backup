## Do not despise

> "Listen to your father, who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old."
> Proverbs 23:22 (NIV)

Despise means more than hate. It means to look down on, to stop taking seriously. Jonathon's mother once bought a car by herself for the first time, and he called the dealership behind her back to get her a better trade. "I took the deal seriously," he says. "Not her." A boy needs his mother's sign-off. A stranger doesn't pay attention. A man takes her seriously without needing her approval.

## The bass

Merle is seventy-one. His wife died a year ago February. He brought her Camry to the church's car-care Saturday, and while it sat on the ramps, the radio still on her gospel station, he sang the bass part along with a quartet. The old mechanic rolled out from under the car. "You sing." "Used to." Now he wants to sing again, and he wonders if that's selfish. Wes doesn't think so. "That's a man who's still alive."

## The work nobody sees

Most of what our mothers did, nobody paid them for. A deacon whose mother died decades ago gives a bag of orange-slice candy to the oldest woman in church every Mother's Day, and only his wife knows why. Wes plans to rehang his mother's dragging screen door without telling her.
