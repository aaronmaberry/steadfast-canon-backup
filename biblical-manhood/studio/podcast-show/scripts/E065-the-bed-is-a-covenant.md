# The Bed Is a Covenant
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 65 · August 17, 2027

At six on a Saturday, Theo climbed into his parents' bed and found them both on their backs, phones up, faces lit. "Are you guys at work?" That afternoon Jonathon moved his charger to the truck in the garage. Dani's phone went in her nightstand drawer, no speech. The second night, without screens, she told him about a nineteen-year-old who came into labor alone and how Dani stayed with her through the delivery on her break.

Wes's father and the neighbor shared a mile of fence for forty years with no paper between them. Whoever saw the post down fixed it. When Wes asked whose fence it was, his father said, "It's the fence." A contract keeps a ledger. A covenant fixes the post.

> "Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral."
> Hebrews 13:4 (NIV)

Honored by all, including the single man and the widower. Kept, the way you keep a fence, every week. And judgment for the man who hides it, mercy for the man who brings it into the light.
