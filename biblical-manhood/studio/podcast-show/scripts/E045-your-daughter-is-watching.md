# Your Daughter Is Watching
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 45 · June 8, 2027

When Wes's daughter was fifteen, he took her to the feed store every Saturday. He asked her the same three questions every week: school, friends, practice. She answered "fine" three times. One Saturday on the way home she asked him, "Daddy, can you ask me something you don't know the answer to?" He drove a mile before he could think of anything.

## Where the good voice goes

Jonathon has a phone voice. It's warm and slow, and it laughs at things that aren't funny. His seven-year-old learned it from the kitchen doorway, holding a cereal bowl to her ear. Later, in the truck, she asked, "Dad, why do you talk nicer on the phone?" Then, "Can I have it?"

> "Impress them on your children. Talk about them when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up."
> Deuteronomy 6:7 (NIV)

Sitting at home, the road, bedtime, the morning. None of those is church. Something is getting pressed into those hours either way, so the only choice you get is what.

## A phone call and a bedtime prayer

Wes called his grown daughter and asked what she'd learned about men from watching him. "I learned to wait," she said. Then she asked what he'd been thinking about all those quiet Saturdays, and he told her the truth. She'd thought he was mad at her. Jonathon prayed over his daughter by name for the first time and borrowed her sentence to finish. She told him he could keep it.
