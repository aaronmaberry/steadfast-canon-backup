## Reading narrative

# The Hill Report
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 50 · June 25, 2027

Wednesday night Wes walked out to the shop for a flashlight and found his sixteen-year-old with the chainsaw clamped in the vise and his phone propped on a coffee can, watching a man in Oregon sharpen a chain. He'd watched it twice. He filed every tooth. Wes stood in the door for about a minute, said "Night," and left.

Jonathon's report was worse. On Monday his four-year-old refused to do his own shoes, cried for six minutes, and Jonathon took over to make a nine o'clock call. By Thursday, Theo was doing them himself. It isn't a straight line.

## Only video games

A man at Wes's Thursday table asked what to do with a fourteen-year-old who only wants games. Jonathon was that kid. After his dad left, a baseball game at a friend's house was the one place where effort paid off. Find out what it's giving him before you take it. Wes would put it in the closet for the summer anyway. They don't agree on that. They agree on the Saturday.

> "Do you see someone skilled in their work? They will serve before kings; they will not serve before officials of low rank."
> Proverbs 22:29 (NIV)

## The Saturday project

One real project with a reason he can see. The same time every week, for a season. Work next to him. Let the phone come as a tool. End with food. One Saturday is an event. Ten is a hill.

## Production notes

- **Book material avoided.** Book chapter 28 and the W23 lecture call screens a machine that fakes the climb ("a thousand levels, and the tap still dry"). The show doesn't use that frame. Its answer comes from Jonathon's history (the game as the one place effort paid) and from Wes's son learning the chain on his phone.
- **Round 8, two-chairs moment 2 of 2 (the batch cap is now used).** Unplug for the summer, or find out what it's giving him first. It's sharper than polite ("That's easy to say with a brother who's got a whole slope of cedar." "That's not fair." "No. It's also where he lives."). It ends unresolved on the closet, with no "I'll keep thinking about it." E051-E056 carry no two-chairs moments.
- **Round 8, go-first went worse.** Theo's furious crying, and Jonathon took over. No "I'll take it." He reports it straight.
- **Round 8, wives aren't oracles.** Jonathon catches his own weak answers. Dani only asks about the car. Wes's wife says "That's your card" and goes back to her book, and Wes admits he wanted more.
- **Round 8, kid lines.** Theo's "I don't WANT to" and the youngest's "Uncle said it's dull" aren't diagnostic of their fathers. Nothing diagnostic in E050.
- **One-word replies.** "Night." / "Night." is a goodnight, not a reply to a hard moment. No "Okay."
- **Deadpan payoff:** "(sips coffee) I'd have taught him wrong." Only one.
- **Knee or age beat:** none.
- **The neighbor's dog.** Pancake doesn't arrive until August (after Theo turns five). This was the neighbor's dog, visiting.
- **Wes's oldest is an electrician's apprentice** (canon). Wes's question about the headset is set for Sun Jun 27. The answer can come in E051 or later, or never. It shouldn't be the family's "Are you sick?" pattern again this batch.
- **Setups for E051 (A Day He Will Not Forget; Josh 4:6-7; Work for God's Pay; book plug).** Jonathon's first widows' ramp Saturday (Jun 26), with Ruby and Theo. The youngest's second cedar Saturday (Jun 26), the creek told once. The oldest's answer (optional).
