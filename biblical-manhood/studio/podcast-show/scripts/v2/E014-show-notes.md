## This weekend

1. **Make your list** of every challenge you've tried since January.
2. **Keep what stuck.** Don't make a speech about it.
3. **Pick one thing that didn't stick** and give it a date.
4. **Start the Thursday text:** one man, every Thursday, "Did you do it?" Same man, same day. One-word answers are fine. If he says no, reply "Next week."

Then tell us the one thing you're keeping at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
