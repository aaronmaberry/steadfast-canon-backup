**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
Everybody's working for something under the paycheck. What's the prize you're actually working for?

Jonathon tells about the ranked email he's opened every Monday at eight for eleven years, before his Bible and before his kids, and the fall he was number one in the country that he can't remember spending with them. Wes confesses his cheap prize has a pulpit: being the man the church calls on to pray, exposed in a grocery aisle while he hoped someone would notice the good coffee.

They explain why the room's pay always feels better on Monday, then open Colossians 3:23-24 and Hebrews 12:2. The reward is an inheritance, not a paycheck, and you can't earn your way in. Jonathon pushes back on heaven as a bribe and on needing his paycheck, then names the one thing Wes doesn't try to fix: he has never heard "well done" from a father. Plus what Mr. Abernathy wrote on the backs of his receipts.

This week's challenge: name your cheap prize, and do one good thing nobody sees. Then tell no one.

## This week's challenge

1. **Name the cheap prize** you've been working for, out loud, to your one man. It's the thing that changes your mood when it shows up or doesn't.
2. **Do one good thing nobody will see.** Then don't tell anybody. Not your one man, and not us.
3. **Married:** pick something at home she won't find.
4. **Single:** the next time you're about to post something you did, don't.
5. **Live alone:** write three men's first names on the back of a card and pray for them by name every day. Don't tell them.
6. **Divorced dads:** stop keeping score with your kids.

Then tell us your cheap prize (not the secret thing) at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
