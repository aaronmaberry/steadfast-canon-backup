Tuesday's challenge was simple: one meal with no phone, one question ("What's Tuesday like for you?") and one text to one man. Here's how it actually went.

Wes asked his wife the question and found out about a Tuesday she's carried alone since August, and he almost fixed it before he heard it. His oldest son answered "How's your Tuesday?" with "Are you dying?" Jonathon missed the first night when his phone buzzed in the junk drawer and Ruby caught him with three words, then tried again Thursday and learned what "pretend I'm not tired" means.

Then a question men ask all the time: how do you start if you've never been the spiritual one? Short prayers, an honest sentence to your wife, one book of the Bible, and a father in Mark 9 who prayed "I do believe; help me overcome my unbelief!" Plus this week's tool: a phone basket that actually works.

## This weekend

1. **Missed Tuesday?** Do it Saturday or Sunday: one meal, phones in a basket, and one real question to whoever's at the table.
2. **Checked the box?** Ask a second person.
3. **Never prayed out loud at home?** One sentence at one meal. "God, thank you for this food and these people. Amen."
4. **Single:** same basket, same question, with whoever comes over to eat. Then tell a brother you're trying.
