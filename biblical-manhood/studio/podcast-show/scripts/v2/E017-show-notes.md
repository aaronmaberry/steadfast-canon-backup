**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
If the trouble is on the inside, what does it look like on an ordinary week? On men it shows up in three shapes: skip the house, run the room, wear the job as your name.

Jonathon is the best dad on the coach-pitch field and skips his own son from four feet away, until Theo asks why he only does the coach voice at practice. A sixty-year-old contractor ran his house like a job site for thirty years, until his wife set down a dish towel and said, "I'm not on your crew." Wes lost a title in a 2019 reorganization, didn't tell his wife for three weeks, and found out she never knew what it said.

Proverbs 14:12: all three look right from the bleachers. The way that's actually right picked up a towel. Plus pushback on leadership, single men, retirement and divorced dads, an argument we don't settle, and "Three kids and your mother."

This week's challenge: name your shape, then do one contrary act this week.

## This week's challenge

1. **Name your shape.** Skip the house, run the room, or wear the job as your name. Just one.
2. **Skip the house:** bring your best twenty minutes home. First twenty minutes in the door, phone away, before the couch.
3. **Run the room:** let someone at home make one real decision, and don't comment. Not afterward, not with your face.
4. **The job as your name:** next time someone asks who you are, answer with something else first.
5. **Single:** give your best twenty minutes to someone who can't help your career.
6. **Married and brave:** ask your wife which shape she sees. Calm night, yours first, don't argue.

Then tell us your shape at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
