**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What kind of man are you on a regular Tuesday? Not your best day. The plain one.

In the first episode of Walk Steadfast, Wes Hart and Jonathon introduce each other, and then correct each other. Wes is fifty-two, from the Hill Country, and has sat at tables with men for twenty-some years. Jonathon is a sales manager in San Antonio, a dad of two, and came to faith at twenty-nine after sitting in a church parking lot for eleven minutes.

Then the real conversation. Jonathon tells how he took Loop 1604 home on purpose for two years to miss bath time, and Wes tells about the fog years, supper in a recliner, and the night his nine-year-old daughter brought her plate and sat on the floor beside him. They open 1 Corinthians 13:11 and Luke 16:10, and Jonathon pushes back on rest, on the big days, and on the man who's great at work and spent at home.

This week's challenge: one meal with no phone, one question ("What's Tuesday like for you?"), and one text to one man.

## This week's challenge

1. Eat **one meal** this week with **no phone**. Not face-down on the table. In another room or in the truck.
2. At that meal ask one question: **"What's Tuesday like for you?"** Then listen. Don't fix anything.
3. **Single:** eat with a roommate, a buddy or your folks, phones away, and ask him the same thing.
4. **Live alone:** invite somebody to supper. If nobody can come, eat at the table, not at a screen, and pray out loud before you eat.
5. **Divorced, with your kids this week:** that's your meal and your question.
6. **Text one man:** "How's your Tuesday?"

Then tell us what your Tuesday looks like at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
