## This weekend

1. **Haven't written it yet?** Write the real sentence on the front of an index card. Leave the back blank.
2. **Haven't read it to a man?** Read it to one, ideally somebody you'll see again Sunday.
3. **Married and still putting off the question?** Ask your wife on a calm night. Say yours first. Don't swing back.
4. **Keep the card** where you'll see it: your Bible, a truck visor, by the coffee maker.

If your sentence is darker than an index card can hold, take it to a pastor or a counselor. There's no shame in that.

Then tell us what yours says at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
