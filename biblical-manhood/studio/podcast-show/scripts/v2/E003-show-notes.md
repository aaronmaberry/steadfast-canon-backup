**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
Who actually sat you down and told you what a man is? For most of us, nobody. We caught a sentence from whoever was standing in the doorway, and we've been living it ever since.

Jonathon tells how his son asked "Daddy, what's a man?" in the bathtub, on the same day Jonathon had told him to "be a man about it." Wes tells about the diner receipt where he first wrote his own sentence, "Pay the bills and don't make trouble," and the thirty-some receipts he found in an old mentor's Bible. Then Jonathon writes his on air: "Win the room."

Jonathon pushes back that manhood talk is already the loudest thing on the internet, and Wes makes the case for a sentence that fits a real weekday instead of a costume. They open Genesis 1:27 and the picture behind "the image of God," and each host tries a better sentence out loud on one of his sons.

This week's challenge: write down the sentence you've actually been living, even if it's a bad one.

## This week's challenge

1. **Write down the sentence you've actually been living.** Not the church one. The real one, even if it's bad. A receipt works.
2. **Married:** ask your wife what she thinks your sentence is. Pick a calm night, say yours first, and don't swing back. Just say thank you.
3. **Single:** ask a friend who's known you a long time, a brother or your dad. Same rules.
4. **Kids at home:** try a better sentence out loud on one of them. It doesn't have to be perfect.

Then tell us what yours says at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
