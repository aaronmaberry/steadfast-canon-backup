Tuesday's challenge: put your hand on one weight with your name on it, and name one you're carrying that isn't yours. Married guys asked, "What do you carry around here that's really mine?"

Wes got good news about his knees and found out his mother will be counting his reps at physical therapy. His wife's two-word answer, "Your family's birthdays," uncovered twenty-seven years of cards she bought, signed and mailed in his name. Jonathon and Dani spent two hours deciding who would raise their kids, he learned the mortgage date, and he found out the Sunday calls he'd been missing were his to make. His nephew discovered his emergency contact was his ex-girlfriend.

Then the question every man asks: what if I'm carrying stuff that isn't mine? Three signs, two places a load can go, and 1 Peter 5:7. This week's tool is a two-column list.

## This weekend

1. **Draw two columns** on a legal pad: "Mine" and "Not mine." Ten minutes.
2. **Sort everything you're carrying.** Can't tell? Ask: if I carry it, does it get lighter for somebody?
3. **Right side:** write whose it is next to each item. Say the sentence to that person, or pray it.
4. **Left side:** write one next step next to each item.
5. **Read the right side to your one man.** Live alone? Text him a picture of the list.

Then tell us what landed on the right side at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
