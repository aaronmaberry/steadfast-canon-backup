## This weekend: the first apology

1. **Go first, even if you're less wrong.** If it's seventy-thirty and you're the thirty, apologize for your thirty.
2. **Name it in their words, not yours.** "I'm sorry I ambushed you." Never "I'm sorry you felt."
3. **Then stop.** Let the silence belong to them.
4. **Further along in faith?** Ask her to teach you one thing. Then start the prayer at supper, even if it's short.
