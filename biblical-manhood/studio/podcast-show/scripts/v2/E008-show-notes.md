## This weekend

1. **Pick one thing** you've been putting off.
2. **Set a timer for ten minutes** and do only that thing, phone in the basket. When it dings, you're allowed to stop.
3. **If it's a phone call,** the ten minutes is getting ready: write down two things to say, then dial.
4. **If it's a doctor,** the ten minutes is making the appointment.
5. **Too tired?** Run the brisket test. If you fail it, go to bed. Tomorrow you'll have ten minutes.
