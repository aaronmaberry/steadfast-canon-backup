**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What does it actually mean for a man to go first, and what do you do when going first will cost you something?

Jonathon tells how he opened the October conversation with Dani by saying, "I've been making you pay for my mom," and what it cost him to stop being the good son. Wes tells how he yelled at his sixteen-year-old in his father's voice, then went first with an apology to a kid wearing earbuds, and got "Okay," twenty minutes of silence, and "Sorry about the stop sign" at the cattle guard.

They open John 10:4: the shepherd walks out front because that's where the snake is, and the sheep follow a voice they've heard on ordinary days. Jonathon pushes back with a father who moved his family four times and called it headship. The bossy man goes first into the decision. The shepherd goes first into the cost. Plus coffee at 5:15, an eighty-one-year-old with a sign-up sheet, a disagreement the hosts leave unsettled, and the first sentence Wes plans to say at his mother's doctor appointment.

This week's challenge: find one place where everybody's waiting, and go first.

## This week's challenge

1. **Find one place where everybody's waiting** for somebody else to move, and go first. One small, true thing before Friday.
2. **Name it to your one man first** so you can't back out.
3. **Married:** say the first sentence of the conversation you two keep not having. A calm night, not the restaurant. Don't grade her answer.
4. **Single:** make the plan. "Dinner Sunday, my place, I'm making chili." Or introduce yourself to the man who sits alone at church.
5. **Divorced dads:** help your kids make a valentine for their mom.
6. **Every dad:** if you blew it with a kid, say "I was wrong" first.

Then tell us how it went at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
