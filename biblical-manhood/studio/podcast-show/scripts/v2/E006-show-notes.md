Tuesday's challenge: write the sentence on the back of your card, pick your weakest mark, tell one man, and ask your wife which mark you do best. Here's what happened.

Wes told his son-in-law his weakest mark, and they stood by the trucks for forty-five minutes. His wife named his best one and asked for more of another: "Go first. With me." Wes also names something he hasn't fixed. Jonathon's contractor friend revealed a binder and a second career, his nephew texted "Pay. I post everything," and Dani said the mark she'd love to see more of is "stop waiting... on your dad."

Then a question from a men's table: do you really need all four marks, or just the one you're weak in? Your strongest mark can carry your weak one, and it can also become your hiding place. Wes reads Philippians 1:6. This week's tool is a sixty-second voice memo, and Jonathon records his live.

## This weekend

1. **Record sixty seconds.** Say your sentence, your weakest mark, and one specific thing you'll do about it tomorrow.
2. **Send it** to your one man instead of a text, or keep it and listen once the next morning.
3. **Look at your strongest mark** and ask whether you've been using it to hide from the hard one.
4. **Single:** nobody's home to hear you say it, so say it to your phone and send it to a brother.

Then send us yours at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
