**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What does a steadfast man actually look like on a regular day? Wes Hart gives the one sentence that goes on the back of your card: a steadfast man stops waiting, picks up the weight, goes first, and works for God's pay, not the room's.

Then Wes and Jonathon take it apart, one mark at a time, each with a weekday picture: a gutter, training wheels, a father's dropped weight landing in his son's yard, the scariest text Jonathon ever sent, and coach-pitch parents saying "great job, Coach." Jonathon worries that "goes first" will be misused and asks whether "pay" means earning your way in. They show where all four marks come from in Philippians 2:5.

Jonathon pushes back that lists don't change anybody and that these aren't just for men, and the two disagree about which mark is hardest. Plus a week of honest one-out-of-four scores, a Christmas money talk, and a customer who heard the truth.

This week's challenge: find your weakest mark and tell one man.

## This week's challenge

1. **Write the sentence** on the back of your index card.
2. **Pick the one mark you're weakest in.** Just one. Tell your one man which one.
3. **Married:** ask your wife which mark she sees you do best. Then, if she wants to tell you, which one she'd love to see more.
4. **Single:** ask a friend the same two questions.
5. **Every night:** before bed, the one-out-of-four check. Did I wait on anything today? Did I pick up what was mine? Did I go first anywhere? Who was I working for?

Then tell us which one at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
