Field Notes, the Friday check-in. Tuesday's challenge: tell one thing you usually explain, with no "because."

Jonathon finally tells his regional VP that the 2019 district deal should have been the rookie's. The VP's answer: "Let's not make this a thing." Then the rep finds out, and he isn't thrilled either. Ruby keeps better records than her dad. Wes tells his older brother he was wrong to leave Thanksgiving early and hears about a pecan pie. The ceiling fan has an appointment. And Wes's twenty-one-year-old apprentice son gets exactly one text out before the "because" comes back.

From the Table: isn't this just blaming myself for everything? No. Find your part, not everybody's part. Psalm 139:23-24, where David asks God to do the searching. If what you hear is "I'm worthless," that isn't conviction. Tell someone today: a pastor, a counselor, your doctor.

## Weekend challenge

1. **Three questions, three nights** (Friday, Saturday, Sunday), about three minutes each.
2. **Where was God today?** Start with the good.
3. **What's mine from today?** One thing, said to God, with no "because."
4. **What do I need for tomorrow?** Ask for it, then go to sleep.
5. **Missed a night?** Don't turn it into a fourth question.

If what you're hearing is "I'm worthless," that isn't conviction. Tell someone today: a pastor, a counselor, your doctor.

Then tell us which question was hardest at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
