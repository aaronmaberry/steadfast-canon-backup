**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
For six weeks we've talked about what got handed to us. Today: what's the part that's yours?

Jonathon tells how he put his name on a twenty-three-year-old rookie's forty-one-school deal, made President's Club and went to Cancun on it. That rookie works for him now. His sister, same dad and same house, would drive back to return a nickel. Wes confesses the headache he didn't have in December 2010, the night his five-year-old was a shepherd.

They talk about the "last sentence" test, why a man who has dealt with his part tells his story short, and Romans 7:18, where Paul puts the problem inside and his next breath is rescue. Jonathon pushes back: I just learned my dad did damage, and now it's my fault? Both are true. If someone hurt you, that's theirs. Plus tacos, "You owe me Cancun," and "Was I a good shepherd?"

This week's challenge: tell one thing you usually explain, to one person, with no "because."

## This week's challenge

1. **Pick one thing you did** that you always explain, the story with a "because" in it.
2. **Tell it to one person with no "because."** One sentence, then stop talking.
3. **Married:** that might be your wife.
4. **Single:** a friend, a brother, or out loud to God in your truck.
5. **Divorced:** say it to God and one man first. Don't use it to reopen the divorce.
6. **The big ones** (an affair, hidden money): talk to a pastor or counselor before you say anything at home.

Then tell us who you told (not what) at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
