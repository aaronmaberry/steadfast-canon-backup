## Reading narrative

# Not in the Lobby
### Walk Steadfast, Field Notes, Episode 58 · July 23, 2027

For years, the first thing Jonathon said to his nephew at Sunday lunch was "Anybody special?" This week he asked, "What's hard at work right now?" and heard twenty minutes he'd never heard: a second-year apprentice electrician, a journeyman who calls him "College," and a job he'd been thinking about quitting. Afterward Jonathon asked him to be one of the men at Theo's morning someday. "Is that a real thing or a show thing?" Wednesday, a text: "you didnt ask about girls sunday. noticed."

## Should I stop dating to focus on God?

A twenty-three-year-old has been seeing a Christian girl for four months, and his singles class was told to stop dating for a year. God isn't in a contest with a good woman. The better question: is she a place you go to God with, or a place you go instead of Him? If you do step back, tell her the truth in person. Don't hand God the breakup.

> "LORD, you alone are my portion and my cup; you make my lot secure."
> Psalm 16:5 (NIV)

## The single man's Tuesday

One weeknight, three things: a table, your hands, and a man ahead. Nothing about it needs a wife, and if a wife comes, she'll be glad you already know how.
