## Reading narrative

# Field Notes: Saying It Out Loud
### Walk Steadfast, Episode 6 · January 22, 2027

This week's challenge asked men to name their weakest mark, tell one man, and (for married men) ask their wives which mark they do best and which they'd love to see more.

## Check the box

Wes named his weakest, stop waiting, and told his 27-year-old son-in-law, a man he'd known for two years and had never told anything true about himself. They stood by the trucks for forty-five minutes. His daughter came out twice to check whether someone was hurt. His wife said he does pick up the weight best, and reminded him of the year he drove her father to treatments. Then she said she'd love more going first "with me."

Jonathon's weakest is working for God's pay. He told the contractor who first invited him to a men's breakfast, who admitted he hasn't told his crew about his wife's surgery. Dani said Jonathon goes first best. He was the one who suggested counseling in their third year of marriage. Then she named what she'd like to see more: stop waiting, "on your dad." A friend request his father sent on Christmas Day is still sitting there, neither accepted nor declined. Wes's response: "You don't have to solve it on a Friday."

## From the table: do I need all four?

Work on the weakest one, Wes says, but the four come together, and your strongest usually carries your weakest. One warning. "Your strongest one can turn into your hiding place." For years Wes fixed sinks and cars instead of having the conversation his marriage needed. "My strength was my recliner. A busier recliner. With tools."

> "being confident of this, that he who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus."
> Philippians 1:6 (NIV)

"You're not the one who has to finish you."

## Tool on the table: the 60-second voice memo

Record sixty seconds. Say the sentence, name your weakest mark, and say one specific thing you'll do tomorrow. Send it to your one man, or listen to it once the next morning. Jonathon recorded his on air (51 seconds).

## Carry this

> Your strongest mark can turn into your favorite place to hide. Watch it.
> Say it out loud. A promise in your head is mush.

- **"(both laugh)."** Four marks, plus a silent Wes laugh.
- **The phone buzz** at the cut is Ruby texting another dog picture (callback to E001). The dog negotiation is a running thread. Wes's "K" reply to the voice memo pays off in E008.
