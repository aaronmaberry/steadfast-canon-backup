## The hit

Packet ranks above this rundown.

A man can look busy and still not have sat down. He skims for the parts that apply to somebody else. He waits on a better week, a wife, a quieter season, a title that will finally make him serious. None of that is this page.

Childhood is not only toys. It is a life that will not take a yes unless the mood is already there. Special days will cover for him. A plain Tuesday will not.

Rest is good. Vanishing and calling it rest is not.

Point at the Introduction and Chapter 1. Do not perform either one.

## Table (packet job, original speech, do not skip)

Mateo asks. Honest, not a quiz. Wait after each. Ellis can sit with the question. He does not rephrase it into a sermon.

1. Where in your week do you keep an aisle instead of a seat?
2. What cover is closest right now: living on options, hiding in a good job, or treating a later season as the real start?
3. Who in reach already knows if you showed up last Tuesday?

If they answer for a man they are not, bring them back. If they write a speech, cut it to one line. If a wounded man is in the chair, do not start with try harder.

## Closer (packet order, do not skip)

Paper out. Phones out for the text. Hosts model it. They do not skip the text because they are on mic.

- **Name it.** Where did you disappear last Tuesday without calling it that?
- **Do this week.** One meal without the phone. One small yes that will still be there if the feeling leaves. Write the move, not a speech.
- **Tell a brother.** Text one man. Ask him what he did with his Tuesday. Let him ask you the same.
- **Carry this.** Tuesday is where a man is made, or he settles.

Ellis lands Carry this. Volume drops. Then he stops.
