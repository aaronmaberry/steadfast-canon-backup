## 3. Hit

**MATEO:** A kid on my crew asked me what a man is. Twenty-two. He laughed first so I could laugh too. Then it got quiet.

**ELLIS:** What did you actually say.

**MATEO:** The weather. The GC. Who was late. I gave him extra words until the question died. I thought I had covered it. I had shrugged.

**ELLIS:** Will asked me last Tuesday. Sixteen. Not joking. We were in the truck. Practice bag still on the floor. He said, what does that even mean, a real man. I talked about working hard and not being a problem. He nodded. I heard the nod. That was mercy.

**MATEO:** Did you know you were shrugging.

**ELLIS:** I knew I was talking. I found out I was shrugging when I got quiet later. Dana did not say anything. She has heard that paragraph.

**[hold]**

**MATEO:** I can keep a crew from coming apart on a bad week. I still did not have four words.

**ELLIS:** That empty does not look like failure. It looks like competence. People ask me what to do about a boiler. They do not ask me this. When they do, the mouth that can run a meeting goes quiet.

**MATEO:** I thought I was the problem. Like wanting a sentence meant I was off.

**ELLIS:** You are not. The world went quiet. That is not in your head.

## 4. Tell on yourselves

**MATEO:** I have a stack. Make money and do not be a problem. Do not be like that guy. Be the hard one on the site and the nice one at church. Whatever the last room rewarded.

**ELLIS:** Mine is useful. A man is the one who does not drop the work. I learned that young. It kept lights on. It did not give Will anything he could stand on.

**MATEO:** Be the hard one still works on me. Wins a Friday. Dies when Andres asks why I am mad at a wire that is not even his.

**ELLIS:** Be the nice one is my cousin of that. Nobody accuses a nice man of having a sentence. They also cannot follow him.

**MATEO:** I believed a clip for three weeks. Borrowed the voice in the truck. Felt tall. Then a younger guy asked me what I meant and I had to search for the clip in my head. Rental.

**ELLIS:** I have procedures. They will keep a floor alive. They will not tell Will who he is. I used enough calm that a man might think I had a definition. He would have been wrong.

**[hold]**

**MATEO:** I sat in polite rooms for years. People. Family. Kindness. I walked out with nothing in my mouth. If I asked, I got a look. I put the hunger in my pocket.

**ELLIS:** Pocketing it does not make it go away. I know. I poured coffee and kept the back wall because sitting would have meant I was in it.

**MATEO:** I wondered if I was the one who was off.

**ELLIS:** You were not.

**MATEO:** I have been loud on a job when a room would clap for it. Then small at 6:30 when a sentence would have cost me. Both are empty. One just photographs better.

**ELLIS:** I never burned a room. I also never quite moved in. I filled the calendar. Other men would have been proud of it. I called the empty mouth peace. It was a closed mouth with a good reputation.

**MATEO:** I used the shrug. Nobody knows anymore, so why try. Sounds wise. I said it to that twenty-two-year-old and watched him file it. That is the part I cannot shake.

**ELLIS:** I said the sentence out loud to you at a coffee you almost skipped. You can be a good man on a site and a missing man at 6:30. I was talking about me.

**MATEO:** I wanted to argue. I did the work instead. I still catch myself handing leftover lines like they are mine.

**ELLIS:** A younger man can smell a shrug. Will can. Your kid on the crew can. They will copy it if we keep using it. That is not a theory. That is last Tuesday.

**MATEO:** I wanted a science fight so I could stay in the pile. I do not get that here.

**ELLIS:** I needed a verse that was not a pile of advice. Genesis 1:27.

**ELLIS:** "So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them."

**[hold]**

**ELLIS:** Not a costume. Not a personality. A making. The Book is not silent. The quiet I grew up in was not His idea.

**ELLIS:** I have despised myself and called it humility. I have blamed the weather and never moved. Same week, sometimes. Neither one handed me a sentence.

**MATEO:** I do not want a comment section. I want four words I can say without looking at my phone.

**ELLIS:** Wanting that is not a defect. The hunger is information.

**[hold. payoff.]**

**MATEO:** If this table is helping, stay with us. Subscribe, and hit the bell if you want next week's talk.

## 6. Houses

**MATEO:** The kid on my crew is already in the weather. If I hand him my shrug, that is on me. I do not get to call that staying out of trouble.

**ELLIS:** Will is already watching my mouth. Vague looks like wisdom in a meeting. It dies in a truck with a sixteen-year-old. I have used that voice.

**MATEO:** Elena has been living under my small sentence while I kept being competent. She did not need a new speech. She needed me to admit the one I was already living.

**ELLIS:** Dana named it without drama. Good provider. Dim after dinner. I hated that it was true. I stayed. I still have to write the ugly one or I will nod and go back to useful.

**MATEO:** I was twenty-six in an apartment that never stopped feeling like a layover. I called it not settling. It was a long childhood with a debit card. A wedding would not have baptized that year.

**ELLIS:** I am forty-seven and I can still lack a sentence. Years do not retire you from this. They make Will more expensive to shrug at.

**MATEO:** I have a man on my crew who is older than both of us. Interesting about the Cowboys. Quiet about this. I used to think that was dignity. It might just be the same empty mouth with more stories.

**ELLIS:** A title will not save it. I can close a week at the hospital and still have been a guest in my own life. The office will not catch that. Will will.

**MATEO:** If you got hurt on the way here, I am not going to joke you through it. I also am not going to make this the night we sit in that house. You can be wounded and still not crazy. Fog is real. Pain is real. Neither one means you are the defect.

**MATEO:** So what have you actually been living. Not the one you wish.

**[hold]**

**ELLIS:** A man is useful, and he does not need a sentence because the work is the sentence. Seeing it written down was ugly. It was also the first honest thing I had done about it in a while.

**MATEO:** Mine is last truck off the site, and he does not need anybody. I would never say that at church. My hours said it anyway.

**ELLIS:** I wanted to skip to a better sentence because we sit here. That is a leftover too.

**MATEO:** Write the one your week already preaches. Read it to one man. Do not explain first. Explanation is how a shrug puts a coat on.
