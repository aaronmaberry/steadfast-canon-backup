## Close, audible

1. **Name it.** "I disappear into ___." One sentence, out loud, tonight. Married men say it to her.
2. **Do this week.** One meal with the phone in another room. If alone, sit at a table for it.
3. **Tell a brother.** Text one man one sentence about your actual Tuesday. Template in script.
4. **Carry this.** "Tuesday is where a man is made, or he settles."
