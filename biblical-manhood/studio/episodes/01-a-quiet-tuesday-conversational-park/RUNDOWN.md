## Closer, audible

1. **Name it.** Name your disappearance in one sentence: "I disappear into ___." Ask the person who lives with you if you cannot fill it in. Married men say it to her out loud.
2. **Do this week.** One meal with the phone in another room. If alone, sit down at a table for it.
3. **Tell a brother.** Text one man about your actual Tuesday. Template provided in script.
4. **Carry this.** "Tuesday is where a man is made, or he settles."
