# CALENDAR-104: Walk Steadfast, 2027

Internal. Two-host video podcast with Wes Hart and Jonathon. **104 episodes**: Tuesday main episodes (odd numbers) and Friday Field Notes (even numbers), Jan 5 to Dec 31, 2027, release time 5:00 AM CT. Seasons follow the church year. Every main episode is tied to a book chapter (all 35 are covered) and, where one exists, the matching training session (W01-W24) or course session. All dates were computed and checked against the 2027 calendar.

## Cadence decision: two a week, with a set review

- **Why two.**
  - In an Oct 2025 survey of 1,200 US adults, 70% wanted new episodes from the shows they follow at least a couple of times a week.
  - Leading conversation shows publish twice a week or more (DOAC, JRE, Huberman), and a short second episode keeps the channel active between full episodes without a second full-length topic.
  - Field Notes is built to be cheap. It's 12-16 minutes with one question and one tool, and it checks the Tuesday challenge, which makes the challenge real (accountability on air).
- **The cost.**
  - About 45-55 minutes of finished generated video a week (a ~35 min main episode plus a ~14 min Field Notes), plus Shorts.
  - Buzzsprout data shows most podcasts publish every 3-14 days. Two a week puts us ahead of the median show, as long as quality holds.
- **Fallbacks, if production cost or quality slips:**
  - **(A)** Field Notes goes audio-only with a static card on YouTube.
  - **(B)** One episode a week, with the challenge check moved to the top of the next Tuesday episode.
  - Change only at a season break, never mid-season.
- **Review gate at E014 (Fri Feb 19).** Keep two a week if:
  - Friday views reach at least 35% of the Tuesday views;
  - average view duration on mains is 35% or higher;
  - production stayed on schedule all of Season 1.
  - If not, switch to fallback A for Season 2.

## Plug key

- **B:** book mention under 20 s, matched to the chapter.
- **T:** training mention under 20 s, matched to the session.
- **FULL:** the E001 launch walkthrough.
- **-:** no mid-episode plug.
- Field Notes never carry a mid plug.
- Every episode, main and Friday, ends with the **standing outro** (SHOW-BIBLE.md, standing outro). It's separate from these plugs.
- The book and the training never appear in the same episode, except E001 and E103 (season premieres carry one or the other).
- No prices on mic. Plug wording is present tense and descriptive ("Wes's book is at walksteadfast.com"), with no "coming", no "out now" and no dates (SHOW-BIBLE section 8a).

## Note on fresh topics

Episodes marked 'fresh' aren't book chapters. They come from the stated scope (every season of a man's life) and the course sessions. Their scripture still follows the NIV rules, and they carry no book plug unless the chapter link is real.
