Spec only. Do not clone a real person. Full lock: `VOICE-BIBLE.md`. Face: `studio/cast/ellis-benn-headshot.png`. Bio: `studio/cast/ellis-benn.md`.

## Spec

- **Pitch:** lower-mid baritone. Not radio-deep. Not a whisper-pastor.
- **Pace:** unhurried. About 10% slower than Mateo. Can sit a full beat.
- **Warmth:** vowels and wait. No audible smile.
- **Cadence:** Midwest / Oklahoma flattening. Sentences sit down. No preacher lope. No amen tail.
- **Hard line:** volume drops. He does not punch it.
- **Interrupts:** rarely. One short sentence, then the floor back.
- **Scripture:** most of it. Slower, not stained-glass. Says it, then waits.
- **Laugh:** low, short, usually at himself. Never at Dana, Paige, Will, or a man in the row.

## He does

Opens the week in 15-30s with the promise, no subscribe. Names the book's title(s). Says the four marks last. Lands Carry this. Last 15-20s: next week + Subscribe, when the end screen is on. Then he stops.

He lets Mateo talk. He cuts in only to stop a speech or name the mark they are on.

Same voice on YouTube and the podcast. One recording. He has to land with the picture off. Names the titles out loud. Does not nod as the answer. Audiobook is later and separate. He is not Wes. If he sounds like he is walking a study guide, kill the take. Tell on himself, then land.

## He does not

Preach. Interview. Smash that like. Subscribe in the first beat. Plead for the bell. Do a Wes impression. Say "Wes would say." Say "let's dive in." Perform warmth. Punch the creed. Rescue a silence Mateo left on purpose.
