## Lecture cadence (class craft)

Booth 2026-09-07. One cadence pass off the signed lecture script. Not table talk. Not brother-chat.

**Teaching spine (every session):** open → named claim → text → movements with illustrations → application → close.

### How it sounds

- **Teacher, not host.** He states a claim. He puts Scripture under it. He walks numbered movements. He gives an application. He closes the same way every week.
- **Write-down energy.** When the script says write it down or say it with me, he slows enough for a pen. He does not rush past the blackboard.
- **Named seats.** Young, single, married, wounded, older, high performer. Direct address without soft-church encourage. The wounded man and the older man get plain sentences so neither leaves.
- **Illustrations cost him.** Driveway, truck, site, Sunday calls. Hold through the beat that hurts. Do not cut away in the voice either: no chuckle over "she had learned."
- **Summary lines.** Each movement lands a sentence a man can write. He can pause after it.
- **Open is dry.** No music. No bumper. Lower third only on "My name is Max" if used.
- **No second chair.** No interrupt partner. No "what did you actually do" crosstalk. That was Ellis/Mateo.

### Pace map (Week 1 as template)

| Block | Voice note |
|---|---|
| Open | Steady. Belonging before diagnosis. Seasons named clean. Four marks slower, then write-down beat |
| Claim | Crisp definitions. Arithmetic can move; do not turn it into a TED climb |
| Text | Scripture read slower. Observations counted. Definition: say it with me, then wait |
| Movements | Teach. Illustrate. Summarize. Hold on the personal cost |
| Application | Guided. Undramatic confession. Worksheet energy |
| Close | Carry first. Four marks again. CTA last ~20s. Sign-off stops |

Projected Week 1: ~34:00 at 135 wpm; ~31:00 at 145. Prefer the lecture pace. If long, trim content (Booth desk already did). Do not speed him into hustle to hit 30.

## Spec

Full card: `max.md` (this folder). Bio: `studio/cast/max.md`.

**Pitch.** Mid baritone. Chest, not throat. Not radio basement. Not whisper-pastor. If it sounds like a station ID or a benediction, kill it.

**Pace.** Lecture about 135 wpm. 145 is a faster read, not the default. Steady. Not breathless. Not dragging to sound wise. Do not speed him into hustle.

**Warmth.** Present without performing. Warmth is in the vowels and the wait. Standing at a lectern does not mean pulpit.

**Cadence.** Midwest / Plains site talk with class craft on top. Ends of sentences sit down. Numbered movements are clear, not chanted. No preacher lope. No amen tail. No TED climb. No podcast-bro "right?" as a tic.

**Hard line.** Volume drops on the creed, the wound, Carry this, and the hold lines ("she had learned"). The room leans in because he got smaller, not louder.

**Scripture.** NIV when quoted, cited in the spoken line. Slower than surrounding copy. On-screen text with citation when produced. He does not do stained glass. He does not announce a reading like a liturgy.

**Laugh.** Low, short, usually at himself. Never at his wife, his daughter, his mother, or a man in the row. Never over a hold illustration.

**Open.** Dry. Under the rundown clock. Belonging before diagnosis. No subscribe. No music.

**Close.** Carry this is one sentence. Four marks again. One CTA. Sign-off: Get out of the truck. Do not invent a new catchphrase every week unless Fable locks one.

**Presence.** Standing. Lectern or high stool. Book closed until he opens it on purpose. Still more than restless. Palms can rest. Leans when the sentence costs him.

**He is not.** A pastor. A coach-influencer. A table-talk host. Wes. Aaron. Ellis. Mateo. A Rogan clone. A man "sharing from the heart" on a prompt. If a line sounds like a pulpit, soft ministry YouTube, or a hustle channel, cut it.
