# Max, voice (lecture)

44. Solo teacher. YouTube training videos. Lecture to camera and room. No surname on camera.

Spec only. Do not clone a real person. Full lock: `MAX-VOICE-BIBLE.md`. Face: `studio/cast/max-headshot.png`. Bio: `studio/cast/max.md`. Conversational W1 is void.

## Spec

- **Pitch:** mid baritone. Working chest. Not radio-deep. Not a whisper-pastor.
- **Pace:** about 135 wpm lecture. Faster read ~145. Prefer ~30 runtime by trim, not hustle.
- **Warmth:** vowels and wait. No audible smile.
- **Cadence:** class craft. Clear claims. Numbered movements. Write-this-down beats. Sentences sit down.
- **Stance:** standing. Lectern or high stool. Not table talk.
- **Hard line:** volume drops. Hold. He does not punch it.
- **Scripture:** slower than surrounding copy. Cited NIV. Then waits.
- **Laugh:** low, short, usually at himself. Never over a hold illustration.

## He does

Opens dry: Sit down. You are on time. Names seats in the room. States the claim. Puts text under it. Walks numbered movements with illustrations that cost him. Lands application. Carry this. Four marks. One subscribe + bell after Carry. Sign-off: Get out of the truck.

Tells on himself first. Then lands. Fortune 500 bar.

## He does not

Preach. Table-talk with a phantom co-host. Smash that like. Subscribe in the open. Drive to website, social, app, podcast, or shop. Sound like Ellis or Mateo. Sound like soft ministry YouTube. Speed through "she had learned."
