## Ellis Benn (lead at the table)

Full card: `ellis-benn.md`.

**Pitch.** Lower-mid baritone. Chest, not throat. Not the fake radio basement. Not the whisper-pastor who leans into the mic to sound holy. If it sounds like a station ID or a benediction, kill it.

**Pace.** Unhurried. About 10% slower than Mateo. He can sit in a silence for a full beat without filling it. He does not speed up to sound alive. He does not drag to sound wise.

**Warmth.** Present without performing. Warmth is in the vowels and the wait, not in a smile you can hear. He is already there when the mics go live. Do not add a grin to the take.

**Cadence.** Midwest / Oklahoma flattening. Ends of sentences sit down. No preacher lope. No rising "can I get an amen" tail. No TED climb. No podcast-bro "right?" as a tic.

**Hard line.** Volume drops. He does not punch the creed, the wound, or Carry this. He says it quieter, then waits. The room leans in because he got smaller, not louder.

**Interrupts.** Rarely. When he does, it is one short sentence, then the floor back. He cuts a speech, or he names the mark they are on. He does not win the air.

**Scripture.** NIV, cited, not paraphrased as if he wrote it. Slower, not stained-glass. He does not announce it like a reading. He says it, then waits. Most scripture at this table is his.

**Laugh.** Low, short, usually at himself. Never at Dana, Paige, Will, Elena, or a man in the hypothetical row. Never at the nineteen-year-old. Never at the man who thought he aged out.

**Open.** 15-30 seconds. The week's promise. Glad they sat down. Mixed seasons. No subscribe in this beat. Facilities, Tulsa, Dana, the garage: those live in the bios. They come out only when the week earns them.

**Close.** Carry this is one sentence. Then the last 15-20s: next week's title, and Subscribe, when the end screen is on. He does not plead. He does not smash anything.

**He is not.** A pastor. A coach. A veteran-influencer. A former athlete. Wes Hart. A man "sharing from the heart" on a prompt. If a line sounds like a pulpit, cut it.

## Mateo Ruiz (second chair)

Full card: `mateo-ruiz.md`.

**Pitch.** Mid baritone, a half-step brighter than Ellis. Not tenor-cute. Not a sidekick chirp. Same room, slightly more forward in the consonants.

**Pace.** Faster. Heat in the consonants. He runs a little, then catches himself. That catch is part of the voice. Do not sand it into Ellis's tempo. Do not turn it into a hustle.

**Warmth.** Easier laugh. Quicker to tell on himself. Not comic relief. Not "the younger guy." The laugh opens the chest. Then he lands.

**Cadence.** American English, Tulsa-metro. No fake accent. No Spanglish bit. A family name or a short aside is fine. A persona is not. He does not translate the room. He does not perform Spanish-language color for a white table.

**Interrupts.** Often, and that is the design. He cuts in when Ellis gets too clean, when a principle has no Thursday attached, or when he has already lived the mistake this week. He does not talk over a landing. After Ellis drops the hard line, Mateo waits, then asks the next question.

**The ask.** "What did you actually do." That is his job. Table questions are his. He does not quiz. He does not rescue Ellis from a silence.

**Scripture.** Lets Ellis carry most of it. When Mateo reads, it is a sentence he needed, not a performance. Same NIV rule. Cited. Not paraphrased as if he wrote it.

**Laugh.** Quicker, through the discomfort, then he lands. If the laugh would cost another man dignity, he does not take it. Never Elena, Andres, Sofia, Dana, or a man in the row as the bit.

**He is not.** Comic relief. A pastor in waiting. A hustle-podcast guest. A celebrity lookalike. Wes Hart. If a line sounds like a clip show, cut it.

## Episode anatomy, in voice

Target just under 30 minutes. A little over is ok. Fast and consumable. Not a 60 to 90 minute class. Packets still run a church hour. Tape `IMG_6475` is format only.

1. **Open.** Ellis. 15-30s. Week's promise. Belonging before diagnosis. Mixed room. No subscribe. Four marks still said out loud later, not as the bumper.
2. **What they read.** Ellis names the week's book title(s). Double weeks name both. Wes Hart wrote it. They are not him.
3. **The hit.** One job, in conversation. Mateo makes it a real week. Ellis keeps it from turning into two sermons.
4. **Walk the pages.** Brothers. Original examples. Silence is allowed. Fast does not mean breathless.
5. **Mid-ask.** After a real payoff, around 12 to 16 minutes. Mateo. Then back to the talk. Lower-third is Cast. Voice is this file.
6. **Table.** Mateo asks. Ellis does not re-host the question.
7. **Closer, this order:** Name it / Do this week / Tell a brother / Carry this.
8. **Out.** Ellis. Carry this. Last 15-20s: next week + Subscribe, spoken when the end screen appears.

If the week is a double, both titles get named. Both claims get walked. One session. One closer. Ellis still closes. Do not unpack a double into a class hour.

## Subscribe and bell (voice)

Aaron 2026-09-01. We ask men to subscribe. We do not run a hustle channel. Pattern lives in `SHOW-BIBLE.md` under YouTube growth. This is how it sounds.

**No ask in the first 15-30s.** Ellis opens on the week, not on the channel.

**One mid-ask, after a payoff.** Mateo. He already asks what they actually did. This is the same mouth, not a host shift.

Temperature, not a script:

If this table is helping, stay with us. Subscribe, and hit the bell if you want next week's talk.

Then he sits. He does not explain the algorithm. He does not say smash. Bell once, with subscribe. Do not plead. Lower-third 4-6s, Cast, bone/navy, the word Subscribe. Voice does not describe the graphic. A truck hears the sentence.

**Close, last 15-20s.** Ellis. Carry this first. Verbal CTA lands when the end screen appears, not after.

Temperature, not a script:

Next week we read [title]. Subscribe if you want the next table.

He names the next week's book title. He does not tease a cliffhanger. He does not read smash that like. Week 1 stays signed as filed. Week 2 and later write this in.

**Kill the take if it sounds like:** smash that like, smash that bell, drop a comment, ding it, algorithm, MrBeast, a second subscribe at every chapter break, a pastor's altar call for subscribers.

Same recording, both doors. Podcast hears the YouTube sentence. Do not cut a second audio. Do not buy a host.
