## 4. Movement one. The seven shapes [10:00]

**MAX:**
Movement one. The seven shapes. Find yours.

Locked drawer. Everything bad that ever happens to a man happens in that room. Fortress. It looks like accepting responsibility. It is built to keep out exactly what the man needs. Scoreboard. Saul had this. It drove him to try to kill the young man who had just won a battle for him. A man always measuring can never be glad for another man's win and can never rest in his own. Podium. Jesus hated it more than any other kind. Already paid in full from people who do not really know them. Throne. Colossians three, nineteen.

Husbands, love your wives and do not be harsh with them.

God saw it coming. A fence in a single sentence. Contempt, the throne's quieter brother. Recliner. On a man it does not look like laziness. It looks like a fellow who has earned his evening, and it costs his house the rafters.

I have worn more than one. The fortress was my favorite work shirt for years. Ask how the hospital job went and I was fine. Ask what I needed and I needed nothing. Crews leaned on me. Home got the weather. I called it strength. It was a corner post with no foundation, and the people nearest me paid for the lean.

Write the list. Circle one or two. Do not circle none. Sin is deceitful. You will need the tests later.

Movement one, summarized. Write this down. Name the shape before you call yourself fine.

## 5. Movement two. David on the roof [15:00]

**MAX:**
Movement two. David on the roof, and why competence lied.

If it could take the best man of his generation it can take you. It starts with passivity that does not look like sin. Earned rest. Empty hands. Roof. Appetite. Power instead of courage. Next hour instead of God's reward. Then the drawer works for months. The kingdom runs. Surfaces stay clean. Bones waste anyway.

I watched a superintendent run three sites clean for a year while his marriage rotted in a locked phone and a second set of books. Early closes. Owner love. Kids reading his truck the way Uriah's brothers never got to read a king coming home honest. Nobody at church knew. Competence is not righteousness. A man can run a kingdom, a church, a household, while he rots.

Notice what did not wake David. His own quiet. The Spirit of God had to send a Nathan. That is almost always what it takes. A wife who finally says it. A brother who will not take fine. A statement in the mail. The hand of God getting so heavy the man cannot stand. That is why you need a Nathan, and why you need to be one. Brothers around a table exist so somebody can say four words in your throne room.

Movement two, summarized. Write this down. Surfaces can win while bones waste. You need a Nathan.

## 6. Movement three. Five words and the tests [20:00]

**MAX:**
Movement three. Five words, and checking the glass.

I have sinned against the LORD. No she came out on the roof. No Uriah should have gone home. No I am the king and I have carried a lot. The sentence stops at the period. That is what a man's confession does. Forgiveness in the same breath.

You are not going to see these shapes by looking harder. You will need tests.

Drawer test. What you would not want your brother to see. Phone, history, accounts, calendar, friendship. If there is a drawer, there is a problem, whatever is in it. The drawer itself is the problem. A man with nothing hidden has nothing to defend. A man with nothing to defend is free.

No test. When you last let somebody tell you no and accepted it. If you cannot remember, you are on a throne.

Wife test. If married, ask her in a quiet moment where she sees you hiding and where you are harsh. Do not argue. Listen. Thank her. If single, ask your closest brother or your mother. Somebody has been watching.

Podium test. What you do only when someone is watching. The gap between the lot and the cab. The gap between Sunday morning and an unwatched night. Whatever is in that gap is what you are performing.

Throne test. Who in your house is afraid of you. Not who respects you. Who is afraid. If the answer is not no one, get off the throne and onto your knees in front of the people who have been reading your weather.

I ran the wife test once after a week of cab storms and foyer smiles. She did not raise her voice. She named the gap in one sentence. I wanted to defend the early closes. I said thank you instead. The drawer got lighter that night because a Nathan had been living down the hall and I had been calling her management.

Movement three, summarized. Write this down. Five words, no but. Run the tests. Thank the watcher.
