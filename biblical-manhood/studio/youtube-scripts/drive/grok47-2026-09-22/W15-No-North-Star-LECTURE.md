## 2. The claim [04:00]

**MAX:**
Chapter seventeen is titled No North Star. Here is the claim.

[Slide. The wall does not get to argue with the string.]

The wall does not get to argue with the string.

Write that down under the four marks. You do not have to understand why a thing does not move to be saved by it. Hold God's Word as the plumb line against your life, or you will keep steering by stars that move with you. Define the moving stars so you can name yours.

Father. What he did was what a man does, until you found he was moving too. Other men. I make more than my brother-in-law. I am home more than the crew. I never cheated like the deacon. Crooked walls against crooked walls. Culture. What everybody knows a man should be keeps jumping. Feelings. The most moving star there is. It changes with sleep and blood sugar and what you ate. Success. If it is working it must be right. David ran a kingdom well for a year with a murdered man in his drawer. Success is a wall that has not fallen yet.

What the claim does not say. It does not say fathers, other men, culture, feelings, or success are worthless. It says none of them is a fixed point. It does not say you must finish Leviticus in February. It says ten minutes with a question is enough light for the next step.

Hold that. Scripture under it.

## 3. The text [07:00]

**MAX:**
Judges, chapter twenty one, verse twenty five. NIV. The saddest summary of a people I know.

[Slide. Judges 21:25 NIV]

In those days Israel had no king; everyone did as they saw fit.

Three observations. Take them down.

Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path.

Third. Second Timothy three, sixteen and seventeen.

All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness, so that the servant of God may be thoroughly equipped for every good work.

Teaching what is true. Rebuking when you are off. Correcting how to get back to plumb. Training until your hands build true. Thoroughly equipped. A man with a fixed point.
