Sit down. You are on time.

Both belong together, because you cannot tell if a thing is broken if you never read the packet, and you cannot walk the second Man's way if you do not know you were born in the first.

This is Steadfast Men. The Way of Godly Men. My name is Max. Session sixteen of twenty four. Same shape. Claim. Text. Movements. Application. Close. One session a week. Notes by hand. Do not binge.

A seed packet is taped to the fridge. Tomato. Full sun. Before anything comes up, the packet says what the thing is for. You cannot tell if the row failed if you never read the packet. A bare patch is just dirt until you know it was supposed to be fruit. Every man I have sat with has wanted to know the same thing under the other things. Not how to fix the marriage or the anger or the son, though he asked about those. Underneath: what was I made for. What is the shape before I bent it.

And then a man stands in the strip of dirt along the garage after the kids are in. Hose. Tomato cage. Dirt on his boots. It does not look like a garden that could decide a life. Eden did not look like much from the road either. Neither did the place He prayed with His face in the dirt. Two gardens. Two men. Two nights when everything was on the line.

## 2. THE CLAIM [04:00]

**MAX:**
Two chapter titles. Two claims. One session.

Chapter eighteen: Before the Fall.

[Slide: You were made to work it and keep it. The job came before the curse.]

Adam as blueprint: made in God's image, blessed before performance, placed, given work with dirt in it and told to guard it, given a word and words of his own, told it was not good to be alone, given a bride, seen and unashamed, given rest. That is what you are for. You are not off the blueprint. You are on it, and it still fits.

Chapter nineteen: Two Men, Two Ways.

[Slide: Two gardens. Which man's way will you walk this week.]

Through the disobedience of one man the many were made sinners. Through the obedience of one man the many will be made righteous. You were born in the first man. God offers to move you into the second. The four marks are His face. Walk His way through your small gardens.

Write both claims under the four marks. What they do not say. They do not say you become a man only when you marry. Adam was a full man before a bride. They do not say try harder to be Jesus. They say be in Him, then walk His way with His Spirit. They do not say the gardens are large. Adam's afternoon was one piece of fruit. Size of garden is not size of what is decided in it.

Hold that. Scripture under both.

## 4. MOVEMENT ONE: THE SHAPE BEFORE THE BEND [10:00]

**MAX:**
Movement one. The shape of a man before the fall.

Made on purpose in an image. Formed and breathed. God got close. Blessed before he had done anything. Given an enormous job that makes you feel alive when you build, plant, fix. That is not only ambition. It is blueprint. Subdue and rule meant flourish, not bully. Placed somewhere specific. Most men treat where they are as a waiting room. A man always looking toward the next place never tends the one under his boots. Work with dirt. Guard the fence. Word and a line. Wide freedom, one no. Words of his own: naming that lands. Not alone: wife, brothers, man ahead, church. Bride and speech. No shame. Rest: seven days, not a machine.

I ignored rest and place for years. Every garden was the next job. Boots never stayed. The blueprint was still true while I was malfunctioning and calling it drive. When a season forced me to stay put, I found the packet on the fridge had been right the whole time: full sun, specific dirt, work it and keep it.

Movement one, summarized. Write this down: image, blessing, place, work, keep, word, words, not alone, bride, rest. Name which piece is most bent.

## 6. MOVEMENT THREE: THE FIRST GARDEN [19:00]

**MAX:**
Movement three. The first garden. Chapter nineteen.

Serpent comes. Does not go to the man. Goes to the woman. Twists the word. Suggests God is holding out. Where is the man. Genesis three, six: she gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate. He was with her. Three words indicting men ever since. He received the command alone. Told to work and keep. When the thing he was supposed to guard came through the fence, he stood there. Watched. Ate. First sin was silence while the word was twisted: passivity, first mark inverted.

Then hide. I was afraid because I was naked; so I hid. Third mark inverted: cowardice. Then blame: the woman You put here. Second mark inverted. Under all of it the fourth: nearer reward, be like God now, fruit over Father.

Four marks, four inversions, one afternoon. Passive, evasive, hiding, grabbing. Default of every man since. Then curse: thorns, death, exile. And mercy: clothes from an animal that had to die. First covering cost a life. And Genesis three, fifteen: a son of the woman who will crush the head. Second Man promised in the first garden.

I know the kitchen version. Wife says something not quite fair. Every fiber wants quiet or fire. That kitchen is the garden. Silence or blame is Adam. Soft clear word is the other way. I have taken Adam's way more Tuesdays than I want in this room. Naming that is how you stop calling it personality.

Movement three, summarized. Write this down: with her, silent, hid, blamed, grabbed. Default. Covering promised.
