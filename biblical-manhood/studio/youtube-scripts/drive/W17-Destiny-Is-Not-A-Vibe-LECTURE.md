## 2. THE CLAIM [04:00]

**MAX:**
Chapter twenty is titled Destiny Is Not a Vibe. Here is the claim.

[Slide: Destiny is serving God's purpose in your own generation.]

Destiny is not a vibe. It is serving God's purpose in your own generation, in the place He put you, for the people He gave you, until it is finished.

Write that down. Define the words so you do not hear them loosely.

Destiny, in this room, is assignment. Not passion hunting. Not a sense that something large is about to arrive. A job handed by Someone above you, done among the people and in the place and time God put you.

Vibe means the weather report inside your chest. Useful for knowing if you slept. Useless as a commission. Moses argued. Jeremiah said he was too young. Gideon asked for signs. Jonah paid for a boat the other way. If feelings were the test, none of them would have gone.

What the claim does not say. It does not say feelings never come. Sometimes they come after the going. Sometimes they never come, and the work still counts. It does not say small work is fake destiny. It says the small day is where destiny happens. It does not say place does not matter. It says your place already has an address.

Hold that. Scripture under it.

## 3. THE TEXT [07:00]

**MAX:**
Acts, chapter thirteen, verse thirty six. NIV. Paul walking through Israel's history, and when he gets to David he says this.

[Slide: Acts 13:36 NIV]

Now when David had served God's purpose in his own generation, he fell asleep; he was buried with his ancestors and his body decayed.

Three observations. Take them down.

First. Served God's purpose in his own generation. Not discovered his passion. Not found what he was born to do. Served. Destiny is a service word. God's purpose, not David's. David had plenty of purposes of his own, and the ones that came from him nearly wrecked everything. The one that came from God is the one Paul is still talking about. Then he died and his body decayed, which Paul includes so you understand that even David's destiny was not about David lasting. It was about the purpose lasting.

Second. Ephesians two, ten. For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do. The works are already laid out. Your job is not to invent them. It is to do the next one. That frees you from the terror of missing your one true purpose. A man hunting a vibe lives in fear he will choose wrong. A man who knows the works were prepared gets up and does what is in front of him.

Third. John four, thirty four. My food, said Jesus, is to do the will of him who sent me and to finish his work. Finish. Not feel fulfilled by it. Paul did not say he found his passion. He said he fought the fight, finished the race, kept the faith. Completion words, from a cell, in places he did not choose.

Say it with me. Served God's purpose in his own generation. Assigned. Local. Finished by faithfulness.

Four movements. Let's go.

## 4. MOVEMENT ONE: THREE LIES [10:00]

**MAX:**
Movement one. Three lies you cannot put down until you have looked at them.

Lie one. You will know it when you feel it. Somewhere out there is a life that will finally feel right, and the not-feeling-right is proof you have not found it yet. A feeling is a weather report, not a commission. The feeling, when it comes, comes after the going. Sometimes it never comes at all, and the work counts anyway.

Lie two. It is big. A real destiny, you are told, has a platform and a story people tell. So a man who has spent years welding, raising children, and showing up for his neighbors concludes he missed it. Jesus praised a servant for being faithful with a few things. Who dares despise the day of small things. God's eyes range the whole earth and rejoice at a man laying a stone. The small day is not the waiting room for destiny. It is where destiny happens.

Lie three. It is somewhere else. The next job. The next town. After a wedding. After the kids are grown. After you retire. Destiny stays one move away, and the move keeps moving. God placed the man. He put him in a specific place and told him to work that one. Paul told the Athenians that God marked out appointed times and the boundaries of their lands. You are not in your town and your century by accident. The purpose is here, and it will not be more here somewhere else.

I lived lie three for a stretch of years. Every site felt like a layover. I called it ambition. It was leaving the slip on the pallet and looking past the truck. When a season forced me to stay put, the fittings were still going to Dock C. The assignment had been printed the whole time.

Movement one, summarized. Write this down: feel it / big / somewhere else. Name which lie has been steering you.

## 5. MOVEMENT TWO: THREE TRUTHS AND FIVE CLUES [14:30]

**MAX:**
Movement two. Three truths, then five clues for finding what is already in your hands.

Truth one. Destiny is assigned, not discovered. You do not find it the way you find a good fishing hole. It was prepared before you arrived. Ephesians two, ten again. Handiwork. Prepared in advance. Take the slip.

Truth two. It is local. Your purpose has an address: a wife's name, children's names, a street, a crew, a church, the men who already know your face. God's purpose for David was Israel, not the whole world. Esther's cousin told her the warning that still holds: if you stay silent, relief will come from another place, and you will have missed your part. For such a time as this. Not some other time. Not a bigger seat. This one.

Truth three. Destiny is finished by faithfulness, not by feeling. It is a work you complete, not a state you achieve. Assigned. Local. Finished by showing up. Every day of it looks ordinary. At the end there is a well done from the only One whose opinion of your life will last.

Now the five clues. Look at the first shape: work a place and keep it, carry a word, speak, do not be alone, love a bride if you have one, rest. Look at your place: town, job, street, church. If God moves you, He will move you. Look at your people: those names are your destiny more than any title. Look at your hands: use the gift you have, not the one you wish you had. Look at this stretch: not the one you left and not the one you are waiting for.

The young man on the dock stayed. Not because the warehouse felt like a calling. It did not. He stayed because that older hand told him to stay three years no matter what and see what God did with a man who stopped leaving. He runs a crew now. Two of those men have started coming to church because of how he treats them. He still says he does not have a calling. He has a purpose. It turned out to be wherever he stopped setting the paper down.

Movement two, summarized. Write this down: assigned, local, finished. Place, people, hands, stretch, first shape. One line under each.

## 6. MOVEMENT THREE: THIS STRETCH AND THE NEW NAME [19:00]

**MAX:**
Movement three. This stretch, seat by seat, and the name your story gave you versus the name God keeps.

If you are single and waiting for a wife or a better title to deliver the assignment, hear the man with the slip. Your destiny is not on the far side of a wedding or a promotion. It is the next assigned thing. Stay and work it.

If there is a baby in your house, your destiny currently weighs about twelve pounds and does not sleep. There is no larger assignment God will ever give you than the one crying in the next room, and the fact that it does not feel like a calling is the point.

If you are in the divorced seat, you may think the story is over, or at least the good part of it, and it is not. Joseph's destiny ran straight through a prison. Peter's ran straight through the worst failure of his life, and Jesus told him what would come after. Luke twenty two, thirty two: I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith may not fail. And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers. Peter's failure did not end his purpose. It became part of the equipment for it. The men who will listen now, some of them, are the ones who will not talk to anybody else.

If you are older, his own generation is still yours. This one. The purpose is not behind you. They will still bear fruit in old age. A breakfast table. A class. A word by a truck. None of that is a vibe.

Everybody has a name his story gave him. The guy whose dad left. The guy who blew up his marriage. The guy who never got the promotion. Those names are real. They are not your name. They are the first man's names: naked, afraid, hiding, blaming. The master story gives you another: made in the image, placed, given a work, son of the Father who does not leave, in Christ, servant of God's purpose in your generation.

Revelation two, seventeen: To the one who is victorious, I will give a white stone with a new name written on it, known only to the one who receives it. God has a name for you that nobody else knows yet. One day He will hand it to you on a stone, and everything your story called you will be gone.

Most of your destiny gets carried out inside a house. That is where we go next week and after: the wife, the kids, the bed, the table. Rooms with your family's names on them. Destiny without a house talk is incomplete. Destiny that waits for a house to start is the waiting room lie again.

Movement three, summarized. Write this down: this stretch has a name on it. Story name versus new name. Stay and work the slip.
