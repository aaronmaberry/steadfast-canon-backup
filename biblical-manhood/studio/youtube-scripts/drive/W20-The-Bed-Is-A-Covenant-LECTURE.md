## 2. THE CLAIM [04:00]

**MAX:**
Chapter twenty five is titled The Bed Is a Covenant. Here is the claim.

[Slide: The bed is a vow kept with a body. A covenant has all of you.]

The bed is a vow kept with a body. A contract has two ends. A covenant has all of you.

Write that down. Define the words.

A contract protects you if the other party fails. It is built for the exit. A covenant binds you regardless. It is built for the staying. In a contract you exchange things. In a covenant you exchange yourselves, which is why a covenant always comes with a sign: a ring, a cup, a body.

The old English wedding rite said so. With this ring I thee wed, with my body I thee worship. Worship there meant honor, worth ship. His body would spend the rest of his life telling his wife what she is worth. The bed is where that vow gets kept or broken. It is the marriage's sign, renewed as often as two people are able.

What the claim does not say. It does not say the bed is the whole marriage. Last week was look up and lay it down. It does not say desire is dirty. God built the bed before the fall, naked and unashamed, and called it good. It does not say single men are off the hook. Every choice about your body right now is a choice about a covenant you have not made yet.

Hold that. Scripture under it.

## 3. THE TEXT [07:00]

**MAX:**
Genesis, chapter two, verses twenty four and twenty five. NIV.

[Slide: Genesis 2:24-25 NIV]

That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and they become one flesh. Adam and his wife were both naked, and they felt no shame.

Three observations. Take them down.

First. One flesh. Naked. No shame. Not recreation where the other person is equipment. Not dirt done quickly with a little shame. One thing, nothing hidden, called good. Hebrews thirteen, four: Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral. Kept pure: not quiet, not minimal, kept for what it is for, between the two it belongs to, and honored. God is not embarrassed by the bed. He built it. He guards it.

Second. First Corinthians seven, four. The wife does not have authority over her own body but yields it to her husband. In the same way, the husband does not have authority over his own body but yields it to his wife. Your body is for her. The bed is not about your release. It is about her delight. The husband who goes in asking how she is made and what would bless her gets more out of the bed than the man who takes.

Third. Ephesians five, thirty one, then the mystery. For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh. Paul says this is a profound mystery about Christ and the church. The bed preaches. Exclusive, generous, present, patient: a small picture of how Christ loves the church. Break the covenant with body or eyes and you preach a lie about God.

Say it with me. One flesh. Kept pure. Body for her. The bed preaches.

Four movements. Let's go.

## 4. MOVEMENT ONE: WHAT THE COVENANT MEANS IN THE BED [10:00]

**MAX:**
Movement one. What the covenant means in the bed.

Exclusive. Drink water from your own cistern. Jesus took it inside: the man who looks lustfully has already committed adultery in his heart. A husband faithful with his body and unfaithful with his eyes has a leak in the cistern, and his wife can tell the water is low even when she cannot find the crack.

Generous. Body for her. Ask how she is made. Ask what would bless her.

Present and patient. A man can be absent in bed the way he is absent in the recliner. She knows the difference between being with you and being used to get somewhere else. Her body runs on a different clock. Honor it. Hurry is a kind of theft. Tenderness is the old vow kept.

Never a weapon. Not withheld as punishment. Not demanded as payment. Not kept on a scoreboard. The moment the bed becomes a way to get your way, it stops being a sign and becomes a negotiation, and Eden is gone from it. If the bed has gone quiet, the answer is never to make her pay. It is to find out what is wrong, which is almost never about the bed.

Honest. Most couples never say a sentence about it. Talk gently, not in the bed, not after a disappointment. Ask what she would want more of and less of.

Protected. Beds go quiet from neglect long before age. A man who only touches his wife to initiate will find one day that she flinches at his hand, because he taught her his touch always wanted something.

That last one was Mitch. He had been counting, and Dana could feel the count in every touch. I asked him to put the ledger down for a season: no counting, no initiating, touch that wanted nothing, and one gentle conversation in the truck on the way home from her mother's. What she said was, I want to stop feeling like a bill that's overdue. He told me months later that she comes to find him now. He had thought the bed was owed to him. It turned out to be the place he owed her the truth about what she is worth.

I know the ledger shape. Not always with numbers. Sometimes with resentment. Sometimes with silence. Putting the ledger down is one of the hardest repentances a husband does, because it feels like losing. It is how the well fills.

Movement one, summarized. Write this down: exclusive, generous, present, patient, never a weapon, honest, protected. Name the crack if there is one.

## 5. MOVEMENT TWO: THE SCREEN IN THE COVENANT FRAME [14:30]

**MAX:**
Movement two. The screen, in the covenant frame.

You have been given tools already in this course: one brother with the whole truth, a filter he holds the password to, the phone charging in the kitchen. But the frame changes the fight. You are not fighting pornography because it is against a rule. You are fighting it because you made a covenant with a real woman, and every image is a small breaking of it. The victim sleeps next to you. It trains an appetite for novelty. It rewires what your body answers to. It lies to you that the problem is her not being enough when the problem is you not being present.

The four marks show up here, in the dark. Stop the waiting that will not tell a brother. Pick up the weight of what the eyes do. Lead by handing over the password. Work for God's pay: the Father who sees what is done in secret and keeps faithful nights on the books.

If the crack is the screen, say the whole truth to a brother: what, how often, since when. Let him hold the password. Charge the phone in the kitchen tonight. Secrecy is the fuel. Light is the kill.

I lost fights in the dark that I won in public rooms. The covenant frame is what finally made the fight about a person with a name in the next room, not about a rule I could negotiate with myself. Rules I can lawyer. A wife I cannot.

Movement two, summarized. Write this down: covenant frame, not only rule frame. Brother. Password. Kitchen charger.

## 6. MOVEMENT THREE: SEASONS, SINGLE, DIVORCED, WIDOWER [19:00]

**MAX:**
Movement three. Her seasons and yours, and if there is no wife yet.

After a baby: her body has been through a war. Your claims are laid down. She is exhausted and touched out. You are not counting. Touch that wants nothing. When she is ready, go slow, ask, stop if she needs you to. A wife who can trust her husband with her body when it is weakest will trust him when it is strong.

In illness the covenant does not pause. It changes shape into tenderness that has nothing to do with the bed.

Middle years: her body will do something new. A husband who does not take it as rejection, who asks and goes to the appointment with her, makes the bed a safe place.

Men who keep the well get to drink from it in old age, slower and sweeter. When it is no longer possible, the covenant holds. A man bathing a wife who no longer knows his name is keeping the marriage bed pure. Nothing is happening in it. He is still one flesh with her.

If you are single and intend to marry: every choice about your body right now is a choice about a covenant you have not made yet. You are building it or spending it. Paul told the Thessalonians to learn to control their own bodies. Learn. Do not date for years without moving toward marriage. Long courtships are where good men fall. Do not be alone with her in a bedroom at midnight and call yourselves strong. Nobody is. If you can carry the weight and you have found the woman, marry her.

Divorced seat: single again is single. Harder to hear when you know what you are missing. The culture will say a grown man who has been married has earned the right to do as he pleases. He has not. No sex outside a new covenant. No moving in because you are basically married. Basically married is not married. Same brother, same filter, same phone in the kitchen, with more at stake, because your children are watching how a man handles a body when it is hard.

Widower: same standard, and I am sorry, because you kept the covenant for a lifetime and nobody warned you about the loneliness of the empty bed. God does not ask you to pretend it is not there. He asks you to keep the bed pure in the empty years, and to trust that the Father who saw every faithful night sees these too.

Movement three, summarized. Write this down: season named. Building or spending if single. Crack named if married.

## 7. MOVEMENT FOUR: WHAT THE BED PREACHES [23:30]

Movement four. What the bed preaches, and hold the chapter until it sticks.

Paul quotes the one flesh verse and says the strangest thing: this is a profound mystery, and he is talking about Christ and the church. When a husband is exclusive and generous and present and patient, when he never turns the bed into a weapon or lets it go silent, he is showing his wife a small real picture of how Christ loves the church: faithfully, self givingly, with delight, forever. Not recreation. Not dirt. A vow kept with a body, for as long as they both shall live. Mitch was wrong about the ends. There are not any. There is only the whole of you, promised, and a bed where you say so.

The bed is where a great many good men have quietly broken faith and told no one. If that is you, bring it into the light. David did, and his standing was restored in a breath. The covenant is not a rule you have failed. It is a vow you can start keeping tomorrow.
