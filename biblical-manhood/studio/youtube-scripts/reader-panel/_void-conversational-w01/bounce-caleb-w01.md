# Week 1 script bounce — Caleb (22, single, church)

Source: `studio/episodes/01-a-quiet-tuesday/DIALOGUE.md` + `RUNDOWN.md`. Max solo. ~31 min. Intro + Quiet Tuesday. Bounce only. No rewrite. No Aaron dump.

---

## What delivered

**Cold open.** “Sit down. You are on time.” Training not a show. Don’t binge. Four marks word-for-word. I’d stay past the first minute.

**My chair is real.** Segment 1 names the young man and the single man before the married dad. “You do not have a wife to hide behind… more unstructured hours… no witness.” Then he comes back later and says my Tuesday is harder, not easier. That is the opposite of guest seating.

**Stories that work.** Driveway / daughter learned not to knock — hits even if I don’t have a kid (I know that window). Sunday calls to his mom — yes-that-survives-the-feeling. Counter dinners from his single years — that is literally me standing with a laptop. “Sit down” at the end of that lands.

**Job is doable.** Last Tuesday inventory. Phone in another room for one meal. Alone = sit at a table. Text template for a brother. Carry: “Tuesday is where a man is made, or he settles.” Closer order is clean.

**CTA.** Subscribe + bell once at the end for the curriculum. No shop / site / app spam. Fine.

**Tone.** Firm, not jabby. Matches FLOW book voice. Max tells on himself without performing.

---

## What did not

**Age stamps in segment 1.** Young / single / married / wounded / older / high performer / fine — clearer than the old ebook stamps, but still a list. I found myself; a weaker cut might skim until “single.” Fine for week 1 setup; don’t do this every week.

**Married weight in the middle.** Driveway + cereal bowl + wife already knows — true and good. I stayed because he kept calling me back. If week 2 forgets the single chair, I bounce.

**~31 min is long for YouTube.** I’d finish week 1. Week 9 when the feeling is gone — we’ll see. The script already names that. Good. Still: don’t creep toward 40.

**“Get out of the truck”** is a perfect sign-off for dads in driveways. For me it’s “sit down / get off the counter.” Both are in the script. Keep both audible; don’t only brand the truck line.

---

## Where to dig

1. Keep the single callback every week (even one paragraph). Week 1 earned trust; don’t drop it.
2. Hold runtime near 30. Cap is 40; don’t live there.
3. On-screen Tuesday hour list (rundown note) would help me actually write it.
4. Don’t let “who is in the room” become a weekly stamp parade.
