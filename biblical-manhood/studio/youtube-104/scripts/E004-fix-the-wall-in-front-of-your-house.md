## Script

## 00:00 Hook

[B: an old stone wall with gaps in it, morning light]
[SHORT 1 IN]
When Jerusalem's wall was a pile of rubble, Nehemiah didn't hand out one big job.
He told a lot of men to fix the part in front of their own house.
That's in the Bible. And it's the best home repair advice I've ever read.
[A: MED]
I'm Wes Hart. This is Steadfast Men.
Tuesday was the second mark of a steadfast man, Pick Up the Weight.
Today's the short version. Where to start.

## 00:30 Each in front of his own house

[GFX: Nehemiah 3]
[B: rough stones stacked in a row, a mason's hands setting one]
A little background.
Jerusalem's walls had been down for well over a hundred years. Gates burned. People living in a city anybody could walk into.
Nehemiah comes back to rebuild it, and chapter three reads like a work roster. Who fixed what.
It's mostly names. I'll be honest, it's not the chapter you underline.
But one phrase keeps showing up in it.
[CARD: Nehemiah 3:28 (NIV) "Above the Horse Gate, the priests made repairs, each in front of his own house."]
"Above the Horse Gate, the priests made repairs, each in front of his own house."
[A: CU]
Each in front of his own house.
[beat]
They didn't start across town.
They started at their own door.
[SHORT 1 OUT]

## 01:12 The hinge

[GFX: The hinge]
[SHORT 2 IN]
[A: MED]
Here's why I think that matters.
Men like big projects.
Give me a whole new budget system. A whole new morning routine. A whole new marriage, by the weekend.
And the big project lets us walk right past the broken thing ten feet away.
[B: a kitchen cabinet door hanging by one hinge]
Last summer we had a kitchen cabinet door hanging by one hinge.
It hung like that till the leaves turned.
My wife opened it real gently. Every day. Like a museum piece.
I spent that whole season thinking about redoing the kitchen.
I did not fix the hinge.
[beat]
That's backwards. Nehemiah would've handed me a screwdriver.
[TEXT: Start at your own door.]
Picking up the weight starts close. With the thing you walk past every day.
[SHORT 2 OUT]

## 02:01 That was the wall

[GFX: The real wall]
[A: MED]
Here's the other thing.
When those priests fixed the wall by their houses, it wasn't a warm-up for the real wall.
That was the wall.
[pause 1s]
A lot of men think the real work is out there somewhere. Ministry. Career. The big calling.
And that work is real.
But your house is part of the wall.
Think about who lived behind those walls. Kids. Old folks. Whole families.
A gap by your house wasn't a cosmetic problem. It was the way in.
Same at your place. The thing you keep walking past is where stuff gets in. Resentment. Distance. The feeling that nobody's minding the house.
The man who keeps the stretch in front of his own door fixed is building the city.

## 02:55 Twenty feet

[GFX: Twenty feet]
[A: MED]
So here's the move, and it's simple.
This week, fix one broken thing within twenty feet of where you sleep.
[TEXT: One broken thing within 20 feet of where you sleep.]
A hinge. A drawer. A light. The pile on the dresser. The smoke detector that's been chirping since October.
[B: a hand with a screwdriver tightening a cabinet hinge]
If it isn't a thing, it might be a person. A word you owe somebody in the next room.
If you're married, ask her. "What's broken around here that's been bugging you?"
Then fix the first one she names. Not the one you'd pick.
If you're single, walk your place like a guest seeing it for the first time. Fix what a guest would notice.
If you're living with your folks, or in a dorm, or a barracks, same idea. Your bunk. Your corner. Your truck.
Small? Sure. That's the point.
[pause 2s]
Ownership starts close.

## 03:54 Recap

[A: CU]
Pick Up the Weight. Carry what's yours, and start with what's closest.
[beat]
That cabinet hinge took two screws and about four minutes.
Now she opens it without thinking about it.
That's what fixed looks like. Nobody notices anymore.
[pause 1s]

## 04:11 Next

[END SCREEN 20s]
[A: WIDE]
Tuesday is the third mark of a steadfast man.
It's about a river in Joshua that didn't part until some men had already stepped into it.
It's called "Somebody Has to Step In First."
If you want it, subscribe. Tuesdays and Fridays.
[beat]
Go find your hinge.
