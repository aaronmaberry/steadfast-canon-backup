# Steadfast Men YouTube calendar, 104 episodes

Internal. Draft for Aaron's sign-off, 2026-10-02. Nothing publishes until Aaron says.

**How to read this.** Two episodes a week for 52 weeks. Tuesday (A) is long-form. Friday (B) is shorter and usually puts Tuesday's idea to work. Dates are a proposal for calendar year 2027 so the holidays line up. If launch moves, shift the whole block and keep the holiday episodes on their weeks.

**Mark** is the focus mark the episode names by title (SW Stop Waiting, PW Pick Up the Weight, GF Go First, GP Work for God's Pay). A dash means no single focus mark. **Len** is the target runtime in minutes.

**Overlap with the 24 training sessions.** The YouTube episodes teach the same biblical ideas from different doors. Lead verses were picked to avoid the verses each training session already leads with, and no training or book story is reused. Where a topic is close to a session (marriage, sons, daughters, depravity), the angle is different and the title is new.

Holiday anchors for 2027: New Year (Jan 1), Valentine's (Feb 14), Ash Wednesday (Feb 10), Good Friday (Mar 26), Easter (Mar 28), Mother's Day (May 9), Memorial Day (May 31), Father's Day (Jun 20), Independence Day (Jul 4), back to school (mid-Aug), Labor Day (Sep 6), Reformation Day (Oct 31), Veterans Day (Nov 11), Thanksgiving (Nov 25), Advent (starts Nov 28), Christmas.

## Totals

- 104 episodes. 52 long-form (about 10 to 13 minutes, Tuesdays). 52 shorter (about 5 to 8 minutes, Fridays). E001 to E004 came in a little under target as written, so treat Len as a ceiling, not a quota. Never pad to hit it.
- About 208 Shorts (two marked per episode).
- Focus marks across the year: Stop Waiting 25, Pick Up the Weight 19, Go First 27, Work for God's Pay 21, no single mark or all four 12.
- 13 seasons, each its own playlist. Season 1 ("Start here") is the playlist every new viewer gets pointed to.

## Notes for sign-off

1. **Mark wording.** These scripts use the renamed marks (2026-10-01). The canon hold on YouTube scripts covers the existing W01-W24 lecture files. New YouTube writing uses the new names now, per the canon's 2026-10-01 rename.
2. **Timing.** The book goes final before anything publishes. A January 2027 launch gives that room. If the book slips, the calendar slides as a block.
3. **Batch before launch.** Have at least E001 to E012 cut and approved before E001 goes up, so the schedule never depends on a single week.
4. **Two-host option.** Every script is written for Wes alone. Any episode can become a Wes and Jonathon table version later without changing the outline.
5. **Scripture.** Calendar references are for planning. Every quoted verse gets checked word for word against the NIV when its script is written.
