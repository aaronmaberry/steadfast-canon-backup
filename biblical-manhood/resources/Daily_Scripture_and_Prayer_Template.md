## Title

A Guide for Personal Time with God in His Word

This guide is meant to help you engage with God by spending time with Him, listening to what He says in His Word, and learning to know, love, hear, and obey Him. This is more than checking boxes or keeping up with a reading plan. We want to create space to listen to God through His Word so we can deepen our love for Him and allow him to transform our will to be more like Jesus'.

## What This Guide Is (and Is Not)

This guide is:
1. A repeatable daily rhythm of reading, reflection, and prayerful response
2. A tool to help you engage with God through His Word and grow in maturity

This guide is not:
1. A fill-in-the-blank workbook
2. A devotional with commentary

God transforms us by his Spirit as we spend time with Him in His word. It's okay if you don't have an incredible revelation every time you read. Consistency over time will lead to a deeper relationship with the God of the universe!

## Daily rhythm (8 steps)

1. Plan: Have a plan for when (time), where (quiet), what (passages)
2. Open in prayer. Ask God to speak to you as you open His word. Tell him you want to know Him, love Him, hear from Him, and be transformed by Him as you obey and experience Him. Ask God to help you.
3. Read the passage slowly. Consider reading several times.
4. Mark any verse(s) that stands out in your mind. When you finish reading the passage or chapter, return to those verse(s) you underlined and re-read them.
5. Reflect on these verses, asking God why he brought them to mind and what, if anything, he wants you to do in response.
6. Record what stood out using your preferred method. If you're starting out, use the template on the next page.
7. Pray in closing.
8. Pass it on to re-enforce it or encourage another.

## Daily template fields

- Passage: The reference for the verse that stood out today; sometimes it helps to write it out.
- Ask God:
  1. What are you telling me in this passage? About you, Father, is there a sin I need to confess, a promise to keep, an example to follow, or a command to obey?
  2. What do you want me to do about it?
- Pray: Have a conversation with God about what you read; writing it out can be helpful.
- Who can I share this with today? Could be what you're learning, hearing, or doing. This might be for accountability; it could be sharing with your "One More"
