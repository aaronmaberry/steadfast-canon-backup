Header: One Mission Bible Church. Mission: love God, love people, and make disciples of Jesus.

**BLESS** = five life patterns modeled by Jesus and his disciples for influencing people far from God with the love of Jesus Christ.

Every disciple's normal ongoing gospel influence activities:

1. **B — Begin with Prayer.** Pray for the heart of Jesus and for people far from God in your spheres of influence (FRANC: friends, relatives, acquaintances, neighbors, coworkers). Matt 9:36-38; Acts 26:29; Rom 10:1; Col 4:2-4.
2. **L — Listen with Care.** Enter spiritual conversations by asking questions, listening, and formulating Jesus-honoring responses. John 4:10-26; Acts 8:26-40; Col 4:5-6.
3. **E — Eat Together.** Share meals with people who do not yet know Christ personally. Matt 9:9-13; Luke 11:37; Luke 19:1-10.
4. **S — Serve with Love.** Serve people and invite them to help you in different ways. Mark 10:45; John 4:7; Rom 15:1-3.
5. **S — Share Your Story and God's Story.** Salvation story, how Christ influences your life, clear gospel illustrations that invite people to trust and follow Jesus. Mark 5:19-20; John 4:28, 39-41; John 9:25; Acts 26:1-23; Acts 2:36-41; Rom 10:8-11; 1 Cor 15:1-11.

Use in the training app as the everyday evangelism rhythm, next to the Daily Scripture and Prayer template. Do not reprint the One Mission layout/photos as Steadfast branding.
