## Prompt outline (past / present / future)

Past: any unfinished business; how you will deal with it; describe its conclusion.

Present: evaluate yourself by the four marks (rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, expects the greater reward, God's reward); biblical responsibilities at this stage; succeeding; struggling; passive; how you will commit and accept responsibility; how well you love, honor, and lead your wife; focus on the greater reward; who is there to help.

Future: what sort of "old man"; what children should receive before they leave home; adjectives your wife would use; how you want to be remembered; spiritual mission; goals that would mean you achieved true manhood.
