1. Joshua 1:7,8 “Only be strong and very courageous; be careful to do according to all the law which Moses My
servant commanded you; do not turn from it to the right or to the left, so that you may have success wherever
you go. This book of the law shall not depart from your mouth, but you shall meditate on it day and night, so
that you may be careful to do according to all that is written in it; for then you will make your way prosperous,
and then you will have success.”
It takes courage to keep God’s Word. It also takes a commitment to read and meditates on the Word of God as
the source of guidance and encouragement.

2. Psalm 119:9 “How can a young man keep his way pure? By keeping it according to Your word.”
The Word of God is the answer for living a life wholly committed to God.

3. Psalm 144:3 “O LORD, what is man, that You take knowledge of him? Or the son of man, that You think of
him?”
Significance and worth come from God. For man to come to the mind of God and to be created from His mind
gives him unprecedented honor, meaning and belonging.

4. Proverbs 1:5 “A wise man will hear and increase in learning, and a man of understanding will acquire wise
counsel.”
Developing the art of wisdom means listening to godly men who have wisdom.

5. Proverbs 8:34 “Blessed is the man who listens to me, Watching daily at my gates, Waiting at my doorposts.”
The man who is attentive to God’s leading is like a man who is waiting, expecting and anticipating that God will
speak in his life and situation. To do so means we also have to “watch” what enters the “gates” of our eyes and
ears.

6. Proverbs 24:16 “For a righteous man falls seven times, and rises again, But the wicked stumble in time of
calamity.”

The godly man is able to overcome obstacles that may come his way throughout life. His ability to continuously
trust God even when it seems as though life has dealt him a bad hand is connected to his relationship with the
Lord.

7. Proverbs 18:16“A man's gift makes room for him, and brings him before great men.”
God has uniquely gifted men to serve Him in His kingdom. God will give men the opportunity to use the gifts. A
man’s attitude about that gift will determine how far God will take him in the development of the gift.

8. 1 Timothy 4:14“Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you by prophecy with the laying on
of the hands of the eldership.”
Men should cultivate the gift of salvation as well as the gift of ministry by maturing in the Lord as well as through
accountability with other men. God has called men to serve Him in ministry. We should not neglect this gift or
let the women do all the work, but rather actively seek ways to serve God in this capacity.

9. 2 Timothy 1:6 “Therefore I remind you to stir up the gift of God which is in you through the laying on of my
hands.”
God has affirmed you as His choice for a specific task. Your gift was not only given to you by God, but it was also
confirmed by godly men around you.

10. Matthew 25:20, 21 “The man who had received the five talents brought the other five. 'Master,' he said,
'you entrusted me with five talents. See, I have gained five more.' His master replied, 'Well done, good and
faithful servant! You have been faithful with a few things; I will put you in charge of many things. Come and
share your master's happiness!”

My Family: I am the spiritual leader of my family. It is my responsibility to live out my life in the way
that shows honor in service to God. All that we have on this earth is God’s, and we are merely stewards.
My family will honor God with the first fruits of our labor. I will model quiet time and the study of His
word. I will teach my children to pray and seek God's guidance. I will pray with my wife for protection
and guidance from the Lord. We are the salt and light of the world called to proclaim the good news of
Jesus Christ. I will go boldly in the service for others' souls and teach my children to do the same. I will
be a servant leader in my family.

My wife: I will love my wife unconditionally as Jesus loved the church. I will honor and treat her as the
suitable helper God intended her to be for me. She alone will hold my passion. I will long for no other.
We will work together, plan together, dream together, and pray together. Our marriage will be the
symbol and example for our children to follow of what it means to love unconditionally without limits,
and in the grace of God. My sons will learn how to treat a woman, and my daughter will learn how a
woman should be treated. And all will learn the blessings of marriage.

My Sons: I will lead by example and through mentoring. They will learn that Christ is the true measure
of manhood. Our identity does not come from man but from God whose image we were created after.
I will teach them to seek answers from the Bible and through prayer to God. They will learn to be
discerning in friendships, to seek out others who fear and revere God. They will learn how to lead, what
to stand up for and how to love a woman.

My Daughter: I will model for her what a man of god looks like. I will demonstrate how she is to be
treated through my actions with her and with her mother. She will experience gentleness, patience and
love.
