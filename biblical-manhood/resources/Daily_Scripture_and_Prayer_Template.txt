This guide is meant to help you engage with God by spending time with Him, listening to
what He says in His Word, and learning to know, love, hear, and obey Him.               This is more than
checking boxes or keeping up with a reading plan, we want to create space t               o listen to God
through His Word so we can deepen our love for Him and allow him to transform our will to
be more like Jesus’.

What This Guide Is            (and Is Not)

    This guide      is :

        1. A repeatable daily rhythm of reading, reflection, and prayer              ful resp onse

        2. A tool to help you engage with God through His Word and grow in maturity

    This guide is          not :

        1. A fill - in - the -blank workbook

        2. A devotional with commentary


God transforms us by his Spirit as we spend time with Him in His word. It’s okay if you don’t
have an incredible revelation every time you read. Consistency over time will lead to a
deeper relationship with the God of the universe!

Use This Guide Each Day Following the Same Simple Rhythm:

    1. Plan: Have a plan for when (time), where (quiet), what (passages)

    2. Open in prayer. Ask God to speak to you as you open His word. Tell him you want to
       know Him, love Him, hear from Him, and be transformed by Him as you obey and
       experience Him.    Ask God to help you.

    3. Read the passage slowly. Consider reading several times.

    4. Mark any verse(s) that stands out in your mind. When you finish reading the passage
       or chapter, return to those verse(s) you underlined and re          - read them.

    5. Reflect on these verses, asking God why he brought them to mind and what, if
       anything, he wants you to do in response.

    6. Record what stood out using your preferred method. If                you ’re starting out, use the
       template on the next page.

    7. Pray in closing.

    8. Pass it on to re - enforce it or encourage another.
