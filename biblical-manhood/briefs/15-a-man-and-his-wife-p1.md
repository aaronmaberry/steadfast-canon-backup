# Brief: 15 A Man and His Wife Part 1

- Title: A Man and His Wife Part 1
- Speaker: Tim Hawks
- Date: 2026-02-18
- YouTube: 3Ps3ezdRO6Y

## Core claims
- Culture runs on unproven "facts" (Drake equation, alien life). Marriage views are the same: now-bubble, not history.
- Live with your wife according to knowledge (1 Pet 3:7), not guesswork, accident, or force. Physical strength and legal power are off limits.
- Wife is co-heir. Mistreat her and prayers bounce; God will not bless a leader who will not bless her.
- Top-down traditional = Gentile kings: lord it over, self-styled benefactors, benefits flow up. Jesus: not so among you. Servant leadership shares benefits down.
- 50/50 egalitarian is mythical: no one owns the whole; each mule thinks it pulls more; women still do ~70% domestic. Linked to falling male work/marriage, fatherless kids (Murray; Mead on roles for men).
- Biblical model (introduced): core roles / responses / concerns. Core = central, not exclusive. Husband = head = servant leader of the big picture and shared blessing.

## Scripture
- 1 Peter 3:7 (according to knowledge; weaker partner; co-heirs; unhindered prayers)
- Luke 22:24-30 (kings of the Gentiles; greatest as servant; I am among you as one who serves)
- Recap of manhood definition (session 14)

## Practices
- Singles stay in: you may marry; you must back married brothers.
- Stop winning arguments by force/logic; listen to meaning, do not dominate.
- Audit blessing flow: who actually benefits in this house?
