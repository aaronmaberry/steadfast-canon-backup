## Core claims
- "Core" means central contribution, not a ban on other work or gifts.
- Biology, psychology, and history converge: human thriving tracks these cores.
- Women: nurturers of life. Honor that and society and women flourish; mock it and value collapses into body-image and sexual use.
- 1 Tim 2 "saved through childbearing" is sozo as deliverance from the Eve-blight (women as ruiners), not a claim women are stupid; Eve as mother of all living is hope, including Messiah.
- Men: bearers of destiny. As men go, so goes society. Unique social and spiritual leadership; defection wrecks everyone (Isa 3).
- Two Adams, two masculinities. Identity is in Adam or in Christ.
- Adam's manhood: earthly course; instinct not revelation; takes life from others; no transcendent cause.
- Christ's manhood: heavenly course; revelation ("will of the Father"); life-giving; kingdom cause.
- Next week compresses these into four marks of a real man, then apply to sex, marriage, kids, work.
