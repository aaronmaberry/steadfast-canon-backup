# 08 The Depravity Wound

- title: The Depravity Wound
- speaker: Tim Hawks (Nate joining staff)
- date: 2025-10-31
- source: YouTube ASR via watch page

## Core claims
- Anecdote: Kathy Box angry because husband Danny and son would not tell her breakfast talk; they blamed "overly bonded to mother" and "talk to Tim."
- Shift from wounds of nurture (father, mother, men/mentors) to a wound of nature: depravity. History observed a selfish bent; last ~50 years the US preferred "humans are basically good" and blamed nurture. Behavior and personhood are related (rapist vs "good person with a glitch").
- 1 Cor 15:21-22: death through a man, life through a man. Genesis 1-11 explains how the world got this way. Genesis 3: serpent's spin ("did God really say"); "know" in Hebrew is experience; they eat, shame, hide, blame. Death is internal: innocence to depravity. Toddlers bang brass on glass; teens drawn to the wrong group; HBO in the hotel.
- Romans 7:18-23: Paul wants good and does evil; sin in the members. Neil Young "Heart of Gold" as a poet who cannot get there. Self-esteem movement: highest scorers include serial killers; result is narcissism. Knowing limits (Bob Sanders 5'8" safety; curve in class; sales vs schmoozers) is strength.
- Like begets like: Cain kills Abel. Westminster Confession: first parents dead in sin, guilt imputed, corrupt nature by ordinary generation. Spock blank slate; 79% of evangelicals say man is basically good. Pink Floyd "brick in the wall," "We Are the World," "children are our future" as naive.
- Definition: fallen and cursed creatures at odds with our creator.
- Implications: (A) separated from God (Ps 51:5; Lam 3:39-44; Eph 2:1-3 objects of wrath). (B) life of futility (Gen 3:17-19 sweat; Eph 4:17-18 futile thinking; Mastercard "priceless" selling time with dad; 1 John 5:19 world under the evil one). (C) enslaved to a corrupt nature (Eccl 7:20; Eccl 3 heart full of evil; Rom 3:23). (D) sentenced to death (Rom 6:23; 1 Cor 15:21).
- Revelation 20 white throne: books of deeds vs book of life. Name in the book because someone else paid. Gospel: Christ paid for depravity.
- Close: John Burke Imagine Heaven; near-hell NDEs; friend/pastor cocaine OD falling toward cries. If you have no relationship with God through Christ, do not leave the parking lot; talk to group, Tim, Nate, or Eric. Next week: how depravity plays out day to day for men.
