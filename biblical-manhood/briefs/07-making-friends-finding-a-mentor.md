# 07 Making Friends, Finding a Mentor

- title: Making Friends, Finding a Mentor
- speaker: Tim Hawks
- date: 2025-10-24
- source: transcript (YouTube ASR). Audio ASR skipped; substantial transcript already present.

## Core claims
- Opening news: Austin clergy plus APD/Sheriff Hamilton on race and police (avoid "next Ferguson"); lunch with the Texas governor, asked him to convene pastors in SA/Houston/DFW/Austin to saturate cities with the gospel. Attendance down ~500 to ~250.
- Recap: father wound, mother wound, then who picks up after dad: men and mentors. Same wound definition as week 06. Average person has 3.4 blind spots (leadership guru); you cannot see your own.
- Isolation recap: no challenge, no accountability, no cheerleaders, no intimacy.
- Personal: Richard Hawks grew up without dad (alcoholic Bill Hawks), raised by a mad-at-men mom and three sisters; 19, dropped Albion, State Farm in Bath MI; Tim wanted out of Bath (bathtub races, factory town) so dad could not carry his vision.
- Paul and Timothy: "my true son in the faith"; 1 Tim 4:12-16 cheerleads youth, gift, diligence, life and doctrine.
- Howard Hendricks (Iron Sharpens Iron) 10 marks of a mentor (ideals; a screwed-up mentor who knows the cost is also useful): (A) has something you need; (B) chooses to cultivate the relationship (grandfather picked Tim); (C) takes a chance on you; (D) respected by other men; (E) network of resources; (F) consulted naturally; (G) talks and listens; (H) consistent lifestyle, lives the advice (Great Commission is "teaching them to obey"); (I) diagnoses real needs (Don Cunningham: public "this is our pastor," private "have you considered"); (J) concerned with your interests.
- Millennials stay at a company if someone will mentor them; older men must be vulnerable, not wax eloquent.
- Impact: closes the masculinity life cycle; symbiotic old/young (power vs gray-hair wisdom; not Cialis); teach from experience over coffee; purpose in latter half ("sage"); missing satisfaction (Chinese proverb via Doug Lawson Give to Live; Jesus: more blessed to give).
- If you lack a mentor: pray and keep praying (ask/seek/knock); if you spot a man, ask (Tim asked seminary pastoral-ministries chair for 5 years; cassette-tape preaching critique, "why do you talk like a woman"); gang up as a group if needed.
- Close: letter about mentor Carl Winger (surgeon, humble, "thorn in my side," buoy back to charted waters).

- Ask a specific man; be aggressive; do not wait.
- Older men: pick younger men, listen as well as talk, be vulnerable.
