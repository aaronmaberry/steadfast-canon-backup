# Brief: 17 A Man and His Wife Part 2

- Title: A Man and His Wife Part 2
- Speaker: Tim Hawks
- Date: 2026-03-04
- YouTube: T4VCs8hIarg

## Core claims
- Complementarian, not authoritarian and not 50/50. Side-by-side equal value, different cores. Core is not cookie cutter.
- Roles: husband = head = servant leader (direction + shared benefits). Wife = helper (exalted word, used of the Trinity): help him be fruitful/multiply and subdue/rule. Helper is not laundry slave.
- Zero-pop-growth cultures (Japan, Germany, US trajectory) show what happens when fruitfulness is lost.
- Responses: he praises and honors, keeping her equality in view (1 Pet 3:7). She submits and respects: vote yes for his leadership; arrange affairs under. "Nagging" questions (where's this family going?) are respect; brush-off is passivity.
- If she seizes lead, many men happily go to games/gym. Mead again: society must assign men a needed role or they idle.
- His needs: companionship (shoulder to shoulder), support (home as supply line; dual 60-hour grind is unsustainable), admiration/respect (not earned like a wage), physical/sexual responsiveness.
- Her needs: companionship that knows her (95% "he never talks"), security (direction + financial flex for nurture crises), significance (honor daily; public praise at the city gate), emotional responsiveness ("tell me more," not fix-it).
- Love is not "unconditional for her / respect only when earned for him." Both are commanded.

## Practices
- Singles stay in; back married brothers.
- Answer her direction questions; do not say leave me alone.
- Plan future with her (no surprise Buffalo job).
- Build a money plan that can flex if a child or parent needs her.
- Praise her in public.
- Buy a marriage book and let her catch you reading it (Dobson; Harley; Keller Meaning of Marriage; Eggerichs Love and Respect).
- If she is not in Rethinking Womanhood, get her the material.
