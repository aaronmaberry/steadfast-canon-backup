## Core claims
- Shift from wounds to a definition of a real man; part 2 next week. Dave Barry man-vs-guy quiz (alien gadget: take it apart; girlfriend wants a future: Cowboys draw on 3rd and 17; three kids for school: "there are three of them").
- Prior week's Genesis objections: a fuller "objections to Genesis" lecture is on the church media player, not this year. Cycles of history repeat failed gender experiments.
- "Core" contributions means central, not comprehensive. Affirming a priority is not forbidding other work. Biology, psychology, and history put the burden of proof on experiments that ignore those.
- Women are the nurturers of life. Where that is honored, society prospers. Every girl is born able to bear and nurture a child; cultures that call that unimportant tear value from women. 1 Tim 2:13-15: Adam formed first, Eve deceived (not "stupid"; Adam sinned without being tricked). sozo here is rescue, not mainly eternal salvation: women delivered from the blight that Eve ruined the race. All women can redeem that blight by the maternal core. Gen 3:20: Eve, "mother of all living," hope including Messiah. Honor that and you get children, family, fidelity, sex as covenant, not women used.
- Gavin McInnes: housewife a superior vocation to making "comic books"; his wife creates life. Margaret Mead: women happiest not with position and power but when maternal creative contributions are upheld. Stephen Clark: feminism devalues what women most desire (wife and mother) and intensifies the felt disadvantage of being female. Simone de Beauvoir: no woman should be authorized to stay home, because too many would. Tocqueville: American women's domestic station was the chief cause of the nation's prosperity.
- Men are the bearers of destiny. As men go, so goes society. Equal, not better. Mead: the hardest social question is appropriate roles for men. Hardwired for cause; no noble cause yields an ignoble one (porn vs Word-and-neighbor).
- Gen 1-2: unique social and spiritual leadership. Isaiah 3: strip male supply and support (hero, warrior, judge, prophet, elder, craftsman) and boys govern, people oppress, "youths oppress my people, women rule over them." In Adam all die.
- Two men, two identities: squeeze fallen Adam and Jesus (second Adam) together for what we were created to be. 1 Cor 15:45: first Adam a living being, last Adam a life-giving spirit. Ridderbos: two figures at two creations; all are comprehended in them. Spiritual destinies: Rom 5:12-19. Also two masculine destinies.
- Adam: natural/earthly course (money, power, now); governed by instinct and reaction, not revelation; draws life from others (taker; stood by while Eve ate); no transcendent cause (small person).
- Jesus: heavenly course (stones/bread, pinnacle, kingdoms refused); based on revelation ("I come to do the will of the Father"); life-giving spirit (Zacchaeus, the adulterous woman; people comfortable he will not use them); transcendent cause is the kingdom.
- Next week: four characteristics of a real man. Then live it in sexuality, marriage, sons, daughters, work, journey. Write a plan.
