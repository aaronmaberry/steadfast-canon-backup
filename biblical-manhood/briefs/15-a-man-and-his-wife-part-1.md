# 15 A Man and His Wife - Part 1

- title: A Man and His Wife - Part 1
- speaker: Tim Hawks
- date: 2026-02-18
- source: transcript (YouTube ASR captions)

## Core claims
- Culture is full of delusion (Sanders math; Drake equation treated as science). Marriage views are often bubble-now: parents, magazines, TV, office, not history or Scripture.
- Recite: reject passivity, accept responsibility, lead courageously, expect the greater reward, God's reward. Singles still need this: you may marry; you must support married brothers.
- 1 Pet 3:7: live with wives according to knowledge; respect as the weaker partner and co-heirs of life, "so that nothing will hinder your prayers." Knowledge source is the Bible. Alternatives: guesswork (health-club gift she never wanted), accident (she smiles, then scowls), force (win by logic/volume/body). "Stop listening to what I'm saying and listen to what I mean." Women equal in value; mistreat her and God does not bless the "leader."
- Model 1: top-down traditional. Luke 22:24-26: Gentiles lord it over, call themselves benefactors. Workplace boss morphs into husband-as-boss; benefits flow up; sitcom June Cleaver. Not the model of Christ. Worldwide machismo / CEO taking millions while laying off. Jesus: greatest like the youngest; among you as one who serves. Biblical leadership: "taking responsibility to ensure benefits of every endeavor are equally shared in a responsible way with all involved." "Get back to traditional family values" is heard as this rejected model.
- Model 2: 50/50 mythical egalitarian. Two flaws: nobody owns the whole; each mule thinks it pulls more. He says she is right that women do about 70 percent more household work. Charles Murray: white working-class men 30s-40s, labor force 96% (1968) to 79% (2015); about one in five not looking for work; almost half unmarried. Mead: society's hardest job is appropriate roles for men. Egalitarian shift: no one takes family responsibility, childbirth and marriage fall, divorce rises, males lose a unique role.
- Model 3 introduced only: core roles/responses/concerns. Core means central, not comprehensive. Husband: me / head / servant leader. Responsible for the big picture and shared benefits (Eph 5:23). Next week Cindy teaches 25 ways to be a servant leader from a wife's eyes; part 2 after that.

## Scripture
- 1 Peter 3:7
- Luke 22:24-27
- Ephesians 5:23

## Practices
- Live with your wife according to knowledge, not guesswork, accident, or force. Treat her as co-heir; prayers are on the line.
- Reject husband-as-boss (benefits to him) and 50/50 (nobody owns the whole). Head means servant leader: shared benefits, big picture.
- Singles: learn this so you can support married brothers; you may marry.
