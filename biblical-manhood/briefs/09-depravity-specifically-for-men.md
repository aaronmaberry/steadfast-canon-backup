# 09 What Depravity Means Specifically for Men

- title: What Depravity Means Specifically for Men
- speaker: Tim Hawks (Nate Eckelberger prays after intro)
- date: 2025-11-07
- youtube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T3vTHDPC__w

## Core claims
- Culture can ban "man up" (Nebraska example) while claiming it is not limiting speech; universities train that confusion.
- Even non-theists (Dawkins, The Selfish Gene; survival of the fittest) assume a selfish bent. You do not have to teach a son to hit his sister.
- Jesus saves from penalty and from the power of sin.
- Seven general meanings of depravity: (1) we are dysfunctional by nature (selfish, greedy, lustful, proud, blame-shifting, shortsighted); never excuse present bad behavior by past hurts. (2) Most real problems are inside me, not outside me. (3) Education, environment, self-understanding, and willpower cannot eradicate it; we must be saved (Nicodemus: born again). (4) We cover it with masks (education, money, status, achievement, attractiveness, life-of-the-party, religion). (5) We must not trust ourselves on feelings, motives, and path; there is a way that seems right that ends in death. (6) Admitting depravity is the first step to finding God (poor in spirit = destitute, Greek ptochos). Bill Bright's two morning prayers: fill me so I am not full of myself; lead me so I do not go my own way. (7) Progress in godliness is increasing apprehension of how deep the wound is, not growing independent of God. Mixed motives (Ben and Christmas) are normal; only what Jesus does in us is pure. Admitting weakness is the positive path.
- Fairness aside: three female depravity tendencies (not on the men's outline): trade nurture for control (Gen 3:16); external beauty over internal character (1 Pet 3:4-5); feelings over Scripture in decisions (Gen 3:6; 1 Tim 2:13-15).
- Interpretive method: repeated biblical themes mark recurring problems (e.g. "not greedy for money" for pastors).
- Three male specific patterns: (1) avoid domestic responsibility (Samuel, David; Adam passive beside Eve, then blames; contrast Eph 5:25-27; Eve deceived, Adam not, 1 Tim 2). (2) Rule wrongly over women and children: curse "desire" plus mashal dominate (Gen 3:16), not an endorsement; contrast Eph 5:28-29 love/care as own body. (3) Get lost in career and lose transcendent cause: cursed ground turns the face from God toward dust (Gen 2:7 vs 3:17-19); men answer "who are you" with a job; seek first the kingdom (Matt 6:33). Recent surveys: women now often justify skipping worship too. Next week: lack of manhood vision.
