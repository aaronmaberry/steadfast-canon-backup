# 03 Healing the Father Wound

- title: Healing the Father Wound
- speaker: Michael Hall (guest). Host opens. Tim Hawks reviewed, not lecturing.
- date: Sept 26, 2025
- source: transcript

## Core claims
- Father wound: an ongoing emotional, social, or spiritual deficit ordinarily met in a healthy relationship with dad that must now be overcome by other means. God made men to pass things along; if they do not, deficits form.
- Review: every son needs lifelong positive memories (deposits for dark nights; Hall's son, years later, remembered being disciplined for drinking and driving and never reminded of it again); direction with solid why answers (Deut 6: teach diligently, not "because I said so"; life skills, pursuing a woman, friendships, the heavenly Father); conviction through modeling (1 Thess 2:11-12: a father encourages, comforts, urges a life worthy of God; boomers notorious for "do as I say"); his father's heart (Matt 3:16-17 at Jesus' baptism: love, affirmation, blessing, repeated).
- Mickey Mantle / Mutt Mantle (Jane Leavy, The Last Boy): rookie slump, dad packed his bag and called him a coward; Mantle never believed he measured up and never told his dad he loved him.
- Satan's scheme: embed a lie in the wound (Fresh Prince clip: "How come you don't want me, man?"). Remedies are proactive: own the past; do not blame it.
- Six remedies: (1) Summon courage to forgive. Dad is human, not God. Deficits from ignorance and sin that entangles. Fathers are not the enemy (not flesh and blood). Forgive as we have been forgiven. Past will not control the future. (2) Risk asking your father's love. Gordon Dalbey / Peter: asked twice; dad finally said "I do love you." Men take responsibility now. (3) Aggressively seek reconciliation. Surface "getting along" is not fellowship. Hall's dad gave up a Bergstrom NCO-academy dream job so Hall could finish senior year; Hall later came to faith that summer. Honor your father. (4) Risk asking your father's blessing. Notebook of clippings proved a lie that dad did not care. (5) Enter a greater love: earthly dad is a temporary provision to the true Father. John 10:10. Spiritual men are whole men. (6) Break the cycle as a responsible dad. Affirmation, time, vision. Do not inflict the wound you received.
- Hall's father, 81, in a wheelchair, came to faith years ago; says "I love you" almost daily. Nothing is impossible for God.

## Practices
- If dad is living and you have unfinished business, take the first step: forgive, ask his love, seek reconciliation, ask his blessing.
- Raise your son in the nurture and admonition of the Lord; do not pass the wound on.
- Groups: attitude toward your father; if you could bare your soul, what would you say; which of the six remedies now; what you are doing or not doing that may wound your children later.
