# 06 Men and Mentors

- title: Men and Mentors
- speaker: Tim Hawks
- date: 2025-10-17
- source: transcript (YouTube ASR). Audio ASR skipped; substantial transcript already present.

## Core claims
- Initiate into a small group; do not wait for Eric to matchmake. Men's ministry initiates.
- Mass-shooting coverage (Ian Mercer / Dylann Roof / Adam Lanza / Vester Flanagan) is quoted via Matt Walsh: fatherlessness and divorce, not gun laws or race, as the common line. Stats: 90% homeless kids, 63% youth suicide, 71% dropouts, 85% of youths in prison from fatherless homes; inner city 4 of 5 without biological father.
- Dad is destiny. Culture's answer (make men feminine) throws out the baby with the bathwater.
- Third wound: absence of men and mentors. Definition: social, emotional, and spiritual blind spots and/or deficits caused by a lack of healthy male comradeship that results over time in foolish actions, serious setbacks, and a short-sighted masculinity. Andy (The Office) as the guy everyone talks about and nobody talks to.
- Every man benefits from the company of men who pull for him, not competitors. Friendless American male: conventional manhood treats other men as rivals.
- Isolation downsides: no challenge (warped perspective; James and the mirror); no accountability (privileges you would not allow others; Hybels parking-lot note, he thanked security); no cheerleaders (paid-off house jig; backyard catch vs Longhorns); no intimacy (lose transparency; US male suicide peak 50-55; Madoff covering one bad year).
- Mentors pick up after dad. Male specialists: teach skills dad lacked; call to pursuits dad would not dare; give resources/confidence/wisdom outside dad; sometimes believe in the son more than dad could.
- Discipleship focuses on instruction; mentoring is initiation.
- Mentor traits: supports not competes; cheerleader not critic; develops gifts while protecting from costly mistakes; encourages; admires and delights because he sees untapped potential.
