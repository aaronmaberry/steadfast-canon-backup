# 21 A Man and His Design

## Core claims
- Personal: A&M 1995, family marina, Faith Bible (Woodlands). Prayed privately that God would have to blow the doors open for vocational ministry. Four months later the senior pastor offered associate youth pastor plus Dallas Seminary (1998). Steve Jobs: design is how it works, not how it looks.
- Eph 2:10: ownership (God's workmanship; created and purchased); workmanship (poiema / poem: unique, no two alike); purpose (good works prepared in advance). Saved to something, not only from something. Holiness is set apart to those works.
- Marriage, two flesh patterns. Natural leader: too assertive, control freak, "give me the rock" (UNC/Villanova last-second shots). Col 3:19-21: love wives, do not be harsh; fathers, do not embitter children. Non-leader: too passive; wife nags, he withdraws because leading was punished. Counsel: she must give permission to fail without punishing; he must risk leading again. Leadership is submitting design to biblical responsibilities rather than feelings. Feelings are the dashboard (check-engine / loose gas cap), not the steering wheel.
- Four responsibilities any personality can obey: follow Jesus closely (enjoy God, not legalistic quiet time; Jay's sheet: "gentleness is your strength"; "nothing to prove and nothing to lose"; Prov 22:4 humility before honor); provide (earn and control spending); protect (physical, spiritual, wife from overcommitment, emotions in the home; daughters 12 and 8); serve (set her up as wife, mother, gospel minister). Men are not victims of personality; in Christ they have what they need for life and godliness.
- Two marriage maps. Compliant wife + natural-leader husband: enabler vs tyrant; her ideas drop out; she becomes irrelevant; invite her as governor. Natural-leader wife + compliant husband: nag vs whipped puppy; the bridge is those four responsibilities.
- Work: match is critical. StrengthsFinder named his lane (Myers-Briggs/DISC already obvious). Naples executive pastor, then Hill Country, then a too-quick pull into business that God thwarted; Mission of Hope put design and role back together at 43. Exxon number two: very few men love their jobs; still not too late; invite other men in.
- Career search: early 20s have-to (get off parents' payroll, try jobs); 30s need-to (convictions, values, passions); midlife 40s must-do (find the lane; wrong lane-change: blame wife/kids, trade up spouse/toys, path of destruction); relaxed 50s can-do (invest in others; Buford, Halftime). Two questions: What do I do well and with satisfaction? What do I want to do but cannot? No is as important as yes (Jobs: say no to a thousand things). Cannot answer alone; do not mute the men who have permission to speak. Col 3:23-24: work as for the Lord.
- Ministry: every man gifted (Rom 12:6-8). Gifts in two categories: speaking and serving. Using them is a main way to experience God. Career and family are not enough until you tie to a transcendent cause that requires God's action (HCBC: every man, woman, and child).
- Also designed for relationships (Trinity; not good for man to be alone; iron sharpens iron; do not isolate); truth (Col 3:16; truth sets free; John 17:17); worship (become like what you worship; Piper: God most glorified when we are most satisfied in him); eternity (Eccl 3:11).

## Practices
- Follow Jesus closely; provide, protect, serve, whatever your personality.
- Answer with other men: what I do well with satisfaction; what I want but cannot. Do not mute them.
- Tie your life to a transcendent ministry using your speaking or serving gift.
- Groups: most significant truth about design; do you know what you do well with satisfaction and what you cannot; how you are gifted for ministry; one thing you want answered about yourself.
