## Core claims
- Much daily talk is untrue (pay-gap myth; "Quran does not teach violence" vs Belgian attacks / Nabeel Qureshi). Same for thriving, masculinity, femininity, raising daughters. Christianity uses reason: if the Bible is true you will see it. Applies to dads, teens (these men may raise your wife), and men learning women.
- Natural strengths: daughters more stable than sons. Development less disruptive (no required break from same-gender parent). Girls more physically and emotionally healthy (flutter in range; boys' crises are severe). Women: fewer crimes, fewer completed suicides; men 50-55 have the highest US suicide rate; men isolate, women get help.
- Personal strengths: daughters more secure in identity. Body itself signals a world-changing contribution (bear and nurture life). Boys have muscles and testosterone with no equally loud identity; Mead: societies must define roles for men.
- Cultural challenge: new supreme pursuit is equality, career, independence (historically wife, mother, complement). Stephen Clark: feminism devalues wife-and-mother and makes women feel female as handicap. First-wave quotes: Friedan (homemakers parasites, pregnancy a disease; husband/children cannot give a self); Greer (motherhood a handicap); Steinem (escape home and husbands); de Beauvoir (do not allow women to stay home, too many would). Third wave: if breasts/uterus/sexuality are not for nursing, marriage, and children, they become tools for equality, career, independence: flirting, sleeping to the top, "hotness." Eating disorders, cutting, body hatred. Books: The Body Project; Wendy Shalit, A Return to Modesty. Carl Zimmerman, Family and Civilization: Rome's family collapse pattern repeats in America (Punic Wars ~ WWII).
- Decline of feminine-values training. Titus 2:3-5: older women train younger women to love husbands and children, self-controlled, pure, busy at home, kind, subject to husbands, so the word is not maligned. Love as doing good needs teaching. Peter Blitchington: mother-daughter relationship should form maternal ability; nations rotate on mother-child interaction more than elections.
- Rise of the absent mom. ~2005: 59% of children under six had a full-time working mom. Burton White (Harvard preschool project): after 30 years he would not put his infant or toddler in substitute care. Hawks tells men because "how you feel" is not the child's good.
- What dads contribute (Institute for Sexual Research; dad present means daily, emotional, not weekend-only). If he is there: she is secure as a woman (learns femininity by playing off masculinity); she relates well to men, complementary; she will have a fulfilling sex life with her husband (female surrender rests on trust). John Mayer "Daughters"; "daddy issues." Early feminist leaders were angry at a man (their fathers), not men in general. If he is not there: shy, insecure, anxious, hard to bond; or inappropriately self-assertive, angry, promiscuous. Counseling story: early-20s woman using one-night stands as ego weapons ("I have this guy right where I want him").
- What dads can do. With wife, define a real woman: rejects worldly temptations for significance; accepts God's design for relationships (interdependent with men, not competing); nurtures the next generation; expects God's greater reward. Parallel to a real man: reject passivity, accept responsibility, lead courageously, expect God's reward. Help mom stay home in formative years (bend lifestyle around the primary nurturer). Support and cheer true feminine values. Date your daughter so her first love is you, not the first boy who pays attention. Ceremonies: puberty/first period (celebrate becoming a woman who can bring life, not a sex object); leaving home; graduation; marriage.
- Barbara Bush at Wellesley 1990: you will not regret one more deal; you will regret missing husband, son, daughter. William Maddox (WSJ, Take Your Daughter to Work Day): on the ride home tell Allison her Phi-Beta-Kappa mother chose her over the workplace.
