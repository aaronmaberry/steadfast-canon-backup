# 17 A Man and His Wife - Part 2

- title: A Man and His Wife - Part 2
- speaker: Tim Hawks
- date: 2026-03-04
- source: transcript (YouTube ASR captions)

## Core claims
- Affirms Cindy's talk. Singles: do not check out. God's design vs cultural sound-bite "wisdom."
- Recap: top-down traditional (benefits up, authoritarian) is not biblical. 50/50 mythical (egalitarian) has no owner of the whole; Satan's lie that responsibility equals being better.
- Third model: complementarian. Masculinity and femininity side by side. Core means essential focus, not the only thing a spouse may do. Two couples can look different if the core is tended.
- Core roles, equal value. Husband: head (Eph 5:22-23) = direction, servant leader, shared benefits, Jesus as the pattern. Wife: helper (Gen 2:18), an exalted word used of the Trinity. Help him be fruitful and multiply and subdue/rule. Population-growth collapse (Japan, Germany, US) is losing "be fruitful." Kids grow men up; happiness studies favor those who have children.
- Core responses. Husband: praise and honor; keep her equality in view (1 Pet 3:7 heirs of life). Wife: submit and respect (Eph 5:22-23); vote yes for his leadership so he does not go passive. Submit = arrange your affairs under, like players with a coach. Questions about direction, money, kids, the marriage are respect, not "nagging."
- Core concerns, husbands: (1) companionship, shoulder to shoulder (March Madness couch); (2) support (Desert Storm 7:1 rear; Durant on Rome: women competing picked up men's vices, not virtues). Not a ban on wives working; unsustainable dual-grind can doom a marriage. (3) Admiration/respect granted, not earned, as love is granted. (4) Physical responsiveness; men bond through sex.
- Wives: (1) companionship face to face, be known; 95% complaint "he never talks to me"; courtship fraud. (2) Security: direction and a financial plan with flex if a child or parent needs her. Buffalo-job surprise destroys trust. (3) Significance/honor daily; employees rank recognition first, managers last; Prov 31 praise at the gates. (4) Emotional responsiveness: "Honey, tell me more about it," not three-sentence solutions. Emotional connection often opens physical connection.
- Application: know your customer. Dobson; Harley His Needs/Her Needs; Keller Meaning of Marriage; Eggerichs Love and Respect. Buy the book yourself; let her catch you reading it because you want to know her.

## Practices
- Know your customer. Buy a marriage book and read it; do not assign her the shopping.
- Grant praise/honor (husbands) and respect/submission-as-team (wives). Treat her questions about plan and money as respect.
- Build a financial plan with nurture-flex. Do not spring a life-change. Talk face to face; ask her to tell you more.
