## Core claims
- Presuppositions filter hearing. Born-good yields a victimization model (prevailing in US/Western Europe): innocent child is hurt, acts out, becomes a victimized adult, society affirms "not your fault," no solution. BF Skinner social engineering (Beyond Freedom and Dignity, Walden 2). Born-selfish yields a salvation model: same hurts, but you own the response; woundedness is used as cover for chosen evil.
- Romans 5:12: death through Adam and because all have sinned (nurture plus nature).
- House-sit story: affluent parents who "ruined" a 19-year-old by buying his narrative. Counsel: change the locks and take the keys.
- Prodigal (Luke 15): he "came to himself," ran to the father; the father ran first. Gospel invitation. Greatest moment is admitting you are broken so you can be fixed.
- Where there is no revelation/vision, people cast off restraint (Prov 29:18). Mall shoppers: 60% had no Christmas budget and spent wild. Exodus 32: Moses gone, golden calf, "revelry"/orgy, "running wild" = same Hebrew as Prov 29:18.
- Wound definition: a stunted or perverse masculinity that prevails over a culture, misdirecting masculine energies into destructive patterns (eat, drink, sex, brand, disposable wife). Military vision produces camaraderie; civilian "get what I want" does not.
- Evidence of masculine anarchy: gender breakdown; UN Human Rights Council 27-4 affirming man-woman marriage while US/West dissent; delayed parenting / near-zero population growth; political correctness blocking facts. Isaiah 3: supply and support stripped, boys as officials, women rule, youths oppress, when male leadership absents itself.
- Robert Lewis seminar: 400 churched teens/parents; zero hands on teaching sons/daughters what a man/woman is, how to treat a woman, what marriage is.
- Conventional vs authentic manhood: does vs is; personally gratifying niche vs fulfilling niche; competition vs community; temporal power vs transcendent purpose; personal perks vs eternal/kingdom rewards; self/life-taker vs others/life-giver; success vs significance.
- Next semester: definition of masculinity, wife, sons, daughters, work, life stages, sexuality. Commit Wednesday mornings; bring someone.
