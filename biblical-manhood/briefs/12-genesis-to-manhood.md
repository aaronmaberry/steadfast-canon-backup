# 12 Genesis to Manhood

## Core claims
- Positive masculinity does not diminish femininity. Best world: both at full strength. Parallel "rethinking womanhood" course exists. Opens with Edmund Cooks poem "How Did You Die?" and Patrick Henry "liberty or death" vs Xbox/ESPN.
- Hebrew thought teaches manhood by story, not a "Book of Man" proposition. Genesis 1-3 is the epic that explains original ideal and fallenness.
- Gen 1:26-27: male and female equally in God's image (reason, will, love, moral capacity), not physical form. Hint of social order: "man" as the title that can subsume both.
- Dual human task (1:28): be fruitful (civilization / next generation) and subdue/rule (represent God to nature). Requires both sexes.
- Gen 2 details before the fall (each detail is loaded because so little is recorded): (A) Adam created first; firstborn/first commandment/first love pattern; 1 Tim 2:11-12 grounds male elders in Adam first. (B) Occupation and worship-work before Eve (2:15, work/keep). (C) God commands the man with the word before Eve; men must not delegate knowing and leading with Scripture. (D) Naming animals = leadership/responsibility. (E) Helper suitable: not a cure for loneliness (Adam has God) but a corresponding partner for the mission; strengths and gaps fit. Responsibility is burden, not superiority (cop/teacher analogies). (F) Adam names her isha/ish, equal root, masculine/feminine forms; woman "fashioned" as masterpiece vs man from dust. (G) The man leaves father and mother and initiates the household (2:24). Culture replaced initiation with group texts; "awkward" is the wrong word for that job. Unplug from mom; wife is not a surrogate mom.
- Gen 3: temptation reverses social/spiritual order. Serpent talks to the woman; she had the command from Adam (adds "don't touch"). Appeal: God is holding out; disobey and be like God. Adam is "with her" and passive; he watches spiritual suicide then joins. God calls the man, not Eve: "Where are you?" Adam blames the woman God gave him. Judgment hits vocation and nurture (ground against the man; pain in childbearing). Headship still after the fall: Adam names her Eve, mother of all living. Romans 5 / Genesis 4: what a man does travels generations (Cain).
- Summary: male created with a unique social and spiritual leadership calling. When men surrender it, societies last maybe two to three generations before free fall (Rome, Greece, Persia, now). Next week: definition of masculinity to reverse the decline.
