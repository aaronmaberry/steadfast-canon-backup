## Core claims
- Nate Eckelberger (Gateway plant, Chopped, adventures) started Monday as men's and marriage pastor; he prays and offers one-on-ones before the break.
- Nebraska banned "man up"; Hawks: banning a word is limiting speech; universities cannot do ABC logic.
- Recap: humans are selfish (Dawkins The Selfish Gene; you did not teach a son to hit his sister). Jesus saves from the penalty and the power of sin.
- Seven general meanings: (1) dysfunctional by nature; never excuse present sin by past hurts. (2) Most real problems are inside, not outside. (3) Education, environment, self-understanding, and willpower cannot eradicate it; Nicodemus must be born again. (4) Masks (education, money, status, achievement, attractiveness, life of the party, religion). (5) Do not trust feelings/motives/path; Prov 14:12 canoe toward Niagara. (6) Admitting depravity is the first step to finding God; poor in spirit uses the destitute Greek. Bill Bright: fill me so I am not full of myself; lead me so I do not go my way. (7) Progress in godliness is increasing apprehension of the wound; 7-year-old Ben and Christmas mixed motives. Positive: admit weakness and get help.
- Women's depravity (fair fight, not on the outline): trade nurture for control (Gen 3:16); external beauty over internal character (1 Pet 3:4-5); feelings over Scripture in decisions (Gen 3:6; 1 Tim 2:13-15).
- Interpretive method: repeated biblical themes mark recurring problems (pastors not greedy for money).
- Three male patterns: (1) avoid domestic responsibility (Samuel, David; Adam with Eve, then blames; contrast Eph 5:25-27; Eve deceived, Adam not, 1 Tim 2). (2) Rule wrongly: Gen 3:16 mashal dominate is curse not endorsement; contrast Eph 5:28-29. (3) Lost in career, lose transcendent cause: cursed ground turns the face from God toward dust (Gen 2:7 vs 3:17-19); men answer "who are you" with a job; Matt 6:33. Recent surveys: women now often justify skipping worship too.
