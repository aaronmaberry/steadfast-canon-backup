## Core claims
- Men name kids as greatest legacy, but they get ~18 direct years with children and ~45 years, 40-50 hrs/week, at work. Work is not merely utilitarian (name, skill, paycheck, retirement).
- Imagined funeral at work: marriage restored, cancer patient kept going by daily prayer, nemesis respected the manner, coworker came to Christ from a parking-lot eternity question. Would your workplace funeral sound like that?
- Accept the mission: work is spiritual service (Adam in the garden); we are junior partners establishing God's rule; approach work as promoting God's glory, not titles/toys. Cathedral vs quarry; saving a life vs emptying a bedpan. Col 3:23-24: work for the Lord, inheritance from him.
- Phil 1: partnership (koinonia) in the gospel is for jailers, slaves, owners, not clergy only. Delegating gospel to paid professionals is why access shrinks unless people enter a church. Be an ambassador/missionary in your industry.
- Adopt the mindset: gospel progress > personal progress (Paul's chains advance the word); others' spiritual progress > preferences (to live is Christ, to die is gain); gospel progress costs (stand firm, contend as one man; granted to suffer). Visible-verbal faith vs closet. Model is not wealth/lifestyle (50%+ of millennials, including Christian kids, name wealth as a major priority).
- Apply the method: (1) pray at a daily marker for boldness, words, open doors (Eph 6:19-20; Col 4:3-5). God already convicts of sin, righteousness, judgment (John 16:8-11). Plant/water; God grows. (2) Inventory speech, ethics, attitude (Col 4:5-6). Do not quit as "moral police" when sinners sin; stay as the alternative. (3) Develop relationships; Austin survey: elders on the same board did not know each other were believers. (4) Grace story: I felt / I found / I feel; 30s, 3 min, 10 min versions. (5) Gospel in 3-4 minutes (sin, judgment, Jesus, how to know him); Nate has training. Elevator line: "God loves you and has a plan for your life."
- CIA-circus image: lion tamer, trapeze, strong man, clown each took a better personal gig and lost the mission. Do not lose Christ's mission to a career.
