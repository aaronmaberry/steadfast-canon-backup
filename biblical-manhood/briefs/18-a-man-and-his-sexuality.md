## Core claims
- Two false philosophies: Puritan (denial, prohibition, repression, sex=sin) vs Playboy (demand, permission, exploitation, sale, sex=everything). Neither is biblical.
- Darwin/Freud/Kinsey pipeline (animals, must-ejaculate-or-neurosis, flophouse samples, "10% gay") does not survive scrutiny.
- Definition: sex is a gift from God given as human glue to cement two people into one (physical, emotional, spiritual). 1 Cor 6: sex with a prostitute still one-flesh; sexual sin is against your own body.
- Tape analogy: each extra partner weakens the bond; porn/masturbation does the same. Promiscuity can flash old faces in the marriage bed; porn can kill arousal (not a Cialis-first problem).
- Glue is personal: one man, one woman, no comparison catalog.
- Three purposes: (1) propagation (Gen 1:28; Ps 127:3); (2) prevention of impurity (1 Cor 7:2-9 bodies belong to each other; 1 Thess 4:3-8 do not defraud a brother); (3) pleasure of mutual love (Deut 24:5 first-year please his wife; Prov 5 drink your own cistern; Song of Solomon). Sex gets better with maturity, not with 19-year-old stamina.
- Men: P then E then S (physical, emotional, spiritual). Women: E then P then S. Isolation cycle: he withholds emotion until sex; she withholds sex until emotion. Both starve what they want.
- Six months without sex in marriage is not "fine." Affairs usually start emotional.
