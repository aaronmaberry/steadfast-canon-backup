## Core claims
- Definition (memorize): a real man rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward.
- Adam: passive in Eden while Eve is deceived. Jesus: manger and Gethsemane reject passivity (disciples sleep).
- Three responsibilities every man has: a will to obey, a work to do, a woman to love. Adam dropped all three; Jesus took the Father's will, the work of the cross, and the church as bride.
- Men were created to lead. Headship is direction, protection, provision (Eph 5), not inventing a private vision. Cowardice is ditching the budget to avoid being the bad guy.
- Greatest obstacle is feelings, not dad, wife, kids, or culture. Jesus faces physical hunger, power/respect, and "have it all" before public ministry.
- Twofold fuel: better life now, plus judgment seat reward (wood/hay/straw vs precious). Moses, Jesus, Paul ran for God's prize, not Egypt's.
