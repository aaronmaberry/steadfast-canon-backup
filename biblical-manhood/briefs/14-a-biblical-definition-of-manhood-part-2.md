# 14 A Biblical Definition of Manhood - Part 2

- title: A Biblical Definition of Manhood - Part 2
- speaker: Tim Hawks
- date: 2026-02-11
- source: transcript (YouTube ASR captions)

## Core claims
- Completes the two-Adams method. Adam: earthly course, death for himself and others. Jesus: heavenly course, life-giving. Four characteristics "pop out."
- A. First Adam fell into passivity (stood by while Eve was tempted). Second Adam rejected passivity (incarnation; Gethsemane while disciples slept). A real man rejects passivity.
- B. First Adam disregarded responsibility. Second Adam accepts it. Three responsibilities every man has: a will to obey (Gethsemane: not my will); a work to do (garden vs save the world); a woman to love (Adam watched Eve; Jesus gave himself for the church, Eph 5).
- Domestic passivity: men can run a company or a nation and still fail to lead a family, budget, romance, or a son's character.
- C. First Adam abandoned leadership (hid, blamed). Second Adam led courageously. "Men were created to lead. When men lead effectively for the right cause, society prospers, women and children thrive." Direction, protection (John 10:11 good shepherd), provision. Eph 5:23: husband is head as Christ is head of the church; head has eyes, ears, brain; direction comes from revelation, not a personal empire. Greatest obstacle is feelings, not dad, wife, kids, government, or church. Jesus after the Father's blessing faces three feeling-temptations (bread, temple, kingdoms). Cowardice story: Spirit prompts him to open his mouth to Cindy; he stays silent, then "Tim, you are a coward."
- D. First Adam sought a greater reward (improve his station). Second Adam expected God's reward. Motivation: better life now plus eternal reward when fruit is years out. Judgment seat: wood/hay/straw vs something to lay at Jesus' feet. Heb 12:1-2 joy set before him; Heb 11:24-26 Moses looking ahead to his reward; 2 Cor 2:9 as spoken (no eye has seen); 2 Tim 4:6-8 crown of righteousness; John 10:10 life to the full.
- Definition to memorize: "when cut from a biblical mold, the real man is one who rejects passivity, accepts responsibility, leads courageously, and expects the greater reward, God's reward."
- Two images: Adam kneeling over Abel; Robert Lewis's son Garrett reciting the definition learned from his dad.
