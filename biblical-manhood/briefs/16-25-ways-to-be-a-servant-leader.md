## Core claims
- Mixed messages from women are usually her confusion, not a plot.
- Jesus leadership is bottom-up blessing flow, not benefits-to-the-leader.
- Spiritual DNA of the home is the husband's (example + talk): church, prayer, kids.
- Sorry/forgive me melts the conversation; never regret it.
- 1 Pet 3:7 commands seeking to understand, not arriving.
- Include her in destiny-planning; she has dots even if she is a different smart.
- Heart of the wife is tethered to the kids; family work is marriage work.
- Provide core living costs on his income so she has freedom to flex (kids, parents, crisis). Debt, will, follow-through, schedule, porn talks with sons: his job.
- This is impossible without the Spirit. Keep starting over. 50 of 100 stumbled steps is still progress.

## Scripture
- 1 Corinthians 11:1 (follow me as I follow Christ)
- 1 Peter 3:7 (live with understanding)
- Proverbs 31:28 (husband praises her)
- Genesis 3 (provision still his after the fall)
- Romans 13:8 (owe no debt)
- Psalm 15:4 (keeps oath even when it hurts)

## Practices
The 25 (she packed extras; treat as the list):
1. Keep leading when it backfires.
2. Blessing-flow check (calendar it).
3. Own spiritual growth + family discipleship.
4. Say I'm sorry / forgive me.
5. Let them into your interior (stories, prayer requests).
6. Seek to understand; interact from that.
7. Include her in envisioning; annual "get away to get it together" (Wellons workbook).
8. Praise her privately and in public (name strengths).
9. Pray with and for her.
10. Guard time for her own growth/interests.
11. Distribute housework clearly and fairly (working wives: she still logs more hours).
12. Provide basics; consult her on major money.
13. Stay out of harmful debt (Financial Peace).
14. Will + plan for kids if you die.
15. Keep commitments even when a better offer appears.
16. Anticipate marriage/kid stages.
17. Manage home schedule; every yes is a no.
18. Kill TV/phone distractions to talk.
19. Traditions and fun outings; be a fun dad.
20. Write biblically supportable family values (Real Family Values series).
21. Teach practical life skills (tires, manners, money, conflict).
22. Teach sex wholesomely (Passport to Purity; porn talk with sons).
23. Join a men group (HCBC AM).
24. Ask for help when floundering.
25. Keep starting over; yield to the Spirit daily.
