## Content rules (Aaron)
- The book chapter is a guideline only. TEACH the idea; never retell the chapter; never reuse the book's examples, stories, names, images or numbers. Every story and illustration is new.
- Say "session(s)", never "week(s)". Say "today", never "tonight". Nothing that dates it or ties it to a weekly meeting ("before our next session", "between now and next time").
- Never announce "Session 1", "Session five", "Lesson 3", "of twenty-four". The topic comes up naturally. Don't say "the Session N pages"; say "your workbook" or "this part of the workbook".
- Don't give Wes a job. He's a husband, a dad, works for a living, has sat in men's groups a long time.
- No staged crowd bits: no applause, no "coffee crew", no scripted audience replies. Light asks of the room are fine (a show of hands, "look at your hands", "turn to the guy next to you"), at most 2 to 3 per session.
- Kickoff: a short warm welcome with a light laugh in the first minute, then into the topic. Don't repeat the Session 1 opening in later sessions; each gets its own fresh, shorter welcome (sessions 2-24 may nod to "last time" without numbers).
- Humor: 3 to 4 light laugh lines, dry and everyday, plus 4 to 5 short personal anecdotes (believable, a little self-deprecating) and 1 to 2 stories about other men he's known, one told long (setup, detail, turn, landing). Each serves the point next to it.
- Scripture NIV, spoken the way it's said ("Luke sixteen, verse ten"). Exact NIV wording. Exactly THREE verses per session, and each verse LEADS a main teaching point (opens the point; Wes teaches out of it). Do not pile on extra verses as late supporting quotes.
- Closer (last 2 to 3 minutes): recap the one big idea, say plainly what he hopes they walk away with, restate the 3 or 4 main questions from that session's leader.md workbook, then send them to their tables to work that part of the workbook together.
- Length: 3,300 to 3,800 spoken words (about 30 minutes with pauses).
- Must sound recorded live in a real room, like a man talking, not reading.

## Live-speech rules (from grok/v5/live-speech-study.md + research/podium-pauses.md, Aaron 2026-09-29)
1. Short thought units: one per line, usually 3 to 14 words. Split long sentences at natural breath points.
2. Pause after each line (seconds), by type:
   rushed join inside a thought 0.2-0.3 | clause 0.3-0.5 | sentence end 0.6-0.9 | topic change 1.0-1.4 | before the key word/line 0.4-0.6 | after a heavy key line 1.2-2.0.
3. Break mix for one ~25-min session (sprinkled, just enough to sound human — see research/podium-pauses.md):
   - Plain silent pause is the main tool (no filler).
   - Discourse markers at the start of a thought (Now, So, Look, Okay, Listen, Well, Here's the thing, Stay with me): about 8-12 per session, never the same one twice in a row.
   - Self-repair / rephrase: about 3-5 per session.
   - Thinking out loud for the right word: 1-2 per session.
   - Short asides ("you know"): 2-3; "you know" / "I mean": 3-5 each at most.
   - Tag questions ("right?", "you with me?", "fair?"): 2-3.
   - Filled "uh" (and one or two "um"): 6-9 total per session, never two in the same paragraph, never at the start of back-to-back lines.
   - Word-repeat restarts ("you— you"): at most 3 per session, spread apart.
4. The funny word comes LAST in its line (the button). No long freeze before a punchline. After a laugh line use ROOM_CHUCKLE (a 1.7s silent hold) instead of a number, at most 4 per session. Wes does not laugh himself. No audience/crowd SFX.
5. After asking for a show of hands, the pause is ROOM_HANDS (a 3.0s silent hold). No audience sound effects ever.
6. No [laughs] tag, no self-laugh, no [beat] marks, no stage directions.
7. Tone tags: NEVER use [quietly] (it comes out whispered and sounds spliced). Rare [warmly] [dry] [amused] only (a handful per session), at the very start of a line. Tone shifts stay in the SAME voice and volume.
8. Contractions everywhere, plain words, connectors real speakers use without overdoing them. Relatable and personable — Wes talks to the men like one of them.

## File format (one file per session)
Plain text. Sections are recorded as separate continuous takes, so:
- Each section starts with a line `# SECTION: <short title>`.
- Each section holds 1,500 to 1,900 characters of spoken text (never over 2,000), and splits only at a topic change.
- Every other line is `spoken text|pause`, where pause is a number of seconds or ROOM_HANDS or ROOM_CHUCKLE.
- The last line of a section uses a topic-change pause (1.0 to 1.4).
- Nothing else in the file.
