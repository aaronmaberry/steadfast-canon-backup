## 5. Where Were You

Can I tell you what he asked me. Years ago an older man, in a group I belonged to, asked me something I still don't enjoy. He'd earned the right. Maybe two. I'd cleared a pile of messages like a hero. I gave him the list the way a boy turns in homework. He nodded. Patient. Almost kind. Then he said, I didn't ask what you finished. I asked where you were. That question clears the furniture out of a man. You can be the hero of your task list and a stranger at your own table. You can, you know, live by yourself and grade yourself kindly, because the walls don't argue. The faucet got fixed. I was still not there. I spent a stretch of my twenties waiting on a feeling.
